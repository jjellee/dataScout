# Wayfair Inc. — Q3 2024 (2024-11-01)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 2024 demonstrated resilience with continued market share gains despite a challenging home category and macroeconomic headwinds.
- Cost discipline enabled a mid-single-digit adjusted EBITDA margin for the second consecutive quarter.
- Strategic initiatives included a brand refresh, the launch of a large-format store, and the rollout of the Wayfair Rewards loyalty program.
- Focus remains on driving adjusted EBITDA in excess of equity-based compensation and CapEx.


### Financial highlights

- Net revenue declined 2% year-over-year and 7.5% sequentially from Q2, in line with seasonal patterns.
- Orders fell 6.1% year-over-year, partially offset by a 4.4% increase in average order value (AOV).
- Gross margin was 30.3% of net revenue, at the lower end of the 30%-31% target range.
- Adjusted EBITDA reached $119 million (4.1% margin), marking the second straight quarter of mid-single-digit margins.
- Free cash flow was negative $9 million, with $49 million from operations and $58 million in CapEx.
- Ended Q3 with $1.3 billion in cash and $1.9 billion in total liquidity, prior to an $800 million high-yield debt raise in October.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q4 revenue expected to be down low single digits year-over-year, with sequential seasonality similar to last year.
- Gross margin guided to the lower end of 30%-31% as price investments continue.
- Advertising spend to rise to 12%-13% of net revenue, reflecting high-value opportunities.
- SOTG&A projected at $400-$410 million for Q4.
- Q4 adjusted EBITDA margin expected in the 2%-4% range.
- Commitment to at least 50% growth in 2024 adjusted EBITDA dollars.
- 2025 adjusted EBITDA dollars expected to exceed 2024, driven by market share gains and ongoing cost discipline.


### Segment performance

- Perigold luxury platform reported strong growth, contributing positively to AOV.
- International operations showed EBITDA improvement versus the first half of the year, reflecting global cost discipline.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Gross margin: 30.3% of net revenue.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin: 4.1% in Q3.
- Advertising: 12.3% of net revenue in Q3, expected to rise in Q4.
- SOTG&A: $388 million in Q3, down over $70 million year-over-year.
- Free cash flow: negative $9 million in Q3.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Persistent macroeconomic headwinds, including a historic slowdown in the housing market and consumer spending caution.
- Uncertainty around the timing and impact of a category recovery.
- Potential impacts from tariffs and trade policy changes, though the supply chain is now more diversified.
- Elevated advertising costs during the election season, though spend is managed quantitatively.


### Significant events and developments

- Launched Wayfair Rewards loyalty program, aiming to increase order frequency and customer retention.
- Opened first large-format branded store, with positive initial results.
- Closed inaugural $800 million high-yield debt offering to pre-fund 2025 convertible maturities.
- Continued cost reductions, with SOTG&A down for nine consecutive quarters.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Focused on deleveraging, using proceeds from debt raise and free cash flow to meet upcoming obligations.
- CapEx discipline maintained, with logistics network investments now in maintenance mode and selective retail expansion.


### Market and industry conditions

- Home category remains pressured by low housing turnover and consumer focus away from large-ticket home purchases.
- Market remains fragmented, with a few players (including Amazon and HomeGoods) also gaining share.
- Signs of pent-up demand, but timing of a rebound remains uncertain.
- Industry has adapted to tariff risks with diversified sourcing.
