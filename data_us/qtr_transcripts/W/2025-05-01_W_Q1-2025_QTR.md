# Wayfair Inc. — Q1 2025 (2025-05-01)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Achieved flat net revenue year-over-year in Q1 2025, with U.S. segment up 1.6% and international down 10.9% due to German business exit.
- Outperformed peers and gained market share despite ongoing category contraction and macroeconomic volatility.
- Supplier competition and platform flexibility provided insulation against tariff headwinds.
- Significant operational improvements, including cost reductions and technology team reorganization.
- Strengthened capital structure with $700M high-yield bond issuance and refinancing of revolving credit facility.


### Financial highlights

- Gross margin was 30.7% of net revenue, reflecting both a refund from the Canada Border Services Agency and investments in customer experience.
- Adjusted EBITDA was $106M, representing a 3.9% margin on net revenue.
- U.S. segment adjusted EBITDA margin was 3.9%; international was 3.7%.
- Free cash flow for Q1 was -$139M, a $60M improvement year-over-year.
- Ended quarter with $1.4B in cash and $1.8B in total liquidity.


### Outlook and guidance

- Gross margin for Q2 expected in the 30%-31% range, likely at the lower end.
- Customer service and merchant fees projected just below 4% of net revenue; advertising at 12%-13%.
- SOT G&A expected at $360M-$370M, showing continued cost compression.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin anticipated in the 4%-5% range if revenue remains flat year-over-year.


### Segment performance

- U.S. business grew 1.6% year-over-year, offsetting international decline.
- International segment down 10.9% due to German business closure.
- Supplier advertising revenue penetration grew over 50% in 2024, now above 150 basis points.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Gross margin: 30.7% of net revenue.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin: 3.9% overall, 3.9% U.S., 3.7% international.
- Free cash flow: -$139M for Q1.
- Capital expenditures: $43M in Q1.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Ongoing macroeconomic uncertainty and category volatility.
- Tariff environment remains dynamic, with potential for further changes affecting supply chain and costs.
- Uncertainty in quarter-to-date performance due to timing of holidays and events.


### Significant events and developments

- Closure of German business to reallocate resources to higher ROI areas.
- Technology team size reduction and reorganization following major replatforming milestones.
- Issued $700M in high-yield bonds and refinanced revolving credit facility, reducing near-term maturities.
- Announced new physical retail locations, including a second Wayfair store in Atlanta and two Perigold stores.


### Capital allocation and financing

- $700M high-yield bond issued at competitive rate; proceeds used to repurchase 2026 convertible notes at a 5% discount.
- Renewed $500M revolving credit facility, now extended to 2030.
- Maintains a conservative approach to liquidity and deleveraging.


### Market and industry conditions

- Home furnishings category remains in contraction, but platform's supplier diversity and substitutability provide resilience.
- Suppliers are reluctant to raise prices due to competitive dynamics and weak demand.
- Significant global manufacturing capacity outside China, supporting supply chain flexibility.
- No significant pull-forward demand observed, except in large appliances.
