# Wayfair Inc. — Q4 2025 (2026-02-19)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q4 2025 revenue grew 7.8% year-over-year (excluding Germany), driven by balanced order growth and higher average order value, despite a contracting category.
- New customer growth and healthy repeat orders marked the third consecutive quarter of customer expansion.
- Strategic focus on technology, physical retail expansion, and the Wayfair Rewards loyalty program are key growth levers for 2026 and beyond.
- Physical retail stores and loyalty initiatives are driving increased customer engagement and share of wallet.


### Financial highlights

- Net revenue up 6.9% year-over-year on a reported basis; U.S. revenue grew over 7%, international nearly 4%.
- Adjusted gross margin was 30.3% of net revenue; contribution margin reached 15.3%, up 250 bps year-over-year.
- Adjusted EBITDA for Q4 was $224 million (6.7% margin), more than double Q4 2024; full-year Adjusted EBITDA grew over 60% to $743 million.
- Free cash flow for Q4 was $145 million, a 40% year-over-year improvement.
- Ended Q4 with $1.5 billion in cash and $1.9 billion in total liquidity.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q1 2026 guided to mid-single-digit revenue growth year-over-year, with continued robust share capture.
- Gross margin expected in the 30%-31% range, likely at the low end; contribution margin targeted at ~15%.
- Adjusted EBITDA expected at 4.5%-5.5% of net revenue for Q1, showing strong year-over-year improvement.
- Continued focus on growing Adjusted EBITDA and free cash flow in 2026.


### Segment performance

- Physical retail stores, especially the Wilmette location, continue to outperform, driving new customer acquisition and higher growth in surrounding DMAs.
- Wayfair Rewards loyalty program surpassed 1 million members, now driving over 15% of U.S. revenue, with members showing higher engagement and spend.
- Specialty retail brands and international expansion of Rewards are planned for 2026.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Contribution margin improved to 15.3% in Q4, up 250 bps year-over-year.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin for the year improved by over 200 bps.
- Net leverage reduced to under 2.5x from 4x at end of 2024 and over 6x at end of 2023.
- Burn rate reduced to 4% in 2025 from 11% in 2022.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Macroeconomic headwinds persist in the home category, with the housing market recovery expected to be gradual.
- Growth strategy is not predicated on a market rebound but on internal initiatives and share capture.
- Ongoing monitoring of advertising efficiency and marketing attribution through continuous holdout testing.


### Significant events and developments

- Issued a third high-yield bond, retired 2025 notes, and repurchased over $200 million of 2027 convertible notes, offsetting over 5 million shares of potential dilution.
- Launched new physical stores in Atlanta, Columbus, and Denver, expanding the successful Chicago model.
- Announced forthcoming Wayfair Delivery Plus service and continued logistics enhancements.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Focused on reducing net leverage and managing dilution, with proactive repurchases of convertible notes.
- Free cash flow increased from $83 million in 2024 to $329 million in 2025.
- CapEx for Q4 was $57 million; 2026 CapEx expected in the $55 million-$65 million range.


### Market and industry conditions

- Home category remains highly fragmented and competitive, with overall market contracting in low single digits.
- Company continues to gain share despite category headwinds, leveraging technology, logistics, and customer experience.
- No reliance on a near-term housing market rebound; strategy is focused on internal growth drivers.
