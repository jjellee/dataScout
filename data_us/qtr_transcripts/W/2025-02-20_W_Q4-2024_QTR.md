# Wayfair Inc. — Q4 2024 (2025-02-20)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Net revenue for Q4 2024 reached $3.1 billion, up 0.2% year-over-year, driven by 1.1% growth in the U.S. segment and strong holiday performance with 8% sequential growth from Q3.
- Adjusted EBITDA for Q4 was $96 million (3.1% margin), and $453 million for the full year (3.8% margin), achieving the goal of approximately 50% year-over-year growth.
- The company exited the German market to focus on higher ROI opportunities and now holds nearly $2 billion in liquidity, with $1.3 billion in cash and equivalents.
- Investments in technology, logistics (CastleGate), and marketing (Wayborhood campaign) are expected to drive future growth and market share gains.


### Financial highlights

- Q4 net revenue: $3.1 billion, up 0.2% year-over-year; U.S. segment up 1.1%.
- Gross margin for Q4 was 30.2% of net revenue.
- Adjusted EBITDA: $96 million in Q4 (3.1% margin); $453 million for 2024 (3.8% margin).
- Free Cash Flow for Q4 was $102 million; $83 million for the full year.
- Equity-based compensation declined by nearly 35% year-over-year in 2024.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q1 2025 revenue expected to be flat to down year-over-year, with a 100 basis point drag from the German exit.
- Gross margin guidance for Q1: 30%-31%, expected near the midpoint.
- Advertising as a percentage of net revenue expected at 12%-13% in Q1.
- SOT G&A expected at $380-$390 million; Adjusted EBITDA margin anticipated in the 2%-4% range.


### Segment performance

- U.S. segment outperformed with 1.1% year-over-year growth in Q4.
- International performance impacted by the exit from Germany.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Gross margin: 30.2% in Q4.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin: 3.1% in Q4; 3.8% for 2024.
- Free Cash Flow: $102 million in Q4; $83 million for 2024.
- Advertising: 13.7% of net revenue in Q4, expected to decrease.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Ongoing macroeconomic challenges, including a depressed housing cycle, high interest rates, and inflation, continue to pressure the category.
- Uncertainty in global trade policy and supply chain dynamics, including tariffs and sourcing shifts.
- Weak market conditions expected to persist, with no immediate catalyst for recovery.


### Significant events and developments

- Exited the German market to reallocate resources to higher ROI initiatives.
- Launched new AI-driven shopping tool, Muse, to enhance personalized home shopping.
- Opened first Wayfair-branded physical store near Chicago, with plans for additional stores.
- Rolled out the Wayborhood brand campaign, driving double-digit growth in app installs and new customer orders.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Paid down a substantial portion of 2025 and 2026 convertible maturities at a discount.
- Ended Q4 with $1.3 billion in cash and equivalents, and over $1.9 billion in total liquidity.
- Positive cash from operations of $162 million in Q4; CapEx of $60 million.


### Market and industry conditions

- The home category remains under pressure due to a weak housing market and high mortgage rates.
- The market is highly fragmented, with significant opportunities for share gains.
- No significant impact from weather events in early 2025; market remains weak post-holiday.
