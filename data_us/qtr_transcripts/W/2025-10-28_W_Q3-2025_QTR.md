# Wayfair Inc. — Q3 2025 (2025-10-28)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Achieved 9% year-over-year revenue growth (excluding Germany) and over 70% year-over-year growth in adjusted EBITDA, with a 6.7% adjusted EBITDA margin, the highest outside the pandemic period.
- Share gains accelerated, driven by strong order momentum, new program launches, and technology investments, despite a sluggish housing market.
- Active customers grew sequentially for the first time since 2023; average order value increased by 2% due to mix shift toward higher-end brands and B2B.
- Outperformance attributed to structural share capture, not transitory factors like tariff pull-forward.


### Financial highlights

- Reported revenue grew 8% year-over-year; U.S. segment up 9%, international up 5%.
- Gross margin was 30.1% of net revenue; contribution margin reached 15.8%, up 150 basis points year-over-year.
- Adjusted EBITDA was $208 million (6.7% margin), up over 70% year-over-year.
- Free cash flow was $93 million, improving by over $100 million year-over-year.
- Ended the quarter with $1.2 billion in cash and $1.7 billion in total liquidity.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q4 net revenue expected to grow mid-single digits year-over-year, with a 100 basis point drag from Germany exit.
- Gross margin guided to 30%-31%, likely at the low end due to reinvestment and seasonality.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin expected in the 5.5%-6.5% range for Q4.
- SOTG&A projected at $360-$370 million; equity-based compensation of $80-$100 million, including a new CEO award.


### Segment performance

- U.S. business revenue up 9% year-over-year; international up 5%.
- Higher-end brands and specialty retail outperformed, with luxury brand Perigold showing notable strength.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Contribution margin at 15.8%, best since 2021.
- Net leverage reduced from over 4x to 2.8x trailing 12-month adjusted EBITDA.
- Mobile app revenue grew double digits; installs up nearly 40% year-over-year.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Housing market remains at multi-decade lows, with recovery dependent on long-term interest rates.
- Industry faces uncertainty from evolving tariff landscape.
- Q3 advertising leverage benefited from one-time holdout tests; not expected to recur in Q4.


### Significant events and developments

- Completed major technology replatforming, enabling faster innovation and new program launches.
- Launched and scaled programs like Wayfair Rewards, Wayfair Verified, and expanded retail stores.
- Invested in generative AI across customer experience, operations, and supplier tools, including proprietary AI features like Muse and Complete the Look.
- Repurchased $101 million of 2028 notes, reducing potential dilution and interest expense.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Used $200 million in cash to repurchase $101 million of 2028 notes, offsetting 2.2 million shares of potential dilution.
- Focused on reducing leverage and managing dilution; net leverage now at 2.8x.


### Market and industry conditions

- Category has stabilized after years of double-digit declines, now trending flat to slightly down.
- Replacement cycles and household formation continue to drive demand despite depressed housing.
- Competitive intensity among suppliers remains high, incentivizing low prices.
