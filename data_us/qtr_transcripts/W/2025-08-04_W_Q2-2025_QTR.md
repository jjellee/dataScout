# Wayfair Inc. — Q2 2025 (2025-08-04)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Achieved strong Q2 2025 results with accelerating sales, market share gains, and expanding profitability, marking the highest revenue growth rate since early 2021, excluding Germany.
- Strategic focus on balancing future investments with current profitability, maximizing EBITDA and free cash flow.
- Growth driven by initiatives like Wayfair Verified, Wayfair Rewards, and expansion in physical retail, with positive customer response and increased repeat purchases.
- Enhanced logistics through CastleGate, with increased supplier participation and multi-channel 3PL services scaling rapidly.


### Financial highlights

- Net revenue grew 5% year-over-year, and 6% excluding Germany; U.S. up over 5%, international up over 3%.
- Gross margin reached 30.1% of net revenue.
- Adjusted EBITDA was $205 million, a 6.3% margin, with U.S. segment at 7.8%.
- Free cash flow was $230 million, the strongest since Q3 2020.
- Ended Q2 with $1.4 billion in cash and $1.8 billion in total liquidity.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q3 2025 revenue expected to grow low to mid-single digits year-over-year, with 100 bps drag from Germany exit.
- Gross margin guided to lower end of 30%-31% range.
- Customer service and merchant fees just below 4%; advertising 11%-12% of net revenue.
- SOTG&A expected at $360-$370 million; adjusted EBITDA margin anticipated at 5%-6%.


### Segment performance

- U.S. business delivered over 5% revenue growth; international segment up over 3% year-over-year.
- Specialty retail brands and Wayfair Professional saw strong double-digit growth.
- Physical retail stores in Chicago, Houston, and upcoming locations in Atlanta, New York, and Denver driving omnichannel momentum.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Gross margin: 30.1% of net revenue.
- Customer service and merchant fees: 3.6% of net revenue.
- Advertising: 11.4% of net revenue, with sequential declines.
- Flow through rate: 15.2% (gross margin less customer service/merchant fees and advertising).
- SOTG&A: $370 million, down $30 million year-over-year.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin: 6.3% overall, 7.8% U.S.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Macroeconomic environment remains flat to down low single digits, with housing turnover still depressed.
- No evidence of demand pull-forward from tariff concerns; higher-end market stronger than mass.
- Supplier competition keeps price increases in check despite cost pressures.


### Significant events and developments

- Expanded CastleGate logistics network, with 40% year-over-year increase in forwarding volume and 25% revenue penetration.
- Launched multi-channel 3PL service, now used by hundreds of suppliers, creating new revenue streams and network efficiencies.
- Retired nearly all 2025 and 2026 notes, leaving a clean balance sheet until September 2027.
- Technology replatforming completed, enabling faster feature development and generative AI-powered enhancements.


### Market and industry conditions

- Home category market has stabilized after several years of decline, now flat to down low single digits.
- Industry remains highly fragmented, with a few large players (including Amazon and HomeGoods) gaining share.
- Increased engagement with AI-driven shopping assistants and influencer marketing channels.
