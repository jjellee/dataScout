# Wayfair Inc. — Q1 2026 (2026-04-30)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Net revenue grew 7.4% year-over-year in Q1 2026, with U.S. up 7.5% and international up 6%.
- Outperformed a home furnishings category that remains in a cyclical downturn, gaining significant market share.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin reached 5.2%, the best Q1 result in five years.
- Continued investment in programs like Rewards, Verified, and Stores to drive customer engagement and loyalty.
- Strategic focus on profitable growth, leveraging technology, logistics, and marketing to widen share gains.


### Financial highlights

- Gross margin was 30.1% of net revenue in Q1.
- Contribution margin reached 15%, up 70 basis points year-over-year.
- Adjusted EBITDA was $151 million, with a 5.2% margin, up 130 basis points year-over-year.
- Free cash flow was negative $106 million, an improvement of $33 million from Q1 2025.
- Ended Q1 with $1.1 billion in cash and $1.5 billion in total liquidity.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q2 2026 revenue expected to grow mid-single digits year-over-year, despite ongoing category contraction.
- Gross margin guidance for Q2 is 29.5%-30.5% of net revenue.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin expected in the 6%-7% range for Q2.
- Continued focus on expanding share gains and profitability regardless of macro normalization.


### Segment performance

- U.S. segment revenue grew 7.5% year-over-year; international segment up 6%.
- Canada achieved highest non-COVID market share, with improved delivery speeds and local product focus.
- U.K. catalog expanded to over 6 million products, with 60% of large parcel orders delivered within two days.
- International markets benefiting from global logistics, technology, and marketing investments.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Gross margin: 30.1% of net revenue.
- Contribution margin: 15% in Q1.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin: 5.2% in Q1.
- Gross leverage reduced to 3.8x, down three turns year-over-year.
- Active customer growth turned positive year-over-year.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Home furnishings category remains in a cyclical downturn, down 25%-30% from 2021 peak.
- Elevated energy and fuel prices impacting consumer spending and fulfillment costs.
- Macro volatility and consumer sentiment continue to pose challenges.


### Significant events and developments

- Repurchased over $300 million in convertible bonds, reducing potential dilution by over 4 million shares.
- Opened new Atlanta store, with more stores planned in 2026.
- Launched Wayfair Rewards in Canada and the U.K., driving increased engagement and repeat purchases.
- Accelerated deployment of AI and agentic technologies to enhance merchandising and customer experience.


### Capital allocation and financing

- $1.1 billion in cash and equivalents at quarter end.
- $1.5 billion in total liquidity including undrawn revolver.
- Reduced convertible bond principal to just over $700 million on 2027/2028 notes and $39 million on 2026 notes.
- Ongoing focus on managing dilution and leverage, with potential for future share repurchases.


### Market and industry conditions

- Home furnishings category contracted double digits in 2022-2024, now down low to mid-single digits in 2025-2026.
- Category remains below pre-pandemic growth trends, with uncertain timing for mean reversion.
- Competitive intensity remains high, especially at the commodity end, but differentiation is stronger in mid-to-upper segments.
