# Wayfair Inc. — Q2 2026 (2026-08-04)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q2 2026 delivered 7.5% net revenue growth, with U.S. revenue up 8.7% and international down 1.3%.
- Orders increased 6% year-over-year and over 12% sequentially from Q1, with active customers up 3%.
- Specialty retail brands grew nearly 20%, and Perigold (Luxury) exceeded 35% growth.
- Perigold now exceeds $400M in annual sales, with margins expanding despite investments in stores and brand.
- Management remains confident in accelerating top-line growth and expanding profitability.


### Financial highlights

- Gross margin was 30.0% of net revenue in Q2.
- Contribution margin reached 15.3%; EBITDA was $242M (6.9% margin), the best since 2021.
- Free cash flow was $301M, up over 30% year-over-year.
- Cash and equivalents ended at $1.1B, with $1.6B total liquidity.
- Stock-based compensation down 40% over two years.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q3 2026 revenue expected to grow at a high-single-digit rate.
- Gross margin guided to 29.5%-30.5%, likely at the lower end due to customer experience investments.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin expected in the 6%-7% range.
- CapEx projected at $60M-$70M; equity-based compensation at $65M-$75M.


### Segment performance

- U.S. segment posted nearly 9% revenue growth, best post-COVID performance.
- International segment declined 1.3% due to weaker consumer sentiment in Canada and the U.K.
- Perigold and specialty retail brands outpaced overall market growth, with Perigold's customer base up 20%.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Gross margin: 30.0% of net revenue.
- Contribution margin: 15.3%.
- EBITDA margin: 6.9%.
- Free cash flow: $301M, up 30% year-over-year.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Macro uncertainty and depressed housing turnover persist, especially outside the U.S.
- Promotional environment remains intense in mass market segments.
- Currency and international rollout timing may impact results.


### Significant events and developments

- Issued $400M high-yield note to redeem 2028 convertible bonds, nearly completing shift away from convertible debt.
- Received a two-notch credit rating upgrade from Fitch.
- Opened new stores in Atlanta and Columbus, with Denver opening in the fall and more planned for 2027.
- Launched new loyalty and trade programs, and expanded AI-driven initiatives for merchandising and imagery.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Free cash flow used to repurchase convertible bonds; future focus on share repurchases to offset stock-based compensation.
- CapEx remains in low single digits as a percentage of revenue, including store build-outs and logistics.
- Only $39M of 2026 bonds and $229M of 2027 bonds remain outstanding.


### Market and industry conditions

- U.S. home category saw first flat-to-slightly-positive year-over-year growth since 2021.
- Luxury and specialty segments outperforming mass market, which remains highly promotional.
- Competitive landscape remains fragmented, with Wayfair leveraging scale and technology for share gains.
