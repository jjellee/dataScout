# Zebra Technologies Corporation — Q2 2026 (2026-08-04)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Achieved record Q2 results with broad-based growth and significantly increased profitability, exceeding outlook due to strong execution and positive demand trends across all segments and regions.
- Raised full-year outlook, supported by continued business momentum and robust multi-year pipeline, especially in transportation & logistics and manufacturing.
- AI-powered solutions and recent acquisitions, notably Elo Touch, are driving synergies and enhancing the value proposition in digitizing and automating frontline operations.
- Strategic priorities focus on sustainable growth, innovation, and disciplined capital allocation to drive long-term shareholder value.


### Financial highlights

- Q2 sales exceeded $1.5 billion, up 20.4% year-over-year (9.2% organic growth).
- Adjusted EBITDA margin reached 27.7%, including a $73 million tariff recovery; excluding this, margin expanded by two points.
- Non-GAAP diluted EPS was $6.35, a 76% increase year-over-year.
- Adjusted gross margin improved 540 basis points to 53.3%.
- Free cash flow year-to-date was $361 million; debt leverage ratio at 1.9x with $925 million credit capacity.


### Outlook and guidance

- Full-year sales growth expected between 14%-16%, a three-point increase at midpoint from prior outlook.
- Full-year adjusted EBITDA margin guided to 23.5%-24%; non-GAAP diluted EPS expected between $20.75 and $21.25.
- Free cash flow for the year projected at least $1 billion, with ~100% conversion rate.
- Q3 sales growth guidance of 17%-20% (about 8% organic at midpoint); adjusted EBITDA margin expected at ~22%.


### Segment performance

- Connected Frontline segment grew nearly 26% (7.5% organic), led by mobile computing and Elo acquisition.
- Asset Visibility & Automation segment grew 11.4%, driven by printing and machine vision.
- Retail, manufacturing, and healthcare end markets saw double-digit growth; healthcare was the highest growth vertical.
- EMEA sales grew 7%, Asia-Pacific 13%, Latin America 15%, and North America 9%.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted EBITDA margin: 27.7% (Q2), 23.5%-24% (full-year guidance).
- Adjusted gross margin: 53.3% (Q2).
- Debt leverage ratio: 1.9x.
- Free cash flow: $361 million YTD; $1 billion full-year expected.
- Share repurchases: $568 million in H1, with $700 million planned for the year.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Memory supply and cost environment remains dynamic and challenging, with ongoing mitigation strategies including supplier diversification and price increases.
- Guidance incorporates potential supply constraints, particularly for memory components.
- Geopolitical challenges in the Middle East impacting EMEA growth.


### Significant events and developments

- $73 million IEEPA tariff recovery benefited Q2 results.
- Substantial restructuring actions completed in Q2 to enhance operating expense leverage.
- Elo Touch acquisition delivering strong growth and commercial synergies.
- Proactive price increases and supplier co-planning to offset $120 million memory cost headwind.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Repurchased $568 million of shares in H1, with an additional $150 million planned for H2.
- Maintained a balanced approach to capital allocation, prioritizing investments in innovation and shareholder returns.
- Debt leverage remains modest at 1.9x, supporting financial flexibility.


### Market and industry conditions

- Strong demand for automation, digitization, and AI solutions across retail, manufacturing, healthcare, and logistics.
- Customers investing in RFID, machine vision, and AI to enhance productivity and visibility.
- Memory market volatility impacting supply chain and pricing strategies.
- EMEA region resilient despite Middle East softness; robust growth in Asia-Pacific and Latin America.
