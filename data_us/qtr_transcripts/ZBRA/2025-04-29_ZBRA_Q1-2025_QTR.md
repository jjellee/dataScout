# Zebra Technologies Corporation — Q1 2025 (2025-04-29)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Delivered Q1 results above outlook, with strong execution and broad-based growth across all major product categories and regions, despite macroeconomic uncertainty and tariff headwinds.
- Maintained market leadership and advanced portfolio through innovation and strategic acquisitions, notably the Photoneo 3D machine vision company.
- Demand environment remained positive into Q2, with no significant changes in customer purchasing behavior observed.


### Financial highlights

- Q1 sales exceeded $1.3 billion, up 12% year-over-year on a constant currency basis.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin reached 22.3%, a 240 basis point increase year-over-year.
- Non-GAAP diluted EPS was $4.02, up 42% from the prior year and above guidance.
- Adjusted gross margin increased 150 basis points to 49.6%.
- Free cash flow was $158 million in Q1; net debt to Adjusted EBITDA leverage ratio at 1.2.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q2 sales growth expected between 4-7%, with Adjusted EBITDA margin around 19% and EPS of $3.00-$3.50.
- Full-year sales guidance unchanged at 3-7% growth, but Adjusted EBITDA margin outlook reduced by 100 basis points to 20-21% due to higher tariff costs.
- Full-year non-GAAP EPS expected in the range of $13.75-$14.75; free cash flow at least $700 million, implying >90% conversion.


### Segment performance

- Asset Intelligence & Tracking (AIT) segment sales grew 18% year-over-year.
- Enterprise Visibility & Mobility (EVM) segment sales increased 9%.
- North America sales up 7%, EMEA up 18%, Asia-Pacific up 13%, Latin America up 18%.
- Double-digit growth in most verticals; manufacturing up high single digits, lagging other sectors.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted EBITDA margin: 22.3% in Q1, expected 19% in Q2, 20-21% for full year.
- Adjusted gross margin: 49.6% in Q1.
- Net debt to Adjusted EBITDA: 1.2x at Q1 end.
- Free cash flow conversion: >90% expected for full year.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Significant headwinds from U.S. import tariffs, with a modeled $70 million gross profit impact for 2025, $50 million higher than prior guidance.
- Ongoing uncertainty in global trade policy and potential for further tariff changes, especially regarding electronics and semiconductor content.
- Customer sentiment and project activity remain positive, but uncertainty around tariffs is weighing on decision-making.


### Significant events and developments

- Acquisition of Photoneo for $62 million to expand 3D machine vision capabilities, expected to contribute 30 basis points to 2025 sales growth.
- Launched Aurora Velocity Scan Tunnel and AI suite for mobile computing, enhancing automation and workflow solutions.
- Increased stock repurchases: $125 million in Q1 and $75 million in April, with continued activity expected.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Strong balance sheet with nearly $900 million in cash and $1.5 billion in credit capacity.
- Capital-light business model with flexible cost structure and outsourced manufacturing.
- Ongoing share repurchases to take advantage of market volatility.


### Market and industry conditions

- Secular trends in digitization, automation, and supply chain visibility driving demand.
- Retail, e-commerce, transportation logistics, and healthcare verticals showing robust growth.
- Manufacturing vertical recovering but lagging other sectors; machine vision market remains challenging.
- No evidence of demand pull-forward or inventory build-up by customers or distributors.
