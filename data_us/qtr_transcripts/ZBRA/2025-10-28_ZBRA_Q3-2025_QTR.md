# Zebra Technologies Corporation — Q3 2025 (2025-10-28)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Delivered Q3 sales of $1.3 billion, up 5% year-over-year, with adjusted EBITDA margin of 21.6% and non-GAAP EPS of $3.88, an 11% increase.
- Growth driven by strong demand, lower-than-expected tariffs, and operating expense leverage, with notable strength in Asia Pacific, Latin America, and North America.
- Retail and e-commerce were key growth drivers, while healthcare faced tough comparisons and manufacturing remained soft.
- Recent acquisition of Elo Touch Solutions expands addressable market and enhances connected frontline offerings.


### Financial highlights

- Q3 sales increased 5% year-over-year, with broad-based growth across most product categories and regions.
- Adjusted gross margin declined 90 basis points to 48.2%, mainly due to higher U.S. import tariffs.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin improved by 20 basis points to 21.6%.
- Non-GAAP diluted EPS rose 11% to $3.88.
- Year-to-date, over $300 million of stock repurchased and acquisitions of Photoneo and Elo Touch Solutions completed.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q4 sales growth expected between 8% and 11%, with 8.5 points from acquisitions and FX.
- Q4 adjusted EBITDA margin projected at 22%, with non-GAAP EPS between $4.20 and $4.40.
- Full-year sales growth forecast at 8%, adjusted EBITDA margin at 21.5%, and non-GAAP EPS at $15.80, a 17% year-over-year increase.
- $500 million share repurchase planned through Q3 2026.


### Segment performance

- Enterprise Visibility & Mobility (EVM) grew 2%, led by mobile computing; asset intelligence & tracking (AIT) grew 11%, led by RFID and printing.
- North America sales up 6%, Asia Pacific up 23%, Latin America up 8%, EMEA down 3%.
- EVM saw strong mobile computing growth but a decline in data capture due to tough comparisons.
- AIT benefited from volume leverage and higher-margin RFID growth.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted EBITDA margin: 21.6% in Q3, expected 22% in Q4.
- Adjusted gross margin: 48.2%, down 90 bps year-over-year.
- Non-GAAP EPS: $3.88 in Q3, $15.80 full-year guidance.
- Organic revenue growth for the year expected at nearly 6%.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Customers remain cautious due to macroeconomic uncertainty, uneven demand across geographies and verticals, and ongoing trade policy impacts.
- Tariffs continue to impact gross margin, though mitigation actions are underway and expected to be fully effective by mid-2026.
- EMEA and manufacturing verticals remain soft; timing of large projects and year-end spend is closely monitored.


### Significant events and developments

- Acquisition of Elo Touch Solutions adds $100 million to Q4 revenue and expands self-service and POS capabilities.
- Elo expected to maintain 5%-7% growth profile, with new wins in POS and self-serve kiosks.
- AI initiatives progressing, with pilots underway and first revenues expected in 2026, ramping in 2027.
- RFID remains a key growth driver, with double-digit growth and expanding use cases.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Over $300 million in stock repurchased year-to-date; $500 million buyback committed for next 12 months.
- Acquisitions funded with cash and existing credit facility; $1.5 billion credit capacity maintained.
- Tax changes expected to reduce cash taxes by $50-$60 million this year and over $200 million in the next two years.


### Market and industry conditions

- Secular trends in digitization, automation, AI, and on-demand economy support long-term growth.
- Retail and e-commerce remain strong; transportation and logistics normalized; manufacturing and healthcare face challenges.
- Asia Pacific and Latin America show robust growth; EMEA remains mixed with softness in Germany and France.
- Supply chain resilience improved, with China imports reduced below 20% for U.S. market.
