# Paymentus Holdings, Inc. — Q2 2025 (2025-08-04)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Delivered a strong Q2 2025, exceeding expectations in all key business areas, with substantial bookings and backlog providing visibility into 2025 and 2026.
- Revenue run rate surpassed $1 billion, with confidence in achieving multi-billion dollar revenue organically in coming years.
- Platform capabilities, pay-per-use model, and operating leverage position the company for long-term disruption and growth.


### Financial highlights

- Q2 2025 revenue was $280.1 million, up 41.9% year-over-year, driven by increased billers and transactions.
- Contribution profit reached $93.5 million, up 22.3% year-over-year; adjusted EBITDA was $31.7 million, up 40.7% year-over-year, with a 33.9% margin.
- Processed 175.8 million transactions in Q2, up 25.2% year-over-year; average price per transaction rose from $1.41 to $1.59.
- Non-GAAP net income was $19.3 million ($0.15/share), up 50% year-over-year.
- Free cash flow for Q2 was $22.5 million; total cash and equivalents at quarter-end were $270 million, with no debt.


### Outlook and guidance

- Raised full-year 2025 guidance: revenue $1.123–$1.132 billion (29.3% growth at midpoint), contribution profit $369–$373 million, adjusted EBITDA $123–$127 million.
- Q3 2025 guidance: revenue $278–$282 million (20.9%–21.8% growth), contribution profit $92–$94 million, adjusted EBITDA $30–$32 million.
- Rule of 40 expected at 52%–54% for 2025.
- Long-term CAGR model remains 20% top-line growth and 20–30% adjusted EBITDA growth.


### Segment performance

- Large enterprise segment showed particular strength, with wins across utilities, government, telecom, banking, insurance, and education.
- Partnership ecosystem expanded with new channel partners in financial services and telecom.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Contribution margin was 33.4% (down from 38.7% prior year) due to higher volume enterprise billers.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin improved by 440 basis points year-over-year; incremental adjusted EBITDA margin was 53.8%.
- Day sales outstanding improved to 31 days from 33 days sequentially.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Seasonality and timing of large enterprise biller launches can impact quarterly growth trends.
- Bad debt expense remains low and is not considered significant.
- Working capital needs may fluctuate, affecting free cash flow forecasting.


### Significant events and developments

- Exceeded Rule of 40 for the quarter at 56%.
- Strong bookings and backlog growth, especially in large enterprise customers, provide visibility into 2026.
- Ongoing investments in R&D, sales, and onboarding to support growth.


### Capital allocation and financing

- No debt; strong cash position supports organic growth and potential M&A opportunities.
- CapEx primarily for capitalized software, running at ~$9 million per quarter.


### Market and industry conditions

- Market trends, including agentic AI, are moving in favor of the company's platform and business model.
- Success in utilities vertical is expanding to government, insurance, and other sectors.
- Operating leverage and scalable platform enable continued profitable growth.
