# Paymentus Holdings, Inc. — Q4 2025 (2026-02-23)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Achieved record revenue of $1.2 billion for full year 2025, surpassing guidance and marking 37.3% year-over-year growth.
- Quadrupled revenue over five years, significantly outpacing long-term CAGR model of 20%.
- Strong performance driven by large enterprise customer growth, innovation, and expanding market share.
- Ended 2025 with substantial backlog and strong bookings, providing high visibility into 2026.


### Financial highlights

- Q4 2025 revenue reached $330.5 million, up 28.1% year-over-year; full year revenue was $1.2 billion, up 37.3%.
- Q4 contribution profit was $106.9 million, up 24% year-over-year; full year contribution profit was $386.3 million, up 23.8%.
- Adjusted EBITDA for Q4 was $39.9 million (37.3% margin), up 46.3% year-over-year; full year adjusted EBITDA was $137.4 million (35.6% margin), up 45.9%.
- Non-GAAP net income for Q4 was $25.4 million ($0.20/share); full year non-GAAP net income was $84.9 million ($0.66/share), up 51.2%.
- Free cash flow for 2025 was $125 million, up over 360% year-over-year; ended year with $324.5 million in cash and no debt.


### Outlook and guidance

- 2026 revenue guidance: $1.39–$1.41 billion (17–17.8% growth); contribution profit: $442–$452 million (15.7–17% growth); adjusted EBITDA: $157–$167 million (17.9–21.5% growth).
- Q1 2026 revenue expected at $330–$340 million (22–24% growth); contribution profit $103–$105 million; adjusted EBITDA $36–$38 million.
- Guidance implies Rule of 40 of 50–54 for 2026, reflecting strong growth and profitability.
- Top end of 2026 guidance achievable without signing new clients.


### Segment performance

- Large enterprise customers drove higher average payment amounts and revenue per transaction, contributing to margin expansion.
- Utilities and insurance verticals were key contributors to revenue per transaction growth.
- Expanded and diversified client base across utilities, telecom, government, education, banking, property management, healthcare, and insurance.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Rule of 40 exceeded: 61 in Q4 2025, 59 for full year.
- Contribution profit per transaction in Q4 was $0.55, up from $0.52 prior year.
- Days Sales Outstanding improved to 28 days from 43 days year-over-year.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin improved to 37.3% in Q4.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Variability in contribution profit due to changes in payment mix and average payment amount; treated as secondary metric.
- Prudent guidance approach due to limited experience with new large enterprise billers and potential seasonality.


### Significant events and developments

- Achieved $1 billion revenue milestone for the first time.
- Onboarded several large enterprises and expanded channel partnerships in key verticals.
- Continued investment in AI and no-code platforms to drive innovation and customer value.


### Capital allocation and financing

- No debt; strong cash position of $324.5 million at year-end.
- Free cash flow generation supports organic growth and potential M&A opportunities.
- Paid $14.9 million in income taxes and generated $9.5 million in interest income in 2025.


### Market and industry conditions

- Industry remains ripe for disruption, with legacy bill pay solutions losing share to modern platforms.
- Only 4.3% market share captured, indicating significant TAM remaining.
- AI and agentic payments seen as major future growth drivers.
