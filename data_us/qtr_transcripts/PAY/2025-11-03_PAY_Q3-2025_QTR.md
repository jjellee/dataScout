# Paymentus Holdings, Inc. — Q3 2025 (2025-11-03)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Delivered a strong third quarter, exceeding expectations in all key business areas with exceptional year-over-year growth and robust bookings and backlog, providing high visibility for the remainder of 2025 and into 2026.
- Continued successful onboarding of large enterprise and mid-market clients across diverse verticals, supporting a vertical expansion strategy and market capture mode.
- Platform innovation, bidirectional payment rails, and AI-driven agentic commerce are driving further TAM expansion and future monetization opportunities.


### Financial highlights

- Q3 2025 revenue was $310.7 million, up 34.2% year-over-year, driven by new billers and higher transaction values.
- Contribution Profit reached $98.3 million, up 22.8% year-over-year; Adjusted EBITDA was $35.9 million, up 45.9%, with a record Adjusted EBITDA margin of 61.7%.
- Processed 182.3 million transactions, up 17.4% year-over-year; average price per transaction rose 14.1% to $1.70.
- Non-GAAP net income was $22.6 million ($0.17/share), up from $14.7 million ($0.12/share) in the prior year.
- Ended Q3 with $291.5 million in cash, up $21.5 million sequentially, and working capital of $321.4 million.


### Outlook and guidance

- Raised full-year 2025 guidance: revenue of $1.173–$1.178 billion (34.9% growth), Contribution Profit of $378–$380 million (21.5% growth), Adjusted EBITDA of $132–$134 million (41.2% growth).
- Q4 2025 guidance: revenue of $307–$312 million (20–21% growth), Contribution Profit of $99–$101 million (16–17.2% growth), Adjusted EBITDA of $34–$36 million (28.2–31.9% growth).
- Rule of 40 score expected in the 56–57 range for the year.
- High visibility into 2026, with similar growth rates expected as in 2025.


### Segment performance

- Strongest growth from large enterprise and upper mid-market clients, with new signings and onboarding across insurance, government, utilities, telecom, property management, finance, banking, credit unions, education, and healthcare.
- Utilities remain 50% of revenue, but expansion into new verticals and B2B is accelerating.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Contribution margin was 31.6%, down from 34.5% due to large enterprise client mix, but Contribution Profit per transaction improved 3.8% to $0.54.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin reached a record 61.7%; days sales outstanding held steady at 31 days, down 30% year-over-year.
- Free cash flow conversion exceeded 100% over the trailing four quarters, driven by high-quality customers and improved DSO.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Contribution Profit and pricing may vary quarter-to-quarter due to client mix, payment mix, and average payment amount.
- Volume discounts for large clients are offset by operating leverage and higher Adjusted EBITDA.


### Significant events and developments

- Early launch of several large enterprise customers originally slated for 2026, contributing to Q3 outperformance.
- Onboarded a large B2B client in a new vertical, demonstrating platform flexibility and opening new market opportunities.
- Added new channel partners in government, telecom, and property management.


### Capital allocation and financing

- $35.1 million cash generated from operations in Q3, offset by $10.1 million in capitalized software investment and $3.4 million for employee RSU settlements.
- 129.2 million diluted shares outstanding, flat sequentially.


### Market and industry conditions

- Industry trends, including AI and agentic commerce, are aligning with platform capabilities, supporting further TAM expansion.
- Increasing demand from large enterprises seeking to modernize legacy and in-house payment systems.
