# Paymentus Holdings, Inc. — Q1 2026 (2026-05-04)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Achieved record Q1 2026 revenue of $358.4M, up 30.2% year-over-year, with strong growth across all key metrics.
- Contribution profit rose 25.2% to $109.7M; adjusted EBITDA grew 41.5% to $42.4M, with a 38.7% margin.
- Exceeded the Rule of 40, reaching 64, reflecting robust execution and high-quality earnings.
- Announced launch of AI-native Service Commerce platform, including BillWallet and Billeo, aimed at transforming service provider-customer interactions.


### Financial highlights

- Processed 203.4M transactions in Q1, up 17.4% year-over-year; average revenue per transaction increased 11% to $1.76.
- Adjusted gross profit was $92.4M, up 27.3% year-over-year; non-GAAP net income was $26.9M or $0.21 per share, up 50% year-over-year.
- Contribution margin was 30.6%, slightly down due to higher mix of large enterprise billers, but offset by lower OpEx margin.
- Free cash flow for the quarter was $20.9M; ended Q1 with $342.1M in cash and no debt.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q2 2026 guidance: revenue $340M–$350M, contribution profit $108M–$111M, adjusted EBITDA $38M–$40M, Rule of 40 at 51%–55%.
- Full year 2026 guidance raised: revenue $1.425B–$1.44B (up 2.3% from prior midpoint), contribution profit $450M–$457M, adjusted EBITDA $165M–$172M.
- Guidance reflects 19.7%–20.4% revenue growth and 22.6%–25.2% adjusted EBITDA growth.
- No material revenue from new AI-native products expected in 2026; momentum expected to impact 2027 and beyond.


### Segment performance

- Expanded and diversified customer base with new clients in utilities, insurance, telecom, government, property management, finance, banking, education, and healthcare.
- Signed new channel partners in education and telecom; strong onboarding of backlog and robust pipeline.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Rule of 40 reached 64 in Q1, a record for the company.
- Contribution profit per transaction improved to $0.54 from $0.51 year-over-year.
- Days sales outstanding at 29 days, better than expected.
- Working capital at $365.4M, up 6.7% sequentially.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Seasonality and onboarding timelines of large enterprise customers introduce some uncertainty in quarterly results.
- Energy price volatility now has only a modest impact due to vertical diversification and enhanced pricing strategies.
- Working capital fluctuations are temporary and expected to normalize within the year.


### Significant events and developments

- Launched AI-native Service Commerce platform with patented BillWallet and Billeo products.
- Early BillWallet adoption: 100K users across 1,000+ cities with high conversion and no marketing spend.
- Patent portfolio expanded, with granted patents in the U.S. and internationally.


### Capital allocation and financing

- No debt; strong cash position supports organic growth and potential M&A opportunities.
- $30.5M cash generated from operations, $9.4M invested in capitalized software, $3.3M for employee RSUs.


### Market and industry conditions

- Competitive landscape evolving, but innovation and platform strength position the company favorably.
- Utilities remain a key vertical, but diversification across multiple sectors continues.
- Enhanced pricing models and vertical mix reduce exposure to energy price fluctuations.
