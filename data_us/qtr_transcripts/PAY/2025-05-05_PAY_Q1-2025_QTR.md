# Paymentus Holdings, Inc. — Q1 2025 (2025-05-05)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Achieved strong year-over-year growth in revenue, contribution profit, and adjusted EBITDA for Q1 2025, driven by robust sales momentum and higher transaction activity from both new and existing clients.
- Ended the quarter with strong bookings and a solid implementation backlog, supporting a positive outlook for the remainder of 2025 and beyond.
- Business is well-positioned in essential, non-discretionary bill payment verticals, providing resilience across economic cycles.


### Financial highlights

- Q1 2025 revenue was $275.2 million, up 48.9% year-over-year, primarily from increased clients and transactions.
- Contribution profit reached $87.6 million, a 26.3% increase year-over-year.
- Adjusted EBITDA was $30 million, up 51.3% year-over-year, with a record margin of 34.2%.
- Processed 173.2 million transactions in Q1, up 28% year-over-year; average revenue per transaction rose to $1.59 from $1.37.
- Non-GAAP net income was $17.6 million ($0.14 per share), compared to $11.8 million ($0.09 per share) in the prior year.
- Free cash flow was a record $41.1 million, with total cash and equivalents at $249.6 million and no debt.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q2 2025 guidance: revenue of $255–$260 million, contribution profit of $89.5–$91.5 million, adjusted EBITDA of $28–$30 million.
- Full year 2025 guidance: revenue of $1.075–$1.09 billion (24.2% year-over-year growth at midpoint), contribution profit of $363–$369 million, adjusted EBITDA of $118–$122 million (27.4% year-over-year growth at midpoint).
- Rule of 40 guidance: 48–52 for Q2 and 49–51 for full year 2025.
- Long-term targets reaffirmed: 20% annual revenue growth and 20–30% annual adjusted EBITDA growth.


### Segment performance

- Signed new clients across utilities, government, telecom, banking, insurance, and education, reflecting platform diversity.
- Onboarded clients in multiple verticals, including healthcare, property management, and government services.
- Large enterprise billers contributed to higher average revenue per transaction and are expected to influence future growth.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Contribution margin was 31.8% in Q1, down from 37.5% a year ago due to a higher mix of large enterprise billers.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin reached a record 34.2%.
- Rule of 40 achieved at 61% for Q1.
- Days Sales Outstanding improved to 33 from 43 sequentially.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Cautious guidance for large enterprise customers due to limited historical data; full four-quarter cycle needed for better visibility.
- Seasonality and payment mix can impact contribution profit and revenue trends.
- Macro uncertainty monitored, but no negative impact observed on sales cycles or implementation pace.


### Significant events and developments

- Record free cash flow generation and improved working capital efficiency.
- Expanded channel partner ecosystem, enhancing go-to-market strategy.
- Continued investments in R&D and sales to support pipeline and bookings.


### Capital allocation and financing

- No debt; strong cash position enables organic growth and selective M&A if attractive opportunities arise.
- M&A is not a primary focus; will only pursue if accretive and strategically aligned.


### Market and industry conditions

- Serving essential, non-discretionary bill payment verticals provides resilience in all economic cycles.
- No slowdown observed in consumer or business payment trends; pipeline and bookings remain strong.
- Value proposition of reducing cost to serve and improving customer experience resonates across verticals, especially in downturns.
