# Paymentus Holdings, Inc. — Q4 2024 (2025-03-10)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Achieved record fourth quarter and full year 2024 results, exceeding expectations across all key metrics.
- Strong sales momentum, robust bookings, and a significant exit backlog support confidence in future growth.
- Business model demonstrates resilience to macroeconomic headwinds due to focus on non-discretionary bill payments.
- Continued innovation and onboarding success across diverse industry verticals.


### Financial highlights

- Q4 2024 revenue reached $257.9M, up 56.5% year-over-year; full year revenue was $871.7M, up 41.9%.
- Q4 contribution profit was $86.2M, up 30% year-over-year; full year contribution profit was $312.1M, up 29.5%.
- Q4 Adjusted EBITDA was $27.3M, up 36.9% year-over-year; full year Adjusted EBITDA was $94.2M, up 62.2%.
- Q4 non-GAAP net income was $16.3M ($0.13/share); full year non-GAAP net income was $56.2M ($0.44/share).
- Rule of 40 reached 62 in Q4 and 60 for the full year, both record levels.


### Outlook and guidance

- 2025 revenue guidance: $1.04B–$1.06B (20.4%–21.6% growth at midpoint/high end).
- 2025 contribution profit guidance: $358M–$366M (16%–17.3% growth).
- 2025 Adjusted EBITDA guidance: $112M–$116M (21%–23.2% growth).
- Q1 2025 revenue guidance: $241M–$249M (32.5%–34.7% year-over-year growth).
- Confident in achieving top-end guidance without new client signings, assuming planned implementations.


### Segment performance

- Growth driven by onboarding large enterprise billers and increased same-store sales from existing clients.
- New clients signed across insurance, government, utilities, banking, consumer finance, and education.
- Partner ecosystem expanded in government, utilities, insurance, and healthcare verticals.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Q4 contribution margin: 33.4% (down from 40.3% prior year, reflecting large enterprise client mix).
- Q4 average price per transaction: $1.55 (up 17% year-over-year); 166M transactions processed (up 33%).
- Q4 contribution profit per transaction: $0.52 (vs. $0.53 prior year).
- Q4 non-GAAP operating expenses: $47.3M (up 28.8% year-over-year).
- Days sales outstanding: 43 days at Q4 end.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Guidance reflects prudent approach due to limited historical data on newly onboarded large enterprise clients.
- Contribution profit and per-transaction metrics may fluctuate due to payment mix and biller composition.
- Macroeconomic volatility considered, but business seen as resilient due to non-discretionary nature of bill payments.


### Significant events and developments

- Material weaknesses in internal controls over financial reporting remediated in 2024.
- No debt; cash position at $209.4M at Q4 end, supporting organic growth and potential M&A.
- Interchange strategy aims to convert cost center into revenue center over time.


### Capital allocation and financing

- $27.9M cash generated from operations in Q4; $19M free cash flow.
- $36M invested in capitalized software and $26M in working capital for 2024.
- M&A opportunities being evaluated for accretive growth; no functional gaps requiring immediate acquisition.


### Market and industry conditions

- Strong demand and pipeline for digital bill payment solutions across all verticals.
- Large enterprise clients increasingly seeking workflow automation and modernization.
- No exposure to federal government contracts; focus remains on local municipalities and non-discretionary billers.
