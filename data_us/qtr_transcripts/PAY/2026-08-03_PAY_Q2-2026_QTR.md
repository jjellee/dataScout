# Paymentus Holdings, Inc. — Q2 2026 (2026-08-03)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Achieved record Q2 revenue of $360.7 million, up 28.8% year-over-year, with strong growth in contribution profit and adjusted EBITDA, both exceeding guidance.
- Growth was broad-based across customer sizes and industry verticals, with large enterprise clients contributing significantly but not exclusively.
- Strategic investments in AI-driven products (Billeo suite, BillWallet) are positioning the company as a premium AI and software provider in addition to billing and payments.
- Customer experience in billing and payments is increasingly critical for brand loyalty and cash flow, as highlighted by recent market studies.


### Financial highlights

- Q2 revenue: $360.7 million (+28.8% YoY); contribution profit: $118.1 million (+26.3% YoY); adjusted EBITDA: $48.8 million (+54% YoY, 41.3% margin).
- Processed 213.4 million transactions in Q2, up 21.4% YoY; average price per transaction rose from $1.59 to $1.69.
- Free cash flow of $39 million; cash and equivalents at $379.7 million, no debt.
- Non-GAAP net income: $32.4 million ($0.25/share), up 66.7% YoY.
- Rule of 40 score at 68, up from 56 last year.


### Outlook and guidance

- Raised full-year 2026 guidance: revenue $1.443B–$1.458B (21.2%–21.9% growth), contribution profit $460M–$465M (19.7%–20.4% growth), adjusted EBITDA $175M–$185M (31%–34.6% growth).
- Q3 2026 guidance: revenue $353M–$363M (15.2%–16.8% YoY growth), contribution profit $112M–$115M (15.5%–17% YoY), adjusted EBITDA $40M–$45M (18.5%–25.5% YoY).
- Guidance philosophy remains disciplined, based on high confidence and current visibility.


### Segment performance

- Growth driven by both new and existing billers, with strong performance across utilities, government, telecom, property management, insurance, banking, education, B2B, and consumer finance.
- Large enterprise segment showed particular strength, but all customer segments and verticals contributed to growth.
- Implementation timelines for new clients, including large billers, are improving, accelerating revenue realization.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Contribution margin: 32.7% (down from 33.4% YoY, due to higher volume enterprise clients).
- Adjusted EBITDA margin: 41.3% (up 740 bps YoY).
- Contribution profit per transaction: $0.55 (up from $0.53 YoY).
- Day sales outstanding: 27 days (improved from 29 days prior quarter).
- Working capital: $393.3 million (+7.6% sequentially).


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Management notes macroeconomic challenges, inflation, and variability in payment mix and average payment amounts as ongoing risks.
- Forward-looking statements subject to risks detailed in SEC filings.


### Significant events and developments

- Launched Billeo AI-native service commerce suite and BillWallet, expanding into AI infrastructure and workflow management.
- Participated in a PYMNTS Intelligence study highlighting the importance of billing experience for customer loyalty and payment timeliness.
- Notable improvement in onboarding speed and efficiency for new clients.


### Capital allocation and financing

- No debt; strong cash position supports organic growth and potential M&A.
- $11.2 million used in investing and financing activities, mainly for capitalized software.


### Market and industry conditions

- Service economy increasingly values billing and payment experience as a key brand differentiator.
- Market moving toward AI-driven solutions, with Paymentus positioned to benefit from this trend.
- Large, diversified, and growing customer base across multiple verticals.
