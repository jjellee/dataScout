# Paymentus Holdings, Inc. — Q3 2024 (2024-11-12)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Achieved a standout third quarter with revenue up 51.9% year-over-year, contribution profit up 30.1%, and adjusted EBITDA up 58.2%.
- Raised full-year 2024 guidance for revenue, contribution profit, and adjusted EBITDA due to strong execution and early onboarding of large clients.
- Accelerated onboarding of large enterprise clients originally planned for 2025, securing recurring revenue and reducing risk for next year.
- Continued to capture market share across diverse verticals, strengthening the platform's competitive moat and scalability.


### Financial highlights

- Q3 revenue: $231.6M, up 51.9% year-over-year; contribution profit: $80M, up 30.1%; adjusted EBITDA: $24.6M, up 58.2%.
- Processed 155.3M transactions in Q3, up 34.6% year-over-year; average price per transaction rose to $1.49 from $1.32.
- Contribution margin was 34.5% (down from 40.3% last year), offset by a 5.7% reduction in OpEx margin.
- Non-GAAP net income: $19.6M ($0.15/share) vs. $10.9M ($0.09/share) last year.
- Rule of 40 score reached 61, marking the sixth consecutive quarter above this benchmark.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q4 2024 revenue guidance: $215M–$220M (31.8%–33.3% year-over-year growth).
- Q4 contribution profit: $79M–$81M (20.7%–22.2% growth); adjusted EBITDA: $22M–$24M (15.6%–20.6% growth).
- Full-year 2024 revenue guidance: $829M–$834M (35.3% growth at midpoint); contribution profit: $305M–$307M (27% growth); adjusted EBITDA: $89M–$91M (54.9% growth).
- Rule of 40 scale for 2024 expected at 56–57, up from 44 in 2023.


### Segment performance

- Bookings strong across insurance, government, utilities, education, telecom, banks, credit unions, and property management.
- Early onboarding of large clients in Q3 will provide full-year benefit in 2025.
- Same-store sales and transaction growth trends improving, benefiting from growth-stage clients.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Contribution profit per transaction: $0.52 (vs. $0.53 last year).
- Adjusted EBITDA margin: 30.7% (vs. 25.3% last year); incremental adjusted EBITDA margin for Q3: 49.2%.
- Days Sales Outstanding: 44 days (vs. 42 last quarter).
- Working capital: $245.8M, up $16.2M sequentially.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Contribution profit and pricing may vary quarter-to-quarter due to client mix and payment modalities.
- Seasonality and limited historical data for new large clients introduce uncertainty in Q4 projections.
- Volume discounts for large enterprise customers could impact margins, but are offset by operating leverage.


### Significant events and developments

- Onboarded a cohort of large clients earlier than planned, de-risking 2025 and providing strategic benefits.
- Network fees rose to 65.5% of gross revenue due to large enterprise client mix; expected to normalize in Q4.
- Platform enhancements and post-pandemic conditions improved onboarding speed and client engagement.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Ended Q3 with $190.8M in cash, down $2.1M sequentially due to $8.8M in investing activities (mainly capitalized software).
- Generated $6.7M in net cash from operations in Q3.
- Diluted shares outstanding: 127.6M, flat sequentially.


### Market and industry conditions

- Strong demand and inbound interest from larger enterprise customers.
- Platform scalability and IPN ecosystem enable expansion into new verticals and larger clients.
- Market capture strategy focused on responsible growth and margin expansion opportunities.
