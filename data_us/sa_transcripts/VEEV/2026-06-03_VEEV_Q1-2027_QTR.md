# Veeva Systems Inc. — Q1 2027 (2026-06-03)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Delivered total revenue of $883 million and non-GAAP operating income of $395 million, exceeding guidance for the quarter.
- Strong execution across all business segments, with notable momentum in AI-driven initiatives and product innovation.
- Significant progress in CRM migrations, Crossix, and the launch of new AI products like Falcon and Vault AI.


### Financial highlights

- Q1 EBIT margin reached 45%, with full-year guidance at 44%.
- Ostro acquisition expected to contribute $10 million to commercial subscription revenue for the remaining three quarters, representing two-thirds of the $15 million annual increase.
- Record quarter for professional services, driven by strong project execution in R&D and consulting.


### Outlook and guidance

- Full-year guidance raised modestly, reflecting continued conservatism due to early-stage scaling of large R&D products.
- Guidance assumes no significant changes in the macro environment, with end markets remaining healthy and stable.
- AI revenue, excluding Ostro, expected to be immaterial for the year; focus remains on customer success and product excellence.


### Segment performance

- R&D business showed strong Q1 growth, especially in Dev Cloud and Quality Cloud, but remains early in scaling key products like eCOA, RTSM, EDC, Safety, and LIMS.
- Crossix delivered another strong quarter, benefiting from increased digital investments by pharma and ongoing product innovation.
- Vault CRM continues to gain traction, with over 150 customers live and a win rate above 80% in top 20 biopharma migrations.
- Professional services revenue outperformed, driven by consulting and digital events, not just implementations.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- EBIT margin at 45% for Q1; full-year margin guided at 44%.
- Over 40 CRM migrations completed; more than 10 customers live on commercial AI agents.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Early-stage scaling of large R&D products introduces some uncertainty in full-year growth.
- Ongoing regulatory and patent changes in customer businesses may impact demand, though customers are adept at navigating these.


### Significant events and developments

- Acquisition of Ostro, a brand engagement platform, to enhance digital engagement with healthcare professionals and patients.
- Launch and development of Falcon, a disruptive AI platform targeting agentic labor in life sciences, with initial focus on document processing and safety case management.
- Continued investment in AI, data network, and product innovation, including Vault AI and Data Cloud.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Ongoing investments in services, AI, and acquisitions like Ostro to drive future growth.


### Market and industry conditions

- Digital end markets remain robust, with pharma increasing investments in digital channels.
- No material customer concentration risk in Crossix; revenue streams are diversified across clients and product lines.
- Macro environment stable, with healthy demand across commercial, Dev Cloud, and Quality segments.
