# Baker Hughes Company — Q3 2026 (2026-10-28)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Strong Q3 execution, cash generation and commercial momentum; adjusted EBITDA was $1.42 billion, with adjusted EPS of $0.70, up 2% YoY.
- First quarter including Chart after the July 16 acquisition close; integration advanced while IET RPO reached a record $39.4 billion, up 23% YoY.
- Strategic focus on integrated, higher-value solutions across energy and industrial markets, including the molecule-to-electron value chain and mature-asset services.


### Financial highlights

- Revenue was ~$8.0 billion, up 14% YoY; acquisitions and divestitures contributed 9% net.
- Adjusted EBITDA rose 14% YoY to $1.42 billion; adjusted EBITDA margin was 17.6%, broadly in line with prior year.
- GAAP diluted EPS was $0.41; adjusted diluted EPS was $0.70, up 2% YoY.
- Free cash flow was $769 million, up 10% YoY, after ~$200 million of Chart integration, restructuring and closing cash costs, nearly all one-time transaction costs.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q4 guidance: revenue of $8.25 billion and adjusted EBITDA of $1.46 billion; sequential revenue growth and margin expansion expected.
- FY 2026 adjusted EBITDA midpoint increased to $5.265 billion; free cash flow conversion expected at 45%-50%.
- FY IET orders outlook raised to $21.5-$23.5 billion; Horizon Two IET orders expected to exceed $50 billion.
- 2027 outlook calls for company book-to-bill above 1x, revenue growth and margin expansion; IET organic revenue growth expected in the low-to-mid-single digits.
- Chart EBITDA margin target is 22%-23% in H2 2028; improvement expected through 2027, with a more meaningful step-up in 2028.


### Segment performance

- IET orders were $5.9 billion, with a 1.7x book-to-bill; revenue was $3.48 billion, up 3% YoY, and EBITDA was $685 million, up 8%.
- IET margin expanded 90 bps YoY to 19.7%; GTS upgrades and transactional activity were strong, while GTE revenue faced mix and Middle East headwinds.
- OFSE revenue increased 6% sequentially to $3.66 billion; EBITDA was $659 million and margin reached 18.1%, up 50 bps sequentially.
- Chart reported post-close revenue of $888 million and EBITDA of $156 million, for a 17.6% margin; orders were $831 million, with a large dry cooler award shifting into Q4.
- Chart expects Q4 revenue of ~$1.1 billion and EBITDA of ~$195 million; this aligns with the $350 million H2 EBITDA outlook.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Company book-to-bill was 1.3x in Q3; year-to-date book-to-bill was 1.4x.
- IET RPO reached $39.4 billion, up 6% sequentially and 23% YoY; 33% of combined GTE and CTS backlog was scheduled to convert in 2027.
- Quarter-end leverage ratio increased to 2.5x, primarily reflecting debt used to fund the Chart acquisition.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Middle East tensions and disruptions may affect activity; Q4 assumptions anticipate lower regional activity, with logistics and supply-chain constraints creating IET revenue pressure.
- Permitting, fuel supply, site development, infrastructure, financing and project costs may shift data-center project timing; management characterized potential delays as primarily timing-related, not broad cancellation risk.
- Aeroderivative supply-chain execution, inflation and project mix remain variables; Q4 logistics and regional disruption could affect execution and results.


### Significant events and developments

- Chart acquisition closed July 16; the transaction expands capabilities in thermal management, cryogenics and carbon capture, with a total addressable market above $400 billion.
- Chart integration actions representing ~$70 million of annualized savings were executed; target remains $325 million of annualized cost synergies within three years of closing.
- Mature Assets Solutions targets high-single-digit revenue CAGR and at least $3 billion revenue by 2030; OGDC work covers evaluation of more than 120 wells.
- Data-center awards reached $4.9 billion year-to-date; a U.S. technology award provides 1.1 GW of power generation capacity.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Near-term priority is balance-sheet deleveraging; debt reduction is expected to begin in Q4 using free cash flow and Waygate divestiture proceeds.
- Target net leverage is below 1.5x by H2 2028, supported by partial term-loan paydown and repayment of upcoming maturities.


### Market and industry conditions

- Global upstream spending is expected to be broadly flat YoY, with an improving outlook into 2027; customers remain disciplined on near-term spending.
- LNG outlook remains favorable: existing and committed capacity could reach ~800 MTPA by 2030, with future FIDs supporting growth to 950 MTPA by 2035.
- AI and data-center growth is driving demand for reliable power; U.S. incremental data-center capacity is expected to reach ~100 GW by 2035, requiring ~18 BCF/day of additional gas supply.
