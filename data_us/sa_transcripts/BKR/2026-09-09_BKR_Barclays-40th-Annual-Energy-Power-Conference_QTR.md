# Baker Hughes Company — Barclays 40th Annual Energy-Power Conference (2026-09-09)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Strategic transformation and market positioning

- Baker Hughes has evolved to focus on interconnected energy and industrial markets, now deriving about 60% of revenue from infrastructure and industrial sectors.
- The acquisition of Chart Industries expands the addressable market to over $400 billion and strengthens the technology portfolio in areas like heat transfer, cryogenics, and carbon capture.
- Integration with Chart Industries is expected to accelerate growth in high-potential markets such as data centers, LNG, industrial gases, and geothermal.
- The combined company aims to deliver integrated solutions across the energy value chain, enhancing customer relationships and recurring revenue streams.
- Exposure to high-growth end markets is projected to increase the addressable opportunity by nearly 60%, from $36 billion to $57 billion by 2030.


### Financial performance and guidance

- OFSE margins have improved by over 600 basis points since 2017, and IET delivered record EBITDA margins of 18.5% in 2025, with expectations to exceed 20% in 2026.
- The company targets 20% adjusted EBITDA margins by 2028, excluding Chart Industries, and expects more than $45 billion of IET orders through the Horizon Two period.
- Chart Industries is expected to contribute $1.85–$2.25 billion in revenue and $300–$400 million in EBITDA from the close date through year-end, with margins around 17%.
- Chart Industries' EBITDA margin is targeted to expand from 17% in the second half of 2026 to 22–23% by the second half of 2028, driven by cost synergies, operational enhancements, and commercial opportunities.
- Free cash flow conversion for 2026 is expected to be 40–45%, reflecting acquisition-related costs.


### Integration and synergy realization

- Integration efforts focus on operational enhancements, cost synergies, and commercial synergies, with a proven business system being applied to Chart Industries.
- Cost synergies are expected to reach $325 million annually by year three, mainly through SG&A optimization, supply chain efficiency, and facility optimization.
- Operational improvements and synergy capture are projected to drive 500–600 basis points of margin expansion over the next two years.
- Commercial synergies are anticipated to provide new growth avenues, especially in data centers and gas infrastructure, with early customer engagement showing positive momentum.
- The integration is supported by dedicated teams, clear governance, and established performance management frameworks.


### Market opportunities and growth drivers

- The combined portfolio enables participation in fast-growing sectors like data centers, geothermal, and industrial gases, leveraging secular trends such as electrification, AI-driven demand, and decarbonization.
- Data center orders have reached $4.2 billion since 2025, with Chart Industries contributing an additional $600 million over two years.
- Integrated solutions for data centers include power generation, cooling, water management, and emissions reduction, enhancing the value proposition.
- Geothermal and other industrial applications benefit from the expanded technology suite, supporting project performance and customer engagement.
- The company is positioned to capture a larger share of customer spending across both equipment and lifecycle services.


### Outlook and strategic priorities

- The business is increasingly weighted toward higher-growth, less cyclical industrial and infrastructure markets, supporting earnings durability and cash generation.
- Integration of Chart Industries is expected to further accelerate the shift toward quality industrial markets and expand enterprise solutions.
- Priorities include disciplined execution, synergy capture, de-leveraging, and strengthening the balance sheet.
- The investment thesis is underpinned by growing global demand for energy and industrial solutions, with a $10 billion enterprise solutions pipeline.
- Management remains confident in achieving long-term value creation through operational excellence and strategic integration.
