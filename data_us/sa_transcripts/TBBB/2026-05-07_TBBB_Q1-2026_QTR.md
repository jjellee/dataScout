# BBB Foods Inc. — Q1 2026 (2026-05-07)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Achieved strong operational and financial performance in Q1 2026, with robust store expansion and revenue growth.
- Opened 123 net new stores, reaching 3,469 total stores and 20 distribution centers.
- Maintained consistent expansion strategy, densifying existing regions and gradually entering new ones.


### Financial highlights

- Revenue increased 33% year-over-year to MXN 23 billion in Q1 2026.
- Same-store sales grew 16% versus Q1 2025, outperforming industry benchmarks.
- Reported EBITDA was MXN 554 million; adjusted EBITDA (excluding non-cash share-based compensation) rose 39% to MXN 1.3 billion.
- Operating cash flow reached MXN 2 billion, up 64% year-over-year.


### Outlook and guidance

- Management remains confident in continued high growth, citing strong business model resilience and long runway for store openings.
- SG&A expected to remain stable as a percentage of revenue for 2026, with long-term decreases anticipated.
- CapEx for 2026 guided at MXN 5.2 billion, covering new stores, distribution centers, and equipment.


### Segment performance

- New stores performed consistently well across all regions, driven by basic goods consumption patterns.
- All product categories showed strong performance, with non-sweetened beverages offsetting declines in sweetened beverages due to new taxes.
- New categories such as frozen and fresh produce are promising, with fresh trials showing ticket uplift in test stores.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Sales expenses as a percentage of revenue increased slightly by 5 basis points to 10.3% year-over-year.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin improved by 22 basis points year-over-year.
- Negative working capital reached MXN 9.4 billion, representing 11.3% of LTM revenue.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Labor costs impacted by minimum wage increases and workweek reductions, but mitigated by efficiency initiatives.
- No significant impact from competitor actions observed in the quarter.
- Lock-up period expiration clarified for August 6th, 2026.


### Significant events and developments

- Continued investment in talent, especially in IT and central administration, to support growth.
- ERP deployment is halfway through a three-year project, with gradual, modular rollout to minimize risk.
- Increasing use of artificial intelligence to drive efficiency and operational improvements.
- Private label penetration continues to trend upward, with no structural changes expected as it increases.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Accelerated growth remains self-funded, supported by strong operating cash flow and negative working capital.
- CapEx plan of MXN 5.2 billion for 2026 is on track.


### Market and industry conditions

- Outperformed industry same-store sales benchmarks by over 14 percentage points.
- Market remains highly competitive, but no material changes in competitive dynamics observed.
- Consumer trends monitored through large-scale annual surveys, informing product and category focus.
