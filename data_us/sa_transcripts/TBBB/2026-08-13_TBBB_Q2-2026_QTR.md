# BBB Foods Inc. — Q2 2026 (2026-08-13)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Opened 155 net new stores in Q2 2026, reaching 3,624 total stores as of June 30, 2026.
- Same-store sales grew 20% year-over-year, significantly outperforming the market.
- Total revenue increased 39% year-over-year to MXN 26 billion.
- Opened one new distribution center, expanding to 21 regions.
- Strong operating cash flow fully funds organic expansion.


### Financial highlights

- Reported EBITDA was MXN 960 million; adjusted EBITDA (excluding non-cash share-based compensation) rose 44% to MXN 1.6 billion.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin increased by 21 basis points year-over-year; excluding a one-time MXN 37 million expense, margin was 6.2%.
- Cash flow from operating activities for H1 2026 reached MXN 4.3 billion, up 119% year-over-year.
- Adjusted negative working capital reached MXN 10.2 billion as of June 2026.


### Outlook and guidance

- Confident in significant long-term growth opportunity; business model expected to remain resilient and competitive as scale increases.
- Continued investment in talent expected, with G&A expenses likely to remain around 3% of revenue in the short term.
- No constraints on real estate pipeline; expansion pace could accelerate if conditions remain favorable.


### Segment performance

- All product categories are growing, with newer categories expanding rapidly from a low base.
- 100% of new stores use the upgraded format, which outperforms older formats.
- Store ramp-ups are faster and consistent with projections; older stores still perform well.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Sales expenses as a percentage of revenue decreased by 56 basis points to 10% year-over-year.
- Admin expenses (excluding share-based payment) increased by 57 basis points year-over-year, reflecting investment in talent and expansion.
- Inventory days remain below 20, with no material impact expected from new categories.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Potential short-term pressure on logistics expenses in Q3 due to opening three new distribution centers.
- Ongoing competitive intensity in the discount retail sector, but market potential remains significant.
- No material impact from World Cup or cardless payment tests.


### Significant events and developments

- One-time cash expense of MXN 37 million related to equity follow-on offering in May 2026.
- Opened one new distribution center in Q2, with three more planned for Q3.
- Ongoing ERP system rewrite, accelerated by AI tools, with enhanced POS functionality and logistics optimization.
- Mechanisms in place to ensure orderly sale of employee stock options post-vesting.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Operating cash flow fully funds organic expansion.
- No constraints on real estate or expansion pipeline; strong cash generation supports potential acceleration.


### Market and industry conditions

- Market remains highly competitive, but sector growth potential is robust.
- No constraints on real estate availability; favorable conditions for continued expansion.
- Internal inflation remains low, supporting strong value proposition.
