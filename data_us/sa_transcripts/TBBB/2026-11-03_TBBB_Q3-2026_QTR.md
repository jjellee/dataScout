# BBB Foods Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-11-03)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 added 141 net stores, bringing the total to 3,765; 603 net stores opened in the LTM, alongside four new distribution centers.
- Revenue rose 40% YoY to MXN 28bn, same-store sales grew 20.5%, and EBITDA excluding non-cash share-based compensation increased 60% to MXN 1.9bn.


### Financial highlights

- Reported Q3 EBITDA was MXN 1.3bn; excluding non-cash share-based compensation, EBITDA was MXN 1.9bn, up 60% YoY.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin expanded 83 bps YoY, supported by sales growth, improved gross margin, and operating efficiencies.
- Operating cash flow for 9M reached MXN 7.1bn, up 128% YoY.


### Outlook and guidance

- FY store-opening guidance remains 590–630; Q4 is typically a heavier opening period and the pipeline was described as healthy.
- Momentum continued into Q4; confidence in growth opportunities and further efficiency gains was reiterated.
- Transportation optimization offers further potential, with more initiatives expected in 2027.
- No change to next-year plans was expected from the proposed tax law based on current information; further guidance is planned on the Q4 call.


### Segment performance

- Same-store sales growth was driven roughly two-thirds by volume, including ticket count and price per ticket; the remainder came from price, mostly mix rather than inflation.
- Average items per transaction rose from 2–3 at IPO to 4–5; Frozen and cosmetics performed strongly, while dairy continued solid growth.
- Newer store vintages start with higher sales and ramp faster; sales curves have steepened and peak sales have risen over time.
- The format remains focused on value-oriented grocery shopping; Frozen and other evolving categories support customer value and growth.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Sales expense was 9.8% of revenue, down 36 bps YoY; admin expense excluding share-based payment declined 6 bps YoY.
- Adjusted negative working capital was MXN 10.8bn at September 2026 versus MXN 7.8bn in 2025, excluding IPO and follow-on proceeds; equal to 10.9% of LTM revenue on the same basis.
- Operating cash flow fully funds organic expansion under the structurally negative working capital model.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Proposed tax-law limits on deductions may temporarily affect cash flows; impact remains under review pending the final law, with updated guidance expected on the Q4 call.
- Private-label sourcing is planned years ahead; supplier count is decided product by product, balancing logistics, growth capacity, and supply continuity.


### Significant events and developments

- Four distribution centers opened in Q3, expanding the network to 25 regions; older centers are becoming more efficient and new sites shorten delivery distances.
- Around 60–80 store pilots run at a time; the assortment remains disciplined at about 900 SKUs, prioritizing high rotation and value.
- Yema had four stores, strong customer reception, and plans to grow at its own pace, with no long-term store target offered.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Operating cash flows fund organic expansion; the self-funding model was cited as supporting scalable growth.
- Refrigeration and Frozen investments are assessed for ROIC; future store changes aim to improve returns while maintaining customer value.
- Proposed tax changes were not expected to alter capital structure plans based on current information; any cash-flow effect was viewed as temporary.


### Market and industry conditions

- Same-store sales outperformed the broader market, maintaining a gap of more than 21 percentage points versus ANTAD; internal inflation remained very low.
- Competitive conditions were described as healthy, with new customer gains and a positive ticket-size trend; the price gap was steady.
