# MongoDB, Inc. — Q2 2027 (2026-09-01)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Total revenue reached $772 million, up 30% year-over-year, marking the highest quarterly growth since fiscal 2024.
- Atlas revenue grew 29% year-over-year for the fifth consecutive quarter, driven by large enterprise customers and AI momentum.
- EA and other revenue grew 36% year-over-year, reflecting broad-based strength and new AI capabilities.
- Added a record 2,900 net new customers, bringing the total to 70,600, with strong traction among AI-native startups.
- AI workloads and features like Atlas Vector Search and Voyage embeddings are driving adoption across industries.


### Financial highlights

- Non-GAAP operating margin was 24%, up from 15% a year ago.
- Non-GAAP net income was $163 million ($1.90 per share), compared to $87 million ($1 per share) a year ago.
- Total non-GAAP gross margin was 75.9%, up 210 basis points year-over-year.
- Operating cash flow was $142 million, free cash flow $138 million, both nearly doubling year-over-year.
- Remaining performance obligations grew 91% year-over-year to $1.52 billion.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q3 revenue expected at $756–$761 million, up 20–21% year-over-year.
- Full-year revenue guidance raised to $2.99–$3.03 billion, 21–23% growth.
- Atlas full-year growth guidance increased to 27%, up 300 basis points.
- EA and other full-year growth guidance raised to 11%, first double-digit growth in three years.
- Operating margin expected to expand by 250 basis points for the year.


### Segment performance

- Atlas: Sixth straight quarter of year-over-year dollar growth, adding $127 million in Q2.
- EA and other: Strongest quarter in three years, with broad-based demand for new AI features.
- Net ARR expansion rate increased to 122%, up from 119% a year ago.
- Nearly 3,000 customers with $100,000+ ARR, up 17% year-over-year.
- 48% of large Atlas customers now use two or more features, up from 42% last year.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Atlas now represents approximately 74% of total revenue.
- Free cash flow conversion expected at the upper end of 80–100% target range.
- Rule of 44 targeted for fiscal 2027 (23% revenue growth + 21% operating margin).


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Guidance remains prudent due to the consumption-based model and seasonality, especially in Q4.
- Multi-year EA deals are inherently hard to predict, leading to conservative guidance.
- AI adoption is early-stage, and future growth depends on continued enterprise and AI-native uptake.


### Significant events and developments

- Launched fully managed MCP server, deepening integration with AI coding agents and platforms.
- Automated Voyage embeddings and new AI models released, driving top leaderboard rankings.
- Search and vector search launched on EA, closing feature gaps between cloud and self-managed offerings.
- Leadership team strengthened with new CRO and continued investment in R&D and go-to-market.
- $100 million allocated to share repurchases in the quarter.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Ended Q2 with $2.4 billion in cash, equivalents, and short-term investments.
- $100 million spent on share repurchases and $59 million for employee RSU taxes.


### Market and industry conditions

- Strong demand for AI workloads across financial services, healthcare, tech, and public sector.
- AI-native startups and large enterprises increasingly adopting Atlas and EA for mission-critical and AI-driven applications.
- Hybrid and multi-cloud deployments gaining traction, supporting operational resilience and regulatory compliance.
- Competitive differentiation through integrated vector search, embeddings, and run-anywhere capabilities.
