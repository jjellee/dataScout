# Aveanna Healthcare Holdings Inc. — Q2 2026 (2026-08-13)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q2 2026 revenue reached $670 million, up 13.7% year-over-year, with growth across all business segments.
- Adjusted EBITDA for Q2 was $95.4 million, an 8% increase, driven by improved rates, volumes, and operational efficiencies.
- Strategic focus on preferred payer agreements and government partnerships is accelerating growth and supporting wage investments.
- The acquisition of Family First Homecare closed in June, with integration progressing well and expected to complete by late Q4.


### Financial highlights

- Home Health and Hospice revenue grew 14.8%, Private Duty Services (PDS) 14.0%, and Medical Solutions 9.4% year-over-year.
- Consolidated gross margin was $218.5 million (32.6%), with PDS gross margin at 28.9%, Home Health and Hospice at 53.9%, and Medical Solutions at 45.1%.
- Q2 free cash flow was $75.4 million, and liquidity at quarter-end was $433 million.
- Refinancing reduced annual interest expense by $10 million.


### Outlook and guidance

- Full-year 2026 revenue guidance raised to over $2.68 billion and adjusted EBITDA above $365 million.
- Long-term organic growth rate for PDS increased to 5%-6% (from 3%-5%), and Home Health and Hospice to 8%-10% (from 5%-7%).
- Medical Solutions growth rate remains at 8%-10%.
- Targeting double-digit annual revenue growth, with continued focus on de-leveraging to sub-3x leverage.


### Segment performance

- PDS: Q2 revenue per hour was $44.62 (+1.7% YoY); preferred payer agreements now cover 64% of MCO volumes.
- Home Health and Hospice: 10,500 admissions, 14,700 episodes (+18.5% YoY), episodic mix at 81%.
- Medical Solutions: 95,000 unique patients served (+4.4% YoY), revenue per patient up 5%.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Gross margin: Consolidated 32.6%, PDS 28.9%, Home Health and Hospice 53.9%, Medical Solutions 45.1%.
- Free cash flow: $75.4 million YTD.
- Liquidity: $433 million at quarter-end.
- Variable rate debt: $1.48 billion, with $1.4 billion hedged.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Labor market challenges persist, but hiring and retention trends are improving.
- Rate increases and wage pass-throughs may impact gross margin percentages, but dollar margins are expected to rise with revenue.
- State and federal reimbursement processes and timing of rate adjustments remain key variables.


### Significant events and developments

- Achieved seven state rate enhancements in 2026, with a major California PDN rate increase effective January 2027.
- Family First Homecare acquisition completed and integration underway.
- Credit rating upgrades from all three major agencies.
- Successful term loan repricing, lowering interest expense.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Family First acquisition funded entirely with cash on hand.
- Focus on disciplined M&A, maintaining leverage below 3x long-term.
- Strong free cash flow supports ongoing deleveraging and future acquisitions.


### Market and industry conditions

- Demand for home and community-based care remains robust, with government and MCOs seeking cost-effective solutions.
- Industry-wide shift toward episodic payment models in Home Health, with improved payer engagement.
- OBBBA legislation and state budget dynamics are influencing Medicaid funding and rate setting.
