# Haemonetics Corporation — Q2 2027 (2026-11-05)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Organic revenue grew 10% in Q2 and 8% in H1 FY27; adjusted EPS rose 6% in both periods to $1.35 and $2.49, respectively.
- Growth spanned plasma, Blood Management, and Interventional Technologies, with expanded CSL commitments and new product approvals supporting longer-term opportunity.


### Financial highlights

- Adjusted gross margin was 61.9% in Q2, up ~140 bps YoY, and 61.2% in H1, up ~60 bps; excluding tariff refunds and FX, Q2 margin was essentially flat.
- Adjusted operating expenses rose 15% to $127.6M in Q2 and 11% to $253.2M in H1, reflecting growth investments, personnel costs, and freight.
- Adjusted operating margin was 26.3% in Q2, down 40 bps YoY, and 24.9% in H1, down 50 bps.
- H1 operating cash flow increased 40% YoY to $180M; free cash flow rose ~57% to $143M despite higher growth-related CapEx.


### Outlook and guidance

- FY27 organic revenue growth guidance increased to 6%-9% from 4%-7%; reported growth guidance rose to 8%-11% from 5%-8%.
- Apheresis organic revenue growth guidance increased to high single digits from low-to-mid single digits; MedSurg organic growth guidance remains mid-single digits.
- FY27 adjusted operating margin expansion outlook narrowed to 0-50 bps from 50-100 bps; adjusted EPS growth is expected in line with reported revenue growth guidance of 8%-11%.
- FY27 free cash flow conversion is expected to exceed 80% of adjusted net income; second-half margin expansion is expected to be weighted toward Q4.
- Apheresis guidance excludes incremental second-half plasma collection growth; it reflects confirmed CSL conversions through Q3, with additional Q4 conversions not yet included.


### Segment performance

- Plasma organic revenue grew 18% in Q2 and 13% in H1, supported by prior share gains, strong collections, and Persona PLUS adoption; global collections grew high single digits in Q2.
- Blood Management Technologies organic revenue grew 7% in Q2 and 8% in H1; transfusion management grew well into double digits, while cell salvage partly offset gains.
- Interventional Technologies organic revenue grew 3% in Q2 and H1; VASCADE MVP and MVP XL grew low double digits in electrophysiology, partly offset by esophageal cooling comparisons.
- Other apheresis was flat in Q2 and down modestly in H1 as red cell collection strength was offset by order timing and portfolio rationalization.
- Transfusion management represented 20% of BMT revenue and continued high-teens-or-better growth; TEG disposables accounted for 85% of TEG business and recurring use per device more than doubled.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted EPS was $1.35 in Q2 and $2.49 in H1, up 6% YoY in each period; lower diluted share count was largely offset by unfavorable FX and higher interest expense.
- Adjusted effective tax rate was 24.7% in Q2 versus a generally consistent prior-year level; H1 was 25.2% versus 24.8%.
- Free cash flow conversion was 166% in Q2 and 111% of adjusted net income on a trailing-12-month basis.
- Quarter-end cash was ~$261M and estimated net leverage was ~2.5x EBITDA under the credit agreement.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Near-term margins face performance-based compensation, elevated production and freight costs, inflation in energy and materials, and investment ahead of plasma conversions; some costs flow through inventory into H2 COGS.
- Persona PLUS may lead some collectors to reduce collections temporarily as higher yield meets near-term demand; collection volume growth is excluded from H2 guidance.
- CSL conversion timing and customer-specific economics remain confidential; additional Q4 conversions are not included until plans and dates are confirmed.
- A cyberattack affecting plasma and blood banks in Europe did not affect operations; management noted cybersecurity risks remain in the environment.


### Significant events and developments

- Expanded CSL relationship includes the full Persona PLUS suite and updated software; conversion is underway, with completion across CSL U.S. plasma centers anticipated before the end of calendar 2027.
- VASCADE MVP XL received CE Mark certification and Japanese approval for an expanded indication; PerQseal Elite received U.S. FDA approval, and PacePro launched.
- PerQseal Elite enters large-bore closure for TAVR and EVAR procedures; the estimated global market is $300M, growing at a high-single-digit rate, with a phased physician education-led launch planned.
- Re-engagement with EFS, a major French collector, is underway; transition to the product line has started.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Capital priorities are organic growth investment, balance-sheet strengthening, and retaining flexibility for shareholder returns; $100M was repaid on the revolver in H1, including $50M in Q2.
- Revolver balance was $200M at quarter-end; $325M remained under the existing share repurchase authorization.
- Elevated CapEx is expected over the next two years for manufacturing capacity, packaging, warehousing, and additional plasma devices.


### Market and industry conditions

- Global plasma collections grew at a high-single-digit rate in Q2; U.S. collections tracked mid-to-high single digits and outside-U.S. collections well into double digits through H1.
- Plasma demand remains strong, while vascular closure markets are stabilizing with renewed growth in access sites following pulsed-field ablation adoption.
- Higher fuel prices, freight costs, and expedited shipments added cost pressure amid strong demand and supply-readiness efforts.
