# BTQ — Q2 2026 (2026-08-14)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Achieved significant milestones across all business lines, including the QPerfect acquisition, which established a European R&D and commercialization center in Strasbourg, France.
- Expanded product validation and commercial readiness, with QSSN powering Korea’s first bank-led stablecoin pilot and MIMIQ going live in Korea and Europe.
- Advanced partnerships and collaborations, including with TIDAL PWR for quantum data center architectures and PASQAL for neutral atom quantum computing.
- Completed internal audit for Bitcoin Quantum, with external audit underway and mainnet launch targeted for later in the year.
- Government mandates in France, U.S., and Korea are driving adoption and standardization of post-quantum cryptography solutions.


### Financial highlights

- Cash balance at quarter end was CAD 9.73 million, with a CAD 150 million ATM program selectively used to fund operations.
- ATM program has performed well, providing ongoing funding flexibility; approximately CAD 100 million remains available on the base shelf for future needs.
- One-time expenses related to QPerfect and other initiatives impacted the quarter.


### Outlook and guidance

- Expectation of revenue generation in the second half of the year, particularly from QSSN and MIMIQ contracts.
- All major product lines (MIMIQ, QSSN, QCIM) are anticipated to be validated and scaled in the next quarter.
- Continued expansion into new markets, including the U.S., Europe, Taiwan, and Saudi Arabia.
- Focus on commercializing QSM IP across semiconductors, data centers, defense, and telecom.


### Segment performance

- QSSN processed over 100,000 mainnet transactions with settlement times reduced from 35 seconds to under 2 seconds, and cost per transaction down by a third.
- QSSN selected as core infrastructure for Korea’s first stablecoin pilot and expanding into the U.S. and Europe.
- Bitcoin Quantum completed internal audit, with mainnet launch and wrapped asset issuance planned for later in the year.
- MIMIQ now commercially available in Korea and Europe, with on-premise and cloud offerings expanding and a SaaS rewrite shipping end of Q3.
- QPerfect advancing digital twin and quantum logic unit development, with multiple grants and collaborations underway.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Cash balance: CAD 9.73 million at quarter end.
- ATM program: CAD 150 million launched, with CAD 100 million remaining on the base shelf.
- QSSN: Over 100,000 transactions processed, settlement time reduced to under 2 seconds.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- AI-driven cryptanalysis is emerging as a new threat, prompting additional security reviews and slight delays in product releases.
- Regulatory and standardization changes across multiple jurisdictions may impact product adoption and timelines.


### Significant events and developments

- Closed QPerfect acquisition, integrating a European R&D hub.
- Launched CAD 150 million ATM program for operational funding.
- QSSN powering Korea’s first bank-led stablecoin pilot.
- MIMIQ live in Korea (QUREKA) and Europe, with new on-premise and cloud partnerships.
- Multiple patents filed and research papers published on quantum error correction and cryptography.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Selective use of ATM program to fund operations, maintaining a strong cash position.
- QPerfect leveraging non-dilutive European grant funding for R&D.
- Remaining CAD 100 million on base shelf available for M&A or large funding needs.


### Market and industry conditions

- Increasing government mandates for quantum-safe cryptography in France, U.S., and Korea are accelerating market demand.
- AI and quantum computing advancements are reshaping security requirements and product development priorities.
- Growing institutional focus on quantum risk in digital assets, with major industry players launching initiatives.
