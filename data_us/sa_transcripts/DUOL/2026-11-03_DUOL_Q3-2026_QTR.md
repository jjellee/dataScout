# Duolingo, Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-11-03)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- DAU growth accelerated to 29% YoY in Q3 from 23% in Q2; bookings, revenue and profitability exceeded expectations. Management expects Q4 DAU growth similar to Q3.
- Product improvements, especially stronger retention, drove most user growth; growth accelerated in 8 of the 10 largest markets.
- Strategy is shifting gradually toward monetization while maintaining user growth and protecting the free experience.


### Financial highlights

- Q3 bookings growth re-accelerated QoQ; subscription bookings grew at double-digit rates in most regions.
- Q3 operating expenses included an $11 million bonus accrual tied to user-growth targets.
- Record Q3 gross margin benefited from stronger revenue and lower-than-expected AI costs.
- Paid marketing was a little over $100 million of non-compensation spend, with paid marketing representing about 60%; management said the spend was profitable.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q4 guidance: bookings growth 13% YoY, revenue growth 14%, and Adjusted EBITDA margin 26.1%; Adjusted EBITDA expected at $84 million, roughly flat YoY.
- FY 2026 guidance: bookings growth 12% YoY, revenue growth 18%, and Adjusted EBITDA margin 26.5%.
- Q4 bookings are expected slightly ahead of prior guidance; DAU growth is expected to remain similar to Q3.
- Q4 gross margin expected to decline slightly sequentially, by about a point, as Video Call reaches all paid subscribers; operating expenses likely to grow faster than revenue.
- Full 2027 outlook planned for February; next year’s priorities include continued user growth and more monetization testing.


### Segment performance

- Chess reached 10 million DAU and is viewed as under-monetized, with further monetization opportunities ahead.
- Math is growing at roughly the same rate as language learning, though slower than Chess; management plans to keep improving the learning experience.
- Ads were flat for the full quarter but exited Q3 growing; plans include higher-quality inventory and direct sales.
- Video Call adoption continued to rise; words spoken per Duolingo active user roughly doubled over the past year, and trial users who try tailored calls are more likely to subscribe.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted EBITDA margin guidance is 26.1% for Q4 and 26.5% for FY 2026; Q4 Adjusted EBITDA is expected at $84 million.
- Video Call AI inference cost fell from about $0.30 per call at launch to well under $0.01, enabling rollout to Super subscribers.
- Experiments in the last month were up about 90% YoY while employee count grew 10%; AI contributed to faster testing and shorter launch latency.
- Performance marketing is profitable, with management expecting further improvement in LTV-to-CAC returns.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Q4 margin faces pressure from broader Video Call access and AI usage, although lower AI costs have been better than expected.
- Max is being phased out; near-term bookings impact is expected to be small, while Max represents a single-digit share of paid subscribers and bookings.
- Lite-plan testing has not produced strong results; rollout may remain limited or target selected regions.
- Monetization changes are subject to testing, with a stated guardrail against degrading the free experience; conversion-rate improvement remains uncertain.
- 2027 guidance has not yet been set, and management cited evolving AI, testing-process and execution needs as areas still being worked through.


### Significant events and developments

- Video Call is moving into Super as Max is sunset; testing showed positive revenue effects and access to roughly 10 times as many users.
- Brawl Stars and Eggy Party partnerships brought users and showed strong retention; management expects partnerships only a few times per year, alongside more first-party events.
- AI-supported experimentation expanded: non-engineers can launch smaller tests, and management expects an integer-multiple increase in experiments next year.
- Max Individual subscribers are moving to Super Family at a similar price; remaining Max Family is the smaller portion of Max bookings and paid users.


### Notes to the financial statements

- Q3 expenses included an $11 million bonus accrual; another portion is reflected in Q4 guidance, with full payment expected in early 2027.


### Market and industry conditions

- Management views the long-term opportunity as reaching one billion learners and expects users to monetize over time, sometimes over multiple years.
- Video Call inference increasingly uses open-weight models, which management said are sufficient for most users and reduce costs versus frontier models.
- Math addresses a global learning audience of about one billion people; management sees substantial opportunity in the category.
- Game partnerships are cross-promotional, with no money exchanged; both sides gained users, and the partnerships also benefited performance marketing.
