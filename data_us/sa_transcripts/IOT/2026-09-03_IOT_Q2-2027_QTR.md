# Samsara Inc. — Q2 2027 (2026-09-03)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Achieved $2.1 billion in ARR, up 30% year-over-year, with $134 million net new ARR and record additions of large customers.
- Surpassed 30 trillion data points collected annually, fueling AI-driven product innovation and customer ROI.
- Launched new products at Beyond conference, including Tracking Label, 360 Camera, Waste Intelligence, and Ground Intelligence.
- Large customer expansions and multi-product adoption are accelerating growth and deepening customer relationships.


### Financial highlights

- Q2 revenue reached $508 million, up 30% year-over-year; ARR from $100,000+ customers grew 38% year-over-year.
- Added 242 $100,000+ ARR customers and 20 $1 million+ ARR customers, both quarterly records.
- Non-GAAP operating margin was 21%, up 6 percentage points year-over-year; free cash flow margin was 13%.
- Achieved fourth consecutive quarter of GAAP profitability with EPS of $0.03.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q3 revenue expected between $514M–$516M, representing 24% year-over-year growth; non-GAAP operating margin to remain at 21%.
- FY 2027 revenue guidance of $2.043B–$2.047B, 26% year-over-year growth; non-GAAP EPS $0.76–$0.78.
- Free cash flow margin expected to be ~100 bps lower than FY 2026 due to higher IoT device investment and supply chain costs.


### Segment performance

- Field services was the largest vertical in Q2, with highest net new ACV mix in over two years.
- Transportation and public sector segments showed sequential acceleration in net new ACV growth.
- International business contributed 18% of net new ACV, with Europe and Mexico showing strong growth.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Dollar-based net retention rate for core customers at approximately 115%.
- 96% of $100,000+ ARR customers subscribe to two or more products; 72% to three or more.
- Rule of 40 surpassed for 16 consecutive quarters.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Elevated supply chain costs and proactive inventory purchases are temporarily impacting free cash flow.
- Supply chain disruptions expected to normalize over time, as seen in previous cycles.


### Significant events and developments

- Launched Samsara Community, connecting over 5,000 members for peer learning and faster ROI.
- AI feature adoption grew 4x in two months; over 1,000 customers engaged with AI Agents.
- Emerging products contributed over 20% of net new ACV for the third consecutive quarter.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Increased investment in IoT device inventory to support accelerated growth and customer demand.
- Well-capitalized position allows for continued innovation and supply chain flexibility.


### Market and industry conditions

- Strong secular growth in physical AI across construction, field services, energy, and utilities.
- Volatility in fuel prices is driving increased demand for data-driven operational efficiency.
- Large, complex customers are digitizing operations, creating long-term expansion opportunities.
