# Ascendis Pharma A/S — Q3 2026 (2026-11-12)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 product revenue was EUR 366 million, up 89% YoY and EUR 51 million QoQ, driven by new-patient demand across YORVIPATH, YUVIWEL, and SKYTROFA.
- Growth strategy rests on existing-market uptake, label expansion, and new TransCon products; targets exceed EUR 5 billion annual product revenue in 2030 and EUR 10 billion in 2035.
- Management described the business as self-sustaining, with increasing operating cash flow and a commercial infrastructure largely built to support future growth.


### Financial highlights

- Q3 operating expenses were EUR 217 million, down EUR 32 million QoQ, primarily due to lower litigation costs and timing of commercial initiatives.
- Reported operating profit was EUR 125 million; non-IFRS operating profit was EUR 156 million, with EUR 189 million of operating cash flow.
- Q3 ending cash was EUR 964 million, up EUR 152 million from Q2, primarily reflecting operating cash flow and partially offset by EUR 52 million of treasury share purchases.


### Outlook and guidance

- FY 2026 operating cash flow is expected to be at least EUR 600 million.
- Q4 outlook: YORVIPATH growth consistent with prior quarters in and outside the U.S.; SKYTROFA revenue relatively stable in the U.S.; YUVIWEL U.S. demand growth and approval trends expected to continue.
- Longer-term growth is expected from geographic expansion, label extensions, and new products; revenue targets are over EUR 5 billion in 2030 and EUR 10 billion in 2035.
- YUVIWEL European Commission decision is expected before year-end; if approved, Germany launch is planned for Q1 2027. Infant-trial top-line results are expected in 2027.


### Segment performance

- YORVIPATH Q3 revenue was EUR 291 million, up 103% YoY and EUR 39 million QoQ; full commercial availability reached 13 countries, with 15 additional markets expected in 2027.
- YUVIWEL Q3 revenue was EUR 16 million, double Q2's EUR 8 million; around 340 U.S. patients were enrolled through October, with over 80% approved for reimbursement.
- SKYTROFA Q3 revenue was EUR 59 million, up EUR 4 million QoQ on higher demand; it remained the leading long-acting growth hormone by value in the U.S.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Non-IFRS operating margin was 42%; management characterized recent operating margins as in the low-to-mid 40% range.
- Q3 total product revenue grew 89% YoY; YORVIPATH revenue grew 103% YoY, while YUVIWEL revenue doubled QoQ.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Forward-looking statements are subject to risks and uncertainties; actual results could differ materially, with risk factors referenced in the annual report and subsequent SEC filings.
- YUVIWEL European launch depends on EC approval; trial plans, including combination studies, remain subject to regulatory requirements and execution.
- Management emphasized safety as a critical consideration for therapies in growing children and called for appropriate safety review of new treatments.


### Significant events and developments

- U.S. national YORVIPATH disease-awareness campaign launched in September; additional campaigns are planned for 2027, with early signs of increased patient engagement reported.
- YUVIWEL received a positive CHMP opinion and a positive COMP opinion supporting maintenance of orphan designation; a potential 10-year exclusivity period depends on approval.
- YUVIWEL infant trial is fully enrolled; phase II adult achondroplasia proof-of-concept and phase III pediatric hypochondroplasia trials were expected to start before year-end.
- Recruitment began for the phase III achondroplasia combination trial; a phase III hypochondroplasia combination trial is planned for 2027.
- Plans call for at least one new product based on a new chemical entity to enter development each year; a new therapeutic area is expected to be disclosed in the coming year.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Through November 11, an additional $273 million was spent repurchasing 1.2 million shares; year-to-date repurchases totaled 2 million shares for approximately $460 million.
- Approximately $330 million of the announced $400 million current buyback program had been spent; management anticipated capacity for sustainable future repurchases supported by free cash flow.


### Market and industry conditions

- U.S. chronic hypoparathyroidism prevalence is estimated at 70,000–90,000 adults; management said over 90% remain potential YORVIPATH candidates, with 3,000–4,000 new patients annually.
- Around 70% of U.S. children with achondroplasia were described as untreated; YUVIWEL enrollment through October was nearly half the number of U.S. patients on pharmacological treatment before its April launch.
- Management expects international growth to build market by market as reimbursement and full commercial availability expand; it is targeting commercialization across more than 50 countries.
