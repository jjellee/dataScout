# AT&T Inc. — Bank of America 2026 Media,Communications & Entertainment Conference (2026-09-10)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Business momentum and strategic priorities

- Achieved faster growth in service revenue, adjusted EBITDA, and EPS in Q2, with full-year outlook reiterated.
- Significant investments in fiber and 5G, including Lumen and EchoStar spectrum acquisitions, are driving accelerated broadband revenue growth.
- Double-digit EPS growth and $45 billion in owner returns targeted over the next three years.
- Focus on converged relationships, leveraging fiber and wireless to disrupt cable-dominated markets.


### Customer behavior, pricing, and execution

- Switching levels among consumers remain steady, with competition healthy and unchanged from last year.
- Converged strategy is driving growth, especially in under-penetrated fiber areas and value segments.
- New pricing constructs and plan flexibility have been well received, supporting ARPU growth and reduced churn.
- Pricing actions are guided by value addition, transparency, and customer choice, resulting in lower-than-expected churn.


### Device strategy and subsidy management

- Maintaining a strict subsidy budget despite rising device prices, focusing on profitability over volume.
- Offers are tailored using trade-in values, rate plan eligibility, and converged household value to optimize customer lifetime value.
- No net add targets; success measured by service revenue and customer value, not just volume.


### Growth segments and market penetration

- Strongest growth seen in value-oriented and under-penetrated segments, especially one- and two-line accounts.
- Converged offers (fiber, fixed wireless, wireless) are targeted to maximize household value and retention.
- Fiber prioritized in markets where available, with fixed wireless used surgically in non-fiber areas.


### Convergence, ARPU trends, and customer value

- Willing to accept fiber ARPU dilution to anchor homes and drive higher overall household value through convergence.
- Converged customers show lower churn, higher add-on adoption, and greater total account value.
- Promotional spending is focused on high-value, converged customers rather than wireless-only accounts.


### Lumen integration and operational improvements

- Lumen acquisition added 4.5 million locations with lower initial penetration, seen as a major growth opportunity.
- Initial focus on leveraging AT&T distribution and marketing to ramp demand, with gradual rollout of full AT&T brand and experience.
- Full integration and brand lift expected to take several quarters, progressing market by market.


### Fiber build economics and cost management

- Long-term supplier and labor agreements provide cost visibility and stability for fiber build-out.
- Self-service connections and lower maintenance costs improve overall fiber economics.
- Confident in ability to scale fiber within planned capital budgets.
