# Elastic N.V. — Q1 2027 (2026-08-27)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Achieved a strong start to fiscal 2027, exceeding all guided metrics and accelerating constant currency revenue growth, particularly in sales-led subscription revenue and AI-driven solutions.
- Customer demand was robust across all solution areas, with notable strength in search, AI, and security, and significant expansion in the $100K+ ACV customer cohort.
- AI adoption surged, with over 37% of high-value customers now using AI features, up from 21% a year ago.
- Continued innovation in AI, observability, and security, including new product releases and strategic acquisitions.


### Financial highlights

- Q1 total revenue reached $478 million, up 15% year-over-year; sales-led subscription revenue grew 18% to $399 million.
- CRPO grew 21% to $1.2 billion; RPO increased 27% to $1.9 billion, reflecting deepening multi-year customer commitments.
- Subscription gross margin was 81%, total gross margin 77%, and non-GAAP operating margin 16.2%.
- Adjusted free cash flow margin was 30%, despite $13 million in restructuring charges.
- Returned $40 million to shareholders via share repurchases in Q1; $380 million used since program inception.


### Outlook and guidance

- Raised full-year fiscal 2027 guidance: total revenue expected at $1.998–$2.010 billion (15.2% growth midpoint); sales-led subscription revenue at $1.682–$1.694 billion (17.4% growth midpoint).
- Non-GAAP operating margin forecasted at 19.4%; non-GAAP EPS $3.29–$3.37; adjusted free cash flow margin 21.5%.
- Q2 revenue guidance: $486–$487 million (14.9% growth midpoint); sales-led subscription revenue $407.5–$408.5 million (16.9% growth midpoint).
- Expect revenue and sales-led subscription revenue growth to accelerate in the second half, with Q4 as the peak growth quarter.


### Segment performance

- Security led segment growth, followed by AI/search AI, then observability.
- Observability business strengthened by new metrics capabilities and the Deductive AI acquisition.
- Over 80 net new $100K+ customers added, the largest sequential increase to date; this cohort now contributes 90% of sales-led subscription revenue.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Net expansion rate (NER) at 111%, expected to improve as revenue acceleration continues.
- Sales productivity and ramped sales headcount showed year-over-year gains.
- Cloud annual growth improved to 27% from 26% last quarter; monthly cloud remained flat.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Forward-looking statements subject to risks and uncertainties, including market demand, competitive landscape, and macroeconomic factors.
- Restructuring-related charges of $20 million in Q1, with $2–$5 million expected for the remainder of the year.


### Significant events and developments

- Launched new AI and observability features, including VectorDB index mode, auto-calibration, and Columnar Mode for time series data.
- Acquired Deductive AI to enhance AI-driven SRE capabilities.
- Achieved FedRAMP High authorization, unlocking new U.S. public sector opportunities.
- Board transition: Caryn Marooney stepping down, Julia Liuson nominated.


### Capital allocation and financing

- $40 million returned to shareholders in Q1 via share repurchases; $380 million used since October 2025 under the $500 million program.


### Market and industry conditions

- AI adoption accelerating across enterprises, regulated industries, and government agencies.
- Competitive differentiation driven by open platform, hybrid deployment flexibility, and integrated AI capabilities.
- Security landscape rapidly evolving, requiring AI-driven automation for threat detection and response.
