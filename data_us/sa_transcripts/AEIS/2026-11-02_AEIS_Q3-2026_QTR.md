# Advanced Energy Industries, Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-11-02)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Record Q3 revenue and earnings exceeded the high end of guidance; revenue rose $122M sequentially and EPS more than doubled YoY.
- Strong demand across target markets, factory execution and new product wins lifted the FY26 revenue growth outlook to ~40%.
- Capacity investments, including the Thailand factory ramp, support planned growth; total revenue-generating capacity is targeted to exceed $5B by end-2028.


### Financial highlights

- Q3 revenue was $696M, up 21% QoQ and more than 50% YoY; Q3 EPS was $3.65 versus $1.74 a year earlier.
- Gross margin was 42.1%, up 300 bps YoY; excluding Q2 tariff refunds, it expanded 140 bps QoQ on volume and manufacturing efficiency.
- Operating income reached a record $170M, up 118% YoY; adjusted EBITDA was $182M.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q4 revenue guidance is $735M ±$25M; gross margin 42%–42.5%, operating expenses $126M–$128M, and non-GAAP EPS $3.90 ±$0.30 on 41.2M shares.
- FY26 revenue growth outlook increased to ~40%; semiconductor revenue is expected to grow >60% YoY in H2, with product revenue excluding service up ~45% for the year.
- 2027 growth is directionally expected near 30%, but supply uncertainty prevents specific guidance; EPS is expected to grow faster than revenue.
- Gross margin is on track to reach a 43% exit rate in 2027, potentially sooner depending on volume; long-term line of sight remains above 43%.


### Segment performance

- Semiconductor revenue was a record $322M, up 16% QoQ and 64% YoY, driven by leading-edge demand, higher etch/deposition intensity and system power growth.
- Data center computing revenue reached a record $256M, up 34% QoQ and 49% YoY; hyperscale programs and initial new-program ramps supported growth.
- Industrial and medical revenue was $91M, up 13% QoQ and 27% YoY; fourth consecutive quarter of YoY growth, with demand and backlog improving.
- Telecom and networking revenue was $28M, up 13% QoQ and 15% YoY on AI-related demand; Q4 revenue is expected to moderate slightly.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Gross margin was 42.1%; operating margin was 24.4%, up 760 bps YoY and the highest level in recent years.
- Operating expenses were $123M; depreciation was $12.2M and adjusted EBITDA was $182M.
- Operating cash flow was $66M; inventory days were 144, turns 2.5x, DSO 61 days, DPO 81 days and net working capital 124 days.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Supply constraints persisted through the year, particularly for ICs, PCBs and specialty ceramics; some impact is built into Q4 and 2027 expectations.
- Revenue capacity depends on adequate key-component supply; management cited uncertainty in forecasting 2027 and noted growth may not be linear due to constraints and product transitions.
- Engineering bandwidth remains a critical path, limiting the number of attractive programs that can be pursued despite available factory capacity.


### Significant events and developments

- Thailand factory began generating revenue; two data center customers were qualified, with two leading semiconductor customers expected to qualify within two to three months.
- Q3 semiconductor wins included plasma power designs for advanced nodes and system-power/test programs; high-volume product ramps are expected from 2027, with dielectric wins ramping in 2028.
- The 800 V Power Distribution Board features 98% efficiency and 350 W/in³ power density; modular customization and liquid cooling support hyperscale and second-wave customers.
- Second-wave data center customers could collectively exceed $300M in annual revenue by 2029; their contribution is not required to meet the 2027 outlook.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Q3 CapEx was $31M and dividends paid were $4.1M; FY26 capital investments are expected toward the low end of the $180M–$195M range.
- Capital investment is expected to remain elevated through 2027 to expand capacity toward more than $5B in annual output.
- Remaining $136M of 2028 convertible notes was redeemed after quarter-end on October 2; Q3 cash and equivalents were $1.42B and net cash was $159M.
- Share repurchases resumed after the note redemption; approximately $30M of common stock was repurchased in October at around $241 per share.


### Notes to the financial statements

- A one-time, non-cash GAAP charge followed a deferred tax asset write-down after a lower long-term tax rate was established in one jurisdiction; Q3 GAAP tax rate was 23.6% versus non-GAAP 15.7%.


### Market and industry conditions

- Semiconductor and data center customers expect demand to strengthen through 2027 and into 2028, supported by leading-edge wafer investment and AI infrastructure spending.
- Management expects semiconductor product revenue to grow as fast as or faster than WFE in 2027, with potential to outpace WFE over the following five to ten years as new designs ramp.
- Industrial and medical demand is recovering from the post-COVID inventory hangover; growth is increasingly driven by high-margin new products and design wins.
