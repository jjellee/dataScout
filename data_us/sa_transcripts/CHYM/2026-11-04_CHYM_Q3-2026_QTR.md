# Chime Financial, Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-11-04)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 revenue grew 32% YoY to $718M, accelerating from 27% in Q2; adjusted EBITDA was $137M at a 19% margin, up 14 points YoY.
- Added a record 1.8M net new active members over 12 months; ended Q3 with 10.9M active members, up 20% YoY, and reported a third consecutive quarter of GAAP net income.
- Raised Q4 guidance as growth and profitability exceeded expectations; management cited stronger member growth, product adoption, and unit economics.


### Financial highlights

- Revenue was $718M, up 32% YoY; net income was $44M and adjusted EBITDA was $137M in Q3.
- Non-GAAP operating expenses grew 13% YoY versus 32% revenue growth; operating expenses were 55% of revenue, down from 65% a year ago.
- Transaction profit rose 41% YoY as transaction margin expanded 5 points to 74%; gross margin reached the 90% long-term target.
- Fraud losses fell 16% YoY while purchase volume grew 19%; adjusted EBITDA incremental margin was 62% in Q3.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q4 revenue guidance is $757M–$767M, up 27%–29%; adjusted EBITDA is $161M–$166M at a 21%–22% margin, with 64% incremental margin at midpoint.
- FY2026 revenue guidance is $2.792B–$2.802B, representing 28% growth; adjusted EBITDA is $519M–$524M at a 19% margin and 65% incremental margin.
- Management expects faster growth and higher profitability in coming years; incremental margins are expected to exceed historical levels, but not remain in the 60s.
- Enterprise is expected to make a truly meaningful contribution to member growth in 2027; 2027 guidance is planned for the Q4 call.


### Segment performance

- Payments and OIT revenue grew 26% YoY; purchase and OIT volume rose 22%. Credit reached 29% of purchase volume, nearly double a year ago.
- Platform revenue grew 52% YoY to $274M, led by MyPay and Instant Loans.
- MyPay originations reached $5.3B; revenue grew 54%, transaction profit hit a record $80M, and loss rate was 1.1%, within the stated 0.9%–1.2% operating band.
- Instant Loans originations were $381M in Q3, up 30% QoQ, with annualized revenue above $115M; repeat-borrower annualized loss rates were up to 50% lower than first-time borrowers.
- Enterprise signed Michaels and the City of Charleston and expanded its Workday partnership; new member contribution is growing, with more meaningful impact expected next year.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- ARPAM rose 10% YoY to $270; Prime members generate more than twice the ARPAM of the average active member.
- LTV:CAC was nearly 10x across active members, versus about 8x at IPO; CAC declined sequentially through 2026 and payback periods improved.
- Net take rate increased 3 bps YoY to 116 bps, ahead of the 2 bps expected; Chime Card earns nearly twice the take rate of debit.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin was 19%, up 14 points YoY; Q3 incremental adjusted EBITDA margin was 62%.
- MyPay average monthly advance per active user rose to $600 in Q3 from $500 in Q2; transaction margin was 58% as the product prioritized transaction profit.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Stride acquisition remains subject to regulatory approval; closing is expected in H1 2027, and approval timing is outside management’s control.
- Instant Loans loss rates ticked up in Q3 due to more first-time loans tied to Prime; management expects a small further increase in Q4 as these loans season.
- The Stride acquisition will include one-time integration costs in 2027; synergies are expected to reach full run rate 12–18 months after close.
- Forward-looking statements are subject to risks and uncertainties described in SEC filings, and actual results may differ materially.


### Significant events and developments

- Announced agreement to acquire Stride Bank, a partner for more than seven years; ownership is intended to speed product launches, reduce funding and partner costs, and expand lending availability nationwide.
- Jade, a personal finance agent, is available to millions of members; secure connections to third-party AI agents are also in development.
- Launched Chime Invest, Visa Flex, and joint accounts; Prime line of credit is in testing, with an unsecured credit card expected to roll out next year.
- Chime Prime, launched six months earlier, is attracting higher-income members; members earning $75,000 or more are the fastest-growing new-enrollment segment.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Pro forma assets were approximately $6.5B at September-end, including $1.6B of commercial loans; core consumer assets were approximately $4.9B.
- Asset-light strategy targets assets below $10B for the foreseeable future; management cited short-duration, low-risk assets and active capital markets as options if balances approach the threshold.
- Stride is expected to generate more than $100M of annual net after-tax synergies and be earnings-accretive from close, despite one-time integration costs in 2027.


### Market and industry conditions

- Management views large banks as the primary competitive set; 85% of new members come from big-bank relationships, while member acquisition continued with sequentially lower CACs.
- Direct deposit visibility supports real-time underwriting and priority repayment; management said lending yields are substantially below those of short-term loan competitors.
- Prime and Chime Card are driving credit mix and spend; management expects the secular shift toward credit to support purchase-volume growth over coming years.
