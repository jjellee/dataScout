# Chime Financial, Inc. — Goldman Sachs Communacopia + Technology Conference 2026 (2026-09-09)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Business performance and growth

- Achieved strong quarter-over-quarter results, establishing leadership as a trusted brand for mainstream Americans seeking modern banking solutions.
- Accelerated revenue growth with Q2 volume up 20% and raised Q3 and full-year guidance to 30% top-line growth and higher adjusted EBITDA margin.
- Chime Prime and lending products are key growth drivers, with Chime Prime members monetized at twice the average rate and Instant Loan originations up 70% sequentially.
- Direct deposit relationships and enterprise partnerships, such as with Allied Universal, are fueling recurring revenue and expanding user engagement.
- Operating leverage remains strong, with incremental margins above 60% and payroll costs planned to stay flat next year.


### Strategic acquisition and rationale

- Announced acquisition of Stride Bank, a long-term partner, to gain full control over banking operations and product velocity.
- Acquisition enables faster product development, streamlined compliance, and greater platform resiliency by integrating modern technology with proven bank infrastructure.
- Deal valued at 1.5x book, below precedent transactions, with Stride Bank showing ROE above 25% and strong profitability.
- National charter will allow geographic expansion and more flexible product offerings, especially in lending.
- Direct connection between brand and bank expected to increase trust among new customer segments.


### Product innovation and technology

- Ownership of core technology (ChimeCore) and now a bank accelerates product launches, including Chime Plus, Chime Prime, lending, and investment services.
- AI and vertical integration enable rapid innovation, personalized experiences, and actionable financial insights through Jade, the financial assistant.
- Product velocity has led to new features like joint accounts, managed investments, and expanded credit offerings.
- Chime Prime offers 5% cash back on spending, 3.75% APY on savings, and perks like Priority Pass, targeting broader and higher-income segments.
- Ongoing beta for unsecured revolving lines and plans to expand eligibility for premium products.


### Operational efficiency and organizational changes

- Workforce reduced by 10% to create a flatter, more accountable organization, leveraging AI for productivity.
- Smaller teams are delivering at the same or higher output, supporting a culture of entrepreneurial spirit and agility.
- Payroll costs to remain flat, supporting continued operating leverage and margin expansion.


### Market expansion and customer retention

- Focus on expanding TAM by targeting higher-income users and small business owners, with no observed graduation risk among affluent members.
- Over 75% of members intend to stay for life, with high retention rates across income levels.
- Enterprise channel provides an evergreen source of new direct deposit relationships, complementing direct-to-consumer growth.


### Financial outlook and unit economics

- LTV to CAC remains strong at 9x, with continued focus on asset-light, payments-driven, consumer-friendly monetization.
- Chime Prime segment delivers double the revenue per active member, supporting further investment in member relationships.
- Confident in long-term sustainability and improvement of attractive unit economics.
