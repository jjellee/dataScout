# AXT, Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-10-29)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 revenue reached $73.4M, up 162% YoY; indium phosphide demand, higher-value wafer transitions and productivity gains are driving a multi-year growth phase.
- Backlog exceeded $500M, with demand outpacing available capacity and customers on allocation.


### Financial highlights

- Revenue was $73.4M, up 54% QoQ from $47.6M and 162% YoY from $28.0M.
- Non-GAAP gross margin was 56.1%, versus 45.0% in Q2 and 22.4% in Q3 2025; GAAP gross margin was 56.0%, versus 44.9% and 22.3%.
- Non-GAAP operating profit was $29.5M; GAAP operating profit was $28.4M, versus $10.4M in Q2 and a $1.1M loss in Q3 2025.
- Non-GAAP net profit was $26.8M, or $0.41 per diluted share; GAAP net profit was $25.7M, or $0.39 per diluted share.
- Cash equivalents and investments rose to $825.9M at Sept. 30 from $748.8M at June 30; accounts receivable increased $3M and inventory rose $7.4M.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q4 revenue of ~$90M was considered highly likely to be realized, based on orders with permits in hand or no permit requirement.
- Q4 non-GAAP and GAAP operating expenses are expected at ~$13M and ~$14M, respectively; estimated diluted share count is 66.1M.
- Q4 non-GAAP earnings guidance is $0.57-$0.59 per share and GAAP guidance is $0.56-$0.58 per share.
- Management expected gross margin to exceed 60% in Q4, while noting that rapidly changing conditions make forecasts uncertain.
- Indium phosphide capacity was targeted to double again in 2027; wafer capacity at year-end 2026 was expected to reach 2.75-3x the year-end 2025 level.


### Segment performance

- Indium phosphide revenue was $54.3M, primarily from data center applications; China revenue grew strongly QoQ and customers there remained on allocation.
- Gallium arsenide revenue was $9.1M; demand across wireless, industrial robotics and data center laser applications grew sequentially, with some seasonal Q4 declines expected.
- Germanium substrate revenue was $344K; consolidated raw material company revenue was $9.7M.
- 4-inch wafers are expanding from photodetectors to laser applications; 6-inch indium phosphide lines entered pilot production, with 6-inch expected to remain single-digit share in 2027.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Non-GAAP operating margin was 40.2%; GAAP operating margin was 38.7% in Q3.
- Non-GAAP operating expenses were $11.7M, versus $10.2M in Q2 and $6.7M in Q3 2025; GAAP operating expenses were $12.7M, versus $10.9M and $7.3M.
- Weighted average diluted shares were 65.9M in Q3.
- DSO improved to 49 days, supported by multiple customer prepayments; accounts receivable increased by $3M.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Forward-looking results are exposed to trade tariffs, import/export restrictions, China export permits, global economic and political conditions, and rising environmental regulation in China.
- Risks also include performance of partially owned supply-chain companies, the timing of export permits, and execution of capacity expansion, yield and efficiency improvements.
- The top five customers generated ~35% of revenue, and one customer represented more than 10%.
- Management viewed indium phosphide oversupply as a low near-term risk but said visibility becomes less certain beyond the next two to three years.


### Significant events and developments

- A long-term supply agreement with Lumentum included a $43.5M wafer prepayment; the agreement was signed in Q3.
- Backlog surpassed $500M across multi-year agreements, shorter-term contracts and spot orders; substantial growth came from run-rate business, not mainly long-term agreements.
- Tongmei advanced its 6-inch indium phosphide capability to pilot production; a newly acquired adjacent building is being designed for additional production.
- New crystal-growth furnace designs are improving productivity and output, supporting wafer-size transitions and manufacturing efficiency.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Tongmei agreed to sell its own shares in a private equity financing, with proceeds invested directly into its operations; maximum investment was stated as $545M.
- At the maximum investment, ownership was expected to decline by ~12.1 percentage points to ~73.4%, retaining control; proceeds are intended for indium phosphide and China raw-material capacity expansion.
- Tongmei continued preparations for a Hong Kong Stock Exchange listing; Jinmei is expanding high-purity indium refining capacity to support substrate supply.


### Notes to the financial statements

- The $43.5M Lumentum wafer prepayment is recorded as a liability and will convert to revenue as products ship under the agreement.
- Q3 depreciation and amortization was $2.6M; stock-based compensation was $1.1M.


### Market and industry conditions

- AI data center build-outs are increasing demand for optical connectivity; 800G and 1.6T transceivers are driving indium phosphide laser and detector demand, with near- and co-packaged optics potential ahead.
- Indium phosphide demand exceeded supply, with customers on allocation and lead times extending beyond nine months in many China orders.
- Higher wafer diameters and product mix, rising indium phosphide prices, lower-defect substrates and productivity gains supported margin improvement; raw material cost increases were a partial offset.
- 2027 demand was expected to shift toward 4-inch laser wafers; larger diameters were expected to increase ASPs and create further gross-margin opportunity.
