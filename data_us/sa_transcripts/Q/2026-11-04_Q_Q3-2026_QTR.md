# Qnity Electronics, Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-11-04)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 marked the 10th consecutive quarter of profitable organic growth, with AI-driven shrink and stack trends supporting demand across semiconductor materials, advanced packaging, interconnect, and thermal solutions.
- POR wins typically support 5–10 years of recurring demand; wins for 2nm and HBM4 are entering production, and 2026 POR wins are on pace to exceed the prior-year record.


### Financial highlights

- Q3 net sales were $1.6B, up 22% YoY and 9% sequentially; adjusted operating EBITDA was $503M, up 36% YoY and 17% sequentially.
- Adjusted EPS was $1.46, up 55% YoY and 23% sequentially; adjusted gross margin was 47.8%, up 270 bps YoY and 120 bps sequentially.
- Adjusted free cash flow was $320M in Q3 and $607M YTD; cash and equivalents were $1.1B at quarter-end.


### Outlook and guidance

- FY26 guidance raised: net sales $5.77B–$5.83B, adjusted operating EBITDA $1.785B–$1.805B, adjusted EPS $4.95–$5.05, and adjusted free cash flow $800M–$900M.
- At guidance midpoints, FY26 net sales growth is 22%, adjusted EBITDA growth exceeds 28%, and adjusted EPS growth is ~50%; Q4 sales growth is expected at ~26% YoY.
- FY26 MSI wafer starts are expected to grow high single digits to low double digits; semiconductor and Interconnect Solutions growth are expected at ~15% and ~30%, respectively, at guidance midpoints.
- Q4 outlook includes normal seasonality and potential year-end inventory management; historical Q4-to-Q1 sales trend is low-single-digit growth, not formal Q1 guidance.
- 2027 MSI growth is currently assumed in the high-single-digit to low-double-digit range; 2027 guidance will follow next quarter, while management expects capacity to support demand through 2028 and beyond.


### Segment performance

- Semiconductor Technologies Q3 sales were $797M, up 15% YoY and 7% sequentially; adjusted gross margin was 50.2% and adjusted EBITDA was $285M.
- Advanced-node sales grew 25% YoY and reached ~44% of semiconductor-related revenue, versus 42% in H1; high fab utilization and scaling advanced logic and memory supported demand.
- Interconnect Solutions Q3 sales were $759M, up 30% YoY and 11% sequentially; adjusted gross margin was 45.3% and adjusted EBITDA was $236M.
- Thermal management, advanced packaging, and AI PCBs together were ~30% of Interconnect Solutions and grew more than 50% in Q3; the rest of the segment grew at a mid-teens rate.
- Kalrez, representing mid-teens of Semiconductor Technologies, grew more than 20%; its recurring replacement demand benefits from fab investment and rising wafer starts.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted EBITDA margin was 32.3% in Q3; adjusted gross margin rose from the 46% range last year to 47%+ YTD in 2026.
- Total debt was ~$4B and net leverage was ~1.7x; the stated historical target is to keep net leverage below 3x.
- Capital expenditures were $78M in Q3 and $290M YTD; FY26 cash CapEx target remains $450M–$470M, with some spending potentially moving into Q1.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Growth outperformance depends on customers successfully commercializing advanced technology roadmaps and bringing planned capacity online on schedule.
- Year-end customer inventory management and normal consumer-electronics seasonality may affect quarterly comparisons; demand challenges are concentrated in lower- and mid-tier consumer devices.
- New fabs require equipment installation and 12–18 months of process qualification before materials demand scales with production.
- Management is monitoring reports of data-center project delays; it cited strong customer demand and expects healthy momentum into next year, while noting AI growth extends beyond data centers.


### Significant events and developments

- First year as an independent business included portfolio innovations, targeted capacity expansions in CMP, lithography, and thermal management, and deeper customer partnerships.
- Inclusion in the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index was cited as recognition of the business’s role across the semiconductor value chain.
- Through Apple’s American Manufacturing Program, U.S.-produced camera film is being supplied for the variable-aperture camera system in iPhone 18 Pro.
- More than 70% of sales were on standalone systems by Q4; work toward full IT independence is continuing into 2027.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Priorities are organic investment, disciplined returns-focused M&A, and shareholder returns through dividends and repurchases, while maintaining a resilient balance sheet.
- Q3 share repurchases were $25M; YTD dividends and repurchases returned $125M to shareholders.
- Investment remains focused on customer capacity, R&D, and go-to-market capabilities; transformation benefits are being reinvested, with manufacturing efficiency and IT independence initiatives ongoing.
