# Circle Internet Group, Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-11-04)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 revenue and reserve income was $865M, up 17% YoY, and adjusted EBITDA was $317M, up 85%; both included $182M of ARC token pre-sale revenue.
- USDC circulation ended Q3 at $73.9B, flat YoY, while on-chain transaction volume rose 49% to $13.9T and USDC accounted for 79% of stablecoin transaction volume.
- Arc launched on mainnet, CPN annualized payment volume reached $41B, and management described distribution, liquidity and platform scaling as central growth priorities.


### Financial highlights

- Total revenue and reserve income was $865M, up 17% YoY and 23% QoQ, including $182M of ARC pre-sale revenue.
- Reserve income was $658M, down 8% YoY; higher average USDC circulation was more than offset by a lower reserve return rate.
- Revenue less distribution costs was $453M, up 55% YoY; adjusted operating expenses were $139M, up 13% YoY.
- Adjusted EBITDA was $317M, up 85% YoY; adjusted EBITDA margin was 70%, or 50% excluding ARC pre-sale revenue.
- Other revenue was $207M; excluding $182M of ARC pre-sale revenue, it was $25M, down 11% YoY and ~$8M QoQ.


### Outlook and guidance

- FY other revenue guidance maintained at $310M-$330M, including $182M ARC pre-sale revenue and $130M-$150M from remaining products; excluding ARC, Q4 other revenue is expected to step up.
- FY revenue less distribution cost margin guidance reaffirmed at 41.7%-43.7%; excluding ARC, full-year margin expected near the midpoint of the original 38%-40% range.
- Adjusted operating expenses are trending toward the high end of the original $570M-$585M range.
- Q4 includes a full quarter of the Hyperliquid arrangement and renewed Binance agreement; the Binance incentives are expected to reduce margin sequentially.
- Management expects material CPN revenue in 2027 following Tazapay’s expected close and integration; formal FY2027 guidance was deferred to the next earnings call.


### Segment performance

- USDC average circulation was $73.3B, up 8% YoY and down 4% QoQ; quarter-end circulation was $73.9B, up 1% QoQ and flat YoY.
- USDC on-chain transaction volume grew 49% to $13.9T; its share of stablecoin transaction volume rose to 79% from 66% YoY, on roughly 27% of supply.
- CPN Q3 annualized payment volume reached $41B, up more than 15x YoY, with 205 enrolled financial institutions; by Oct. 30, volume exceeded $58B.
- Arc launched Sept. 16; by late October it had about $1.5B of USDC, cirBTC and EURC, with more than $30B USDC moved since launch and about 500,000 daily transactions.
- EURC grew 92% YoY to €413M; USYC assets reached $2.4B, up 230% YoY.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Reserve return rate was 3.54%, down 61 bps YoY; average rates rose 6 bps QoQ following the Federal Reserve’s September rate hike.
- Net reserve margin was 37.8%, up ~70 bps YoY and down ~80 bps QoQ, reflecting the first full quarter of the Hyperliquid arrangement.
- Revenue less distribution cost margin was 52.4%, up 13 percentage points YoY; excluding ARC pre-sale revenue, it was 39.7%, roughly flat YoY and down ~150 bps QoQ.
- Adjusted operating expenses declined ~$8M, or 5%, QoQ, largely due to non-recurring Q2 costs.
- Record USDC minting and redemption totaled $208B, up 42% YoY, or about $2.3B daily; year-to-date volume exceeded $520B.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- USDC quarter-end circulation was flat YoY amid a soft digital asset market; management cautioned that long-term growth will not follow a straight line.
- Lower reserve rates pressured reserve income despite higher average circulation; partner distribution incentives, including the full-quarter Binance arrangement in Q4, also affect margins.
- Management highlighted accelerating cyberattacks, including AI-enabled attacks, and the security exposure of digital assets; stronger industry coordination and regulatory scrutiny are expected.
- Tazapay acquisition consideration is $400M in Class A common stock, with closing expected in 2027.
- Forward-looking statements are subject to risks and uncertainties; actual results may differ materially from expectations.


### Significant events and developments

- GENIUS Act implementation in January is expected to establish a federally recognized framework for digital dollars; management expects it to support broader institutional adoption.
- Circle National Trust received an OCC federal charter alongside the existing New York Trust charter.
- A new five-year Binance agreement was signed, alongside a $100M equity investment from Binance; USDC is positioned as a primary digital dollar across its platform.
- Arc mainnet launched with 11 founding validators and more than 100 institutions and applications; planned network sectors include privacy, payments and AI agents.
- Tazapay acquisition agreement announced to extend CPN’s local payout reach; Tazapay serves 100+ payout markets through 60+ bank and fintech partners.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Adjusted operating expenses include continued investment in Arc and AI infrastructure and marketing, including the Chelsea Football Club partnership.
- Binance made a $100M equity investment alongside its renewed five-year distribution and product agreement.
- Tazapay consideration is $400M in Class A common stock; transaction close is expected in 2027.
- Management said investments in distribution, partnerships and platform scaling will continue to support growth.


### Notes to the financial statements

- ARC pre-sale revenue is recognized as performance milestones are met; approximately 75% of the $242M total pre-sale revenue was recognized in Q3 following token delivery and public mainnet launch.
- ARC staking rewards, selective token sales and ecosystem incentives are recognized as other revenue; ecosystem incentives have an offsetting distribution cost.
- The 25% ARC token allocation’s value is not reflected under GAAP until tokens are sold or distributed; network appreciation is not recorded before then.


### Market and industry conditions

- Total stablecoin supply was about $274B, up 8% YoY; USDC carried 79% of Q3 stablecoin transaction volume on roughly 27% of supply.
- Management cited the GENIUS Act, pending accounting standards for payment stablecoins and improving integrations as factors that could enable corporate and institutional adoption.
- Management described competition as a distribution and liquidity contest, with partner incentives viewed as a trade-off against broader circulation and profitability.
- Tokenization of financial contracts and real-world assets was identified as a developing growth area, with institutions building on Arc and related infrastructure.
