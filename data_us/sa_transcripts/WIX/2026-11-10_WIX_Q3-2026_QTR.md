# Wix.com Ltd. — Q3 2026 (2026-11-10)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Growth, speed and efficiency advanced through monetizing Base44, Symphony and Studio Two, supported by AI-native infrastructure and a leaner organization.
- Base44 ARR approached $250M, while Symphony reached millions of dollars in ARR within months of launch; Studio Two began rolling out to partners.
- Headcount was 25% lower at Q3-end than at 2022 year-end, alongside double-digit bookings growth and expanded product reach.


### Financial highlights

- Total non-GAAP gross margin was 69% in Q3, flat YoY and up 2 percentage points QoQ; Base44 non-GAAP gross margin was 58%.
- Non-GAAP operating income was 12% of revenue, reflecting higher sales and marketing investment, primarily for Base44.
- Free cash flow margin excluding acquisition and restructuring costs is now expected at ~16% for FY26, down from the prior high-teens target due mainly to Base44 investment.
- Free-user AI costs fell more than 50% QoQ; total AI costs for free and paid users are expected to be less than 30% of 2H bookings.


### Outlook and guidance

- FY26 guidance remains bookings growth in the low teens and revenue growth in the low-to-mid teens YoY; Q4 revenue growth is expected in the low double digits.
- Q4 bookings growth is expected to lag revenue growth; strong Base44 ARR momentum is expected to translate gradually into bookings and revenue in coming quarters.
- Q4 sales and marketing investment is expected to increase QoQ, within established TROI guardrails; investment levels will respond to demand and returns.
- Base44’s 2H non-GAAP gross margin is expected to be ~60%, with Q4 higher than Q3; margin could approach core Wix levels next year.
- GPU prepayments create an estimated $10M Q4 working-capital headwind; management expects FY26 free cash flow margin to reflect accelerated Base44 investment.


### Segment performance

- Base44 ARR neared $250M in less than two years; new-user conversion, retention, bookings dollar retention and annual-plan mix improved, with annual plans exceeding 20% for the September 2026 cohort versus ~5% a year earlier.
- Base44’s first quarterly cohort exceeded 12-month retention expectations; bookings dollar retention benefited partly from subscription upgrades, and renewal trends improved across cohorts.
- Symphony, launched in August as an SMB multi-agent operations product, reached ARR in the millions within three months; management characterized early results as encouraging.
- Studio Two launched as an open professional editor integrated with AI tools and React workflows; it can extend DIY sites into partner projects and support enterprise hosting use cases.
- Core Wix free cash flow improved through stronger gross margin, greater headcount efficiency and more efficient marketing; Base44 partner revenue was described as small but growing.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Q3 total non-GAAP gross margin was 69%, consistent with Q3 2025 and up 2 percentage points sequentially.
- Base44 Q3 non-GAAP gross margin was 58%; management expects ~60% for 2H and margin similar to core Wix next year.
- Q3 non-GAAP operating income equaled 12% of revenue.
- FY26 free cash flow margin, excluding acquisition and restructuring costs, is expected to be ~16%.
- Cash and cash equivalents were ~$1.05B at Q3-end, versus $1.63B in short- and long-term debt.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Base44’s accelerated marketing and enterprise-sales investment depends on maintaining TROI and continued demand; investment could weigh on near-term free cash flow.
- Vibe coding remains a newly forming market, with consumer awareness still developing and competing AI-agent products entering the market.
- Partner demand and customer behavior are shifting with AI tools; management cited some demand moving to Base44 or other tools, while Studio Two’s impact remains unproven.
- Base44’s path to profitability depends on scaling and marketing levels; management expects improving profitability as it grows but could not specify timing.


### Significant events and developments

- Nir Zohar became co-CEO, leading go-to-market, operations and scaling, while Avishai Abrahami focuses on product, AI and technology.
- Studio Two opened its workflow to external AI tools and connects with Wix Harmony through a shared underlying document, creating a partner lead and upgrade path.
- Symphony launched in August; several major AI companies announced agent products within a month, which management viewed as validation of the opportunity.
- A Base44 enterprise sales team is being built and expected to ramp in coming quarters, with organizational growth also supported by reallocating existing headcount.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Marketing investment is being accelerated to capture Base44 demand, with Q4 spending planned higher than Q3 and subject to TROI discipline.
- Base44 investment includes prepayment for GPUs needed to run models over the next couple of years, creating an estimated $10M Q4 working-capital headwind.
- Base44 build-out has relied substantially on reallocating core Wix headcount; further resource shifts are expected to favor Base44 without increasing overall workforce size.
- Q3-end cash and cash equivalents were ~$1.05B, with $1.63B of short- and long-term debt.


### Market and industry conditions

- Vibe coding and AI fluency are expanding the addressable market; management sees Base44 positioned to compete with legacy application and website creation methods.
- Base44 has gained a leading position in North America and the U.S.; management views the U.S. as the largest and most important market.
- Management sees SMB-focused AI operations as a distinct opportunity for Symphony, supported by existing small-business data, workflows and payment relationships.
- Management reported no significant Q3 impact from changes in Google Search and distribution.
- Open and headless workflows may let professionals and businesses start creation across more tools and internet entry points, broadening Wix’s reach beyond its direct funnel.
