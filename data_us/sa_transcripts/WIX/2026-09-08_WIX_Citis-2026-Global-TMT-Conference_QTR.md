# Wix.com Ltd. — Citi’s 2026 Global TMT Conference (2026-09-08)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Industry trends and platform evolution

- AI is transforming website creation and interaction, with agentic traffic now exceeding human traffic online.
- The role of websites is evolving, not diminishing, as AI enables more complex and personalized experiences.
- Integration of AI tools like Harmony and Gemini streamlines user intent recognition and content creation.
- Complexity in online transactions is increasing, requiring seamless synchronization across platforms and agents.
- The shift to an AI-driven internet presents both significant risks and massive opportunities for growth.


### Product strategy and customer adoption

- Users gravitate toward different solutions: Harmony for simplicity, Base44 for applications, and headless for professionals.
- Base44's ARR grew from $100M to $150M in just over two months, with continued momentum.
- Expansion of Vibe coding and natural language tools is expected to drive broader adoption as awareness increases.
- Enterprise sales efforts are ramping up, aiming to widen the customer funnel and category reach.
- Retention trends for Base44 are positive, especially among business users, with expectations for further improvement.


### Financial performance and operational efficiency

- Proprietary AI models (Harmony LLM, Base 1) have reduced inference costs to 5% of frontier alternatives.
- Cost savings are significant, but protecting IP and tailoring models to specific use cases are seen as even more valuable.
- Gross margins for Base44 are improving, with expectations for further gains next year.
- Marketing investment will be balanced against profitability, with guidance to provide more detail on strategy.
- The mix between Base44 and core products will influence consolidated margins going forward.


### Partner ecosystem and new product opportunities

- Partner bookings faced a $50M headwind, with no recent change in the magnitude of these pressures.
- Agencies are evolving their roles, focusing on helping business owners navigate new AI-driven tools.
- Ongoing dialogue with top agencies aims to co-develop solutions for the changing landscape.
- Symphony by Wix targets a new, independent TAM, offering AI-powered business solutions beyond the core platform.
- Rapid product development in the AI era enables pursuit of new opportunities with small, agile teams.
