# SolarEdge Technologies, Inc. — Investor Day 2026 (2026-09-10)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Strategic progress and turnaround

- Achieved positive free cash flow in 2025 and 2026, with six consecutive quarters of revenue and margin growth, moving from $212M to $345M in quarterly revenue and from negative to positive EBITDA.
- Exited non-core activities, reduced expenses, and ramped up U.S. manufacturing, now producing the majority of inverters and optimizers domestically.
- Focused on four pillars: financial stability, market share gains, innovation, and U.S. manufacturing scale.


### Core market evolution and growth drivers

- Residential and C&I markets are shifting from PV-only to PV-plus storage, expanding the addressable market by 2%-3% annually over the next 3-4 years.
- Storage attach rates are rising, doubling revenue per new residential installation and creating a large retrofit opportunity with over 4 million existing sites, 90% of which lack batteries.
- C&I market share grew from 29% in Q4 2024 to 57% in Q2 2026, driven by technology, safety, and regulatory advantages.
- Signed $1.7B in firm contracts for delivery through 2030, with a total opportunity of $3.3B, potentially exceeding $5B with a 30% storage attach rate.


### Product innovation: SolarEdge Nexis

- Launched Nexis, a next-generation residential energy system, offering 10.5% more energy harvested and up to $7,000 additional lifetime savings per home versus competitors.
- Nexis is modular, scalable, and durable, with rapid installation, AI-driven energy management (Sera), and strong appeal to homeowners, installers, and TPOs.
- Recognized by Forbes and Green Builder as a transformative product for home energy.


### AI factories and DC-native architecture

- AI data centers face power bottlenecks; industry is shifting to 800V DC-native architecture, with 67% of new builds expected to be DC-based by 2030.
- SolarEdge’s solid-state transformer (SST) achieves over 99% efficiency, reducing power losses from 12% to less than 1%, translating to significant revenue for AI factory operators.
- Partnerships with NVIDIA and Infineon support safety and protection standards for high-voltage DC systems.
- Expect pilot SST installations in 2027, initial revenue in 2028, and $600M in AI factory revenue by 2029.


### Financial outlook and margin expansion

- Projecting $2.4B revenue and 35% gross margin in 2029, with 23% CAGR from 2026-2029; $1.8B from core business and $600M from AI factories.
- Gross margin improvement driven by Nexis, AI factories, operational excellence, and full benefit from U.S. manufacturing incentives (45x IRA).
- Operating expenses expected to decrease as a percentage of revenue, with ongoing cost discipline, automation, and offshore initiatives.
- Strong balance sheet with positive free cash flow and increased net cash position.


### Market positioning and competitive advantages

- Over 60% of Fortune 100 companies use SolarEdge systems; more than 65 GW installed and 4.6 million sites monitored.
- Deep expertise in DC power conversion, safety, and large-scale manufacturing underpin leadership in both core and emerging markets.
- Defensible IP with multiple patents filed around SST and safety technologies.
