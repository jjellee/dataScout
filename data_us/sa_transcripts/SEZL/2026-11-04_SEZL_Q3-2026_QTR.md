# Sezzle Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-11-04)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Total transaction volume (TTV) rose 34.9% YoY to $1.4B; revenue grew 42.6% to $166.5M, with net income of $47.8M and adjusted EBITDA of $70.2M.
- Active subscribers reached 955,000, up 68.1% YoY; quarterly transaction frequency increased to 7.8x from 6.5x.
- Full-year 2026 outlook was raised to ~42.5% revenue growth, $190M adjusted net income and $5.45-$5.50 adjusted diluted EPS; preliminary 2027 adjusted EPS outlook is $7.20.


### Financial highlights

- Revenue increased 42.6% YoY to $166.5M; GAAP net income rose 79.3% to $47.8M and adjusted net income rose 89.9% to $47.7M.
- Adjusted EBITDA increased 77% YoY to $70.2M; profit growth outpaced revenue growth alongside margin expansion.
- TTV reached a record $1.4B, up 34.9% YoY and 11.6% QoQ; revenue grew faster than volume.
- Net transaction margin rose 70% YoY to $107.7M; transaction-related costs were $58.8M, or 4.2% of TTV versus 5.2% a year ago.
- Marketing spend was $15.9M, down from $19.4M in Q2 but up 81% YoY; non-transaction operating expenses were $46.2M, or 27.8% of revenue.


### Outlook and guidance

- FY2026 revenue growth outlook increased to ~42.5% from 35%; adjusted net income guidance rose to $190M from $185M.
- FY2026 adjusted net income per diluted share guidance rose to $5.45-$5.50 from $5.25; preliminary FY2027 adjusted diluted EPS outlook is $7.20.
- FY2026 credit-loss provision outlook is ~2.5% of TTV, replacing 2.5%-3% of GMV; guidance includes the typical seasonal rise in Q4.
- Revenue yield is expected to decline in Q4 due to seasonality; full-year take rate was expected to be slightly higher YoY.
- Guidance reflects a measured view of new products; SezzleCash and Sezzle Send are being managed carefully for risk and marketing as they mature.


### Segment performance

- Subscribers added 101,000 net in Q3; average quarterly revenue per monetized user rose 28.1% YoY to $41.
- Pay in 5 was the most established new offering; management said it represented ~70% of core BNPL product volume and supports customer acquisition and retention.
- SezzleCash: over 15% of eligible new Anywhere subscribers started with a cash advance; over 90% of Q3 cash users also made BNPL purchases, and over 60% took multiple advances.
- Sezzle Send was in beta through Q3 and opened more broadly in October; it enables transfers paid in full or in five installments, with recipient onboarding as a potential acquisition channel.
- Sezzle Up enrollment conversion increased 18% after September enhancements, and average weekly enrollments nearly doubled.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Net income margin was 28.7%; adjusted EBITDA margin was 42.1%.
- Revenue less transaction-related costs was 64.7% of revenue, up 10.5 percentage points YoY and near the top of the 55%-65% target range.
- Revenue as a percentage of TTV was 11.9%, up 60 bps YoY and flat QoQ.
- Provision for credit losses was 2.7% of TTV versus 3.1% a year ago; YTD provision was ~2.2% of TTV.
- Repeat usage was 97.4% of transactions, up 80 bps YoY; total transactions increased 30.1% YoY to 12.4M.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- SezzleCash and Sezzle Send remain early-stage offerings; management cited the need for further learning and product refinement, with new lending products managed cautiously for risk.
- New Cash and Send products may carry lower margins and initially higher credit-loss rates; future profitability depends partly on product mix and maturation.
- The national bank charter application is a long process with an uncertain outcome; the antitrust case remains in discovery, expected to continue through 2027.
- Q4 revenue yield is seasonally lower, and credit-loss provisions typically rise through the year after a Q1 low.


### Significant events and developments

- Applied for a national bank charter in October; the application was identified as a long-term strategic project.
- Launched SezzleCash in June and Sezzle Mobile during 2026; Sezzle Send expanded beyond beta in early October.
- Soft-launched Sezzle Anywhere in Canada at the start of Q4, extending subscription and open-loop virtual card access to Visa-accepting merchants in Canada.
- AI handled 40% of support contacts without human involvement; over 90% of Q3 engineering merges involved AI, while AI cost per unit of work fell 27% QoQ.
- Sezzle Up on-time subprime users lifted credit scores by an average of 17 points in four months; Money IQ surpassed 338,000 participants and 2M completed learning modules.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Quarter-end liquidity was ~$239M: $70.1M unrestricted cash and $169.3M available under a $300M credit line; $104M was drawn.
- Debt was 0.4x trailing 12-month adjusted EBITDA and 0.4x equity; YTD operating cash flow was $227.9M, up 64%.
- Notes receivable grew 72% YoY to $315.9M; stockholders’ equity increased 79% YoY to $277.7M.
- Repurchased $28M of stock YTD under a $100M program; management prioritizes organic product investment and evaluates excess capital for the best return.
- A new $300M credit facility entered in May at a lower rate contributed to net interest expense declining 29% YoY to $2.8M.


### Market and industry conditions

- Q4 is typically the lowest quarter for revenue yield, while Q1 and Q4 are typically strongest for bottom-line performance.
- Management sees potential for higher consumer wallet share and transaction frequency, citing current usage of 7x-8x per quarter versus other services with consumers transacting 15+ times monthly.
- AI shopping assistants are an emerging commerce channel; Anywhere virtual cards can be loaded into shopping assistants, with partnership opportunities under evaluation.
