# Materion Corporation — Q3 2026 (2026-11-04)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Record Q3 value-added sales of $313.7M, up 19% YoY, and adjusted EPS of $1.93, up 37% YoY; both were quarterly records.
- Growth and earnings gains spanned all three segments and multiple end markets; adjusted EBITDA margin expanded for a fourth consecutive quarter YoY.


### Financial highlights

- Adjusted EBITDA was $70.7M, or 22.5% of value-added sales, up 27% YoY with 150 bps of margin expansion.
- Higher volume and favorable price mix drove Q3 earnings growth and strong incremental profitability.


### Outlook and guidance

- FY26 outlook raised for the third time: high-teens top-line growth and adjusted EPS of $7.10-$7.40 vs. prior guidance of $6.80-$7.20; midpoint implies ~33% YoY growth.
- Q4 expected to deliver meaningful sales acceleration; Performance Materials expects a sales step-up, while Electronic Materials and Precision Optics expect continued strong or improving results.
- Midterm adjusted EBITDA margin target remains 23%; performance is approaching the target sooner than expected, but consistent delivery is the immediate focus.


### Segment performance

- Performance Materials VA sales were $178.7M, up 14% YoY; adjusted EBITDA was $42.6M, or 23.8% of sales, up 12%, with higher volume and pricing partly offset by unfavorable mix.
- Electronic Materials VA sales were $103.4M, up 30% YoY; record adjusted EBITDA of $33.2M was up 54%, with a 32.1% margin and 500 bps expansion.
- Precision Optics VA sales were $31.6M, up 17% YoY; adjusted EBITDA was $5.5M, up 72%, with a 17.4% margin and 560 bps expansion.
- Semiconductor sales reached a quarterly record; aerospace and defense sales rose more than 40% YoY, while telecom and data center sales increased more than 70% YoY.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Net debt was ~$382M; available credit-facility capacity was $276M, and leverage was 1.5x, at the low end of the targeted range.
- Free cash flow was $46M in Q3 and $85M YTD; conversion was ~120% for Q3 and 88% YTD.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Consumer electronics was the sole end market with negative YTD growth, tied to a specific PMI project issue; PMI production had returned to pre-quality-issue levels, or slightly higher, entering Q4.
- Forward-looking expectations are subject to factors that could cause actual performance to materially differ from current expectations.


### Significant events and developments

- Incoming orders and backlog reached record levels; backlog was up ~35% YoY and order rate was ~25% higher than at the start of the year on a YoY basis.
- Semiconductor growth was broad across data centers, memory, logic, communications, high-performance computing, and atomic layer deposition; YTD semiconductor sales growth was ~26% versus wafer-start projections of ~11%.


### Capital allocation and financing

- A $65M defense-capacity investment is expected to be completed by the end of 2027; prior capacity investments also support high-performance computing and tantalum demand.
- Capacity plans include investment in Korea and Europe, with continued organic investment and potential capacity-related M&A near customers.


### Notes to the financial statements

- Adjusted EBITDA, net income, and EPS exclude special items, non-cash charges, and certain discrete income-tax adjustments; prior-year periods were adjusted for comparison.
- Value-added sales exclude pass-through precious-metal costs.


### Market and industry conditions

- Industrial sales grew 8% in Q3 and 11% YTD; the PMI index remained above 50, and beryllium-nickel demand benefited from U.S. build activity and infrastructure improvements.
- Demand was broad-based across semiconductor, aerospace and defense, telecom and data center, industrial, and energy markets, supported by customer investment and long-term secular trends.
