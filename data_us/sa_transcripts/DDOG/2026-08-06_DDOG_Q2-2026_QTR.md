# Datadog, Inc. — Q2 2026 (2026-08-06)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q2 2026 revenue reached $1.12 billion, up 36% year-over-year, surpassing guidance high end.
- Customer base grew to 33,400, with 4,720 customers generating $100,000+ ARR, accounting for 91% of ARR.
- AI-native and non-AI customer segments both accelerated, with non-AI revenue growth in the high 20s% year-over-year.
- Product adoption deepened: 58% of customers use 4+ products, 37% use 6+, and 13% use 10+.
- Major deals included a $30M+ TCV with a global media company and a nine-figure renewal with a leading AI company.


### Financial highlights

- Free cash flow was $279 million, with a 25% margin.
- Gross margin was 79.6%, slightly down from 80.2% last quarter.
- Operating income reached $257 million (23% margin), up from 20% a year ago.
- Billings grew 38% year-over-year to $1.18 billion; RPO rose 43% to $3.47 billion.
- Cash and equivalents totaled $5 billion at quarter end.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q3 2026 revenue expected at $1.135–$1.145 billion (28–29% year-over-year growth).
- Full-year 2026 revenue guided to $4.45–$4.47 billion (30% year-over-year growth).
- Non-GAAP operating income for Q3 expected at $260–$270 million (23–24% margin); full year at $1.01–$1.03 billion (23% margin).
- Guidance incorporates usage reduction from the largest customer.
- CapEx and capitalized software expected at 4–5% of revenue for 2026.


### Segment performance

- AI customer cohort grew to 750+, including 31 spending $1M+ and 8 spending $10M+ annually.
- Non-AI customer revenue growth accelerated for the fifth consecutive quarter.
- RUM (Real User Monitoring) ARR exceeded $200M, growing over 50% year-over-year.
- Strong new logo bookings, especially in enterprise, with annualized bookings more than doubling year-over-year.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Trailing 12-month net revenue retention in the low 120s; gross revenue retention in the mid to high 90s.
- Free cash flow margin at 25%.
- Gross margin at 79.6%.
- Operating margin at 23%.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Guidance reflects conservatism due to usage reduction from the largest customer.
- Management highlights variability in large customer commitments and usage.
- Ongoing market risks and uncertainties referenced in SEC filings.


### Significant events and developments

- Over 100 new products and features announced at DASH user conference.
- Expansion of Bits AI and Datadog for AI, including new observability and security capabilities.
- Acquisition of Adaptive ML to accelerate AI research.
- Named a leader in the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Observability Platforms for the sixth consecutive year.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Ended Q2 with $5 billion in cash and marketable securities.
- CapEx and capitalized software expected at 4–5% of revenue for 2026.
- Net interest and other income for 2026 expected at $180 million.


### Market and industry conditions

- AI adoption is a major growth driver, with all top 10 AI leaders as customers.
- Rapid growth in agentic activity and AI-related workloads.
- Customers increasingly seek observability and security for complex, multi-model AI environments.
- Growing demand for cost optimization and fine-grained analytics as AI usage scales.
