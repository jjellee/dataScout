# Tower Semiconductor Ltd. — Q3 2026 (2026-11-02)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 revenue reached a record $526M, up 33% YoY, with $120M net profit; accelerating silicon photonics demand and customer commitments underpin planned growth into 2027 and beyond.
- Expansion and technology development across silicon photonics, silicon germanium and other differentiated analog platforms are supporting higher capacity, customer line value and long-term growth.


### Financial highlights

- Q3 revenue was $526M, up 14% QoQ from $460M and 33% YoY; gross profit was a record $179M, up 92% YoY from $93M.
- Operating profit was $127M, 2.5x Q3 2025; net profit was $120M versus $54M, with 23% net margin versus 14% a year earlier.
- EPS was $1.06 basic and $1.05 diluted, versus $0.48 basic and $0.47 diluted in Q3 2025.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q4 2026 revenue mid-range guidance is $600M; achievement would bring FY 2026 revenue to $2B, versus $1.57B in FY 2025.
- 2027 opening revenue run rate is targeted above $2.4B, with continued growth expected rather than typical Q1 seasonality; Q1 2027 revenue starting point targeted over 45% above Q1 2026.
- Updated model targets $3.6B annual revenue, $1.63B gross profit at 45% margin, $1.38B operating profit at 38% margin and $1.2B net profit at 33% margin.
- Updated model assumes 85% fab utilization after installation and qualification of $920M SiPho/SiGe investments; targets remain subject to pricing, costs, tool qualification and customer orders.
- SiPho capacity plan supports a stated run rate above $2.8B by Q3 2027; integrated laser and additional expansion opportunities are excluded from that baseline.


### Segment performance

- SiPho generated $240M revenue in Q3; year-end capacity is expected to be ~2x the level supporting Q3 shipments, with capacity targeted at 3x by Q3 2027 versus the Q3 2026 base.
- More than 10 top customers are engaged in NPO prototypes; NPO shipments are expected in H2 of the coming year, subject to market adoption, and carry a higher starting price point than pluggables.
- SiGe TIA/driver demand is served from three 200 mm fabs and one 300 mm fab; H6 technology has strong design-in momentum, while a Semtech capacity agreement was announced.
- BCD demand grew ~10% YoY across 200 mm and 300 mm; long-term agreements at higher ASPs support forecast blended prices 20% higher in 2027 versus 2026.
- RF-SOI revenue at Agrate reached a quarterly high; Fab 10 recorded a new high in product tape-outs. CIS production began for a high-speed global-shutter sensor, and OLED-on-silicon capacity is being prepared for a 2027 ramp.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Gross profit was $179M; net margin was 23%. Assets totaled $4B, equity $3.2B or 80% of assets, and the current ratio was ~3.7x at September 2026-end.
- Long-term model assumes 55%-58% incremental gross profit; Q3 incremental gross margin was cited at 62%, with further depreciation expected as additional tools enter service.
- Updated model targets a 7-point gross-to-operating margin gap: 45% gross margin and 38% operating margin.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- NPO growth depends on market adoption; competitive entry is expected, and future market share was not quantified.
- Model outcomes depend on successful tool, process and customer-product qualification, wafer pricing, costs and order timing; additional depreciation will affect incremental margins.
- Yen and shekel exchange rates can fluctuate; natural hedges and zero-cost cylinder transactions are used to limit margin exposure.


### Significant events and developments

- NewPhotonics entered initial production using indium phosphide laser chiplets integrated into SiPho die; die-to-wafer attach is also being developed for thin-film lithium niobate and modulators.
- NPO, optical circuit switches and coherent SiPho applications broaden the addressable opportunity; 3.2T and 400 Gb-per-lane engagements were noted, as were 16- and 32-lane NPO tape-outs at 300 mm.
- High-speed global-shutter sensor production began with a leading DSLR/MILC industry participant; OLED-on-silicon customers are preparing for an expected steep 2027 ramp.


### Capital allocation and financing

- The $920M SiPho/SiGe CapEx program is ahead of schedule and supported by customer contracts and prepayments; ~65% was paid through Q3 2026, with the balance expected in Q4 2026 and H1 2027.
- Management expects full ROI within one year on the $920M investment; Track-1 Japan 300 mm tool payments are scheduled for 2027 and 2028.
- Track-2 expansion is planned to quadruple Japan 300 mm capacity; building work is expected to start in 2028, with expansion anticipated in 2029 and possible acceleration by one quarter.
- Expansion is planned to remain self-funded; long-term customer prepayments support capacity reservations and investments.


### Notes to the financial statements

- SiPho customer prepayments, including $290M received in Q1 2026, are recorded as short- and long-term customer-advance liabilities and classified as operating cash flow.
- Long-term assets reflect the Newport fab lease prepayment and other prepayments.


### Market and industry conditions

- AI infrastructure demand is accelerating SiPho programs, while AI/cloud data centers, mobile and automotive applications support demand across analog technologies.
- All 200 mm fabs ran at 85%-90% utilization in Q3; the 300 mm fab was fully utilized, above the 85% utilization model.
- Capacity is committed ahead of installation, with customer agreements extended through 2029; management cited high yields and performance across 200 mm and 300 mm ramps as competitive strengths.
