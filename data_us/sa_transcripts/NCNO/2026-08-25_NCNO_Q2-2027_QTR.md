# nCino, Inc. — Q2 2027 (2026-08-25)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Outperformed all financial guidance metrics in Q2 FY27, with strong execution and momentum across customer segments and geographies.
- Renewed multi-year contracts with 20 of the largest U.S. enterprise customers, with an average ACV increase of over 10%.
- AI-powered platform and Banking Advisor capabilities are driving measurable customer outcomes and accelerating adoption.
- International expansion continues, with notable wins in Japan and Germany, and strong performance in community and regional banks and credit unions.
- Sales pipelines remain healthy and diversified, supporting confidence in continued growth.


### Financial highlights

- Total Q2 FY27 revenues: $161 million, up 8% year-over-year.
- Subscription revenues: $143.5 million, up 10% year-over-year; excluding U.S. mortgage, up 12%.
- Non-U.S. subscription revenues: $30.9 million, up 13% year-over-year.
- Non-GAAP operating income: $40.8 million (25% margin), up 36% year-over-year.
- Free cash flow: $34 million, up 170% year-over-year.
- Share repurchases: 15.8 million shares since April 2025 for $300 million; new $100 million authorization approved.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q3 FY27 revenue guidance: $161.25M–$163.25M; subscription revenues $143.25M–$145.25M (7–8% growth).
- Excluding U.S. mortgage, Q3 subscription revenue growth expected at 11%.
- FY27 revenue guidance: $644M–$647M; subscription revenues $573.5M–$576.5M (9–10% growth).
- Non-GAAP operating income for FY27: $171M–$174M, up 33% year-over-year.
- Free cash flow guidance raised to $137M–$142M, up 69% year-over-year.
- U.S. mortgage subscription revenue outlook reduced due to higher-for-longer rates and IMB churn.


### Segment performance

- U.S. mortgage subscription revenues: $20.6M in Q2, down 1% year-over-year; excluding mortgage, subscription revenues up 12%.
- International revenues: $36.4M in Q2, up 9% year-over-year.
- Community and regional banks, as well as credit unions, expanded adoption across multiple product lines.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Professional services gross margin: 3% in Q2, up from -3% a year ago.
- Non-GAAP operating margin: 25% in Q2, with 500 basis points expansion expected for FY27.
- Churn forecast for FY27: $25M, with about one-third from mortgage.
- 48% of total ACV now on platform pricing, up from 40% last quarter.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- U.S. mortgage market faces headwinds from persistent high rates, impacting IMB churn and revenue outlook.
- FX headwinds expected to impact international subscription revenues by $200,000 per quarter in Q3 and Q4.
- Adoption of new AI capabilities subject to customer security and governance reviews, potentially affecting pace of revenue realization.


### Significant events and developments

- Over 230 customers have purchased AI Intelligence Units, with increasing monetization as customers exceed initial bundles.
- Launched Continuous Credit Monitoring (CCM), leveraging LLMs and proprietary models for daily portfolio risk assessment.
- Released mortgage MCP, opening platform to third-party integrations and expanding SI ecosystem.
- Signed what is expected to be the largest international deal of the year early in Q3.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Repurchased 15.8 million shares since April 2025 for $300 million; new $100 million share repurchase program authorized.
- Free cash flow continues to be allocated to opportunistic share repurchases.


### Market and industry conditions

- AI adoption and platform innovation driving sales momentum and customer expansion.
- Mortgage market remains challenged by high rates, but core banking and international segments show robust growth.
- Competitive differentiation based on unified AI-powered platform, domain expertise, and regulatory compliance.
