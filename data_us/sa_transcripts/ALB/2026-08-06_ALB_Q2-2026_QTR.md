# Albemarle Corporation — Q2 2026 (2026-08-06)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Net sales reached $1.7 billion in Q2 2026, up 31% year-over-year, driven by higher pricing and volumes in both energy storage and specialties.
- Adjusted EBITDA more than doubled to $858 million, with a margin of 49%.
- Net income was $480 million, with diluted EPS of $3.52.
- Cash from operations was $710 million, with free cash flow of $638 million, reflecting strong cash conversion and operational discipline.


### Financial highlights

- Energy storage pricing rose 73% year-over-year; specialties pricing and volumes increased 11% and 8%, respectively.
- Energy storage adjusted EBITDA up 229%; specialties up 61%.
- Specialties segment net sales were $424 million, up 20% year-over-year, with adjusted EBITDA of $118 million and a 28% margin.
- Cost and productivity improvements contributed $100 million in run-rate savings year-to-date.


### Outlook and guidance

- Expecting to reach the high end of full-year cost and productivity improvement target ($100–150 million).
- Raising full-year specialties outlook: net sales to $1.4–1.6 billion and adjusted EBITDA to $275–325 million.
- Energy storage sales volumes for 2026 expected at 225,000–235,000 tons LCE, flat to down 4% year-over-year due to the CGP3 fire, partially offset by Wodgina outperformance.
- Third quarter net sales and EBITDA expected to decrease sequentially due to lower volumes and pricing.


### Segment performance

- Energy storage: Q2 net sales up 78% year-over-year; adjusted EBITDA up 229%; average realized price $20/kg LCE.
- Specialties: Q2 net sales up 20% year-over-year; adjusted EBITDA up 61%; margin up 700 basis points.
- Specialties outlook increased due to strong bromine pricing and proactive cost management.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Enterprise adjusted EBITDA margin expanded to 49%.
- Operating cash conversion exceeded 80% in Q2.
- Working capital ended Q2 at 19% of sales, below the typical 25%.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Supply chain disruptions in the Middle East estimated to impact full-year results by $70–90 million.
- Bromine market volatility and ongoing geopolitical risks could affect pricing and supply.
- Lithium market remains tight due to slow supply increases and low inventories.


### Significant events and developments

- Fire at Greenbushes' CGP3 plant in June delayed ramp-up; plant restarted August 1 and is ramping back up.
- Wodgina JV outperformed expectations, offsetting some CGP3 impact.
- Submitted environmental permit for DLE project at Salar de Atacama; pilot plant demonstrated >90% lithium recovery.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Capital spending reduced due to efficiency efforts; focus remains on brownfield projects and maintaining a strong balance sheet.
- No significant debt paydown expected; cash to be used for growth projects and maintaining optionality.


### Market and industry conditions

- Global lithium demand up 45% year-over-year, outpacing supply growth.
- Stationary storage battery production forecast for 2026 raised to 900–1,100 GWh; 2030 forecast also increased.
- EV sales up 10% year-over-year, with strong growth in Europe and emerging markets.
- Lithium inventories at historic lows; market remains tight.
