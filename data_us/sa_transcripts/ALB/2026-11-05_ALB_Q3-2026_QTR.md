# Albemarle Corporation — Q3 2026 (2026-11-05)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 net sales were $1.6B and adjusted EBITDA was $646M; YTD cash from operations reached $1.5B and free cash flow was $1.2B.
- Disciplined cost and productivity actions, higher lithium pricing, and stronger specialties pricing and volumes supported results.


### Financial highlights

- Net sales rose 19% YoY to $1.6B; adjusted EBITDA increased 186% to $646M, with adjusted EBITDA margin of 42%.
- Net income attributable to Albemarle was $297M, or $2.15/share; adjusted EPS was $2.58, excluding restructuring and related charges.
- YTD operating cash flow was $1.5B, free cash flow was $1.2B, and operating cash conversion was 67%.


### Outlook and guidance

- FY2026 specialties adjusted EBITDA guidance increased to $350M-$375M on strong YTD results, steady expected Q4 demand, and cost and productivity actions.
- FY2026 energy storage sales volumes remain 225,000-235,000 tons LCE; 2027 volume guidance is 240,000-260,000 tons LCE, supported by CGP3 and incremental output.
- Q4 energy storage net sales, adjusted EBITDA, and margins are expected to decline sequentially if current lower pricing persists; volumes are expected steady QoQ.
- FY2026 CapEx guidance fell to $300M-$350M from ~ $500M; 2027 CapEx is expected to rise modestly as growth projects advance.


### Segment performance

- Energy storage adjusted EBITDA increased 351% YoY on higher lithium pricing; Q3 shipments of 56,000 tons LCE declined YoY against a high prior-year comparison.
- Specialties adjusted EBITDA rose 31% YoY on higher pricing and volumes, including stronger bromine demand; Jordan Bromine Company achieved record quarterly production.
- Corporate and other declined, primarily because prior-year results included Ketjen refining catalyst solutions EBITDA; Ketjen was divested in Q1 2026.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted EBITDA margin was 42%; YTD operating cash flow conversion was 67%, within the stated 60%-70% benchmark range.
- Cost and productivity improvements exceeded $150M on a run-rate basis YTD, reaching the high end of the full-year target.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Middle East conflict drove higher fuel, raw material, logistics, and other costs; specialties had $70M-$90M of expected unmitigated cost escalation, with 2027 exposure dependent on conflict duration.
- Lower lithium pricing could pressure Q4 energy storage results and margins as higher-priced spodumene inventory flows into production costs.
- Supply additions face economic and execution uncertainty, including Australian marginal-cost capacity, African logistics, Zimbabwe export restrictions, and permitting hurdles for lepidolite projects.
- Lithium spot-market declines appeared disconnected from physical-market conditions; management cited healthy inventories, strong demand, and uncertain timing of announced supply.


### Significant events and developments

- Greenbushes CGP3 delivered 30 days at nameplate performance in September and resumed its ramp; full expansion benefits are expected in 2027.
- Wodgina operated three trains with higher production and recoveries; Salar de Atacama production rose YoY through September, and its DLE project advanced to feasibility.
- Rag Udd is scheduled to become CEO on February 1, with Kent Masters moving to executive chairman; this was Masters’ final earnings call as CEO.


### Capital allocation and financing

- CapEx reduction reflects capital-efficiency gains and resequencing early-stage projects; 2026 sustaining capital spending is targeted below $300M while safety, maintenance, and regulatory needs remain priorities.
- Planned brownfield growth priorities include Wodgina, Greenbushes, and Salar de Atacama; management described buybacks as an option if project timing is not right, while prioritizing long-term growth investment.
- Debt was reduced earlier in 2026, potentially lowering interest expense; a share conversion event is expected in March and may affect EPS.


### Market and industry conditions

- Global lithium consumption increased 48% YTD, with demand outpacing supply growth; stationary storage production nearly doubled YoY through September.
- Stationary storage lithium battery demand is forecast to grow at a 25% CAGR through 2030; roughly 1,000 GWh of projects are in the 2027 pipeline.
- Global EV sales rose 11% YoY through September; Europe increased 35% and rest-of-world sales rose 94%.
- Lithium contract pricing can lag market movements by three months; increased China-linked demand may also influence the spot-versus-contract mix.
