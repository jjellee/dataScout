# 10x Genomics, Inc. — Q2 2026 (2026-08-06)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q2 2026 revenue reached $151M, with $1.6M from a Takara settlement; excluding non-recurring items, revenue was $149M, up 3% YoY.
- Strong customer response and high order volume for the new Atera platform, with demand exceeding expectations.
- Robust growth in Chromium consumables and continued strength in spatial analysis, especially Xenium.
- Acquisition of Proteintech Genomics to enhance proteomics and multi-omics capabilities.
- AI adoption is driving demand for single-cell and spatial technologies across academia and biopharma.


### Financial highlights

- Consumables revenue grew 7% YoY, with single-cell up 3% and spatial up 16%.
- Instrument revenue declined 47% YoY, with Chromium down 46% and spatial instruments down 48%.
- Gross margin increased to 74% from 72% YoY, aided by lower manufacturing costs and tariff refunds.
- Operating expenses were $132.1M, nearly flat YoY when excluding litigation settlement gains.
- Cash, equivalents, and marketable securities totaled $552M, up $105M YoY.


### Outlook and guidance

- Full-year 2026 revenue guidance raised to $610M–$630M, representing 2–5% growth over 2025, excluding non-recurring items.
- Expect to ship ~40 Atera instruments in 2026, with most shipments in Q4; booked orders already exceed this number.
- Q3 revenue expected to see a modest sequential step down due to spatial product transition, with a significant Q4 step up as Atera ramps.
- Guidance assumes no improvement in the macro environment or academic funding flows.


### Segment performance

- Spatial consumables (Xenium and Visium) showed sequential growth, with Xenium as the main driver.
- Single-cell consumables growth driven by Flex Apex adoption, especially in biopharma.
- APAC revenue declined 19% YoY due to prior year pull-forward in China; Americas up 6%, EMEA up 15%.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted gross margin (excluding non-recurring items) rose to 74% from 67% YoY.
- Operating expenses flat YoY when excluding settlement gains.
- Chromium instruments represent about 2% of total sales.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Ongoing macroeconomic and academic funding challenges persist, with no improvement assumed in guidance.
- Transition to Atera is moderating purchases of existing spatial products, impacting near-term revenue.
- Manufacturing capacity is the main constraint for Atera shipments, not demand.


### Significant events and developments

- Launch of Atera, a next-generation spatial platform, with strong early demand and broad application across disease areas.
- Acquisition of Proteintech Genomics to expand proteomics and multi-omics offerings.
- New partnerships with Cleveland Clinic and Lausanne University Hospital for oncology biomarker research.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Ended Q2 with $552M in cash, equivalents, and marketable securities, providing flexibility for long-term investment.


### Market and industry conditions

- AI is a major structural tailwind, driving demand for high-quality biological data.
- Academic funding sentiment is improving, but actual spending remains delayed due to administrative bottlenecks.
- Biopharma and academic customers are increasingly integrating single-cell and spatial technologies for large-scale, AI-driven research.
