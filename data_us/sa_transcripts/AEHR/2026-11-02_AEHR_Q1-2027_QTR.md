# Aehr Test Systems, Inc. — Q1 2027 (2026-11-02)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Record Q1 FY2027 revenue of $33.0M, up 200% YoY and 75% sequentially; bookings reached $46.7M and effective backlog $109.3M as of Nov. 1.
- Growth is led by AI and data-center demand across wafer-level FOX and package-level Sonoma burn-in, with production opportunities spanning more than a dozen customers.
- Raised FY2027 revenue outlook to $150M and expects non-GAAP net income of at least 20% of revenue.


### Financial highlights

- Revenue was $33.0M versus $11.0M in the prior-year quarter; non-GAAP net income was $9.4M, or $0.28 diluted EPS, versus $0.2M, or $0.01.
- Bookings were $46.7M, up more than 300% YoY from $11.4M; book-to-bill was 1.4.
- Quarter-end backlog was $100.9M versus $80.6M at FY2026 year-end; $8.4M in post-quarter bookings lifted effective backlog to $109.3M.
- Contactors and related products generated $13.5M, 41% of revenue, versus $2.6M and 24% a year earlier; revenue grew more than fivefold.


### Outlook and guidance

- FY2027 revenue guidance raised to $150M from a previous $130M-$150M range; management described this as 200% YoY growth and triple FY2026 revenue.
- FY2027 non-GAAP net income expected to be at least 20% of revenue; consumables are expected to be ~35% of revenue.
- Management expects Q2 to remain strong, with quarterly revenue potentially increasing through the year and Q4 possibly the highest; Sonoma shipments may make Q2 gross margin lower than Q1.
- Guidance was characterized as conservative; current capacity can support shipments above $150M, and management said additional orders could still ship within FY2027.


### Segment performance

- FOX wafer-level burn-in demand is accelerating; a lead AI processor customer placed a $22M follow-on order, while another major AI supplier purchased an XP cell for Taiwan OSAT production evaluation, planned for early 2027.
- Sonoma shipments and installations exceeded a dozen systems per month for a hyperscale customer's first AI processor; its second processor qualified, with follow-on orders supporting sampling and an anticipated 2027 ramp.
- Silicon photonics customers placed additional FOX-XP production orders; silicon carbide WaferPak orders and installed-system utilization improved, and GaN gained two production customers in six months.
- Consumables are expected to contribute ~35% of FY2027 revenue versus 29%-30% in FY2026; management cited a long-term model near 30% and potential for a higher mix as installed systems drive WaferPak repurchases.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Non-GAAP gross margin was 48.5% versus 37.5% YoY, an 11 percentage-point improvement; FY2027 expected to remain near this level, within approximately 0.5 percentage point, with quarterly mix variation.
- Non-GAAP operating expenses were $7.8M versus $5.7M YoY; expenses rose approximately 36% while revenue increased 200%.
- Income tax benefit was $0.3M, producing a negative 3.7% effective tax rate, primarily from discrete stock-option and RSU tax items.
- Non-GAAP net income excludes stock-based compensation and acquisition-related adjustments; sequential non-GAAP net income increased 163%.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- The Taiwan production evaluation requires installation, validation, and production-level qualification; capacity decisions may follow data review in early calendar 2027, and management cautioned that execution involves substantial work.
- NAND opportunities are progressing more slowly than expected due to limited incremental manufacturing capacity and timing of new high-bandwidth flash volumes.
- Customer order timing and production ramps can vary significantly quarter to quarter; management identified high visibility in the new AI customer opportunity as both upside and risk.
- Q2 gross margin may be below Q1 due to a higher volume of Sonoma system shipments; annual results remain subject to product mix and shipment timing.


### Significant events and developments

- A fully configured XP wafer-level burn-in cell is planned for installation at a Taiwan OSAT in early 2027; management anticipates production-evaluation data and potential capacity decisions in the following months.
- Added Sonoma manufacturing through a Southeast Asian contract manufacturer, adding capacity for more than 20 systems monthly; initial shipments to Taiwan proceeded smoothly.
- Added Hsinchu customer-service resources and planned selective sales and support hiring; welcomed Vijay Rao, with 30 years of technology experience, to the board.
- DiTC technology enables independent thermal control of die at wafer level and will be included in the production evaluation system.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Cash used in operating activities was $3.1M; cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash totaled $105.1M versus $116.5M at FY2026 year-end.
- Remained debt-free; operating cash use reflected working-capital investment, including long-lead components for expected AI production demand.
- Manufacturing expansion and supplier commitments support higher throughput; asset-light contract manufacturing added capacity without comparable infrastructure or capital spending.


### Market and industry conditions

- AI processor complexity is increasing through multi-die packages combining accelerators, CPUs, HBM, and optical or electrical I/O, raising thermal-management and reliability-screening needs.
- Wafer-level burn-in can screen devices before costly advanced-package assembly; management sees growing adoption interest across AI processors, silicon photonics, power semiconductors, and memory.
- Silicon carbide demand is improving, with utilization described as near full; use cases include EV traction inverters, charging infrastructure, and solar, while data-center volumes are viewed as a longer-term opportunity.
- Management described standard system-level test platforms as distinct from accelerated burn-in, which applies elevated temperature and voltage to screen early-life failures; it sees FOX wafer-level and Sonoma package-level platforms addressing different needs.
