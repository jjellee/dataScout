# Rubrik, Inc. — Q2 2027 (2026-08-27)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Achieved 10th consecutive quarter of outperformance, exceeding all guided metrics for top line and profitability.
- Accelerated net new subscription ARR growth, with strong demand for agentic cyber resilience solutions.
- Raised outlook for the year, citing robust market trends and early adoption of AI-driven security products.


### Financial highlights

- Subscription ARR reached $1.66 billion, up 33% year-over-year.
- Net new subscription ARR was $96 million for the quarter.
- Subscription revenue totaled $407 million, up 37% year-over-year.
- Subscription NRR remained strong at over 119%.
- Free cash flow was $65.7 million, up from $57.5 million a year ago.
- Total revenue was $427.3 million, up 38% year-over-year.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q3 revenue expected between $429 million and $431 million, up 23% (31% normalized for material rights).
- Full-year subscription ARR guidance raised to $1.88–$1.885 billion, up 29% year-over-year.
- Full-year total revenue expected at $1.685–$1.693 billion.
- Non-GAAP subscription ARR contribution margin expected at 15.5% for the year.
- Free cash flow guidance for the year is $323–$333 million.


### Segment performance

- Customers with $100,000+ in subscription ARR grew 23% to 3,084, now contributing 88% of subscription ARR.
- Customers with $1 million+ in subscription ARR grew over 57%.
- Identity resilience business is one of the fastest-growing product lines, with significant new wins and early penetration.
- Rubrik Agent Cloud (RAC) has over 15 paying customers, with strong proof-of-concept to production conversion.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Non-GAAP gross margin was 81%, within the long-term target range of 77–82%.
- Subscription ARR contribution margin improved by 460 basis points year-over-year to 14%.
- Cash and equivalents at quarter-end were $1.75 billion, with $1.13 billion in convertible debt.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Forward-looking statements subject to risks, including market trends, execution, and AI-driven threats.
- Geopolitical and regulatory factors driving demand for non-cloud and sovereign deployments.


### Significant events and developments

- No ARR contribution from the Strata Identity acquisition in the quarter or guidance.
- Launched Rubrik Flex, a unified platform contract vehicle, with early large customer wins.
- Introduced new capabilities in Identity Resilience, including identity roll forward.
- Mythos and Frontier AI models cited as inflection points driving demand for cyber resilience.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Continued investment in R&D and go-to-market, especially in data security, AI, and emerging products.
- Focus on balancing growth with improved efficiency and profitability.


### Market and industry conditions

- AI-driven threats and agentic overreach are accelerating demand for cyber resilience.
- Customers consolidating onto unified platforms for data, identity, and agent security.
- Non-cloud business growing due to regulatory, sovereign, and geopolitical concerns.
- No material impact from hardware costs or supply constraints; software-centric business model.
