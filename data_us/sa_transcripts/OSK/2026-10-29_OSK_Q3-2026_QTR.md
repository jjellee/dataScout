# Oshkosh Corporation — Q3 2026 (2026-10-29)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 sales were $2.8B and adjusted EPS was $2.41, both below expectations; fire-truck production and delivery challenges drove the revised 2026 outlook.
- Strong demand and backlogs across segments support the outlook for 2027, while production execution—especially fire trucks and NGDVs—remains the near-term priority.


### Financial highlights

- Q3 consolidated sales rose $153M, or 5.7% YoY, to $2.8B; higher Access volume and pricing across all segments offset lower Vocational volume.
- Adjusted operating income was $217M, down $57M YoY, primarily from unfavorable mix and higher SG&A, partly offset by volume and improved price-cost dynamics.
- Free cash flow was $275M in Q3 and $433M YTD, supported by customer advances and improved inventory management.


### Outlook and guidance

- FY2026 adjusted EPS is expected at $8.50-$9.50; the low end assumes the next NGDV order slips into 2027.
- FY2026 revenue is expected at ~$11B and adjusted operating income at ~$850M; free cash flow guidance increased $50M to $600M-$700M.
- FY2026 segment outlook: Access revenue $4.8B and adjusted operating margin ~9.75%; Vocational revenue $3.7B and adjusted margin 12.5%; Transport revenue $2.3B and operating margin ~4%.
- Fire-truck production is expected to stabilize in Q4, reach ~10% above prior-year levels in 2027, and support 25%-30% growth through 2028; 2028 Vocational margins are expected at 16%-18%.
- NGDV production is expected to reach the upper end of the 16,000-20,000 annualized range by mid-2027; NGDV operating margin is expected near 10% in 2028.


### Segment performance

- Access sales rose 21% YoY to $1.3B; adjusted operating margin was 11.2%, up 20 bps, with a 1.5 book-to-bill ratio and $2.7B ending backlog.
- Vocational sales fell $68M YoY to $900M; production and shipments were ~20% below Q3 2025, while adjusted operating income declined $49M to $102M.
- Vocational adjusted margin was 11.4%; more than $10M of the income decline reflected additional production-improvement spending.
- Transport sales fell $26M, or 4%, YoY to $562M; operating income was $8M, down $28M, partly reflecting last year’s nonrecurring software IP licensing.
- Transport NGDV production improved sequentially, with supplier constraints and production buffers shaping the ramp; operating margins are expected to improve in Q4 and 2027.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted EPS was $2.41 for Q3; FY2026 adjusted EPS guidance is $8.50-$9.50.
- Access adjusted operating margin was 11.2%, up 20 bps YoY; Vocational adjusted margin was 11.4%, versus ~13.5% in Q2 and 15.5% in Q3 2025.
- Adjusted operating income was $217M; free cash flow was $275M in Q3 and $433M YTD.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Fire-truck part routing and material-flow variability disrupted production; the high-flow manufacturing transition remains slower and more challenging than planned.
- NGDV production ramp depends on critical suppliers; additional buffers are being installed, with production normalization targeted by mid-2027.
- Tariffs, raw-material prices and freight costs pressure margins; pricing and cost-management actions are intended to offset the effects.
- The timing of the next USPS order could affect Q4 earnings by up to ~$40M, or $0.50 per share; guidance’s low end assumes the order is delayed beyond year-end.


### Significant events and developments

- A strategic investment in Nextera Robotics will support work on visual AI, autonomous robotics and digital mapping, complementing JLG connected equipment and ClearSky Smart Fleet technologies.
- The U.S. Air Force ordered 34 Striker 6x6 aircraft rescue and firefighting vehicles, with initial deliveries expected later in 2026.
- The Royal Moroccan Armed Forces placed a follow-on order for HET A1 tractors and trailers.
- NGDV fielded units have logged more than 60M miles; a follow-on USPS order is expected, though timing may extend into 2027.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Repurchased ~556,000 shares for $82M in Q3.
- Moody’s upgraded the credit rating to Baa2, citing balance-sheet strength and resilience.
- FY2026 free cash flow guidance rose $50M to $600M-$700M.


### Market and industry conditions

- Access orders reached $2B in Q3 and backlog was $2.7B; mega projects, especially data centers and related activity, are driving demand from national rental companies.
- Refuse and recycling orders recovered during Q3; customer discussions and backlog indicate 2027 demand may be similar to or slightly stronger than 2026, though recovery remains measured.
- Airport infrastructure and modernization support strong AeroTech demand; Jetway investments are expected to increase passenger-boarding-bridge output in 2027.
- Defense and delivery businesses have multi-year contracts; new FMTV and FHTV contracts include economic price-adjustment clauses to help protect against inflation.
