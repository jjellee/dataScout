# Oshkosh Corporation — Q1 2026 (2026-05-08)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- First quarter adjusted EPS was $0.85, modestly below expectations, with consolidated sales of $2.3 billion, flat year-over-year.
- Full-year adjusted EPS guidance of $11.50 is maintained, with stronger performance expected in the second half due to improved price/cost, higher fire truck production, and NGDV ramp-up.
- Strong backlog and demand across segments, especially in Access and Vocational, support confidence in achieving 2028 targets.


### Financial highlights

- Adjusted operating income was $96 million, down from $192 million year-over-year, mainly due to adverse mix and higher overhead costs.
- Free cash flow improved to -$189 million from -$435 million last year, reflecting better working capital management.
- Orders exceeded $1.5 billion in Access, with a book-to-bill ratio of 1.6 and backlog of $1.8 billion.
- Vocational segment backlog stands at $6.6 billion; fire truck production up 10% year-over-year, but shipments delayed by weather.
- Transport segment sales rose $50 million to $513 million, with delivery vehicle revenue up 30% sequentially.


### Outlook and guidance

- Full-year adjusted EPS guidance remains at $11.50; about 30% of earnings expected in the first half, with a stronger back half.
- Free cash flow guidance unchanged at $550 million–$650 million.
- Access segment may contribute modestly more than prior expectations; Vocational contribution slightly reduced due to delivery shortfalls.
- NGDV production to be at the low end of 16,000–20,000 units for the year, with ramp-up continuing.


### Segment performance

- Access: Sales of $943 million, flat year-over-year; strong order intake and healthy used market; double-digit margins expected.
- Vocational: Sales of $825 million, down due to lower refuse vehicle and fire truck deliveries; adjusted operating margin at 11.4%.
- Transport: Operating income of $4 million, up $3.6 million year-over-year; higher delivery vehicle revenue, defense revenue lower.
- AeroTech: Backlog continues to build, double-digit margins, capacity investments ongoing.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted operating income margin for Access: 4.1%.
- Vocational segment margin: 11.4%.
- Book-to-bill ratio in Access: 1.6.
- Free cash flow: -$189 million for the quarter.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Dynamic cost environment with ongoing tariff changes and geopolitical uncertainty, including inflationary pressures from global conflicts.
- Section 232 tariffs and IEEPA refunds expected to largely offset each other; negligible net impact anticipated.
- No significant pre-buy expected in Vocational; any upside would be incremental.


### Significant events and developments

- $13 million IEEPA refund benefit recorded in Q1; $23 million expected for the year.
- $47 million in share repurchases during the quarter.
- Refinanced revolving credit facility with $1.6 billion capacity at a lower interest rate.
- Continued capital investments in fire truck production and AeroTech capacity.
- Launch of AI-enabled material contamination detection technology in Vocational segment.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Share repurchases of 300,000 shares for $47 million.
- Refinanced revolving credit facility, maintaining $1.6 billion capacity at a lower rate.
- Ongoing capital investments in production capacity, especially in Vocational and AeroTech.


### Market and industry conditions

- Mega projects, especially data centers, driving Access segment demand; non-residential construction remains uneven.
- Refuse vehicle market expected to be down 25–30% for the year due to cautious customer CapEx.
- Fire truck market remains strong with aged fleets and robust backlog.
- Inflationary pressures from steel, aluminum, and oil prices are embedded in guidance.
