# Cloudflare, Inc. — Investor Day 2026 (2026-06-09)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Strategic vision and market positioning

- Emphasizes a long-term commitment to building a better internet, with a focus on cost efficiency, scalability, and developer empowerment, while recognizing the need to evolve the mission as the internet shifts to an agentic era.
- Highlights the unique architectural choice of Isolates over VMs/containers, enabling efficient, scalable execution for AI agents and positioning the platform as the foundation for the agentic internet.
- Notes that agentic traffic has already surpassed human traffic online, driving a fundamental change in internet business models and monetization strategies.
- Stresses partnerships with leading AI companies and the ability to serve both supply (content creators) and demand (AI agents/companies) sides of the new ecosystem.
- Positions the platform as the only cloud prepared for the agentic era, with a global network, developer tools, and security integrated at scale.


### Product innovation and AI integration

- Developer platform growth is accelerating, with 5.5 million developers and recent acquisition of VoidZero (Vite) to enhance tooling.
- Cloudflare OS, an internal agentic platform, enables automation, agent management, and secure access to company data, serving as a model for customers.
- AI Gateway allows dynamic routing of inference jobs to the most cost-effective or capable models, optimizing spend and performance.
- Isolates technology enables running millions of agent-generated applications efficiently, offering up to 75% cost savings at scale compared to traditional cloud approaches.
- Security and Zero Trust offerings are increasingly adopted as AI drives new requirements for secure, cost-effective agent deployment.


### Financial outlook and business model evolution

- Revenue models are shifting from flat-rate subscriptions to usage-based, cap, and pool-of-funds contracts, with lag effects on revenue recognition.
- Total addressable market (TAM) is projected at $238 billion for 2026, with significant growth from developer and SASE products.
- Rule of 50 (growth + profitability) is set as the new North Star for 2027, up from Rule of 40, reflecting confidence in accelerating growth and margin expansion.
- Long-term operating model targets gross margins of 70%-77%, operating margins of 30%, and free cash flow margins of 30%-35%, with GAAP profitability expected by 2028.
- Act Four (agentic internet monetization) is expected to deliver the lowest cost to serve and book, potentially shifting unit economics off the current chart.


### Go-to-market and organizational transformation

- Sales strategy has shifted to targeted segmentation, platform selling, and increased partner contributions, driving enterprise adoption and larger deals.
- Sales productivity and capacity are at all-time highs, with continued investment planned to capture market momentum.
- AI is being applied internally to automate and optimize go-to-market, G&A, and engineering workflows, reducing support infrastructure and increasing efficiency.
- Leadership transition announced, with a focus on maintaining execution and preparing for the next phase of growth.


### Industry trends and future outlook

- Human traffic to websites is declining rapidly, replaced by agentic traffic, fundamentally altering content monetization and value exchange.
- New payment protocols (e.g., X402) and microtransactions are being developed to enable machine-to-machine commerce at internet scale.
- The platform is positioned to enable both content creators and AI companies to thrive in the agentic era, with a focus on fair value exchange and ecosystem health.
- Anticipates a golden age of content creation if new monetization models reward real knowledge and unique content.
- Expects rapid enterprise adoption of new cloud primitives as AI and agentic workloads become mainstream.
