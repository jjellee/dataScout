# The Estée Lauder Companies Inc. — Barclays 19th Annual Global Consumer Conference (2026-09-09)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Strategic transformation and brand momentum

- Six brands now exceed $1 billion in sales, with The Ordinary nearing this milestone, reflecting portfolio strength and sequential organic sales improvement for most major brands in fiscal 2026.
- The new One ELC operating model has increased agility, accountability, and speed, supporting competition with indie brands and simplifying organizational structure.
- Completion of the two-year PRGP restructuring program has resulted in greater efficiency, streamlined partnerships, and improved resource allocation to consumer-facing investments.
- Reported sales rose 5%, organic sales 3%, gross margin expanded 150 bps, operating margin 320 bps, and diluted EPS grew 66% in fiscal 2026.
- Cash flow from operations increased 39% despite higher restructuring costs, supporting reinvestment and strong momentum into fiscal 2027.


### Brand and geographic performance

- M·A·C reversed years of decline, returning to market share leadership in the U.S. through distribution expansion and innovation.
- KILIAN PARIS and The Ordinary demonstrated rapid growth, validating the Beauty Reimagined playbook for both large and indie brands.
- Growth is now more balanced across geographies, with China, Korea, Japan, and key European markets all showing strong momentum and diversified brand performance.
- In China, six brands achieved double-digit growth, and the R&D center in Shanghai now generates 30% of global innovation.
- North America is seeing stabilization and improvement, with a revamped sales force and expanded presence in fast-growing channels like Sephora and TikTok Shop.


### Innovation, digital, and consumer engagement

- Accelerated innovation across all categories, including PDRN launches for Clinique and The Ordinary, and new products in skincare, makeup, and fragrance.
- Unified global media model with WPP and expanded Shopify partnership are driving higher conversion and more efficient, targeted consumer engagement.
- AI is being leveraged to improve R&D efficiency, reduce time-to-market, and enhance ingredient and product development.
- Consumer-facing investments increased by 7% in fiscal 2026, with a focus on efficiency and emotional brand communication.
- Direct-to-consumer business is strengthening, with new digital platforms and higher conversion rates.


### Financial outlook and operational discipline

- Fiscal 2027 targets include 3%-5% organic sales growth, 150-230 bps operating margin expansion, and 24%-34% diluted EPS growth.
- Margin improvement is expected to continue, supported by PRGP benefits, reduced fixed costs, and improved segment profitability.
- Travel retail now represents 15% of business, with a more balanced model across regions and enhanced experiential retail focus.
- New travel retail team and tighter coordination with China operations are driving discipline, accountability, and value creation.
- Ongoing efficiency gains in R&D and consumer-facing investments are expected to support future growth and profitability.
