# The Estée Lauder Companies Inc. — Q4 2026 (2026-08-19)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Fiscal 2026 saw a return to growth, with organic sales up 3% and reported sales up 5% year-over-year, driven by broad-based brand and category performance.
- Operating margin expanded 320 basis points, and diluted EPS grew 66% year-over-year, significantly exceeding initial outlooks.
- Strategic transformation under the Beauty Reimagined initiative delivered faster innovation, streamlined costs, and empowered teams, positioning the company for sustainable growth.
- Travel retail, online channels, and emerging markets were key growth drivers, with notable share gains in China, the U.S., and the U.K.
- The company remains focused on core business growth, minority and single-brand M&A, and disciplined capital allocation.


### Financial highlights

- Gross margin expanded 150 basis points for the year, reaching 75.5%, and operating margin rose to 11.2%, up 320 basis points.
- Net cash flows from operations reached $1.8 billion, up from $1.3 billion last year.
- Capital expenditures were $457 million, down from $602 million, reflecting disciplined investment.
- Diluted EPS for the year was $2.51, up 66% year-over-year.
- Five of six billion-dollar brands delivered sequentially improved organic sales; Jo Malone London and TOM FORD joined the billion-dollar club.


### Outlook and guidance

- Fiscal 2027 organic net sales growth expected in the range of 3%-5%, with stronger growth in the first half.
- Operating margin guidance raised to 12.7%-13.5%, with continued SG&A optimization and modest gross margin expansion.
- Diluted EPS expected between $3.10 and $3.35; effective tax rate projected at 33%-34%.
- Net cash flows from operating activities expected between $1.3 billion and $1.4 billion, reflecting higher restructuring payments.
- Capital expenditures to be approximately 4% of sales, prioritizing consumer-facing investments.


### Segment performance

- Skincare organic sales grew 4%, with strong performance across price tiers and brands.
- Fragrance delivered 10% organic sales growth, led by hero scents and new launches.
- Makeup stabilized and improved organic sales trend by 500 basis points, led by M·A·C and TOM FORD.
- Haircare showed signs of turnaround in the U.S., though not yet back to organic sales growth.
- Online sales reached 34% of reported sales, up 3 percentage points year-over-year.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Gross margin: 75.5% (up 150 bps year-over-year).
- Operating margin: 11.2% (up 320 bps year-over-year).
- Adjusted effective tax rate: 35.7% (down from 38.8%).
- Free cash flow improved, with $3.5 billion in cash on hand at year-end.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Business disruptions in the Middle East impacted EUKEM growth by 2% in Q4; impacts not expected to be material in fiscal 2027.
- Macro environment and sales volatility remain monitored, with scenario planning in place.


### Significant events and developments

- Completion of PRGP restructuring approvals, with $823 million in cumulative charges, mainly employee-related.
- Launch of new brands and innovation pipelines, including Balmain Beauty and new product franchises.
- Expansion of AI-enabled consumer insights and unified global media model with WPP and Meta.
- Acceleration of year-end reporting timeline, with fiscal 2027 results to be reported on August 4th.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Focus on deleveraging, with cash used to pay down debt and fund consumer-facing CapEx.
- Dividend payments and disciplined approach to M&A, with transformational deals off the table.


### Market and industry conditions

- Prestige beauty market in China grew high-single-digits, with six consecutive quarters of share gains.
- Travel retail returned to growth, especially in Hainan and Korea, with inventory managed to demand.
- North America and the U.K. saw sequential improvement and share gains, with strong momentum in online and specialty channels.
