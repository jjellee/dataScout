# Semtech Corporation — Analyst Day 2026 (2026-10-15)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Financial transformation and execution

- FY 2024 net cash interest cost was $82M and FCF was -$123M; by Q2 FY 2027, annual FCF reached $193M. Net debt fell from $1.3B and 10.5x leverage to $299M and 1.1x.
- Over the last 10 quarters, revenue grew in every quarter and midpoint revenue and EPS outlook was met or exceeded; revenue rose 66%, adjusted EPS 1,083%, adjusted gross margin 470 bps and adjusted operating margin 1,220 bps.
- Transformation reduced high-cost debt, lowered cost of capital and improved liquidity, supporting focused R&D investment, customer and supplier assurance, and growth initiatives.


### Q3 FY 2027 outlook and portfolio mix

- Raised Q3 FY 2027 midpoint outlook: revenue $417.5M, up 56% YoY; adjusted gross margin 59% consolidated and 64% excluding cellular modules; adjusted operating margin 33%; adjusted diluted EPS $1.14, up 138% YoY.
- Data center and LoRa each represented about 7% of FY 2024 revenue; excluding cellular modules, Q3 FY 2027 data center is expected at 40% of revenue and LoRa at 19%.
- Data center revenue rose from $61M for all FY 2024 to an expected $145M in Q3 FY 2027; LoRa rose from $61M for FY 2024 to an expected $68M in Q3 FY 2027.
- Excluding cellular modules, adjusted gross margin increased 410 bps from Q2 FY 2025 to Q2 FY 2027; Q3 FY 2027 outlook implies a 950-bp increase. Adjusted operating margin rose 1,020 bps over the eight quarters, with an 860-bp Q3 increase expected.


### Investment, capital allocation and balance sheet

- R&D spend increased 27% from FY 2025 to LTM Q2 FY 2027, focused on customer-aligned, high-return projects; SG&A rose 8%, while SG&A as a share of revenue declined from 18% to 15%.
- Q3 FY 2027 midpoint adjusted operating expense is 26% of revenue, down 910 bps from FY 2025, 670 bps from FY 2026 and 600 bps from LTM Q2 FY 2027.
- At Q2 FY 2027, debt was $503M, cash $204M, net debt $299M and total available liquidity $561M; $100.5M matures in FY 2028, none in FY 2029-30, and $402.5M in FY 2031.
- Expected Q3 trailing-12-month adjusted EBITDA of roughly $360M and growing cash generation are expected to move net leverage below 1x, toward a maintenance target of less than 0.0x.
- Capital priorities: customer-aligned organic investment, capability-driven portfolio M&A and balance-sheet strength; HieFo adds photonics and domestic indium phosphide expertise, with data center-level gross margins and R&D and go-to-market synergies.


### FY 2030 financial framework

- FY 2030 revenue target: $3.2B, a 38% CAGR from FY 2026, excluding $166M of FY 2026 cellular module revenue expected to be divested in Q4.
- Data center revenue is projected to compound at 75% to $2.1B by FY 2030; it is expected to more than double in FY 2027 vs FY 2026 and more than double again in FY 2028.
- LoRa revenue is expected to compound at 30% through FY 2030; growth drivers include capability enhancements and smart buildings, security, drone control, edge AI connectivity and Amazon Sidewalk.
- FY 2030 targets: adjusted gross margin 70%, adjusted operating margin 48%-50% and FCF margin 35%; mix and scale support gross-margin expansion, with customer-aligned R&D a key operating-margin variable.
- Adjusted diluted EPS is projected to reach $12.40-$13, a 64%-66% CAGR; assumptions include a 20% non-GAAP tax rate and 104M weighted-average diluted shares.


### Product and market developments

- Next-generation LoRa emphasizes substantially lower standby current, longer battery life, higher data rates and SDKs; Gen 5 was cited at 6.4 Mbps with 2-3 dB greater sensitivity, enabling higher-fidelity real-time video and audio applications.
- HieFo photonics products are expected to earn data center-level gross margins; the acquisition adds AI connectivity capabilities and domestic indium phosphide manufacturing, with capacity expansion planned within the liquidity framework.
- NPO volume is expected to start near the end of next year, with CPO to follow; NPO is expected to capture about 85% of CPO's power savings while retaining an open socket.
- FiberEdge share rose from 18% to 50%, with further gains targeted; the signal-integrity strategy focuses on linear redrivers across cable and board applications as link rates increase.
- FY 2027-28 data center growth expectations are grounded in backlog, with design wins and customer engagement layered into FY 2028; outer-year assumptions use market CAGR estimates and can rise if markets exceed those assumptions.
