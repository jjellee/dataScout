# Semtech Corporation — Q1 2027 (2026-05-26)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Achieved record Q1 revenue of $291 million, up 6% sequentially and 16% year-over-year, driven by strong performance in data center and LoRa segments.
- Adjusted diluted EPS reached $0.51, up 34% year-over-year, with robust bookings and backlog supporting future growth.
- Advanced R&D and strategic initiatives, including the integration of the HieFo acquisition and portfolio optimization efforts.
- Final stages of divestiture for the cellular module business, with transition and integration discussions progressing well.


### Financial highlights

- Adjusted gross margin was 53%, 20 bps above outlook midpoint; total semiconductor products gross margin was 60.7%.
- Adjusted operating income was $59.3 million, with an adjusted operating margin of 20.4%.
- Adjusted EBITDA was $66.4 million, with a margin of 22.8%.
- Operating cash flow for Q1 was $36.2 million, up 30% year-over-year; free cash flow was $28 million, up 7% year-over-year.
- Q1 ending cash and cash equivalents were $163.3 million; principal debt remained at $503 million.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q2 net sales expected at $328 million ±$5 million, up 13% sequentially and 27% year-over-year at midpoint.
- Data center segment projected to grow 35% sequentially in Q2, with accelerating shipments of 800G and 1.6T components.
- Adjusted gross margin expected at 54% ±50 bps; semiconductor products gross margin at 62.1% ±50 bps.
- Adjusted operating margin at 21.9% and adjusted EBITDA margin at 24.2% for Q2.
- Adjusted diluted EPS for Q2 expected at $0.61 ±$0.02, up 20% sequentially and 49% year-over-year.


### Segment performance

- Infrastructure net sales were $98.8 million, up 14% sequentially and 36% year-over-year, led by data center growth.
- Data center net sales hit a record $71.6 million, up 14% sequentially and 39% year-over-year, with strong demand for 800G FiberEdge and TIA solutions.
- High-end consumer net sales were $38.4 million, up 5% sequentially and 8% year-over-year, with TVS business outpacing handset volumes.
- Industrial net sales were $153.9 million, up 2% sequentially and 8% year-over-year, driven by LoRa-enabled sales of $44.9 million, up 12% sequentially and 14% year-over-year.
- IoT systems and connectivity net sales were $88.3 million, down 2% sequentially but up 2% year-over-year.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted gross margin: 53% (Q1), expected 54% (Q2).
- Adjusted operating margin: 20.4% (Q1), expected 21.9% (Q2).
- Adjusted EBITDA margin: 22.8% (Q1), expected 24.2% (Q2).
- Adjusted diluted EPS: $0.51 (Q1), expected $0.61 (Q2).
- R&D was 17.6% of net sales in Q1, up 17% year-over-year; SG&A was 15.1% of sales, down 200 bps year-over-year.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Forward-looking statements subject to risk factors outlined in the latest annual report and earnings presentation.
- Capacity constraints in the supply chain are being actively managed, with ongoing expansion efforts.


### Significant events and developments

- HieFo acquisition completed in March, enhancing the signal integrity segment and data center offerings.
- Divestiture of the cellular module business nearing completion.
- Capacity expansion underway to meet strong demand for gain chips and other products, targeting 3x-4x increase by year-end.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Continued investment in high-conviction R&D programs, especially for data center and LoRa.
- SG&A as a percentage of sales is declining, with operational leverage improving.
- Net interest income position maintained in Q1.


### Market and industry conditions

- Strong demand across data center, LoRa, and high-end consumer markets, with broad-based growth in 800G, 1.6T, and emerging optical and copper solutions.
- Industry trends favoring power-efficient, high-bandwidth connectivity solutions, with increasing adoption of linear optics and copper technologies.
- LoRa business rebounding, driven by new application verticals and edge AI opportunities.
