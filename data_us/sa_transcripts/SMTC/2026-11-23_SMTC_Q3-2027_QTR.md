# Semtech Corporation — Q3 2027 (2026-11-23)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Record Q3 revenue of $423M, up 24% QoQ and 58% YoY; record adjusted EPS of $1.17, up 144% YoY.
- Growth was led by data center and LoRa; management cited record backlog, bookings and design wins as support for continued momentum.


### Financial highlights

- Q3 adjusted gross margin was 59.3%, up 480 bps QoQ and 630 bps YoY; excluding cellular modules, margin was 64.0%.
- Adjusted operating income was a record $139M, with 33.0% margin; adjusted EBITDA was a record $146M, with 34.5% margin.
- Adjusted diluted EPS was a record $1.17, up 65% QoQ and 144% YoY; operating cash flow was $65M and free cash flow was $55M.
- CapEx was $10M, approximately 2% of sales, including photonics capacity expansion; Q3 cash was $251M.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q4 FY27 sales guidance is $480M ±$5M, up 13% sequentially and 75% YoY at midpoint; guidance includes cellular modules for the full quarter.
- Q4 adjusted EPS guidance is $1.33 ±$0.03; adjusted operating margin is 33.3% at midpoint and adjusted EBITDA margin is 34.7%.
- Q4 adjusted gross margin guidance is 58.9% ±50 bps, or 64.3% excluding cellular modules; management expects margins to progress toward its 70% target.
- FY27 data center revenue is expected to reach roughly $520M, up 133% YoY; management expects FY28 data center revenue to exceed $1B.
- NPO revenue is expected to begin in 2H FY28; the new onboard linear-equalizer application is expected to ramp in 1H FY28.


### Segment performance

- Infrastructure sales were $175M, up 42% QoQ and 125% YoY; data center sales were $145M, up 45% QoQ and 158% YoY.
- Industrial sales were $208M, up 16% QoQ and 41% YoY; LoRa sales reached a record $68M, up 18% QoQ and 69% YoY.
- High-end consumer sales were $39.2M, flat QoQ and down 6% YoY; TVS grew sequentially despite smartphone memory supply constraints.
- Data center growth spanned optical, copper and photonics; LoRa strength reflected broader applications and product adoption.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted net operating expenses were $111M, 26% of sales, versus 30% in Q2 and 32% a year ago.
- Adjusted normalized tax rate was 18%; Q3 remained in a net interest income position.
- Cash of $251M and debt principal of $503M resulted in net debt of $252M; net leverage was 0.7x, down from 1.1x at Q2 end.
- Q3 adjusted gross margin excluding cellular modules was 64.0%; Q4 guidance for the same basis is 64.3%.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Photonics capacity expansion creates near-term cost and margin pressure as capacity is built ahead of shipments; management expects the business to reach data center-level gross margins as it scales.
- Memory supply constraints are weighing on the broader smartphone industry; a mid-quarter cellular-module divestiture close would reduce module revenue but is expected to have no meaningful adjusted EPS impact.
- Assembly delays associated with Thai flooding and 3-nm DSP shortages were raised in Q&A; no component pushouts or cancellations had been experienced.
- Potential restrictions on Chinese transceiver modules could shift shipments among customers; management expected no meaningful impact based on broad customer coverage.


### Significant events and developments

- CopperEdge secured additional ACC platform wins with an existing hyperscaler and an onboard linear-equalizer win for a second hyperscaler’s inference platform.
- The onboard design matched or exceeded retimer signal integrity while reducing power by 90% and removing added latency; deployment is expected to begin in 1H FY28.
- LoRa Plus combines LoRa, Z-Wave and Wi-SUN on one chip; Ring launched LoRa-based sensors in the U.S., and Amazon Sidewalk expanded to Canada and Mexico.
- Cellular-module divestiture remained subject to regulatory clearances, with closing expected in Q4 FY27.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Q3 CapEx of $10M included investment in photonics capacity; capacity is planned to expand 3–4x by year-end and another 3–4x by the end of next year.
- R&D spending is increasing for customer-backed data center, photonics and LoRa programs, while SG&A is declining as a percentage of revenue.
- Q4 adjusted net operating expenses are guided to $123M ±$2M, including increased R&D investment.


### Market and industry conditions

- Data center architectures are transitioning from 800G to 1.6T and beyond; demand for lower-power, lower-latency connectivity supports linear analog solutions, NPO and future CPO adoption.
- Management cited record bookings and backlog, customer demand pull-ins, and broad engagement across optical, copper and photonics programs as evidence of robust demand into 2028.
- Foundry capacity is not a current limitation; back-end diversification and capacity expansion are intended to support growth, though some test equipment has longer lead times.
- NPO is viewed as a nearer-term opportunity than CPO, with potential to add TIAs, drivers and lasers; CPO laser contributions are expected in later years.
