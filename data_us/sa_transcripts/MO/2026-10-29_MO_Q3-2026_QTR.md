# Altria Group, Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-10-29)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Adjusted diluted EPS grew 4.8% in Q3 and 5.1% in 9M, supported primarily by smokable products; FY guidance was raised after strong 9M performance.
- Continued investment in smoke-free products alongside dividends and share repurchases; on! expanded its higher-strength and flavor lineup.


### Financial highlights

- Adjusted operating companies income rose 3.9% to $3.1B in Q3 and 4.1% to $8.8B in 9M.
- Price realization was 4.4% in Q3; reported domestic cigarette volume declined 2.9% in Q3 and 2.8% in 9M.
- Adjusted equity earnings from ABI were $191M in Q3, up 21.7% YoY.


### Outlook and guidance

- FY adjusted diluted EPS guidance raised to $5.66-$5.74, growth of 4.5%-6% from the 2025 base of $5.42; prior guidance was 2.5%-5%.
- Guidance includes smoke-free investment, particularly on! PLUS 12 mg and flavor extensions; management cited continued consumer pressure from inflation and elevated gas prices.
- Current contract manufacturing arrangements are expected to contribute relatively evenly across Q3 and Q4; the Philip Morris agreement begins in 2027, with no 2027 duty-drawback range provided.


### Segment performance

- Smokable adjusted OCI grew 3.9% to $3.1B in Q3; adjusted OCI margin expanded 0.1 percentage point to 64.5%.
- Domestic cigarette volume, adjusted for trade inventories, declined an estimated 4% in Q3 and 9M, slightly below the estimated 5% industry decline.
- Marlboro premium-segment share reached 60%, up 0.3 share point YoY; Basic share rose 1.8 points YoY, while total PM USA retail share increased 0.3 point YoY.
- Oral tobacco adjusted OCI fell 3.7% to $443M in Q3 and 4% to $1.3B in 9M; adjusted margins were 66.4% and 66.8%, respectively.
- on! Q3 shipment volume rose 13.3% to 47.8M cans; retail share was 8.3%, up 0.1 point YoY and down 0.3 point sequentially.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted OCI margin was 64.5% for smokable products in Q3 and 66.4% for oral tobacco products.
- Debt-to-EBITDA was 1.9x at September 30, in line with the approximately 2x target.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Persistent inflation and elevated fuel prices are pressuring household budgets and discretionary spending, weighing on adult smokers.
- Oral tobacco results faced lower moist smokeless tobacco volumes; growth in on! did not offset those declines.
- The e-vapor market remains predominantly illicit; management said legal participation depends on sustained enforcement and faster FDA authorizations.
- FDA enforcement guidance may change, and management said a durable, predictable authorization process requires rulemaking.


### Significant events and developments

- Helix expanded on! PLUS 12 mg nationally across mint, wintergreen, and tobacco; Blueberry Mint and Mango Pineapple launched, with Watermelon Mint planned for select stores in November.
- Helix and NJOY sued the FDA over PMTA review timing; the FDA subsequently announced plans to evaluate changes to the framework and pursue rulemaking.
- NJOY ACE patent issues were addressed; a return to e-vapor is planned, with timing unannounced and a disciplined, profitability-focused approach.
- A retail trade program secured premium on! placement in stores representing approximately 90% of its volume; the inaugural on!vitational launched as a planned multi-year brand platform.


### Capital allocation and financing

- More than $6B returned to shareholders in 9M, including $5.3B in dividends and $735M in share repurchases.
- Quarterly dividend increased 4.7% in August to $1.11 per share, the 61st increase in 57 years; the stated goal is mid-single-digit annual dividend growth.
- Board authorized a $1B expansion of the existing share repurchase program, which now expires December 31, 2027.


### Market and industry conditions

- Oral tobacco category volume was estimated up 5.5% over six months; nicotine pouches gained 5.8 share points in Q3 and exceeded 60% of the category.
- Discount cigarette retail share grew 2 points in Q3 to 34.1%, as economic pressure continued to shift demand toward discount products.
- Estimated adult vapers totaled 20M at September-end, down 1M YoY, primarily due to fewer consumers using illicit products.
- Management attributed moderation in cigarette declines partly to reduced cross-category movement from illicit e-vapor; it said cigarette price elasticity remained -0.35.
