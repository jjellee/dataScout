# Teradyne, Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-10-28)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 revenue topped $1.3B, up 70% YoY, with non-GAAP EPS of $2.29, up over 150%; AI-related revenue exceeded 60% of sales.
- All three business groups grew YoY; revenue and non-GAAP EPS exceeded the high end of guidance.
- Management cited strong demand signals, backlog and growth opportunities across AI infrastructure, memory, Product Test and Robotics.


### Financial highlights

- Q3 revenue exceeded $1.3B for the third consecutive quarter; 9M revenue was $3.9B and non-GAAP EPS was $7.31, up over 85% and 235% YoY, respectively.
- Q3 non-GAAP operating income was $413M, with a 31.6% operating margin.
- 9M free cash flow was $806M, up nearly 250% YoY; Q3 free cash flow was $227M.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q4 revenue guide: $1.25B–$1.35B; non-GAAP EPS: $1.95–$2.25; gross margin: 57.5%–58.5%; operating expenses: 28.5%–29.5% of sales.
- Q4 revenue midpoint of $1.3B implies ~20% YoY growth and FY revenue of ~ $5.2B, up over 60%; Q4 is expected to be the fourth consecutive quarter above $1.25B.
- 2027 is expected to deliver healthy growth, with each quarter projected to grow versus the corresponding 2026 quarter; Q1 is expected to grow from Q4, subject to quarterly variability.
- Management expects memory to remain strong in Q4 and significant YoY memory growth in 2027, led by HBM; compute, Product Test and Robotics are also expected to grow in Q4.
- An updated target earnings model is planned for the Q4 earnings call; 2027 ASIC programs are expected to be more back-half weighted.


### Segment performance

- Semi Test revenue exceeded $1B, up 77% YoY; SoC was $676M, memory $301M and IST $95M.
- Semi Test compute revenue grew nearly 50% YoY; auto and industrial rose nearly 90%, while mobile increased 60% from a low base and remained below historical levels.
- Memory revenue reached a record $301M, its fourth consecutive quarter above $200M; IST set a record at $95M, up ~150% YoY.
- Product Test revenue was $138M, up 56% YoY, with book-to-bill of 1.4x; the group grew nearly 30% QoQ.
- Robotics revenue was $96M, up 28% YoY; the group posted its highest Q3 revenue and achieved quarterly profitability.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Q3 gross margin was 59.1%, up 60 bps YoY and down 70 bps QoQ, with the sequential decline driven primarily by product mix.
- Q3 non-GAAP operating margin was 31.6%; Q4 non-GAAP operating profit rate is guided to 28%–30%.
- FY 2026 gross margin was projected at ~59.4%, up ~110 bps YoY; Product Test was described as accretive to the overall portfolio.
- Q3 cash and investments totaled $625M, up 21% QoQ.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Longer component lead times and unforecast demand could extend delivery lead times; capacity additions and procurement are intended to support demand and responsiveness.
- 2027 gross margin faces volume, mix, inflation and supply-cost pressures, partly balanced by growth and cost-related tailwinds; management described forces as roughly balanced.
- Compute and robotics revenue timing is uneven; ASIC programs and a major robotics customer's deployment schedule may weight growth toward H2 2027.
- CPO adoption timing depends on test economics, high test intensity and broader assembly-flow challenges; the pace and scale of deployment remain uncertain.
- Robotics visibility beyond its largest customer remains short-term and pipeline-driven, with customer deployment pace still uncertain.


### Significant events and developments

- Introduced UltraFLEXplus instruments for next-generation AI and data-center devices, including deeper-memory, high-power and PCIe Gen 6 test capabilities.
- Launched Iris 100 optical test for microLED and VCSEL emitters, new Titan HP burn-in capabilities, UltraFLEXplus S for edge AI, and Magnum E2 DRAM tester.
- Announced a FormFactor partnership for optical testing and other opportunities; a FormFactor investment was expected to close in Q4, subject to regulatory approval and customary conditions.
- Universal Robots introduced its Gen 7 cobot platform and PolyScope X features tailored to electronics assembly and server/rack build applications.
- Robotics partnerships include NVIDIA, Analog Devices, Scale AI, Generalist, Bright Machines and Sereact.


### Capital allocation and financing

- 2026 investment across R&D and CapEx is expected to approach $1B, with R&D representing a double-digit percentage of sales.
- Capital priorities include reinvestment, maintaining cash reserves, dividends, share repurchases and retaining capacity for accretive M&A.
- Q3 dividends totaled $20M and share repurchases were $99M.
- Inventory increased to support future growth; management attributed the build roughly equally to demand preparation and longer component lead times, alongside normalization from unusually low Q1 levels.


### Market and industry conditions

- AI-related revenue exceeded 60% of total sales; management described the industry shift toward data-center build-outs and semiconductor capacity expansion as structural.
- Test's share of semiconductor CapEx rose from 6.4% in 2025 to 8.1% for the first eight months of 2026; semiconductor CapEx forecasts for 2027 and beyond were revised upward.
- HBM, DDR, LPDDR and flash capacity expansion is increasing memory test demand; lower HBM stack heights can mean more stacks tested from a given DRAM wafer supply.
- HDD exabyte growth exceeded 20% in 2026 as data-center nearline storage demand accelerated; a third major customer also contributed to HDD test expansion.
- CPO test-equipment TAM was estimated near $100M in 2026 and $300M–$700M by 2028, with potential to reach $1B or more over time; NPO, pluggables and copper remain alternative connectivity paths.
