# Coty Inc. — Q4 2026 (Q&A) (2026-08-20)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Leadership emphasized improvements in Q4 but acknowledged dissatisfaction with current performance, outlining a clear framework to strengthen execution and close the gap to market.
- Strategic focus is on driving sell-out, market share, and profitability, with a shift from a sell-in to a sell-out culture, including changes to incentive structures for FY 2027.
- The company is rolling out performance improvement programs in both the U.S. and Europe, focusing on SKU reduction, innovation, and targeted advertising.
- A strategic review of the Consumer Beauty business is ongoing, with an aspiration to complete by the end of calendar 2026.


### Outlook and guidance

- Guidance provided for Q1 only, with a target to exceed $50 million in FY 2027 EBITDA and free cash flow close to FY 2026 levels.
- FY 2027 is characterized as a transition year, with sequential improvement expected after Q1 and upside potential tied to faster sell-out and market share gains.
- The company aims to return the underlying portfolio (excluding Gucci) to growth in FY 2028, focusing on incremental innovation and portfolio strength.


### Segment performance

- U.S. consumer brands like COVERGIRL and Sally Hansen have reduced their gap to the market, with Sally Hansen now growing ahead of the market.
- European consumer brands are beginning to see traction, with interventions rolling out to brands like Rimmel, Max Factor, and Bourjois.
- Brazil has returned to growth, contributing positively to mass body and skincare.
- Travel retail is growing and remains important for both sales and brand image.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- $20–$30 million in costs built in for oil prices between $90–$100 per barrel.
- Potential $30 million upside from a pending tariff refund.
- Low double-digit percentage of sales and healthy profit associated with the Gucci brand, with compensation secured for a year of profit and cash.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Pace of sell-out and market share gains is uncertain and a key risk for FY 2027 performance.
- Middle East market conditions and oil price volatility could impact costs and profitability.
- Shelf space stability is contingent on continued sell-out improvements; risk of loss if performance lags.
- Competitive promotional environment and pricing actions by peers could affect margins.


### Significant events and developments

- Strategic review of Consumer Beauty business underway, with a strong intent to complete by end of 2026.
- Gucci brand transition to Kering, with full compensation for profit and cash, and a restructuring program planned to right-size costs.
- Introduction of new brands like Swarovski and Etro planned for next year.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Proceeds from the Gucci deal will be used to pay down debt and fund restructuring.
- CapEx in makeup is decreasing due to procurement improvements and vendor negotiations.


### Market and industry conditions

- Promotional and pricing environment remains dynamic, with companies shifting to more targeted pricing strategies.
- Retailers are prioritizing high-turn SKUs, and shelf resets are being managed to maintain or improve shelf space.
- Travel retail is evolving from price-driven to experience-driven, benefiting brand launches.
