# Coty Inc. — Q4 2026 Prepared Remarks (2026-08-19)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Fourth quarter results exceeded expectations, driven by strong cash discipline and improved execution, despite ongoing profitability headwinds.
- Fiscal 2027 is positioned as a transition year, focusing on strengthening core franchises, simplifying the portfolio, and setting up for sustainable growth.
- Leadership transition with Soraya appointed as CFO, ensuring continuity in financial strategy.


### Financial highlights

- Q4 like-for-like sales declined 1%, outperforming guidance for a mid-single-digit decline; full-year like-for-like sales declined 5%.
- Adjusted gross margin in Q4 was 60.9%, down 140 bps year-over-year; full-year adjusted gross margin was 63%, down 190 bps.
- Adjusted EBITDA declined 26% year-over-year in Q4 and 22% for the full year; adjusted EPS was breakeven in Q4 and $0.34 for the year.
- Free cash flow reached $348 million, up $70 million year-over-year, despite a $200 million EBITDA decline.
- Net debt at year-end was $2.9 billion, down $840 million year-over-year; leverage at 3.4x.


### Outlook and guidance

- Fiscal 2027 Q1 like-for-like revenue expected to decline low to mid-single digits; adjusted gross margin to decline 50–100 bps year-over-year.
- Q1 adjusted EBITDA expected to decline by low teens percentage; adjusted EPS forecasted at $0.11–$0.13.
- Free cash flow for H1 fiscal 2027 expected to exceed $300 million.
- No full-year fiscal 2027 guidance issued due to ongoing strategic review and portfolio changes.


### Segment performance

- Prestige division Q4 like-for-like sales down 0.5%; prestige fragrance revenues declined 1% in Q4, but prestige cosmetics saw double-digit growth.
- Consumer beauty Q4 like-for-like sales declined 3%, with improvements in Sally Hansen and COVERGIRL; Rimmel gained share in the UK.
- Prestige adjusted EBITDA margin was 20.5% for the year; consumer beauty EBITDA under pressure but improved sequentially in Q4.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted gross margin: 60.9% in Q4, 63% for the year.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin for Prestige: 20.5% for fiscal 2026.
- Net debt: $2.9 billion; leverage: 3.4x.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Ongoing macroeconomic and geopolitical volatility, including the Middle East conflict and oil price fluctuations, may impact cost of goods.
- Anticipated headwinds from the Gucci Beauty license exit by fiscal 2028 and normalization of variable compensation.
- Quarterly performance variability expected during the transition and strategic review period.


### Significant events and developments

- Early transition of the Gucci license to Kering, securing $400 million in cash proceeds plus inventory, with $250 million received and $150 million due by September 2027.
- Wella monetization and strong free cash flow contributed to significant debt reduction.
- Implementation of the Coty.Curated strategic framework, focusing on core brands, simplification, and operational discipline.
- Launch of major innovation initiatives, including Marc Jacobs Beauty and new fragrance lines.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Debt reduction remains a top priority, with proceeds from Gucci transaction allocated to debt paydown, core brand investment, and organizational optimization.
- Strong free cash flow supports strategic investments and balance sheet strengthening.


### Market and industry conditions

- Prestige beauty market grew 6% and mass beauty 5% in H2 fiscal 2026, but company sell-out trailed both segments.
- Consumer demand for beauty remains resilient, though competition is intense and consumers are more selective.
- Innovation and targeted launches are key to closing the gap to market growth.
