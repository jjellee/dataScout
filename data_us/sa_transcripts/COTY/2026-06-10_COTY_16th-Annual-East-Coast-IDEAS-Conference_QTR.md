# Coty Inc. — 16th Annual East Coast IDEAS Conference (2026-06-10)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Industry trends and consumer behavior

- Gen Z consumers now use multiple fragrances weekly, driving a shift from single-signature scents to fragrance wardrobes and fueling U.S. prestige fragrance market growth to over $10 billion in six years.
- The global beauty market has shown resilient annual growth of 3%-5%, with Coty's categories growing 4%-5% year to date despite macro uncertainty.
- Beauty is positioned as a staple, discretionary, and luxury category, making it less price elastic and more resilient to economic cycles.
- Coty leverages a diversified portfolio across price points and geographies, reducing reliance on any single brand or region.


### Strategic framework and operational focus

- Coty Curated, a new strategic framework, prioritizes core brands and markets, reallocates resources, and aims for sustainable long-term growth.
- Recent leadership changes include a new chairman and interim CEO, with a focus on sharper priorities and execution.
- The company is concentrating marketing spend on consumer and influencer engagement, cutting smaller launches, and focusing on market share improvement.
- A strategic review of the consumer beauty division and broader portfolio is underway, with plans to communicate an end-state framework this year.


### Brand portfolio and market position

- Coty is a top-three player in prestige fragrances globally with a 12% market share and operates a unique licensing model, recently adding Swarovski, Etro, and Marni.
- 85% of the portfolio is owned, perpetual, or long-term licensed, reducing license duration risk.
- Prestige division accounts for two-thirds of sales and over 90% of profit, with significant growth in brands like Burberry, Hugo Boss, Chloé, and Marc Jacobs.
- Coty is the number two player in mass cosmetics globally, with strong brands like COVERGIRL, Rimmel, and Sally Hansen.


### Financial performance and capital structure

- From fiscal 2021 to 2024, Coty achieved double-digit sales growth, margin expansion, and increased EPS, but faced challenges in fiscal 2025 due to category normalization, retailer destocking, and executional issues.
- Gross margin expanded by 500 basis points to 65% but recently declined to 63.3%; EBITDA margin reached 18.4% before recent declines.
- Over $850 million in cost savings were realized in five years, with an additional $200 million targeted this year.
- Leverage reduced from nearly 7x to 2.7x, with multiple credit rating upgrades; first major debt maturities now due in 2028.


### Challenges, lessons, and future outlook

- Recent underperformance attributed to spreading resources too thin across initiatives, leading to market share loss and executional challenges.
- Gucci will exit the portfolio in the coming years, requiring external communication on future impacts.
- Management is focused on core business execution, targeted adjacencies like ultra-premium fragrances and fragrance mists, and leveraging pricing power without overuse.
- Vertically integrated manufacturing and in-house R&D provide cost and product differentiation advantages over asset-light competitors.
