# Coty Inc. — TD Cowen 10th Annual Future of the Consumer Conference (2026-06-02)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Leadership transformation and strategic framework

- Division shifted from fragmented leadership to an integrated model under a single president, driving simplicity and agility in decision-making since late 2025.
- Leadership team now combines brand and innovation roles to align innovation with core portfolio goals.
- New operating model includes creation of innovation and advocacy hubs in the U.S. and EU to manage brands and product development more efficiently.


### Brand portfolio and market focus

- Portfolio includes COVERGIRL and Sally Hansen in the U.S., and Rimmel, Max Factor, and Bourjois in Europe, with COVERGIRL as the flagship.
- Brand strategies are tailored to target core consumer segments, with COVERGIRL and Max Factor focusing on Gen X, who hold over 70% of global beauty purchasing power.
- Each brand is being repositioned to sharpen relevance and visual identity, with specific equity agendas for different markets.


### Innovation and product strategy

- Innovation is now curated and focused, reducing SKU intensity in seasonal launches and ensuring new products support core franchises.
- Product development emphasizes accessibility, speed to market, and addressing real consumer needs, with a new center of excellence for innovation.
- Trend-driven launches are balanced with core product support, especially in high-loyalty segments like face and eye.


### Channel and market execution

- Brick-and-mortar remains the largest channel, but digital platforms like Amazon and TikTok Shop are growing and used to drive awareness and demand.
- Brand-market combinations are prioritized for growth, such as COVERGIRL at Walmart and Rimmel at Boots.
- Influencer marketing and PR are prioritized by redirecting non-working spend to direct brand-building activities.


### Financial performance and profitability

- Gross margin contraction of 400 basis points was driven by supply chain overhead, tariffs, and excess inventory.
- Profitability improvement initiatives include indirect procurement, portfolio right-sizing, and a new operating model to optimize fixed costs.
- Innovation hub and R&D right-sizing are underway to rebalance product development and cost structure.


### Performance highlights and future outlook

- COVERGIRL and Sally Hansen show strong momentum, with Sally Hansen outpacing the category in U.S. nail care.
- European brands like Rimmel, Max Factor, and Bourjois are posting mid-single to 10% growth in key markets like Poland.
- Lifestyle scenting is a growing segment, with focus on brands like Adidas and expansion in markets such as Brazil.


### Technology, AI, and consumer trends

- AI partnerships are being leveraged for scalable, efficient asset production and marketing.
- The future of beauty is seen as more efficient and consumer-centric, with AI enabling sharper execution.
- Consumer demand for authenticity, emotional connection, and efficacy is shaping product and brand strategies.


### Organizational culture and talent

- Transformation agenda relies on motivated, capable teams who embrace change and drive impact.
- Emphasis on being maverick, curious, and consumer-focused to fuel innovation and growth.
