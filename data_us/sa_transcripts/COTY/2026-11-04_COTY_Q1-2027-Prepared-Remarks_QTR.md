# Coty Inc. — Q1 2027 Prepared Remarks (2026-11-04)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q1 FY 2027 results exceeded guidance and expectations, with like-for-like revenue down 1.6%; FY 2027 remains a transition year, and recovery is not expected to be linear.
- Priorities are quality growth, productivity-funded reinvestment, and stronger cash generation, with execution and accountability focused on sustainable results.


### Financial highlights

- Net revenue was $1.56B, down 1.6% like-for-like; adjusted EBITDA was $274M, down 7% YoY, with both ahead of consensus.
- Free cash flow was $85M, up $73M YoY; adjusted EPS excluding the equity swap was $0.15, flat YoY and above $0.11-$0.13 guidance.
- Interest expense fell $15M YoY to $31M, reflecting debt reduction and lower financing costs.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q2 like-for-like revenue is expected to decline at a low-single-digit rate; FX is expected to be a low-single-digit revenue headwind, driven by BRL depreciation.
- Q2 adjusted EBITDA is expected to decline at a high-single-digit rate; adjusted EPS excluding the swap is forecast at $0.16-$0.18.
- Top six core Prestige brands are expected to grow in Q2, at a more moderate pace than Q1; H1 free cash flow is expected to exceed $300M.
- Strategic review completion is targeted by end-calendar 2026; a longer-term outlook and financial framework is intended after its completion.


### Segment performance

- Prestige revenue was $1.06B, down 1% like-for-like; Consumer Beauty revenue was ~$500M, down 3%, with both divisions improving sequentially versus FY 2026.
- Prestige sell-out declined 2% as the market grew 3%; the top six core brands returned to sell-out growth, up 1%, and grew 5% like-for-like in Q1.
- Consumer Beauty sell-out declined 3% against 5% market growth; top four core brands were broadly in line with the division in Q1.
- Prestige Asia Pacific grew strongly, led by China and Southeast Asia, while the Americas and EMEA declined; Consumer Beauty remained under pressure in all three regions.
- Kylie Cosmetics delivered double-digit like-for-like growth; Marc Jacobs fragrance grew double digits, while Burberry and Hugo Boss fragrance each recorded low-single-digit growth.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted gross margin was 63.3%, down 120 bps YoY and below the expected 50-100 bps decline; adjusted EBITDA margin was 17.6%, down 120 bps.
- Adjusted EBITDA was $274M, down 7% YoY; total A&CP was 25% of revenue and slightly lower YoY.
- Free-cash-flow conversion, measured as FCF/adjusted EBITDA, improved to ~31% from 4% in the prior-year quarter.
- Net debt was ~$2.6B, down ~$300M since June; quarter-end leverage was ~3.1x.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- FY 2027 remains a transition year, with volatility across brands and markets and recovery expected to be uneven; performance remains below ambitions.
- Middle East conflict-related freight and material costs pressured Q1 gross margin; at current oil prices, the cost headwind is expected to persist through FY 2027.
- Excess and obsolescence costs, elevated versus prior-year demand forecasts, and unfavorable Brazil volume mix weighed on margin; European markets and Consumer Beauty remain under pressure.
- External macroeconomic uncertainty and weaker consumer sentiment pose risks, while prestige sell-out remains below market and Consumer Beauty competitiveness requires further improvement.


### Significant events and developments

- The Marc Jacobs license was extended to a 40-year term with WHP Global and G-III Apparel Group; Marc Jacobs Beauty launched in ~600 Sephora stores across four markets, with retail sales exceeding targets.
- A global Brandlight partnership will support visibility in AI-driven search and large language models, initially for six Prestige brands across five markets.
- First-half FY 2027 Prestige launches, including BOSS Bottled Beyond For Her, Marc Jacobs Beauty makeup, Burberry Goddess Amber Vanilla, and Kylie Jenner Mood Stones, were tracking ahead of plan.
- A $250M payment was received in July in connection with the Gucci transition; Gucci is scheduled to exit the portfolio next year, with continued investment in line with contractual commitments.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Net debt reduction was supported by Q1 free cash flow and $250M of Gucci-transition proceeds; deleveraging remains a priority, alongside selective reinvestment in high-return opportunities.
- A&CP allocation to the top six Prestige brands rose to 76% in Q1 from 64% in H1 FY 2026; allocation to the top four Consumer Beauty brands increased to 71% from 64% YoY.
- Non-people fixed costs declined 9%, while headcount savings were largely offset near term by labor-cost inflation; simplification and productivity are intended to fund reinvestment.
- Improved inventory productivity and working-capital management are priorities to support more consistent cash conversion and further deleveraging.


### Market and industry conditions

- Prestige and mass beauty markets each grew 5%? Prestige growth moderated to 3% in Q1 from 6% in H2 FY 2026, while mass beauty grew 5%.
- Core Prestige fragrance grew 5% in Q1, matching H2 FY 2026; Prestige sell-out declined 2% against 3% market growth.
- U.S. prestige beauty sales continued to trend higher despite weaker economic sentiment; broader beauty demand was described as resilient amid macroeconomic uncertainty.
