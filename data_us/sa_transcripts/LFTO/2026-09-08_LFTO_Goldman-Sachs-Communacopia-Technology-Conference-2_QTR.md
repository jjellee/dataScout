# Liftoff Mobile, Inc. — Goldman Sachs Communacopia + Technology Conference 2026 (2026-09-08)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Market overview and opportunity

- Mobile app usage averages three hours daily, driving the need for advanced user acquisition and monetization strategies.
- The app ecosystem is still in its early stages, with significant growth potential across gaming and non-gaming verticals.
- Monetization in third-party apps is currently $0.07 per user-hour, much lower than TV, indicating room for growth.
- Generational shifts, especially among Gen Z, are increasing mobile engagement and commerce.
- The total addressable market is projected to grow from $80B to $130B in five years, with a 13-14% CAGR.


### Technology and platform innovation

- Cortex, a neural net-backed recommendation engine, was launched in Q4 2023 to enhance ad targeting and pricing.
- The platform leverages recursive learning, allowing models to improve autonomously and adapt to new data.
- AI advancements in both the platform and customer apps are creating a beneficial data cycle.
- The system is designed to efficiently scale features and compute, optimizing for ROI-positive user acquisition.
- Investment focus is on advancing Cortex and retaining top technical talent to drive innovation.


### Go-to-market strategy and verticalization

- The business model is built to quickly adapt to new verticals, supporting diverse KPIs and creative needs.
- Non-gaming verticals such as prediction markets, e-commerce, and collectibles are emerging growth areas.
- The operating model is highly adaptable, leveraging technology and systems to scale across verticals.
- Strategic investments prioritize reinvesting in technology, deleveraging, shareholder returns, and selective M&A.
- The company aims to be the first mover in supporting new app-based business models.


### Competitive landscape and future outlook

- The supply of ad impressions far exceeds qualified demand, making effective recommendation engines critical.
- Few companies have both the software footprint and AI capabilities to deliver ROI-positive users at scale.
- The market is not winner-take-all; advertisers seek as many ROI-positive users as possible across channels.
- The focus remains on enhancing the recommendation engine and adapting to evolving consumer experiences.
- Ongoing innovation in content creation and app experiences will drive future growth and differentiation.
