# Liftoff Mobile, Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-11-12)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 revenue rose 32% YoY to $238M and Adjusted EBITDA reached $145M, with both above the high end of guidance; this was the 12th consecutive quarter of revenue growth.
- Growth reflected broad app-economy demand, customer investment and Cortex improvements; management expects continued compounding growth and margin expansion.


### Financial highlights

- Revenue was $238M, up 32% YoY and 8% QoQ; core advertising revenue increased 33% YoY and exceeded guidance by $16M.
- Existing customers contributed 55% of YoY core advertising revenue growth; customers added in the prior 12 months contributed 45%.
- Adjusted EBITDA was $145M, up 45% YoY; net income was $87M and diluted EPS was $0.47.
- Free cash flow was $93M versus $28M YoY; trailing-12-month free cash flow was $249M versus $97M.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q4 revenue guidance is $244M-$249M, implying 26%-28% YoY growth; the outlook includes market growth, Cortex learning and benefits from improvements already deployed.
- Q4 Adjusted EBITDA guidance is $150M-$154M, with a 61%-62% margin.
- FY 2026 revenue guidance is $907M-$912M, implying 32%-33% growth; Adjusted EBITDA guidance is $547M-$551M at ~60% margin.
- Q4 guidance excludes gains from future discrete model advances whose timing or impact cannot yet be reliably forecast.
- Management expects margin expansion to continue into 2027 and beyond, but did not provide 2027 guidance.


### Segment performance

- Growth spanned social media, entertainment and finance; more than half of advertiser revenue continued to come from outside gaming.
- The 12 months ended September 30, 2026, included 396 customers generating over $100,000 of core advertising revenue, versus 362 in the prior-year period.
- Sports-related demand persisted beyond the World Cup into the NFL season; management also cited holiday commerce as a Q4 demand opportunity.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted EBITDA margin was 61%, up ~1 percentage point QoQ and 5 percentage points YoY; it exceeded the guidance high end by 3 percentage points.
- Adjusted EBITDA exceeded the guidance high end by $17M; operating leverage and lower infrastructure costs as a share of revenue supported margin expansion.
- CapEx was $14M, ~6% of revenue; quarter-end net leverage was 2.1x net debt to trailing-12-month Adjusted EBITDA, down from 2.4x at Q2 end.
- Quarter-end cash was $386M and total debt was $1.4B; common shares outstanding were 169M, with weighted-average basic and diluted shares of 169M and 183M.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Q4 guidance does not include potential benefits from future discrete Cortex advances because their timing and impact are not reliably forecastable.
- Management said the business has no particularly discernible seasonal pattern; quarterly results reflect innovation, app-economy growth and shifting consumer attention alongside seasonal events.
- Changes in search and discovery, including AI-driven disruption to web traffic, may shift advertiser focus toward apps; management expects this to play out over time.


### Significant events and developments

- Cortex was enhanced to predict value from users monetized through in-app ads and to learn from longer histories while emphasizing recent behavior.
- A revised data-training architecture enables Cortex to learn from significantly more data; initial results were described as encouraging.
- Cortex makes ~1 billion ad predictions per second; the platform's SDK is integrated in over 160,000 apps.
- Beginning with Q1 2027 results, reporting of core advertising revenue will end; customer metrics will use total GAAP revenue.


### Capital allocation and financing

- The capital-light model generated $93M of Q3 free cash flow while spending $14M on CapEx; management said it will continue investing in R&D and infrastructure.
- Management expects natural deleveraging as growth and excess free cash flow increase balance-sheet flexibility for future growth.


### Market and industry conditions

- Independent ad tech platforms' gross serviceable addressable market is estimated to reach ~$136B by 2030; monetization was cited at $0.07 per user hour versus $0.38 for traditional TV.
- More than half of advertiser revenue came from outside gaming, while management described app advertising as under-monetized and open to multiple scaled players.
- New Apple App Store releases reached roughly 560,000 in H1 2026, double the prior-year level, as AI lowers app-development barriers and increases competition for attention.
- Management sees AI-driven changes in search and discovery encouraging businesses to build app experiences and acquire users directly; AI apps also need user acquisition and monetization.
