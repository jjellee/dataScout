# Liftoff Mobile, Inc. — Q2 2026 (2026-08-12)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Achieved 11th consecutive quarter of revenue growth, with Q2 2026 revenue up 35% year-over-year to $220 million and adjusted EBITDA margin expanding to 60%.
- Growth driven by market expansion, machine learning breakthroughs in Cortex, and increased advertiser demand during major events like the FIFA World Cup.
- Platform serves both demand and supply sides of the app economy, with over half of demand and one-third of supply from non-gaming verticals.
- Majority of Q2 growth came from existing customers, with 391 customers contributing over $100,000 in core advertising revenue.


### Financial highlights

- Q2 2026 revenue grew 7% sequentially and 35% year-over-year to $220 million; core advertising revenue was $219 million.
- Adjusted EBITDA reached $132 million, with a 60% margin, up 2 points sequentially and 8 points year-over-year.
- Net loss of $4 million included $45 million in non-cash IPO-related expenses.
- Free cash flow was $50 million in Q2, up from $15 million a year ago; trailing 12-month free cash flow reached $184 million.
- Net leverage at quarter end was 2.4x net debt to adjusted EBITDA.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q3 2026 revenue expected at $217–$222 million, up 21–24% year-over-year; adjusted EBITDA of $124–$128 million (57–58% margin).
- Full-year 2026 revenue guidance of $870–$880 million (27–28% growth); adjusted EBITDA of $510–$518 million (59% margin, up 4 points vs. 2025).
- Guidance philosophy is prudent, focusing on visibility and excluding unconfirmed model breakthroughs.


### Segment performance

- Non-gaming verticals are expanding faster than gaming, with non-gaming demand and supply growing as a share of the business.
- Gaming remains foundational, with growth in both demand and supply, and innovation from gaming influencing other verticals.
- Major events like the World Cup and NFL season drive spikes in demand across multiple app categories, including sports betting, finance, and e-commerce.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted EBITDA margin: 60% in Q2 2026, up 8 points year-over-year.
- Incremental adjusted EBITDA margin: 82% year-over-year.
- Net leverage: 2.4x net debt to adjusted EBITDA.
- 58% of growth from existing customers, 42% from new customers over the last 12 months.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Forward-looking statements are subject to significant risks and uncertainties, including market conditions and the timing of model breakthroughs.
- Risks detailed in SEC filings, including the S-1 prospectus and earnings release.


### Significant events and developments

- IPO in Q2 resulted in $45 million of non-cash expenses, including stock-based compensation and debt extinguishment.
- Model breakthroughs in Cortex led to durable performance gains and contributed to Q2 outperformance.
- World Cup drove increased demand in sports-related verticals, with a flywheel effect expected to benefit future quarters.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Capital-light model with capex limited to internal software; no heavy infrastructure.
- Priorities: reinvest in R&D and new verticals, maintain net leverage below 3x, consider return of capital as leverage normalizes, and remain opportunistic on M&A.
- Paid down $418 million of debt year-to-date; ended Q2 with $305 million in cash.


### Market and industry conditions

- Third-party in-app advertising market projected to grow at 11% CAGR to $136 billion by 2030; non-gaming verticals at 14%.
- App economy is structurally under-monetized, with ad dollars lagging user attention.
- Platform benefits from major cultural and sporting events, with diversified exposure across verticals reducing reliance on any single category.
- Ongoing innovation in AI and machine learning is driving both internal performance and broader market growth.
