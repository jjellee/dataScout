# Everpure, Inc. — Q3 2027 (2026-11-18)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 revenue grew 51% YoY and operating profit rose 76%, both above guidance; operating margin reached a record 23.6%.
- Most dollar growth came from core, while new growth areas expanded faster than the overall business; hyperscale solutions led new-business momentum.
- Full-year FY27 revenue growth guidance was raised to 42% at the midpoint; preliminary FY28 outlook was reiterated, not issued as formal guidance.


### Financial highlights

- Product revenue, including hyperscale royalties and excluding Evergreen//One shipments, rose 77% YoY to $948M.
- Subscription services revenue increased 19% YoY to $512M, or 35% of total revenue; ARR grew 20% to more than $2.2B.
- RPO rose 44% YoY to more than $4.1B; storage-as-a-service TCV increased 57% to $188M in Q3.
- U.S. revenue was $972M, up 42%; international revenue was $488M, up 73%, and represented 33% of total revenue.
- Financial metrics and growth rates were non-GAAP unless identified as revenue, RPO, or cash and investments.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q4 revenue guidance is $1.505B-$1.515B, implying ~43% YoY growth at midpoint; operating profit guidance is $350M-$360M, implying ~57% growth.
- FY27 revenue guidance is $5.20B-$5.21B, or 42% YoY growth at midpoint; operating profit guidance is $1.08B-$1.09B, or 72% growth.
- FY27 annual free cash flow is expected at $600M-$800M; midpoint operating profit growth implies a Rule of 40 score well above 70.
- FY28 preliminary outlook from September was reiterated; management characterized it as a preliminary outlook, not formal guidance, and maintained confidence in growth.
- Hyperscale sales are expected to expand in Q4 and into FY28; a second hyperscaler is expected to begin shipping next year, likely early to midyear.


### Segment performance

- Core business generated most dollar revenue growth; core AI sales benefited from more enterprises deploying GPU environments on-prem.
- Evergreen//One TCV grew 57% YoY for nine consecutive quarters of double-digit growth; storage-as-a-service TCV is approaching a FY27 annualized run rate above $1B.
- Hyperscale solutions sales increased substantially, with deployments contributing to product revenue and gross margin; hyperscale gross margins were cited at 75%-85%.
- FlashBlade//EXA ranked first on all submitted checkpointing and KV cache metrics in MLPerf Storage v3.0; sales pursuits span AI providers, quantitative trading, and neoclouds.
- Modern data software gained traction: Data Intelligence File Edition and native MCP integration launched; Portworx passed 100 joint production deployments with Nutanix.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Total gross margin was 69.2%; product gross margin was 67.3%, up 110 bps sequentially, while subscription services gross margin was 72.6%, down 230 bps sequentially.
- Product gross margin excluding hyperscale contribution is managed toward the lower end of the 65%-70% range while semiconductor costs remain elevated.
- Operating profit was $345M, up 76% YoY; operating margin was a record 23.6%, and operating expenses were below 50% of revenue.
- Cash from operations was $443M; free cash flow was $265M, with an 18.1% free cash flow margin.
- Long-term objectives include operating margin above 25% and annualized Rule of 40 above 60; Rule of 40 is revenue growth plus non-GAAP operating margin.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- NAND and other component costs continued to rise in Q3, though more moderately; management expects costs to stabilize toward year-end and remain high into next year.
- Higher component prices and broader uncertainty around AI infrastructure spending and data-center build-out pace remain market concerns.
- RPO YoY comparisons are expected to become more challenging after unusually strong growth quarters in H2 FY26, potentially moderating reported growth despite healthy demand.
- Management said component allocation constraints had eased, but future supply availability could again become an issue; supply-chain and component procurement remain risks.
- Demand may respond to changes in storage pricing; management said the timing and pace of any NAND price declines and resulting volume recovery remain uncertain.


### Significant events and developments

- Q3 price levels were maintained from Q2; average product prices were reduced at the start of Q4 following value engineering and data-reduction improvements.
- PureKVA was delivered to pre-stage inference context in GPU memory, with up to 20x faster time to first token for large language models.
- Gartner named the business a Leader in Infrastructure Platform Consumption Services for the second consecutive year; Portworx was also recognized as an IDC MarketScape leader.
- The finance and IT teams shortened the earnings close process to 17 days after quarter-end, advancing a continuous-close goal.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Cash and investments exceeded $1.1B at quarter-end; capital expenditures were $178M, primarily supporting Evergreen//One growth and reflecting higher component prices.
- Repurchased 542,000 shares for approximately $67M; paid $101M in employee-award withholding taxes, offsetting dilution of approximately 1M shares.
- Approximately $109M remained under the $400M repurchase authorization announced in Q4 FY26.


### Market and industry conditions

- Customers are prioritizing higher-performance, higher-scale and lower-latency workloads; management described this shift as data getting hotter across hyperscale, neocloud, and enterprise markets.
- Improving open-weight models and the economics of running them on-prem are prompting standard enterprises to deploy GPU-based AI systems; management expects this trend to grow substantially over the next year.
- Higher component prices have shifted demand toward higher-performance workloads and value; Evergreen//One is benefiting as customers seek flexibility, operating efficiency, and cost predictability.
- Management sees hyperscale opportunity across different data-center build-out scenarios, citing cost, performance, and operational-efficiency advantages over legacy approaches.
