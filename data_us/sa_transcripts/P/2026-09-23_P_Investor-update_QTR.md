# Everpure, Inc. — Investor update (2026-09-23)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Data primacy and Data Intelligence

- AI is driving a shift from application-centric IT toward data primacy: shared context and reusable data across systems, rather than copying data into separate applications.
- Data integration is estimated to account for 80%–90% of enterprise spend; data primacy aims to reduce integration and duplication while improving data coherence and lowering AI token costs.
- Data Intelligence discovers, classifies and contextualizes structured and unstructured data across cloud, on-premises, mainframes and different storage platforms.
- Data Intelligence market estimated to grow from $6B today to $21B by 2030; CIO, CISO and CDO involvement is driving broader budgets for these tools.
- Customer examples included reducing a credit-card company’s Data Subject Access Request response time from 21 person-weeks to under three minutes, and identifying 91M exposed card records at a payment processor.


### Unified platform and AI capabilities

- Enterprise Data Cloud combines Evergreen architecture, a unified data plane and Intelligent Control Plane to unify workloads, apply data context and automate intent-based infrastructure operations.
- Fusion had 2,300 customer adopters, with adoption nearly doubling in the prior six months; Options Technology reported hundreds of staff hours saved per month.
- Data Stream vectorizes enterprise data for retrieval-augmented generation and applies governance to vectors; key-value acceleration delivered 20x faster inference in cited testing.
- Evergreen has delivered nine hardware generations over 15 years without disruption; software innovation adds about 200 net new features annually.
- Data Intelligence also supports data visibility, governance, compliance and security use cases, with capabilities offered on top of different storage environments.


### Neocloud and FlashBlade//EXA

- Rising training, inference and agentic workloads increase demand for GPU capacity; storage performance and reliability bottlenecks can limit utilization, with a third-party study finding AI clusters generally below ~85% utilization.
- FlashBlade//EXA separates metadata and data performance, scaling both independently for Neocloud workloads while using open standards and avoiding GPU-server software requirements.
- STN simplified its storage stack with FlashBlade//EXA and improved performance 3x and beyond; per-node throughput rose from 30–40 GB/s to 100 GB/s.
- STN reported 10%–20% faster end-to-end training and inference, including GPU time, alongside higher GPU utilization.
- The same core software and IP underpin enterprise, Neocloud and hyperscale offerings, with configurations tailored to their different operating requirements.


### Adoption and strategic positioning

- Data Intelligence adoption spans existing and new customers; existing users are consolidating toward a unified data plane, while new prospects can start with intent- and policy-based management.
- Management cited an 84 NPS and presence in 64% of Fortune 500 organizations, alongside customer trust in mission-critical data workloads.
- The strategy positions a neutral data layer across distributed systems, complementing platforms such as Databricks and Snowflake rather than requiring customers to move all data into one ecosystem.
- Management described data primacy as a potential 10–20-year architectural shift, with Data Intelligence as an initial step toward applications and agents sharing contextualized data.
