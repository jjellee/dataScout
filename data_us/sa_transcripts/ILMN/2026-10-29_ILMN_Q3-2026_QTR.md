# Illumina, Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-10-29)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 revenue, margins and EPS exceeded guidance, supported by strong clinical sequencing demand and more than 80 NovaSeq X placements; FY guidance increased and long-range targets remain on track.


### Financial highlights

- Revenue was $1.17B, up 4.7% YoY on a rest-of-world organic basis; currency and acquired revenue added just under 3 percentage points to reported growth.
- Sequencing consumables revenue was $792M, up 6% YoY and 5% on a rest-of-world organic basis; service and other revenue was $158M, up 8% organically.
- Gross margin was 69.4%, up 20 bps YoY; operating margin was 25%, 100 bps above guidance; diluted EPS of $1.44 rose 7% YoY.


### Outlook and guidance

- FY26 revenue guidance increased $30M at the midpoint to $4.64B–$4.66B; rest-of-world organic growth is expected above 5%.
- FY26 operating margin is expected at up to 23.6%; diluted EPS guidance is $5.40–$5.45, implying 12%–13% YoY growth.
- Q4 revenue guidance is $1.22B–$1.24B, with rest-of-world organic growth of ~4.5%; EPS of $1.51–$1.56 and operating margin of ~25%.
- FY26 rest-of-world organic sequencing consumables growth is expected in the mid-single digits, with low-double-digit instrument growth; clinical consumables growth remains expected in the mid-teens.
- Management reaffirmed the strategic path to high-single-digit consumables growth in 2027; new products and BioInsight are expected to add 1–2 points of growth, with more detail on 2027 guidance at the Q4 call.


### Segment performance

- Rest-of-world clinical consumables grew 14% in Q3; research and applied consumables declined 6%.
- Sequencing instruments revenue was $106M, down 2% YoY on a rest-of-world organic basis; higher NovaSeq X placements were offset by weaker sales in Europe, Middle East and Latin America.
- Microarrays and other revenue was $110M, up 2% on a rest-of-world organic basis; results included SomaLogic revenue above the initial deal model.
- NovaSeq X placements exceeded 80 in Q3 and totaled ~360 over the last four quarters; 84% of high-throughput volume and 60% of revenue were on X.
- X transition reached at least 90% of research and applied volume; clinical conversion was 78%, with 80%–85% expected in coming quarters and lower YoY transition-pricing headwinds in 2027 than 2026.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Q3 cash flow from operations was $419M and free cash flow was $378M.
- Quarter-end cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments totaled ~$1.34B; total debt was $1.79B and gross-debt/LTM EBITDA leverage was ~1.4x.
- Second-half 2026 operating margin is expected at ~25%, about 400 bps above 2024.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- China-related regulatory and geopolitical developments remain uncertain; management said it was assessing a newly learned situation while continuing customer support and regulator engagement.
- Electronic-component pricing, shipping costs and tariffs were margin headwinds during 2026, although margins expanded despite these pressures.
- Clinical NovaSeq X conversion remains slower than research and applied; ongoing transition pricing pressure is expected, though lower in 2027 than in 2026.


### Significant events and developments

- Billion Cell Atlas was more than halfway complete; GSK joined the consortium, and interest from additional biopharma companies is growing.
- BioInsight launched with analysis applications, AI models and curated biological data; SpliceAI2 identified 17% more disease-relevant splice variants than other splicing models in rare-disease research data sets.
- Connected high- and mid-throughput instruments delivered gigabase output growth above 30%, indicating continued sequencing activity demand.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Repurchased $9M of stock in Q3; ~$1.8B remained under current repurchase authorizations, with opportunistic repurchases planned.
- Management cited strong cash flows and the balance sheet as providing flexibility to invest in strategic growth areas.


### Notes to the financial statements

- Q3 tariff refunds positively affected GAAP and non-GAAP operating income, margins and EPS, but negatively affected revenue; all discussed financial measures excluded the refunds, and revenue growth rates also excluded them.


### Market and industry conditions

- Clinical sequencing demand remained strong, supported by broader sequencing-based diagnostics and more sequencing-intensive assays; management cited a rich pipeline of clinical trials and prospective tests.
- Research activity showed signs of stabilization, with sequential revenue growth in each of the last two quarters; management expects mid- to high-single-digit research and applied consumables declines for 2026.
- Management characterized AI-ready, standardized biological data and multi-omics as long-term opportunities; demand may include both data-generation services and instrument placements.
