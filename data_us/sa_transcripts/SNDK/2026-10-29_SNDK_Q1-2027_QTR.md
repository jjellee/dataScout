# Sandisk Corporation — Q1 2027 (2026-10-29)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q1 FY27 revenue was $11B, non-GAAP gross margin 85.1%, and non-GAAP EPS $49.51; results exceeded guidance.
- AI-driven NAND demand, technology leadership, and long-term customer partnerships underpin confidence in durable cash generation and growth.


### Financial highlights

- Revenue rose 23% QoQ and 378% YoY; volume contributed ~60% and pricing ~40% of the increase.
- Non-GAAP gross margin was 85.1%, versus 84.6% in Q4; non-GAAP operating margin was 80.4%, versus 79.2%.
- Non-GAAP EPS was $49.51, versus $39.25 QoQ and $1.22 YoY; diluted shares were 154M.
- Cash from operations was $6.4B; adjusted free cash flow was $5.7B, ~52% of revenue.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q2 FY27 revenue guidance is $12.2B–$12.6B; non-GAAP gross margin 84.5%–85.5%, including modest like-for-like price increases.
- Q2 non-GAAP EPS guidance is $55–$57 pre-split, on 151M diluted shares; split-adjusted EPS is $6.88–$7.13 on ~1,208M shares.
- Calendar 2027 NAND bit demand is expected to grow ~40% YoY after more than doubling in 2026; data center is forecast to exceed 55% of the market.
- Pricing is expected to increase modestly through FY27; fiscal Q3 is typically the weakest sequential quarter, so management advised conservative Q2-to-Q3 assumptions.


### Segment performance

- Data center revenue was $4.8B, up 61% QoQ; growth outpaced the market as supply allocation increased, supported by three U.S. hyperscalers.
- Edge revenue was $5.8B, up 7% QoQ; premium and mainstream strength partly offset lower-end contraction, with automotive and IoT growing.
- Consumer revenue was $397M, down 29% QoQ as the market adjusted to higher pricing.
- Management expects next-year exabyte growth across PCs, smartphones, and data center despite slightly declining PC and mobile unit counts.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Non-GAAP operating expenses were $513M, or 4.7% of revenue; non-GAAP operating margin was 80.4%.
- Gross CapEx was $378M, or 3.4% of revenue; adjusted free cash flow margin was ~52%.
- Quarter-end cash and cash equivalents were $5.1B, with no debt.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Fab output and product mix constrain short-term supply allocation; wafer production takes ~120 days, limiting near-term flexibility.
- Potential SLC demand remains an early-stage customer discussion; SLC deployment could materially affect available TLC or QLC supply.
- HBF market size, use cases, and commercialization pace remain dependent on customer sampling, product integration, and feedback.
- Forward-looking expectations are subject to risks and uncertainties that could cause actual results to differ materially.


### Significant events and developments

- Two NBMs closed in Q1; ~50% of FY27 bits and two-thirds of FY28 bits are under NBM, with the share expected to rise.
- NBM demand pipeline exceeds supply through the end of the decade; negotiations and commitments remain in progress.
- HBF memory die taped out in Q1 and fabrication began; initial product samples are targeted for next calendar year.
- The board approved an 8-for-1 stock split; additional shares are expected Nov. 19, with split-adjusted trading expected Nov. 20.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Repurchased ~3.2M shares for $5B in Q1; 6M shares, ~4% of shares outstanding, were repurchased over two quarters.
- Policy is to invest in the business, remain debt-free, and return 100% of excess cash to shareholders.
- FY27 gross capital spending is expected to rise to a mid-single-digit percentage of revenue.
- Most NBM financial guarantees are held by third-party institutions; balance-sheet prepayments are the smallest portion, with no legal restriction on their use.


### Notes to the financial statements

- GAAP-to-non-GAAP reconciliation included $91M of stock-based compensation, 0.8% of revenue, and a $513M gain on equity securities, primarily related to the Kioxia investment.


### Market and industry conditions

- AI inference expands NAND use for KV cache, persistent context, data lakes, and other infrastructure workloads; management sees NAND’s role broadening across the AI stack.
- Data center grew from ~20% of industry bits early in the decade to roughly half today; management expects it to exceed 55% of the market in calendar 2027.
- KV cache is expected to represent ~one-third of the AI data-center flash market by decade-end; the AI data-center opportunity could approach the size of today’s NAND industry.
- PC units are expected to be slightly down next year, but higher content per device is expected to drive PC exabyte growth; mobile exabytes are also expected to grow on slightly lower units.
