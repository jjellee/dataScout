# H&R Block, Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-05-06)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Delivered strong results ahead of expectations, with improvements across all key metrics and raised full-year outlook based on year-to-date performance.
- Strategy focused on expert-led, technology-enabled experiences drove measurable gains in client engagement, retention, and satisfaction.
- Stabilized assisted channel market share in a highly competitive environment, with favorable performance each week of the tax season.
- Enhanced client experiences through automation, AI, and streamlined processes, resulting in higher conversion and product attach rates.
- Continued focus on attracting higher complexity clients, improving business durability and economics.


### Financial highlights

- Third quarter revenue was $2.4 billion, up 5.3% year-over-year.
- EBITDA increased 5.9% to $1.1 billion; adjusted EPS up 12% year-over-year.
- Net income from continuing operations rose 17.4% to $848.8 million; EPS from continuing operations increased 24.2% to $6.61.
- Adjusted net income was $773.7 million, up 5.8%; adjusted EPS was $6.02, up 11.9%.
- Operating expenses increased 4.8% to $1.4 billion, mainly due to higher field wages.
- Recognized a one-time non-cash tax benefit of $84.1 million, reducing tax expense and boosting EPS by $0.65.


### Outlook and guidance

- Raised full-year guidance: revenue expected at $3.91–$3.92 billion, EBITDA at $1.025–$1.035 billion, effective tax rate ~14%, and adjusted diluted EPS at $5.10–$5.20.
- Long-term growth algorithm remains unchanged: 3–6% revenue growth, adjusted EBITDA at 1.5x revenue growth, and double-digit EPS growth.


### Segment performance

- Assisted channel market share held flat year-over-year, marking the third consecutive year of improvement.
- Assisted volume up 2.1%, NAC up 3.9%, reflecting a healthy balance of price, volume, and mix.
- DIY channel saw a 140 basis point improvement in mix between free and paid, with growth in higher AGI clients; focus remains on high lifetime value clients.
- Franchise operations underperformed company offices by about 2% in volume, mainly due to local market differences and ongoing franchise buybacks.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Effective tax rate for the quarter was 16.5%, down from 24.6% last year.
- Operating cash flow for nine months was $586.7 million.
- Returned $560.9 million to shareholders via dividends and share repurchases in nine months.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Forward-looking statements subject to material risks and uncertainties; actual results may differ due to various factors.
- Potential impact from future tax law changes and adjustments in employer withholding tables.


### Significant events and developments

- Introduced AI-powered tools such as Sidekick and AI Tax Assist, driving efficiency and client engagement.
- Automated and scaled Second Look, leading to a 600+ basis point higher retention rate for new clients who received it.
- Recognized by CNET for best online tax product and best use of AI.
- Board approved an incremental $100 million share repurchase for Q4, with $700 million remaining under the program.
- Completed 150 franchise buybacks year-to-date, up from 124 last year.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Capital allocation priorities: invest in business, grow dividend, return excess capital via share repurchases.
- Board to review dividend policy in summer; incremental share repurchases do not impact dividend plans.


### Market and industry conditions

- Industry growth rate aligned with expectations; tax law changes led to higher average refunds and more clients receiving refunds.
- Extensions are up, suggesting continued strong performance through extension season.
- Competitive environment remains intense, especially in assisted and DIY channels.
