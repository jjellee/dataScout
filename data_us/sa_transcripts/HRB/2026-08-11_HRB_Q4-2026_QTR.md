# H&R Block, Inc. — Q4 2026 (2026-08-11)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Fiscal 2026 delivered strong results with revenue up 4.9%, EBITDA up 8.3%, and adjusted EPS up 13.9% year-over-year, marking the best financial performance in five years.
- Strategic focus on converting and retaining higher-value, more complex clients, supported by technology and expert-led services, drove improved business quality and client outcomes.
- Significant progress was made in omni-channel service delivery, automation, and AI integration, enhancing both client and tax pro experiences.
- $714 million was returned to shareholders via dividends and share repurchases, reflecting strong cash flow and disciplined capital allocation.


### Financial highlights

- Revenue reached $3.95 billion, up 4.9% from the prior year, driven by higher net average charge, U.S. assisted tax prep volume, international growth, and small business momentum at Wave.
- EBITDA was $1.06 billion, up 8.3% year-over-year, with 80 basis points of margin expansion.
- Net income from continuing operations was $736 million; adjusted net income was $688 million.
- Adjusted EPS was $5.31, up 13.9% year-over-year, aided by share repurchases and higher adjusted net income.
- Free cash flow was $756 million, a meaningful increase year-over-year.


### Outlook and guidance

- Fiscal 2027 revenue is projected at $4.11–$4.16 billion, adjusted EBITDA at $1.11–$1.14 billion, and adjusted EPS at $6.04–$6.24.
- Effective tax rate expected to rise to approximately 23%.
- $400 million in share repurchases planned for fiscal 2027; quarterly dividend increased by 10% to $0.46 per share.
- Industry growth expected to moderate, but company expects to maintain or grow assisted category market share and continue small business services expansion.


### Segment performance

- U.S. assisted tax preparation saw higher net average charge and volume, with a healthier balance of volume, price, and mix.
- Wave, the small business segment, achieved double-digit revenue growth for the second consecutive year, driven by paid pro tier subscriptions and higher payments volume.
- International performance benefited from favorable FX rates and a strong tax season in Australia; Canada performed well but was impacted by regulatory changes.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- EBITDA margin expanded by 80 basis points in fiscal 2026; further 50 basis points expansion guided for 2027.
- Effective tax rate dropped to 14% in 2026 due to an $84.1 million one-time non-cash tax benefit; expected to normalize at 23% in 2027.
- Repurchased 10.5 million shares (7.9% of outstanding) for $500 million.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Forward-looking statements are subject to risks including industry growth moderation, job market trends, and regulatory changes.
- Industry growth is expected to moderate due to slower job growth, though unemployment remains stable.
- Tax law changes increased average refunds and reduced IRS balances due, giving DIY a tailwind, but assisted remains over 55% of the market.


### Significant events and developments

- $84.1 million one-time non-cash tax benefit recognized in Q3 from IRS examination resolution.
- Transition from seasonal to year-round office leadership model, resulting in an $8.3 million severance charge (excluded from outlook).
- Over 150 experiments run during the year to accelerate innovation and refine client experience.
- Expansion of consultative, expert-led, technology-enabled client experience to a full designated market area in 2027.


### Capital allocation and financing

- $714 million returned to shareholders via dividends and share repurchases in fiscal 2026.
- Board approved a 10% increase in quarterly dividend to $0.46 per share.
- $600 million remains under current $1.5 billion share repurchase authorization.
- Continued opportunistic franchise acquisitions, with 160 buybacks in 2026 and similar activity expected in 2027.


### Market and industry conditions

- Industry growth expected to moderate to around 1% due to slower job growth, but no decline or stagnation anticipated.
- Assisted tax prep remains the dominant category, with DIY growth focused on higher AGI, more complex clients.
- Pricing power remains, with low single-digit price increases planned for 2027.
- AI and automation viewed as tailwinds, enhancing expertise rather than replacing it.
