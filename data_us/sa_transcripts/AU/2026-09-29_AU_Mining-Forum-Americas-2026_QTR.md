# AngloGold Ashanti plc — Mining Forum Americas 2026 (2026-09-29)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Operational performance and safety

- Consistently meets production guidance and maintains strong cost control despite external factors like royalties and inflation.
- Safety remains the highest priority, with recent incidents reinforcing the focus on daily vigilance.
- Flooding and a fatality impacted operations at Iduapriem and Obuasi, but medium-term production targets remain unchanged.
- Portfolio breadth and Tier 1 asset scale help offset operational disruptions.


### Asset portfolio and growth projects

- Majority of production comes from Tier 1 assets, with ambitions to reach 80% Tier 1 by mid-next decade.
- Quebradona copper project in Colombia is being fast-tracked with government support, aiming for approval within 18–24 months and production by decade's end.
- Five key brownfield projects (Geita, Cuiabá, Obuasi, Sukari, Siguiri) targeted to add 350,000–450,000 oz over three years through gradual increases.
- Obuasi's Block 11, with higher grades, expected to drive significant growth from 2029.
- Nevada's Arthur Gold project is set to become the largest and most valuable asset in the 2030s, with reserves expected to grow and production to average 700,000–800,000 oz for decades.


### Financial discipline and capital allocation

- Maintains a clear capital allocation strategy: fund CapEx from operations, return at least 50% of free cash flow to shareholders via dividends and buybacks.
- No debt expected for the remainder of the decade, supporting funding for major projects.
- Buyback program delayed in South Africa due to regulatory permissions but will proceed.
- Plans to return more than 50% of free cash flow if gold prices remain high, with a 62% return in the previous year.


### Project execution and risk management

- Nevada and Quebradona projects face no major technological hurdles; environmental permitting is the main risk.
- Confident in obtaining approvals for North Bullfrog and Arthur, with improved water management strategies.
- Quebradona benefits from a supportive Colombian government, expediting licensing and development.
- Drilling in Nevada focuses on reserve conversion and feasibility, with sufficient water resources secured.


### Strategic initiatives and sector outlook

- Actively seeds junior exploration companies, particularly in Canada, to secure future district-scale opportunities.
- Sector-wide derating has increased dividend yields; strong free cash flow per share growth distinguishes performance.
- Free cash flow per share grew 36% in Q2, outpacing peers.
