# Nebius Group N.V. — Q3 2026 (2026-11-12)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Record Q3; demand strengthened as customers signed larger, longer-term agreements at higher prices and with more upfront payments.
- Q3 contract pricing exceeded $25 million per megawatt, versus $20 million-$25 million in Q2; agreements signed since Q3 began exceeded $12 billion in total contract value.
- Prepayments on Q3 wins covered more than 100% of associated CapEx, while new-capacity payback shortened to 1 year 7 months from 1 year 10 months in Q2.


### Financial highlights

- Q3 group revenue was $980.8 million, up 571% YoY and 68% QoQ; AI Cloud revenue was $973.8 million, up 643% YoY.
- Q3 included approximately $325 million of non-recurring asset-light revenue recognized as partners deployed the platform on partner-funded capacity.
- Group adjusted EBITDA was $465.8 million versus a loss of $5.2 million a year earlier and $236.2 million in Q2.
- Q3 CapEx was approximately $7.3 billion, primarily for server and network equipment and owned data-center expansion.


### Outlook and guidance

- 2026 revenue guidance narrowed to $3.0 billion-$3.2 billion from $3.0 billion-$3.4 billion, reflecting third-party capacity shifts into Q2 2027.
- 2026 adjusted EBITDA margin guidance remains approximately 40%; 2026 CapEx is now expected at approximately $27 billion, versus $20 billion-$25 billion previously.
- February ARR is expected to exceed $7 billion before New Jersey reaches full capacity; management will then stop providing ARR guidance.
- 2027 revenue is projected at $12 billion-$13 billion, around 4x; pre-tax break-even is expected in Q4 2027, using five-year depreciation for servers and network equipment.
- 2027 CapEx is projected at $40 billion-$45 billion for more than 1 GW of new capacity; 75%-85% is expected to be funded by prepayments and asset-backed financing.


### Segment performance

- AI Cloud generated approximately 99% of Q3 group revenue and delivered a 53.2% adjusted EBITDA margin, up from 50% in Q2.
- Group adjusted EBITDA margin was 47.5%, up from 40.6% in Q2; the gap to AI Cloud reflected investments in Avride and TripleTen.
- Token Factory, a managed inference platform, was on track for multi-hundred-million-dollar ARR with better margins; it runs on owned GPUs rather than rented capacity.
- Enterprise momentum is growing, with first multi-year agreements signed in Q3 with Databricks, Datadog and Snowflake; Palantir selected the platform for a go-to-market offering.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Q3 group adjusted EBITDA margin was 47.5%, versus 40.6% in Q2; AI Cloud adjusted EBITDA margin was 53.2%, versus 50% in Q2.
- More than 90% of Q3 contract value included prepayments; the weighted-average prepayment share rose approximately 20 percentage points QoQ.
- Prepayments on Q3 wins exceeded 100% of related CapEx, including GPUs, networking and data-center costs, versus 50%-60% in Q2.
- Q3 ended with $7.4 billion in cash and cash equivalents; 4.6 million Class A shares were issued through the ATM at a weighted-average $229.1 per share.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Colocation sites, including New Jersey, were behind schedule; some capacity shifted into 2027, moving a portion of planned 2026 revenue into early 2027.
- Vineland delays followed an earlier-than-expected wind-down of temporary power; Bloom generation was live, with ramp-up underway, and Microsoft capacity deliveries continued.
- Power delivery and project timing remain key industry constraints; management cited greater control over schedules at owned sites than at colocation projects.
- Quarterly margins can fluctuate as new sites incur rent, power and staffing costs before generating revenue; Q3 included revenue recognized at a point in time that does not recur in the same form.


### Significant events and developments

- Q3 agreements with Databricks, Datadog and Snowflake marked first multi-year deals with those enterprise AI leaders; Q4 signing activity included multi-billion-dollar AI Cloud agreements.
- The asset-light model generated approximately $325 million of Q3 non-recurring revenue through platform deployment on partner-funded capacity; management expects such activity to become more regular as it scales.
- Eigen AI and Clarifai were fully integrated, and Inferize was acquired to add model-loading capabilities aimed at reducing cold starts and improving GPU utilization.
- Owned-site capacity is expected to make up most of the next gigawatt; Alabama, Missouri and two Finland projects are expected to contribute in 2027.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Customer prepayments are now standard; management expects $10 billion in committed prepayments in 2026 and $17 billion-$20 billion in 2027.
- A $775 million asset-backed facility closed in July at SOFR plus 250 bps, secured by long-term investment-grade customer agreements; more than $40 billion of similar commitment revenue was cited as a repeatable financing base.
- A $5.75 billion convertible senior notes offering closed in August; ATM issuance generated approximately $1.0 billion net proceeds, with 7.6 million shares remaining available.
- Management expects 75%-85% of 2027 CapEx to be funded through prepayments and asset-backed financing, with flexibility to use external capital when cost-effective.
- The balance sheet held $7.4 billion of cash and cash equivalents at quarter-end.


### Market and industry conditions

- Management described AI demand as strong, with customers already seeking capacity for 2028 and 2027 capacity prices rising.
- Open-model adoption accelerated; management said model diversification expands the customer and partner base and supports more enterprise AI deployment.
- Pricing tools include reserve-capacity auctions, public spot pricing and short-term capacity offers, enabling adjustments between short-term consumption and long-term reservations.
- Chip supply was described as secured for 2027, with integration and testing completed for Vera CPUs, Vera Rubin GPUs and Groq.
