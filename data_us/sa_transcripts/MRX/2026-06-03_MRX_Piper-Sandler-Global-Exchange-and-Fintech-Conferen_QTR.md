# Marex Group Limited — Piper Sandler Global Exchange and Fintech Conference (2026-06-03)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Business performance and resilience

- Achieved consistent profit growth for eight consecutive quarters, including a record first quarter, despite market volatility and declining exchange volumes in some periods.
- Maintained strong client balances and gained market share, with performance remaining robust even as exchange volumes dropped 15–30% in the second quarter.
- Structural growth in exchange volumes is expected to continue at around 10% annually, with no significant threats identified.


### Market positioning and client growth

- Outperformed initial post-IPO growth expectations, with profit before tax (PBT) reaching GBP 418 million versus a forecast of GBP 300 million.
- Growth is driven by winning mandates from large clients, with the number of clients paying over GBP 5 million rising from 36 to 49 and contributing GBP 300 million more year-over-year.
- Now competes successfully for major mandates against leading global banks, not just picking up business banks deprioritize.


### Margins, investment, and technology

- Adjusted PBT margin improved from 15% five years ago to 22% in the latest quarter, with expectations to reach mid-to-high 20% soon.
- Investments focus on new assets, geographies, and products, with technology and AI expected to further boost productivity and margins.
- Margin expansion is occurring faster than anticipated, with no clear terminal cap, and potential to exceed 30% in the long term.


### M&A strategy and integration

- Recent acquisitions include Hamilton Court (FX), Aarna (Middle East clearing), Agrinvest (Brazilian commodities), Winterflood (UK equities), and Levmet.
- Acquisitions are chosen for strategic fit and cultural alignment, with rapid integration onto the platform and immediate profitability improvements.
- Preference for multiple smaller deals with high return on equity and quick payback, but open to larger transformational deals if value is clear.


### Capital allocation and liquidity

- Maintains substantial liquidity through public debt issuance and structured notes, with recent GBP 500 million and $500 million hybrid capital raises.
- Prioritizes investment-grade rating, organic growth, and disciplined acquisitions over share buybacks, though buyback authorization is now in place for opportunistic use.


### Key metrics and outlook

- Focuses on growth in quality earnings, client balances, and market share, with a high Sharpe ratio for earnings stability.
- Believes the market underestimates the organization’s ability to deliver stable, high-quality growth across cycles.
