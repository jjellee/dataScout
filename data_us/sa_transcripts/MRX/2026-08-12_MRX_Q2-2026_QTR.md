# Marex Group Limited — Q2 2026 (2026-08-12)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Achieved sixth consecutive record profit quarter since IPO in April 2024, with Q2 2026 revenues up 39% year-over-year to $696 million and adjusted profit before tax up 56% to $166 million.
- Adjusted profit before tax margin expanded to 24%, and return on equity reached 37.5%.
- Diversified business model and structural growth have driven sustainable earnings, with 19 of the last 20 quarters showing year-over-year adjusted profit growth.
- Growth is primarily organic (80% in Q2), with disciplined M&A contributing to platform expansion and synergies.
- Strong client relationships, with 77 clients generating over $5 million in annual revenue, up from 49 in 2025.


### Financial highlights

- Q2 2026 revenue: $696 million (+39% YoY); adjusted PBT: $166 million (+56% YoY); adjusted PBT margin: 24%.
- Adjusted EPS for H1: $3.29; reported EPS for H1: $3.61; trailing 12-month reported EPS: $5.72.
- H1 2026 revenue: $1.39 billion; adjusted PBT: $319 million, matching full-year 2024 profit.
- Return on equity: 37.5% in Q2; adjusted ROE: 37.8%.
- Profit after tax in Q2: $155 million, including $35 million gain from Winterflood Custody sale.


### Outlook and guidance

- Confident in maintaining growth at the high end of the 10%-20% target range, with M&A expected to contribute around 20% of growth.
- Margins expected to remain strong, with potential for slow, steady increases.
- Q3 trends in July and early August consistent with H1 performance.
- Healthy pipeline for new client balances and continued growth in clearing.


### Segment performance

- Clearing: Q2 revenue up 16% YoY to $161 million; average client balances rose to $19.1 billion; adjusted PBT margin at 49%.
- Agency & Execution: Q2 revenue up 35% YoY to $351 million; prime revenue at record $120 million; adjusted PBT up 69% to $117 million; margin at 33%.
- Market Making: Q2 revenue up 106% YoY to $118 million; strong performance in metals and securities; adjusted PBT at $45 million, margin at 38%.
- Solutions: Q2 revenue up 74% YoY; adjusted PBT nearly quadrupled to $25 million; margin at 35%.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted PBT margin: 24% in Q2.
- Adjusted ROE: 37.8% in Q2.
- RAC ratio at end of June: ~12%, above S&P's 10% threshold for strong capitalization.
- Liquidity headroom: $1.8 billion; total funding sources: $8.1 billion.
- Average daily VAR in H1: $5.8 million; 87% of trading days profitable.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Forward-looking statements subject to risks and uncertainties; actual results may differ materially.
- Market conditions, including volatility and interest rates, remain important to performance.
- Isolated client default in January impacted H1 clearing results.


### Significant events and developments

- Sale of Winterflood Custody business generated $35 million gain.
- Issued $500 million hybrid capital and $500 million senior unsecured notes to support growth.
- Announced acquisition of Bright Point, expected to close late 2026 or early 2027, strengthening Asian clearing presence.
- Innovations in market infrastructure: first to offer cross-margining on US Treasuries, enabled USDC stablecoin margining, executed on-chain repo, and preparing to clear prediction markets.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Issued $500 million hybrid capital and $500 million senior unsecured notes, both oversubscribed.
- Maintains significant excess capital and liquidity, targeting investment-grade credit ratings.
- 80% of assets driven by client activity; total assets at $42.1 billion as of June 30.


### Market and industry conditions

- Q2 saw reduced exchange volumes (-17% sequentially), lower volatility, and flat interest rates, but elevated commodity prices.
- Growth in client balances driven by both new and existing clients, with majority of Q2 growth outside the US.
- Prime brokerage benefiting from industry capacity constraints at larger banks and broadening client base.
