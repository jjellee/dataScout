# Penguin Solutions, Inc. — Q4 2026 (2026-10-06)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q4 net sales rose 68% YoY to $567M; AI-driven businesses and Integrated Memory were 78% of sales and grew 141% YoY.
- FY26 net sales reached $1.73B, up 26%; record backlog and stronger bookings supported an upgraded FY27 growth outlook.
- EPS growth is expected to exceed sales growth as scale, operating leverage, and disciplined expense growth lift profitability.


### Financial highlights

- Q4 gross profit was a record $163M, up 57% YoY; operating income reached a record $90M, up 129% YoY.
- FY26 gross profit was a record $508M; operating income was a record $241M, up 44%, and diluted EPS was $2.87, up 51%.
- Record Q4 adjusted EBITDA was $93M, up 115% YoY; FY26 adjusted EBITDA was $256M, up 37%.
- Q4 product sales were $510M, up 86% YoY; services sales were $57M, down 11% amid lower hyperscale volumes, partly offset by AI factory managed services.


### Outlook and guidance

- FY27 net sales growth expected at ~40% at midpoint, ±10 percentage points, to ~$2.43B; midpoint growth is ~45% using the prior FY26 outlook midpoint.
- FY27 diluted EPS expected at ~$4.45, ±$0.70, or ~55% growth at midpoint; outlook assumes ~63M diluted shares and ~20% tax rate.
- FY27 gross margin expected at ~28%, ±2 percentage points; operating expenses ~ $275M, ±$10M, with spending growth well below sales growth.
- FY27 segment sales outlook: Advanced Computing growth ~40% ±10 points; Integrated Memory ~50% ±10 points; Optimized LED relatively flat YoY.


### Segment performance

- Advanced Computing Q4 sales were $154M, up 11% YoY; non-hyperscale AI infrastructure grew 99% and was 66% of segment sales. FY26 sales were $559M, down 14%.
- Integrated Memory Q4 sales were a record $341M, up 158% YoY and 24% QoQ; FY26 sales were $924M, up 99% and 53% of total sales.
- Optimized LED Q4 sales were $72M, up 7% YoY; FY26 sales were $249M, down 3%.
- FY26 non-hyperscale AI infrastructure in Advanced Computing grew 72%; the segment’s growth offset ongoing runoff in traditional businesses.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Q4 gross margin was 28.8%, up 70 bps QoQ; operating margin was 15.8%, up 4.2 percentage points YoY.
- FY26 gross margin was 29.3%, down 1.7 percentage points, while operating margin expanded 1.7 points to 13.9%.
- Q4 diluted EPS was $1, up 133% YoY; FY26 diluted EPS was $2.87, up 51%.
- DSO improved to 46 days from 53 last quarter; inventory days were 48 and DPO was 52. Cash conversion cycle was 42 days, versus 33 last quarter and 49 a year ago.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- FY27 results may vary with customer deployment timing, product availability and lead times, component costs, product mix, and evolving memory market conditions.
- Memory remains cyclical; memory supply, pricing, and costs may change during FY27, affecting sales cadence and margins.
- Large customer projects may shift between quarters; extended component lead times can alter project and shipment timing.
- Significant working-capital investment supported growth; improving working-capital efficiency remains a FY27 priority.


### Significant events and developments

- Stephen Cumming joined as CFO; Aaron Johnson, interim CFO since July, returned to his VP of Finance and Accounting role.
- Added 17 AI infrastructure customers in FY26 and six in Q4; 12 existing AI infrastructure customers expanded during FY26.
- Neocloud engagements include designing, deploying, and operating AI factories, including a 36,000-GPU project in Norway; many projects are already underway.
- Advanced ClusterWareAI with agentic operations capabilities; named an NVIDIA AI Factory Specialized Partner and added AI hardware and memory supply relationships.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Completed an oversubscribed $750M 0% convertible note offering due 2031; initial conversion price ~$116.70, with capped calls intended to reduce dilution up to ~$175.05.
- Exchanged ~$296M of 2029 and 2030 notes for cash and shares, repaid $100M under the credit agreement, and ended Q4 with $647M cash.
- Operating cash use was $163M in Q4 and $152M for FY26, primarily from working-capital investment; capex was $4M in Q4 and $12M for FY26.
- FY27 investment priorities remain AI infrastructure and memory; the asset-light model supports scaling with limited capital spending.


### Notes to the financial statements

- Gross sales were $1.57B and gross cost of goods sold $1.42B in Q4; differences from net sales primarily reflect memory logistics services accounted for on an agent basis.


### Market and industry conditions

- Agentic AI and inference are increasing data-center demand across GPUs, CPUs, and memory; on-premises AI factories appeal to enterprises seeking improved economics, control, and performance.
- Neocloud demand accelerated; customers value end-to-end AI factory design, build, deployment, and managed operations supporting service-level commitments.
- AI is viewed as a structural driver of data-center memory demand; CXL expansion products are seeing stronger bookings and pipeline for inference workloads.
