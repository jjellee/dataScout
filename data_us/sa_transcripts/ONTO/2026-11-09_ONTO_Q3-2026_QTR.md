# Onto Innovation Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-11-09)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 revenue, operating margin and EPS exceeded the high end of guidance; gross margin expanded 60 bps QoQ as operational improvements supported strong demand.
- Record backlog exceeded $1.3B, up 20% since the prior update, supporting confidence in sequential revenue growth through at least H1 2027.


### Financial highlights

- Q3 revenue was $410M, up 19% QoQ and nearly 90% YoY; advanced packaging and specialty device products grew 35% QoQ and represented slightly more than half of revenue.
- Q3 gross margin was 57.6%, up 60 bps QoQ and 300 bps from Q4 2025; operating margin was 32.6%, up 260 bps QoQ.
- Non-GAAP Q3 EPS was $2.44, up 26% QoQ and above the prior guidance range.
- 2026 midpoint outlook implies record revenue of $1.485B, growth above 45%, and record EPS.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q4 revenue guidance increased to $430M–$450M; midpoint represents more than 7% sequential growth and implies H2 2026 revenue of $850M, up 34% from H1.
- Q4 midpoint guidance: 34% operating margin and $2.69 non-GAAP EPS, assuming a ~16% non-GAAP tax rate and ~50M shares.
- Q4 gross margin is expected to improve another 0.5 percentage point, exiting 2026 slightly above 58%; further expansion is expected in 2027.
- Sequential revenue growth is expected through at least H1 2027, with continued growth in H2 possible; 2027 gross margin expansion is expected to average ~50 bps per quarter.


### Segment performance

- Advanced packaging revenue is on track to grow more than 80% in 2026, with increases relatively evenly split across 2.5D logic and HBM; 2027 growth is expected to exceed 35%.
- Dragonfly G5 revenue has reached more than 10 customers; 2027 purchase orders already represent double 2026 G5 revenue, while new application requests doubled in Q3.
- 3Di revenue through Q3 exceeded the prior full-year total; Q4 revenue is expected to exceed the combined first three quarters of 2026, driven by new applications and customer wins.
- Advanced nodes generated approximately $135M in Q3 revenue, about 60% memory and 40% logic; the segment is on pace to grow over 35% in 2026, with faster growth expected in 2027.
- Panel revenue is expected to double in 2026; backlog supports over 40% growth in 2027, with JetStep orders extending into 2028.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Q3 gross margin was 57.6% and operating margin was 32.6%; both were record levels, supported by factory model, productivity and SG&A leverage initiatives.
- Q4 midpoint outlook implies a 34% operating margin and $2.69 non-GAAP EPS; Q3 non-GAAP EPS was $2.44.
- Approximately $80M of free cash flow was generated in Q3.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Supply-chain pricing pressures, including memory costs, remain a headwind; mitigation includes design changes, lower memory use, productivity and extended-factory efficiencies.
- Suppliers may face bottlenecks, but active support and alternative sourcing have addressed issues so far; no portfolio-wide lead-time extensions were reported.
- Management cited uncertainty around data-center spending but reported no customer hesitation or change in order patterns; demand continued to exceed capacity.


### Significant events and developments

- Closed a 27% investment in Rigaku for $720M and completed Semilab integration during Q3.
- Atlas G6 achieved the fastest OCD platform launch in the business's history, with adoption supporting advanced-node growth.
- Dragonfly G5 front-end evaluations are progressing well; customer decisions are expected to begin closing in 2027.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Cash and short-term investments totaled approximately $1.25B at September 30, including Q3 free cash flow and the $720M Rigaku investment payment.


### Notes to the financial statements

- A ~$40M unrealized loss on the Rigaku investment was recorded, principally reflecting its September 30 closing share price of JPY 1,740; future gains or losses will be marked to market quarterly in GAAP other income/loss.
- Non-GAAP results exclude Rigaku unrealized gains or losses and related effects on the GAAP tax rate.


### Market and industry conditions

- AI demand and expanding use cases are driving expectations for an extended period of semiconductor growth; customers are making multi-year commitments and planning capacity ramps.
- Packaging process-control intensity is rising with chiplet, 2.5D and 3D complexity and higher die value; demand is increasing across advanced packaging, power semiconductors and silicon photonics.
- Advanced-node metrology is shifting toward direct in-die measurement, raising sampling rates and capital intensity; logic and DRAM demand are particularly strong.
