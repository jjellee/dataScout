# Oracle Corporation — Investor Day 2026 (2026-10-28)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Financial outlook and capital allocation

- FY2027 revenue growth guidance: +34% YoY; FY2030 revenue commitment: $225B+, a stated 35% CAGR and $158B+ addition over four years.
- Free cash flow expected to turn positive in H2 FY2028 and exceed $30B in FY2029; FY2030 non-GAAP operating margin target 40%+ and non-GAAP EPS $21+.
- Peak CapEx expected in FY2027-FY2028, stepping down in FY2029; around 7 GW of incremental capacity planned over the next couple of years, reaching around 10 GW by FY2028-end.
- Final funding commitment for the current investment cycle: $20B, split evenly between $10B equity and $10B gross debt; upfront cash needs to self-funding expected to fall around 50% through financing flexibility.
- Gross debt expected to remain flat over the next couple of years after new debt; net debt/EBITDA target below 3.5x by FY2029, with continued growth expected after self-funding begins.


### Infrastructure expansion and economics

- RPO reached $664B, with more than 90% tied to infrastructure; guidance reflects around two quarters of potential delay at large sites, with costs ramping as construction progresses.
- Q1 FY2027 infrastructure delivery was 850 MW across 38 projects in 11 countries, 4x the prior-year pace; one customer’s racks began generating revenue 21 days after data-center access.
- Around 7 GW of projects are expected to ramp into strong cash-flow contributions; five large sites account for around 5 GW, with Abilene nearing completion and four sites expected to begin service in calendar 2027.
- Illustrative 1 GW project economics: around $37B CapEx, around $16B annualized revenue, around 35% gross margin and around 34% project operating margin; fully ramped, converts 100% of non-operating profit after tax plus depreciation into annual cash flow.
- For a 1 GW example, 70% five-year vendor financing lowers first-two-year capital needs to $18B; bring-your-own-hardware can reduce capital needs to $6B or less, with lower revenue but higher margins and returns.


### Applications and AI monetization

- Fusion Applications usage is shifting beyond seat subscriptions to AI-unit consumption, creating usage revenue without adding seats; AI-unit margins benefit from separating AI reasoning and planning from deterministic compute execution.
- New Fusion Claw enables specialist, long-running enterprise work, including large-scale reconciliation, simulations and optimization; 75 agentic applications announced, including 25 Claw-enabled applications.
- Fusion Companion adds a universal AI entry layer across applications; machine-readable setup skills are expected to cut implementation work and time to Fusion by 50%-70%, including migrations from on-premises and competing systems.
- Fusion AI usage exceeded 150M calls in Q1 FY2027, rising 42% from June to August; September usage approached 500B tokens, up 22.7% MoM, while deployed agents grew from over 1,000 in May to 3,500 in September.
- Fusion recorded 5.7M agent runs last quarter; more than 100,000 builders certified on AI Studio, with 7,000 customers and 3,600-3,700 agents in production.


### Industry growth and customer evidence

- On-premises-to-cloud moves produce a stated 3x-4x pricing uplift; adding applications can deliver another 3x bump at each expansion step. North American cross-sell opportunity exceeds $5B.
- Industry applications are now positioned across the broader portfolio rather than as a separate business; AI, faster implementations and unified industry-focused selling support cloud migrations, cross-sell and new agent-based offerings.
- NetSuite serves 44,000 mid-market customers; new AI features and industry offerings, including healthcare, hospitality, construction and local government, extend the cross-sell strategy.
- Grupo Bimbo uses Fusion to unify acquired businesses and supply-chain data; finance close time fell from 16 days to 3 days, while supply-chain improvements represent hundreds of millions of dollars in annual opportunity.
- Vodafone migrated end-of-life databases to dedicated OCI regions and uses Oracle Database@Azure and Oracle Database@AWS to retain database capabilities as applications move to public cloud.


### Strategic positioning and execution

- AI is positioned as an expansion of the opportunity, activating the full technology stack across infrastructure, database, data platforms and applications; priorities include differentiated growth, profitability and free cash flow, and disciplined capital allocation.
- Integrated security, data access and business permissions are being embedded into AI agents; products such as NetSuite Next and Fusion Claw make AI a workflow interface across applications and systems.
- OCI is expanding hardware and site flexibility, including multiple accelerator choices, modular data centers, fungible capacity and more than 100 MW of L2Arc deployments supporting accelerators in an air-cooled data center.
- AI infrastructure demand is expected to remain strong over the next three to five years; capacity strategy combines gigawatt-scale campuses for training with distributed 50-200 MW sites for inference and latency-sensitive workloads.
- AI Data Platform and multi-cloud database availability broaden data workloads; the database business was live in about 71 regions quarter-to-date and growing over 209%.
