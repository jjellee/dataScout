# X-Energy, Inc. — Q1 2026 (2026-06-04)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Completed IPO in April 2026, now listed on NASDAQ under ticker XE, marking first earnings as a public company.
- Focused on commercializing advanced nuclear technology, with proprietary TRISO-X fuel and Xe-100 small modular reactor (SMR) design.
- Secured major partnerships with Dow, Amazon, and Centrica; advancing first deployment at Dow's Seadrift, Texas facility.
- Achieved significant regulatory milestones, including NRC fuel fabrication license and environmental assessment approval for Dow project.
- Commercial pipeline exceeds 11 GW, with ongoing expansion in the U.S., U.K., and Canada.


### Financial highlights

- Q1 2026 total revenues and grant income reached $43.4 million, up 109% year-over-year.
- Services revenue was $39.9 million, primarily from ARDP activities; grant income was $3.5 million from Dow's demonstration reactor.
- Operating expenses totaled $109.5 million, up 133% year-over-year, driven by ARDP activity, headcount, and professional fees.
- Net cash used in operating activities was $67.3 million, up 61% from Q1 2025; investing activities used $166.0 million, mainly for investment securities and capex.
- Ended Q1 with $944 million in liquidity; post-IPO liquidity increased to approximately $2 billion.


### Outlook and guidance

- No specific forward-looking financial guidance provided; expense trajectory not to be straight-lined from Q1 due to accelerating construction and headcount.
- Next 18 months focused on TX-1 construction, Dow permit issuances, and announcing the next 1 GW project.
- Multiple advanced discussions underway for new projects; at least one new 1 GW project expected to be announced in 2026.


### Segment performance

- Revenue streams include licensing fees, service fees, and fuel sales, with revenue beginning in pre-commercial stages and continuing through plant lifetimes.
- Fuel fabrication business anchored by TX-1 facility, expected to support 11 reactors at steady state; TX-2 expansion planned to support up to 55 reactors.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Zero debt outstanding at end of Q1 2026.
- IPO net proceeds of $1.1 billion, with 50.9 million shares issued at $23 per share.
- ARDP federal cost share reimbursements totaled $508 million as of March 31, 2026.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Forward-looking statements subject to significant risks and uncertainties as outlined in SEC filings.
- Project timelines are long-dated, with regulatory and customer-driven milestones potentially impacting schedules.


### Significant events and developments

- Received NRC Part 70 commercial fuel fabrication license for TX-1 and TX-2, first new NRC-licensed facilities in over 50 years.
- Submitted application for U.K. Generic Design Assessment for Xe-100, advancing partnership with Centrica.
- Secured first core of HALEU fuel for Dow project from U.S. Department of Energy.
- Non-cash mark-to-market loss of $100.8 million in Q1 due to warrant exercise.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Significant capital raised through IPO, enhancing liquidity for project development and supply chain de-risking.
- Investment in fixed income securities and ongoing capex for TX-1 and related facilities.
- Business model limits balance sheet risk to fuel manufacturing and testing facilities; project construction risk borne by customers.


### Market and industry conditions

- Growing demand for advanced nuclear driven by AI, industrial electrification, and high-priced power markets.
- Regulatory environment improving, with NRC reforms and new licensing pathways under evaluation.
- U.K. government support and high market interest for advanced nuclear solutions.
