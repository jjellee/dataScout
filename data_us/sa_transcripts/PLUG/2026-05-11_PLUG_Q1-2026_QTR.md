# Plug Power Inc. — Q1 2026 (2026-05-11)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q1 2026 revenue rose 22% year-over-year to $163.5 million, with growth in material handling, electrolyzers, and hydrogen fuel.
- Gross margin improved from -55% to -13% year-over-year, a 42 percentage point gain.
- Project Quantum Leap cost actions are driving margin improvements, expected to continue sequentially through 2026.
- Focus remains on achieving positive EBITDA in Q4 2026 and long-term profitability.


### Financial highlights

- Electrolyzer revenue surged from $9.2 million in Q1 2025 to $40.8 million in Q1 2026.
- Hydrogen fuel sales grew 10% year-over-year; material handling platform grew 15%; electrolyzer platform grew 343%.
- Adjusted EPS for Q1 2026 was -$0.08, improved from -$0.17 in Q1 2025, excluding $140 million in non-cash charges.
- Ended Q1 with $223 million in unrestricted cash and $579 million in restricted cash, totaling $802 million.
- CapEx was nominal at $7 million for the quarter.


### Outlook and guidance

- Full-year 2026 sales growth expected at 13%-15%.
- Q2 revenue projected to grow sequentially from Q1, with margin rates expected to improve quarter-over-quarter.
- Positive EBITDA targeted for Q4 2026, positive operating income in 2027, and full profitability in 2028.
- Inventory reduction of at least $100 million targeted for 2026, with most progress in the second half.


### Segment performance

- Material handling: Strong customer engagement, productivity gains, and reduced grid dependence; major refresh cycles with Amazon and Walmart expected.
- Electrolyzers: Significant revenue growth, major projects in Spain, Portugal, and Canada advancing; strong pipeline in aviation and synthetic fuels.
- Hydrogen fuel: 20% top-line growth year-over-year, margin improved by 54 percentage points.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Gross margin improved by 71% year-over-year.
- GenDrive service costs down over 30% year-over-year.
- Fuel margin rate improved by 50.4 percentage points.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Forward-looking statements subject to risks and uncertainties, including those detailed in SEC filings.
- Project complexity and regulatory/bureaucratic delays can impact timing of large-scale projects.
- Geopolitical instability and energy supply constraints affecting fuel markets and project urgency.


### Significant events and developments

- Asset monetization initiatives, including Stream Data Centers, expected to generate $275 million in proceeds.
- Sale of Section 48 investment tax credit for Louisiana JV targeted at $39.2 million, with better terms than Georgia.
- Major customer refresh cycles with Amazon and Walmart to drive demand in coming years.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Debt restructuring in Q4 2025 lowered cost of capital and extended maturity profile.
- Restricted cash expected to release at ~$50 million per quarter over several years.
- Existing capital, asset monetization, and restricted cash releases expected to fund 2026 operations.


### Market and industry conditions

- Rising electricity prices and grid constraints increase attractiveness of hydrogen solutions.
- EU mandates for green hydrogen adoption driving refinery projects.
- Aviation sector demand rising due to jet fuel shortages and energy security concerns.
