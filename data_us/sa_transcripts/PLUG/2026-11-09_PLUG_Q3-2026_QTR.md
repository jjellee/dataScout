# Plug Power Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-11-09)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 revenue reached $201 million, up 13% YoY and QoQ; gross margin improved sharply YoY, while net cash usage fell to a quarterly low of $28 million.
- Priorities remain sales growth, margin improvement and lower cash use; management is targeting positive EBITDAS in Q4.


### Financial highlights

- Nine-month revenue was $542 million, up 12% YoY; Q3 revenue was $201 million.
- Gross margin was -2.7%, versus -68% in Q3 2025, but slightly below Q2 2026.
- Operating expenses were $70 million versus $229 million a year earlier; normalized near-term run rate estimated at $70 million-$75 million.
- Net income attributable was $99.8 million, or $0.07 basic EPS; excluding $192 million of non-cash fair-value gains, results would have been a net loss, albeit narrower YoY.
- Net cash usage was $28 million, versus $61 million in Q2 and $145 million in Q1; down 54% QoQ, with no capital raises.


### Outlook and guidance

- Reaffirmed FY2026 revenue growth guidance of 15%-16%; implied Q4 revenue of ~$280 million, supported by backlog, schedules and equipment deliveries.
- Q4 net cash usage expected to be approximately flat; management is focused on positive EBITDAS, with volume and mix key to delivery.
- Electrolyzer revenue expected to be slightly down for FY2026; Q4 equipment margins expected to improve on material-handling volume and contribution.
- Q4 Walmart refresh cycle expected to cover 2,000-2,500 GenDrive units; the 20,000-unit refresh opportunity is expected over the next two to three years.


### Segment performance

- Equipment revenue was $106 million versus $97 million; material handling drove growth, with 1,136 GenDrive deployments, more than double Q3 2025.
- Service revenue rose 47% to $29 million; margin reached 31% versus 27% in Q2 and -2% a year ago, supported by reliability and lower parts consumption.
- Fuel revenue was $38 million, up 6% YoY; margin improved to -52% from -79%, but Q3 plant outages increased external purchases.
- PPA revenue rose 13% to $28 million; margin improved to -16% from -30% in Q2 and -85% YoY, reflecting pricing, lower maintenance and lease costs.
- Electrolyzer revenue fell ~$40 million YoY on deployment timing; 280 MW Arcadia agreement signed, with a separate cooperation agreement covering four additional projects and >1 GW potential capacity.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Equipment margin was approximately break-even versus -77% a year earlier; restructuring, volume, pricing and lower inventory adjustments drove improvement.
- Q3 gross margin improved by roughly 65 percentage points YoY to -2.7%; it remained slightly below Q2.
- Management cited potential 30%-40% contribution margin on incremental material-handling equipment sales; fuel margin improvement opportunity of $5 million-$7 million from reduced external purchases.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Unplanned plant downtime hurt Q3 fuel margins by forcing external purchases; management said the issues were largely resolved and expected Q4 improvement.
- Stream asset monetization proceeds depend on regulatory and permitting steps in Texas and New York; no Stream proceeds were included in the Q4 forecast.
- Q4 EBITDAS depends on execution, sales volume, mix and closing commercial items; management noted substantial work remains.
- Electrolyzer projects remain subject to FID and notice-to-proceed timing; large-project revenue recognition depends on execution milestones.


### Significant events and developments

- Signed a 280 MW GenEco supply agreement with Arcadia eFuels for Project ENDOR in Denmark; deliveries begin after notice to proceed.
- Secured a 50 MW Orica order after FID for the Hunter Valley Hydrogen Hub; shipped a 1 MW system to HWR Hydrogen and continued work on Hy2gen Courant.
- Commissioning continued on 25 MW Iberdrola/BP and 100 MW Galp projects; early deployments are supporting follow-on opportunities with these customers.
- Approximately 8 GW of qualified electrolyzer opportunities remains active; Arcadia, Orica and Carlton Power projects are progressing, with some revenue expected in 2027.
- Walmart refreshes are starting in Q4; Southwire has become a customer, and discussions are underway with four or five prospective customers for 2027.


### Capital allocation and financing

- No equity or debt capital was raised in Q3; asset monetizations and operating improvements supported cash needs without dilution.
- Stream transactions generated $51 million year to date, including $46 million in Q3; further proceeds were excluded from Q4 forecasts.
- Targeting release of $62 million of restricted cash tied to a Texas power PPA by end-Q1 2027; legacy PPA-related restricted cash releases are running at about $40 million per quarter.
- Inventory declined $49 million from year-end, with another meaningful reduction expected in Q4; quarter-end cash was $134 million unrestricted and $469 million restricted.


### Notes to the financial statements

- Q3 net income included $192 million of non-cash mark-to-market gains on convertible notes and warrant liabilities, contributing about $0.12 of diluted EPS; quarterly adjustments may continue until maturity or settlement.


### Market and industry conditions

- Management sees energy security and independence, alongside decarbonization, accelerating hydrogen adoption; Europe imports roughly 85% of its natural gas.
- EU renewable-energy rules are being translated into national requirements for refining, transport and sustainable aviation fuels; internal estimates point to ~10 GW of electrolyzer demand in Spain by 2030.
- Supply-chain cost pressure has not been significant; rising sales volumes are improving supplier leverage and manufacturing efficiency, while pricing remains based on customer value and hydrogen production costs.
