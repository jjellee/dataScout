# Plug Power Inc. — Q2 2026 (2026-08-10)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q2 2026 revenue reached $178.3 million, up 9% sequentially, with gross margin improving to -0.9% from -30.7% year-over-year.
- Operating expenses declined 50% year-over-year to $62 million, reflecting restructuring and asset monetization efforts.
- Net cash usage improved to $61 million, a 58% reduction from Q1, with unrestricted cash at $161.9 million at quarter end.
- Full-year revenue growth guidance raised to 15%-16%, with positive EBITDA targeted for Q4.


### Financial highlights

- H1 2026 revenue totaled $342 million, up 11% year-over-year and slightly ahead of prior guidance.
- Adjusted EPS loss narrowed to $0.07 from $0.18 year-over-year; GAAP EPS loss was $0.14, impacted by $104 million in non-cash charges.
- Service revenue grew 82% year-over-year to $29.8 million, with a 27% service margin.
- Fuel revenue rose 15% year-over-year to $39.5 million; fuel gross margin improved to -48% from -91%.


### Outlook and guidance

- Full-year revenue growth guidance increased to 15%-16%, up from 13%-15% previously.
- Majority of H2 growth expected in Q4, driven by equipment volume and year-end deployment cycles.
- Positive EBITDA targeted for Q4 2026; operating income positive in Q4 2027 and overall profitability in 2028.


### Segment performance

- Material handling: 1,666 GenDrive units deployed, more than double last year; two major customers plan to refresh 20,000 units over three years.
- Electrolyzer: Multiple large projects progressing, including 30 MW Barrow (UK), 50 MW Orica (Australia), and 275 MW Hy2gen (Canada).
- Hydrogen fuel: Revenue up 15% year-over-year, with improved plant utilization and logistics driving margin gains.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Gross margin improved to -0.9% from -30.7% year-over-year and -13% sequentially.
- Operating expenses down 50% year-over-year to $62 million.
- Net cash usage reduced by 58% sequentially to $61 million.
- Inventory down $28 million from year-end; $100 million reduction targeted for full year.
- Capital spending under $9 million in H1; total cash (unrestricted + restricted) over $670 million.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Forward-looking statements subject to risks outlined in annual and quarterly SEC filings, including market, regulatory, and execution risks.
- Regulatory changes, such as the Texas data center moratorium, could impact asset monetization timelines.


### Significant events and developments

- Asset monetization program underway, with $47 million received post-quarter and $80 million near-term liquidity expected.
- $39.7 million recovery from a customer contract dispute contributed to lower operating expenses.
- European regulatory tailwinds (RED III, subsidies) expected to drive electrolyzer demand.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Asset monetization and non-dilutive financing targeted to unlock $275 million; $52 million received to date.
- Convertible debt is termed out eight years, with no near-term amortization; focus remains on non-dilutive funding.
- Restricted cash releases provide a built-in non-dilutive funding stream, with $155 million scheduled for release in the next 12 months.


### Market and industry conditions

- European hydrogen market accelerating due to RED III and national mandates, with Spain alone potentially driving 10 GW electrolyzer demand by 2030.
- Data center market under review in Texas, but ongoing asset sales and customer engagement continue.
- Multi-year growth runway supported by regulatory and commercial tailwinds in hydrogen and electrolyzer segments.
