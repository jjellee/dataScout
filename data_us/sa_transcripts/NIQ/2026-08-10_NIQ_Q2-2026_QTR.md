# NIQ Global Intelligence plc — Q2 2026 (2026-08-10)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Achieved fifth consecutive quarter exceeding top-end guidance across all key metrics, with strong organic constant currency revenue growth of 5.8%, adjusted EBITDA up nearly 22%, and margin expansion of 270 basis points to 23.3%.
- Adjusted EPS reached $0.27, above guidance; levered free cash flow turned positive at $74.1 million, and leverage reduced to 3.1x.
- Raised full-year 2026 guidance across all metrics, reflecting continued strong demand, robust retention, and emerging AI-driven growth opportunities.
- AI innovation, consumer panel expansion, and technology enhancements are driving competitive differentiation and future growth.


### Financial highlights

- Q2 reported revenue grew 8% to $1.1 billion; organic constant currency revenue up 5.8% year-over-year.
- Adjusted EBITDA increased 21.9% to $262 million; adjusted net income improved by $80 million to $78.7 million.
- Adjusted EPS was $0.27; levered free cash flow improved by $137 million year-over-year to $74.1 million.
- Net loss of $30.5 million, primarily due to lower FX gains versus prior year.
- Net leverage ratio improved to 3.1x, down from 3.4x in Q1.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q3 2026 guidance: reported revenue growth of 4.9%-5.3%, organic constant currency growth of 5.2%-5.5%, adjusted EBITDA growth of 15%-17%, margins of 23%-23.5%, and adjusted EPS of $0.22-$0.24.
- Full-year 2026 guidance: reported revenue growth of 7.1%-7.4%, organic constant currency growth of 5.2%-5.6%, adjusted EBITDA growth of 15%-17%, margins of 23.5%-23.9%, adjusted EPS of $1.08-$1.12, and levered free cash flow of $245-$255 million.
- Expect to be below 3x net leverage by year-end; Q4 outlook implies further margin expansion and nearly double Q3 adjusted EPS.


### Segment performance

- Americas: 8.3% organic constant currency growth, adjusted EBITDA up 10.5% to $143 million, margins at 31.4%.
- EMEA: 4.9% organic constant currency growth, adjusted EBITDA up 26.1% to $179 million, margins expanded 550 bps to 35.3%.
- APAC: Returned to growth at 1.9% organic constant currency, adjusted EBITDA up 9.2% to $32 million, margins at 19.8%.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Gross and net dollar retention at 99% and 105%, respectively.
- Annualized intelligence subscription revenue grew 5.8% to over $3 billion.
- Operating expenses increased 5.7%, with $36 million in one-time restructuring costs.
- Depreciation and amortization at $154 million (14% of revenue); GAAP interest expense $55 million, down $40 million year-over-year.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Guidance and outlook subject to macroeconomic conditions and client demand trends.
- Foreign currency volatility less impactful in Q2 2026 compared to prior year.


### Significant events and developments

- Acquisition of YiMian to strengthen digital commerce capabilities in China and Southeast Asia.
- Launch of new AI-native products (Optiq Suite, Cadence, Bridge, ConnectAI) and first charter clients for ConnectAI.
- Completed most actions under 2026 restructuring program, achieving majority of $70-$80 million expected run-rate savings.
- Data consumption on platform grew 25% year-over-year; AI-native solution revenue up 34% in Q2.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Capital priorities: fund growth, expand margins, pay down debt.
- $417 million in cash and equivalents, $747 million undrawn revolver, total liquidity ~$1.2 billion.
- CapEx at 6.5%-7% of revenue, focused on AI and technology investments.


### Market and industry conditions

- Strong demand for measurement and analytics solutions across all regions and products.
- AI adoption accelerating among clients, driving new use cases and budget allocations.
- Competitive wins in e-commerce, retail media, and ad tech; expanding into adjacent markets.
