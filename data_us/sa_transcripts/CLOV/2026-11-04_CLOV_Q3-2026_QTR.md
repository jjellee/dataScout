# Clover Health Investments, Corp. — Q3 2026 (2026-11-04)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- 9M 2026 membership grew 49% YoY; revenue rose 57%, consolidated gross profit increased $161 million, and Adjusted EBITDA more than doubled to $97 million.
- First full-year GAAP net income profitability is expected in 2026; 2027–2028 earnings expansion is expected from cohort maturation and operating leverage.


### Financial highlights

- Q3 revenue was $759 million, up 53% YoY; consolidated gross profit was $130 million, up 77%.
- Q3 Adjusted EBITDA was $16 million versus $2 million YoY; GAAP net income was $6 million versus a $24 million loss.
- 9M revenue was approximately $2.3 billion; consolidated gross profit was $442 million, Adjusted EBITDA $97 million, and GAAP net income $62 million.
- Q3 net income included approximately $11 million of other income, including a $4 million favorable investment valuation adjustment.


### Outlook and guidance

- FY26 guidance raised at the low end, with high ends unchanged: average MA membership 157,000–158,000; revenue $2.97–$3.00 billion; gross profit $540–$555 million.
- FY26 Adjusted EBITDA guidance is $75–$85 million and GAAP net income guidance is $25–$35 million; first full-year GAAP profitability expected.
- Q4 expected to reflect seasonal gross profit declines and higher planned AEP SG&A, resulting in the seasonal Adjusted EBITDA loss included in guidance.
- No formal 2027–2028 guidance; early AEP activity supports confidence in another strong membership-growth year and meaningful earnings expansion in 2027.


### Segment performance

- Counterpart Health is progressing toward a goal of serving as many people through Counterpart as through the growing MA membership base.
- Clover Assistant coverage accelerated in H2 2026, tracking ahead of the prior-year pace and supporting clinical engagement and cohort economics.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted SG&A was $114 million in Q3 and $345 million YTD; adjusted SG&A was approximately 15% of YTD revenue, improving 120 bps YoY.
- FY26 adjusted SG&A ratio improvement expected at or above the original 100–150 bps target.
- Historical insurance gross profit per member per month rose approximately $70 from year one to year two and $85 from year two to year three.
- 9M operating cash flow was $203 million; quarter-end cash equivalents and investments were approximately $513 million, with no debt outstanding.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Government appeal remains pending; management expressed confidence in its case but said potential outcomes would be addressed if the appeal does not go its way.
- Outpatient utilization was elevated YoY but in line with initial outlook; inpatient utilization continued to trend favorably.
- New-member onboarding may create a near-term 2027 earnings drag, though management expects to absorb growth and progress members along the cohort-maturation curve.
- Planned Q4 medical-cost seasonality and higher AEP investment are expected to reduce gross profit and produce a seasonal Adjusted EBITDA loss.


### Significant events and developments

- Flagship PPO earned five Stars for payment year 2028, described as the nation’s largest five-Star PPO contract; HMO earned 4.5 Stars, placing all members in plans rated at least 4.5 Stars.
- Core HEDIS measures ranked number one nationally among comparable PPO plans for a third consecutive year.
- Clay Thornton was appointed CFO after serving as interim CFO.
- 2027 plan designs emphasize broad PPO choice, $0 primary care, low specialist copays, broad $0-premium options, and low Part D deductibles.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Liquidity and cash generation are expected to self-fund growth, meet regulatory capital requirements, and support further investment.
- Investments continue in Clover Assistant clinical capabilities, Counterpart Health, and core insurance operations, alongside fixed-cost leverage and administrative efficiency initiatives.
- 2027 investment plans are still under assessment; the portion of potential earnings gains to retain versus reinvest will be addressed with future guidance.


### Market and industry conditions

- AEP was tracking as expected, with planned market exits, fewer PPO options, and broader benefit repricing; early enrollment returns were described as strong.
- New Jersey dynamics include Braven’s exit affecting low-40,000 MA lives, Devoted’s entry, and large national insurers retreating from $0 PPOs.
- Near-term growth focus remains New Jersey and Georgia; management cited room to expand in both markets.
- Management expects AI to improve care, member service, and health-plan operating efficiency as the business scales.
