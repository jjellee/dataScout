# The Gap, Inc. — Goldman Sachs Global Consumer and Retail Conference (2026-09-14)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Gap brand momentum

- Gap marked its 11th consecutive quarter of growth and second double-digit growth quarter; denim ranking improved from No. 10 to No. 6, and kids and baby from No. 6 to No. 4.
- Traffic, AUR and units increased; more sales came at full price, supporting healthier margins.
- Product improvements and culturally relevant storytelling helped attract Gen Z while preserving existing customer appeal.
- GapBag and fragrances launched, with further product drops and marketing planned to build category momentum.


### Old Navy reset

- The latest quarter marked Old Navy’s first comp decline in 11 quarters; H1 seasonal-category misses reflected dress assortment and inventory-mix issues.
- Product and assortment mix improved, while marketing shifted toward sharper price points, value and product-specific messages; denim, active, kids and baby are priorities for the back half.
- Cardi B-led denim and knit campaigns are resonating; Cardi’s cardigan sold over 500,000 units in a short period.
- Michael Francis is set to become Old Navy CEO on November 2, following a transition period working alongside Haio and leading customer initiatives.
- Beauty products are rolling into stores, and the Fanatics sports partnership has launched, with additional announcements expected.


### Beauty and accessories

- Beauty and accessories moved from announced plans to launches; teams added category experts and developed brand-specific product strategies.
- Old Navy Beauty launched last month; Gap fragrances include Dream, currently the leading fragrance, while GapBag debuted with signature styles such as the Hoodie Tote and Five-Pocket Tote.
- The categories are in an early momentum-building phase, with limited near-term material impact; they are expected to support long-term growth and enhance margins.


### Fashiontainment and licensing

- Fashiontainment treats brands as stories, using original music-video content and cultural partnerships to generate virality and conversation.
- Plans include aligning product narratives with the entertainment calendar; Disney is the portfolio’s largest DTC licensee, with expanded business and further opportunities across entertainment studios.
- Fashiontainment, licensing, beauty and accessories are positioned as long-term growth enablers.


### Long-term value creation

- The phased model moves from fixing fundamentals to building momentum and then accelerating growth; apparel growth is targeted at a low-single-digit rate, alongside disciplined P&L management and continued bottom-line improvement.
- New categories and licensing are expected to add growth over time; strong cash flow and a strong balance sheet support the next phase.
