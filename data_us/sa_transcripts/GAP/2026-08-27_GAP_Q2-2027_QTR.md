# The Gap, Inc. — Q2 2027 (2026-08-27)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Net sales declined 2% year-over-year to $3.7 billion, with mixed brand performance and strong gross margin results driven by disciplined pricing and inventory management.
- Gap brand delivered 10% comparable sales growth, marking its 11th consecutive quarter of positive comps, while Old Navy comps declined 4% due to seasonal assortment and marketing challenges.
- Banana Republic posted its fifth consecutive quarter of positive comps (+3%), and Athleta comps declined 12% as turnaround efforts continue.
- Shareholder returns were prioritized through $600 million in share repurchases and $62 million in dividends in the quarter.


### Financial highlights

- Adjusted gross margin was 41.4%, up 20 basis points year-over-year, with merchandise margin expanding 80 basis points.
- Adjusted operating margin was 7.1%, down 70 basis points, primarily due to timing of investments.
- Adjusted EPS was $0.52, compared to $0.57 last year.
- Inventory at cost was flat year-over-year; unit inventory up 4% due to higher in-transit inventory.


### Outlook and guidance

- Full-year net sales growth expected at 1%-1.5%, with comparable sales roughly in line.
- Adjusted operating margin outlook raised to 7.4%-7.6% for the full year.
- Adjusted EPS now expected at $2.35-$2.45, up 10%-15% year-over-year.
- Third quarter net sales expected to increase 1.5%-2.5% year-over-year.


### Segment performance

- Gap: Net sales up 9%, comps up 10%, driven by strong product, storytelling, and lower discounting.
- Old Navy: Net sales and comps down 4%, with improvement expected as seasonal headwinds abate and new marketing initiatives launch.
- Banana Republic: Net sales up 1%, comps up 3%, with broad-based strength and new leadership.
- Athleta: Net sales and comps down 12%, with a measured approach to inventory and marketing as turnaround continues.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- SG&A was $1.3 billion, or 34.3% of net sales, deleveraging 90 basis points.
- Year-to-date free cash flow was $261 million; cash, equivalents, and short-term investments totaled $2.5 billion.
- Capital expenditures were $154 million for the quarter, with full-year capex expected at $650 million.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Outlook incorporates potential volatility in energy prices and U.S. tariffs.
- Inventory levels impacted by geopolitical disruptions, particularly in-transit inventory.


### Significant events and developments

- Leadership transition at Old Navy, with Michael Francis appointed as brand president and CEO effective November 2.
- Launch of Old Navy Beauty Co. nationwide and expansion of Fanatics partnership.
- Gap relaunched its fragrance line and is expanding into accessories, starting with bags.
- $600 million in share repurchases year-to-date, with $400 million remaining under current authorization.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Prioritized capital investments in new stores, remodels, technology, and supply chain.
- Paid $62 million in dividends and repurchased $200 million of stock in the quarter.
- Board approved a third quarter dividend of $0.175 per share.


### Market and industry conditions

- Maintained or gained market share across brands, with Gap and Old Navy holding strong positions in denim and active categories.
- Consumer and macro environment viewed as largely unchanged, but with caution around volatility.
