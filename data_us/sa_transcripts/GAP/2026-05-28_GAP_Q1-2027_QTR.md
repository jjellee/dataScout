# The Gap, Inc. — Q1 2027 (2026-05-28)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Achieved ninth consecutive quarter of positive comparable sales, with comps up 2% year-over-year and net sales up 1% to $3.5 billion.
- Three of four brands delivered positive comps; Gap brand led with 10% comp growth, while Old Navy and Banana Republic posted modest gains and Athleta declined.
- Outperformed gross margin outlook and continued to gain market share, especially in key categories like denim and active.
- Raised full-year adjusted EPS outlook to $2.30–$2.40 despite moderating revenue growth expectations.


### Financial highlights

- Net sales: $3.5 billion, up 1% year-over-year; comparable sales up 2%.
- Gross margin: 40.5%, down 130 bps year-over-year but ahead of guidance; merchandise margin down 100 bps, ROD deleveraged 30 bps.
- Adjusted operating margin: 5.2%, down 230 bps; reported operating margin: 12.7%.
- Adjusted EPS: $0.38 vs. $0.51 last year; reported EPS: $0.90, including a $313M legal settlement gain and $50M donation.
- Cash and equivalents: $2.6 billion, up 15% year-over-year.


### Outlook and guidance

- Full-year net sales growth expected at 1–2%, with Old Navy comps flat to up 1% and Gap comps in high single digits.
- Adjusted operating margin outlook unchanged at 7.3–7.5%.
- Full-year adjusted EPS raised to $2.30–$2.40, up 8–12% year-over-year.
- Second quarter net sales expected flat to down 1% year-over-year; Old Navy comps projected down low single digits.
- Gross margin for the year expected flat to up slightly; $80M net tariff relief reserved for fuel and promotional flexibility.


### Segment performance

- Old Navy: Net sales $2B (+1%), comps +1%; strength in denim, active, kids and baby, but weakness in women's dresses and seasonal categories.
- Gap: Net sales $796M (+10%), comps +10%; strong growth in denim, fleece, and kids and baby, with reduced discounting and cultural relevance.
- Banana Republic: Net sales $431M (+1%), comps +2%; balanced growth in men's and women's, strong engagement from collaborations.
- Athleta: Net sales $270M (–12%), comps –11%; ongoing inventory clearance and brand rebuild, with new product launches showing early promise.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted SG&A: $1.2B, 35.3% of net sales.
- Free cash flow: $78M; net cash from operations: $213M.
- Inventory levels flat year-over-year, with units down.
- Dividend increased 6% to $0.175/share; $63M paid in Q1.
- $400M in share repurchases YTD; $600M remaining authorization.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Tariff environment remains fluid; outlook assumes potential reversion to higher rates after July 24.
- Elevated fuel costs and potential for increased promotional activity could impact margins.
- No tariff refunds assumed in outlook due to uncertainty in timing and eligibility.


### Significant events and developments

- $313M legal settlement gain and $50M charitable donation in Q1, excluded from adjusted results.
- Appointment of Michael Francis as Chief Customer Officer for Old Navy and Donald Kohler as President and CEO of Banana Republic.
- Expansion of beauty and accessories, with full rollout at Old Navy and fragrance relaunch at Gap.
- Launch of Fanatics sports licensing partnership and AI-powered technology initiatives.


### Capital allocation and financing

- $650M in planned capital investments for 2026, focused on stores, technology, and supply chain.
- Continued focus on balanced capital allocation: reinvestment, dividend growth, and share repurchases.


### Market and industry conditions

- Consumer remains resilient across income cohorts; store traffic up 3% while online sales declined due to Athleta and dress category weakness.
- Promotional environment rational but closely monitored; beauty category seen as a significant growth opportunity.
