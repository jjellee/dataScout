# RadNet, Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-11-09)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 2026 consolidated revenue and adjusted EBITDA reached quarterly records, rising 22.1% and 23.8% YoY, respectively.
- Growth reflected higher imaging volumes, advanced-imaging mix, new centers, acquisitions, health-system partnerships, reimbursement mix, and Digital Health expansion.


### Financial highlights

- Revenue was $638.3 million versus $522.9 million YoY; adjusted EBITDA was $105 million versus $84.9 million.
- Adjusted diluted EPS, excluding unusual or one-time items, was $0.30 versus $0.24 YoY.
- Imaging Center adjusted EBITDA rose 25.3% to $102 million; Digital Health revenue increased 32.6% to $32.9 million.


### Outlook and guidance

- Imaging Center FY26 revenue guidance increased to $2.385 billion-$2.435 billion and adjusted EBITDA to $350 million-$363 million.
- Imaging Center FY26 free cash flow guidance rose to $120 million-$130 million; CapEx guidance increased to $170 million-$180 million.
- Digital Health FY26 revenue guidance was reaffirmed at $135 million-$145 million; adjusted EBITDA guidance rose to $11 million-$13 million before non-capitalized R&D.
- Digital Health expects year-end ARR above $140 million and Q4 revenue growth of ~20% sequentially; Q4 revenue is underpinned by deployments and signed contracts.


### Segment performance

- Imaging volumes increased 19.9% in aggregate and 8.9% same-center; overall procedures rose 10.8% and 3.2%, respectively.
- Advanced imaging reached 30.6% of procedural volume versus 28.2% YoY; MRI and PET/CT represented 38.6% and 10.2% of payments, respectively.
- Digital Health ARR was $112.6 million, up 67% YoY; excluding Gleamer, ARR increased 21%. External ARR more than doubled to $73 million.
- Digital Health revenue rose 33% YoY to $32.9 million; segment adjusted EBITDA was $3 million versus $3.5 million YoY, reflecting investment and acquisition-related margin dilution.
- Digital Health contract value totaled ~$71 million in Q3 and ~$108 million for 9M; its pipeline exceeded $300 million, and portfolio procedures topped 18 million, up >170% YoY.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Consolidated adjusted EBITDA margin expanded 23 bps to 16.5%; Imaging Center adjusted EBITDA margin improved 511 bps YoY.
- Cash was $750.6 million, net debt was $590.2 million, and net debt-to-adjusted EBITDA was 1.66x at quarter-end.
- Days sales outstanding were 31 days; the $282 million revolver was fully available.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Staffing shortages, especially among technologists and radiologists, continue to pressure costs and limit imaging capacity; radiologist scarcity is extending report turnaround times industry-wide.
- Digital Health faced seasonal implementation softness, legacy iCAD migration to cloud subscriptions that reduces upfront revenue, and temporary implementation-capacity constraints.
- CMS's 2027 Medicare final rule is estimated to reduce revenue by ~$5 million; any congressional reversal of the rate reduction is uncertain.
- ONRAD closing timing and benefits, reimbursement, acquisitions, integration, and Digital Health sales remain subject to execution and other uncertainties.


### Significant events and developments

- The announced ONRAD acquisition is expected to establish Radiology Professional Services, adding teleradiology and hospital staffing across 225+ hospitals in 26 states.
- ONRAD is expected to contribute $190 million-$200 million of revenue and $60 million-$65 million of adjusted EBITDA in its first post-close year.
- Digital Health received FDA clearances for breast arterial calcification, breast ultrasound, and next-generation chest X-ray; its portfolio has 28 FDA clearances and 26 CE marks.
- Reporting Pro covered >30% of imaging exams; clinical AI coverage exceeded 50%, with >75% of studies targeted by Q3 2027 and full Reporting Pro deployment targeted by end-Q1 2027.
- The University of Maryland Medical System partnership expanded; 161 of 439 centers were in health-system partnerships at quarter-end.


### Capital allocation and financing

- ONRAD consideration includes $350 million cash and 5 million common shares at closing; an earnout pays 15x each dollar of 2027 EBITDA above $56 million, in cash or stock at election.
- Imaging Center CapEx guidance rose by $5 million at both ends to $170 million-$180 million, reflecting additional growth investments.
- Management plans continued organic investment, de novo openings, and tuck-in acquisitions; 10 new centers were expected to open in 2026 versus an original plan of seven.


### Market and industry conditions

- Demand for MRI, CT, and PET/CT is increasing with aging demographics, broader applications, and improved scanner throughput; management expects advanced-imaging growth to continue into 2027.
- Medicare accounts for ~25% of business mix; 2027's conversion factor fell 2.3% to $32.63, partly reflecting expiration of the temporary 2.5% 2026 increase.
- The 2027 OPPS final rule applies lower Physician Fee Schedule rates to certain non-contrast imaging in off-campus hospital departments, potentially encouraging health-system partnerships.
- Advanced PET/CT scanners can complete studies in under 15 minutes versus one hour previously; newer equipment and remote technologists help expand effective capacity.
