# Ginkgo Bioworks Holdings, Inc. — Q2 2026 (2026-08-05)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Focused investment in autonomous labs, with Nebula in Boston as a flagship system, aiming to shift the majority of work onto this platform and demonstrate its capabilities to potential buyers in biopharma, national labs, and research universities.
- Significant progress in reducing cash burn, with further improvements expected in the second half of 2026, supported by $302 million in cash and $87 million in restricted cash.
- Divestiture of Biosecurity segment completed; company now operates as a single segment focused on autonomous labs and related services.


### Financial highlights

- Q2 2026 revenue was $20 million, down 48% year-over-year; first six months revenue was $40 million, down 49% year-over-year.
- Excluding a $7.5 million non-cash item in 2025, revenue for the first six months of 2026 declined approximately 42%.
- R&D expense decreased 4% year-over-year to $30 million; G&A expense decreased 26% to $12 million, reflecting restructuring benefits.
- Net loss from continuing operations was $57 million in Q2 2026, compared to $53 million in Q2 2025.
- Adjusted EBITDA was negative $36 million in Q2 2026, versus negative $25 million in Q2 2025.
- Cash burn in Q2 2026 was $45 million, up from $38 million in Q2 2025; first six months cash burn was $93 million, a 3% decrease year-over-year.
- $14 million payment to Google Cloud in Q1 2026 increased cash burn but reduced future commitments by over $100 million and extended the term to six years.
- Raised $17 million through at-the-market equity program in Q2 2026.


### Outlook and guidance

- Reaffirmed full-year 2026 cash burn guidance of $125 million–$150 million, balancing cost efficiency with continued investment in AI, robotics, and software for autonomous labs.
- Focus for 2026 is on cost efficiency and strategic investment in autonomous lab capabilities and customer services.


### Segment performance

- All financial commentary and results now relate to continuing operations as a single segment after the Biosecurity divestiture.
- Automation deals, including new academic contracts, recognize revenue upon delivery and installation, with ongoing SaaS and support revenue post-deployment.
- Datapoints and solutions business recognize revenue over time, with projects typically spanning three to nine months.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted EBITDA includes $14 million in carrying costs for excess lease space in Q2 2026, up from $12 million in Q2 2025.
- Cash and cash equivalents at quarter-end totaled $302 million, with an additional $87 million in restricted cash.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Forward-looking statements involve risks and uncertainties; refer to SEC filings for details.
- Revenue recognition for large automation deals is dependent on delivery and installation timing, introducing variability in quarterly results.


### Significant events and developments

- Selected to build autonomous labs for MIT, Caltech, Maryland, and Northwestern, expanding reach into academic research and training.
- Launched ADME-One service, offering drug property assays at a fraction of traditional CRO costs, validated against industry standards.
- Expanded Nebula autonomous lab in Boston to 105 racks, now running 24/7 and handling high protocol variability.
- Divestiture of Biosecurity segment completed, with retrospective recasting of prior periods.


### Capital allocation and financing

- $17 million raised through at-the-market equity program in Q2 2026.
- $14 million payment to Google Cloud in Q1 2026 reduced future commitments by over $100 million and extended contract term.


### Market and industry conditions

- U.S. government and NSF investing heavily in autonomous lab infrastructure to maintain competitiveness in biotechnology versus China.
- Offshoring of CRO work to China remains a major industry trend; automation positioned as a cost-competitive alternative.
- Academic and government demand for autonomous labs is rising, with new grants and institutional funding supporting adoption.
