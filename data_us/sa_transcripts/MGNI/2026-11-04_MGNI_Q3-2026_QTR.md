# Magnite, Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-11-04)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 contribution ex-TAC was $201M, more than $10M above expectations; CTV and DV+ both exceeded expectations, lifting top-line and earnings results.
- Management raised FY26 contribution ex-TAC growth, adjusted EBITDA growth and margin, and free cash flow growth guidance; Q4 and 2027 outlooks also indicate continued momentum.


### Financial highlights

- Revenue was $204M, up 14% YoY; contribution ex-TAC was $201M, up 20% YoY.
- Net income was $34M, up 70% YoY; GAAP diluted EPS was $0.23 versus $0.13, and non-GAAP EPS was $0.33 versus $0.20.
- Total operating expenses, including cost of revenue, were $154M, flat YoY; adjusted EBITDA operating expenses were $117M.
- Operating cash flow, defined as adjusted EBITDA less CapEx, was $68M; CapEx was $15M.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q4 contribution ex-TAC is expected at $227M-$233M, representing 16%-19% growth; CTV guidance is $124M-$127M and DV+ is $103M-$106M.
- Q4 adjusted EBITDA operating expenses are expected at $125M-$127M, implying an adjusted EBITDA margin of 44%-46%.
- FY26 contribution ex-TAC growth guidance rose to ~17%; adjusted EBITDA growth to ~30%, margin to ~38.5%, and free cash flow growth to above 60%; CapEx remains ~ $60M.
- FY27 growth excluding political is expected above 15% and above 10% on a reported basis; adjusted EBITDA margin is expected to be at least 38.5%. Guidance excludes potential Google remedies benefits.
- Political contribution ex-TAC is now expected to exceed $40M in 2026, versus prior guidance of $15M; long-term CTV growth is projected at ~25%.


### Segment performance

- CTV contribution ex-TAC was $105M, up 39% YoY and above guidance of $98M-$100M; it represented 53% of Q3 contribution ex-TAC.
- DV+ contribution ex-TAC was $95M, up 5% YoY; mobile in-app grew 17% and represented ~35% of DV+.
- Political contributed ~$12M in Q3, including over three-quarters benefiting CTV; political represented ~9% of CTV and ~3% of DV+.
- Q3 contribution ex-TAC mix was 53% CTV, 33% mobile and 14% desktop; retail and financial services were the strongest non-political verticals.
- Sports is a significant and rapidly growing programmatic contributor; live sports inventory already sits on the platform, with management expecting meaningful contribution in 2027.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted EBITDA grew 46% YoY to $83M; margin was 42%, versus 34% in Q3 2025.
- Leverage was -0.2x at quarter-end, reflecting more cash than debt; cash was $393M, up from $333M at Q2-end.
- Streaming cost per billion ad requests fell over 50% YoY as capacity shifted on-prem; cloud costs remained roughly flat YoY.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Management cited macroeconomic conditions as a factor in future CTV growth; DV+ faces pressure from open-web search and referral traffic shifts toward answer engines.
- Political spending crowded out some other advertising in certain states; post-election recovery timing remains uncertain.
- International DV+ faces pricing and economic challenges in some markets, while streaming has held up relatively well.
- Google remedies implementation timing remains uncertain pending the judge’s final order; some technical work could take 6-18 months, based on industry and Google estimates.
- Agentic advertising adoption remains at an exploratory stage, with limited spend; management expects 2027 to remain a year of testing.


### Significant events and developments

- LG Ad Solutions signed a three-year extension and expansion, including exclusive SpringServe ad serving and agentic buying on the LG TV home screen; Telly and HP TV+ also expanded or selected partnerships.
- Pinterest selected Magnite as its primary SSP for global programmatic deals; OpenAI partnership work involves supplying demand to OpenAI advertising inventory.
- ClearLine carried ~20% of Q3 political ad spend; fees for the buying tool add incremental contribution ex-TAC.
- Google remedies could improve auction fairness, transparency and access to AdWords demand, but benefits are excluded from FY27 guidance.
- AI tools are accelerating product development and contributing to productivity gains across engineering, operations, sales and G&A.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Repriced $358M Term Loan B and $175M revolver; term loan margin fell 50 bps to Term SOFR +2.5%, generating ~$1.8M in annualized cash interest savings.
- Nearly 1.2M shares were repurchased or withheld for ~$29M in Q3; YTD activity was ~5.5M shares for ~$86M, with $145M remaining under authorization.
- FY26 CapEx guidance was reaffirmed at ~$60M, below the prior year.


### Market and industry conditions

- Programmatic is increasingly becoming the preferred way to transact on streaming TV; management expects CTV to grow ~25% over the long term, compared with industry growth generally in the low teens.
- Programmatic demand tends to gain importance when supply or macro conditions soften; CTV offers attractive price points that can draw spend from open-web video.
- International CTV is a healthy growth area, with streaming launches by Netflix, Paramount and Disney supporting expansion; international publishers rely heavily on programmatic.
- Display challenges are offset in DV+ by growth areas including mobile in-app, commerce media and audio; management views DV+ as roughly flat to modest, low-single-digit growth.
