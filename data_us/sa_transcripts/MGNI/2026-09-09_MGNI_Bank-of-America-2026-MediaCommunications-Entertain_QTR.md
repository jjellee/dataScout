# Magnite, Inc. — Bank of America 2026 Media,Communications & Entertainment Conference (2026-09-09)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Growth and market performance

- CTV advertising revenue grew 36% year-on-year in Q3, outpacing the industry’s mid-to-high teens growth rate, with top 10 advertisers growing 40% year-on-year.
- Long-term annualized growth target is set at 25% CAGR, with expectations to consistently outpace industry averages.
- Political advertising is expected to provide a boost, but growth targets are set as a CAGR, not a floor.
- Upselling to global clients and expanding into new markets are key drivers of growth.
- Market penetration is ongoing, with newer publishers starting at lower take rates that are expected to improve over time.


### Programmatic and product mix evolution

- Shift towards biddable programmatic is accelerating, with invite-only auctions becoming the norm for premium streaming inventory.
- Open auction programmatic is not seen as the ultimate goal for premium inventory, but broader adoption is expected for device manufacturers and mid-tier inventory.
- Product mix changes, such as publisher-sold programmatic, initially carry lower take rates but transition to higher-value services as comfort with programmatic grows.


### Margin profile and operational efficiency

- Margin profile has improved, with adjusted EBITDA exceeding 40% in strong quarters and no cap on future margin expansion.
- Cost structure remains stable due to judicious hiring and cloud-to-on-premise migration, enabling higher revenue without proportional cost increases.
- Investments in infrastructure are primarily for streaming, but also position the company to absorb increased bidding volume from potential market share gains.


### DOJ ruling and open web dynamics

- DOJ ruling is expected to introduce behavioral remedies, making auctions and ad servers more fair and efficient, benefiting non-dominant players.
- A 1% market share gain in open web could add $50 million in margin, with the company well-positioned to benefit due to strong publisher relationships.
- Open web growth is challenged by declining search referrals, but DV+ is diversified with mobile app, digital out-of-home, and audio segments.
- Market is expected to consolidate around premium publishers, favoring partners with deep, long-standing relationships.


### Innovation and future outlook

- Agentic technology is expected to reduce operational friction and improve targeting, potentially driving more ad dollars to the open web.
- Open sourcing auction data could lead to more efficient bidding and higher publisher yields.
- Mobile in-app advertising is a key growth area, with two-thirds of DV+ revenue from mobile and half of that from apps.
- Behavioral remedies from the DOJ are expected to be enacted quickly, with most changes being policy-driven or requiring minimal technical lift.
- Growth from DOJ-related changes is expected to play out over 12–24 months, not as a one-time event.
