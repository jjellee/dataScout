# TG Therapeutics, Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-11-09)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 global revenue reached ~$260M, including $233M in U.S. BRIUMVI net revenue; FY2026 global revenue guidance increased to ~$1B.
- Commercial momentum and development progress support continued growth, with IV initiation and self-administered subcutaneous BRIUMVI among key potential expansion drivers.


### Financial highlights

- Total revenue was ~$260M, up 61% YoY; net product revenue was $240M, including $233M U.S. BRIUMVI and $7.4M in ex-U.S. partner sales.
- License, milestone, royalty and other revenue totaled $19.9M, including a $10M sales milestone from the ex-U.S. partner.
- Operating income was $86M vs. $29M in Q3 2025; net income was $55.5M, or $0.35 per diluted share.
- R&D and SG&A, excluding stock-based compensation, totaled ~$107M, including ~$10M for subcutaneous and secondary-source manufacturing.


### Outlook and guidance

- FY2026 U.S. BRIUMVI net product revenue guidance rose to $915M-$920M; global revenue guidance is approximately $1B, including projected ex-U.S. partner payments.
- Q4 U.S. BRIUMVI revenue is expected to exceed $260M; FY2026 R&D and SG&A, excluding stock-based compensation, remain guided at $450M-$500M.
- ENHANCE single-dose IV initiation could receive a label update around mid-2027; commercial impact is expected to build in late 2027 and into 2028, subject to approval.
- Subcutaneous phase III results are expected around year-end 2026 or early 2027, with a potential 2028 launch; planned launch presentation is an auto-injector.


### Segment performance

- U.S. BRIUMVI net revenue was $233M, up 52% YoY and marking the 14th consecutive quarter of sequential growth.
- New patient starts accelerated late in Q3 and into Q4; October was a record month for total prescribers, and September and October were the strongest hospital months since launch.
- Treatment-naive patients were ~40% of new patient business vs. ~25% at launch; about one-third of starts continued to come from patients switching from another CD20 therapy.
- U.S. BRIUMVI gross margin was 87%; consolidated gross margin was ~84%, reflecting sales and revenue mix.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- U.S. BRIUMVI gross margin was 87%; consolidated gross margin was ~84%.
- U.S. BRIUMVI net revenue grew 52% YoY; total revenue grew 61% YoY.
- Operating income was $86M, compared with $29M in Q3 2025; diluted EPS was $0.35.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- 2027 growth and potential product launches depend on continued commercial momentum and regulatory approvals, including the ENHANCE label update and subcutaneous BRIUMVI review.
- Gross-to-net may face pressure from contracting, payer mix and government discounts; price increases are used to help offset discounts and stabilize net price.
- 340B policy changes were expected to have minimal impact based on current estimates; Medicare patients at 340B hospitals were ~5% of business.


### Significant events and developments

- Seven-year ULTIMATE follow-up showed an annualized relapse rate of 0.018 and ~88% freedom from 24-week confirmed disability progression, with no new safety signals.
- ENHANCE met its primary endpoint for bioequivalence of single-dose versus standard initiation; day-one infusion reactions were 16% vs. ~26%, with no grade 3+ reactions in either arm.
- Subcutaneous phase I data showed ~62% bioavailability; all patients on the 400 mg regimen achieved B-cell depletion within four days, with only grade 1 injection reactions.
- In a phase I progressive MS cohort of 26 patients, no dose-limiting toxicities or specified severe safety events were observed; nearly 90% of evaluable patients had stable or improved EDSS at day 90.
- Phase II programs are evaluating BRIUMVI in generalized myasthenia gravis and treatment-resistant schizophrenia.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Cash equivalents and investment securities totaled ~$557M at September-end.
- Q3 repurchases totaled $100M, or ~2M shares at an average ~$49 per share; cumulative repurchases were $300M, or ~8.9M shares, at an average below $34.
- Capital allocation balances business investment with shareholder returns.


### Notes to the financial statements

- Q3 2025 reported earnings included a substantial non-cash income tax benefit from releasing the deferred tax valuation allowance, affecting the YoY net income comparison.


### Market and industry conditions

- Management estimates ~35% of new anti-CD20 starts choose self-administration; quarterly subcutaneous BRIUMVI is viewed as a multi-billion-dollar opportunity and potentially faster uptake than the IV franchise.
- Management views BTK inhibitors as competing mainly in the oral category, rather than the high-efficacy CD20 space, citing concerns about efficacy and twice-daily dosing.
- Physician experience, clinical and real-world evidence, expanding prescriber reach and patient awareness were cited as supporting adoption in an expanding anti-CD20 market.

Turning to ENHANCE, this phase IIIb study initially focused on evaluating switches to BRIUMVI from prior anti-CD20 therapy, and has evolved over time to also explore alternate dosing regimens for BRIUMVI, yielding a number of valuable datasets. Together, these findings support the potential to simplify BRIUMVI initiation without compromising drug exposure or the observed safety profile. We are advancing the regulatory process and, subject to approval, anticipate a potential update to our label to incorporate this new regimen around the middle of 2027. Interestingly, this switch cohort also contained a number of patients who experienced incomplete B-cell depletion or early repletion on ocrelizumab, yet were able to obtain robust B-cell depletion with BRIUMVI. We believe these findings further support the potential for meaningful biological differences among anti-CD20 therapies, and we intend to continue investigating these observations and their potential clinical significance. On that note, it is worth noting a recent publication in the journal Multiple Sclerosis and Related Disorders, which was a five-year comparative analysis of ublituximab against both ocrelizumab and ofatumumab.
