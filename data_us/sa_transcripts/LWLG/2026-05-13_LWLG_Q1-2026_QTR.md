# Lightwave Logic, Inc. — Q1 2026 (2026-05-13)

### Executive summary

- AI-driven infrastructure growth is accelerating demand for high-speed, efficient data movement, positioning photonics as a critical technology for next-gen networking architectures.
- Electro-optic polymer platform is designed to address challenges in AI, data center, and telecom markets, with expanding market opportunities and customer engagement.
- Four major Fortune 500 customers are at the prototyping stage, with additional Tier 1 customers expected to advance in 2026.


### Financial highlights

- Q1 2026 revenue was $29,000, a 27% year-over-year increase.
- Net loss for the quarter was $6.3 million, or $0.04 per share, compared to $4.7 million in the prior year; loss per share remained flat.
- R&D investments rose to $3.5 million, supporting technical and commercial progress.
- Ended Q1 with $75 million in cash and equivalents; cash on hand as of May 11, 2026, is approximately $100 million.


### Outlook and guidance

- High-volume production with a lead customer is anticipated to begin in 2027.
- Foundry supply constraints are expected to ease over the next 12 months as new investments and players enter the market.
- The optical transceiver market could exceed $70 billion by 2030, with silicon photonics projected to surpass 70% market share.


### Segment performance

- Serviceable addressable market for electro-optic polymer modulators increased to $2–$4 billion.

### Capital allocation and financing
