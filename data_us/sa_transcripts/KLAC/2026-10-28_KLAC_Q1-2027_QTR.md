# KLA Corporation — Q1 2027 (2026-10-28)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- September-quarter revenue set a record at $4.14 billion, beating the midpoint of guidance across key metrics; December-quarter revenue guidance of $4.7 billion signals accelerating H2 2026 growth.
- AI infrastructure investment, leading-edge process-control intensity and advanced packaging supported performance; growing backlog and sales visibility underpin confidence in significant 2027 growth.


### Financial highlights

- Revenue was $4.14 billion, up 13% sequentially and 29% YoY; non-GAAP diluted EPS was $1.22 and GAAP diluted EPS was $1.18, both above guidance midpoints.
- Non-GAAP net income was $1.60 billion; GAAP net income was $1.55 billion. Gross margin was 62.7%, and operating margin reached a record 46.1%.
- Cash flow from operations was $1.53 billion; free cash flow was $1.34 billion. Operating expenses were $688 million, including $395 million R&D and $293 million SG&A.


### Outlook and guidance

- December-quarter revenue guidance is $4.7 billion ±$200 million; non-GAAP diluted EPS $1.44 ±$0.11 and GAAP diluted EPS $1.41 ±$0.11.
- December-quarter gross margin guidance is 62.5% ±1 percentage point; operating expenses are expected at ~$710 million. Planning tax rate is 14.5%.
- H2 2026 revenue is expected to rise ~25% over H1; sequential growth is expected for the next several quarters, with a more linear 2027 pattern than the H2 2026 step-up.
- Wafer equipment market outlook is mid-$150 billion range in 2026 and above $200 billion in 2027; significant growth is expected through 2027.


### Segment performance

- Services revenue was $850 million, up 14% YoY, supported by a larger installed base and high customer focus on tool performance, productivity and availability.
- Advanced-packaging process-control systems revenue is on track for ~$1.1 billion or more in 2026, up more than 70% YoY; growth is expected to outpace the market in 2027.
- December-quarter semiconductor process-control revenue mix to semiconductor customers is forecast at ~60% foundry/logic and ~40% memory; memory mix is ~84% DRAM and 16% NAND.
- China is expected at 27%-29% of revenue in Q4 2026 and the low-20% range in 2027; China growth is expected below wafer equipment growth outside China.
- Non-semi process-control businesses, including PCB and component inspection, benefited from technology transitions and high-performance computing requirements.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Gross margin was 62.7% and operating margin 46.1%, up 240 bps sequentially; operating margin exceeded 46% for the first time.
- Long-run model targets 40%-50% incremental operating-margin leverage on revenue growth; unusually high growth can drive leverage above that range.
- Diluted weighted-average shares were 1.311 billion in the quarter; December-quarter EPS guidance assumes ~1.306 billion fully diluted shares.
- Cash and marketable securities totaled $4.7 billion; debt was $5.95 billion. Debt maturities were described as flexible, with investment-grade ratings from all three major agencies.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Demand exceeded available supply during 2026; average lead times were ~12 months, with some products taking longer, while long-lead optical components remain critical to shipments.
- Fab readiness, cleanroom construction, power availability and raw-material access can delay customer installations even when equipment is available.
- High memory costs pressure margins; memory purchasing costs rose as demand strengthened, with improvement dependent on system memory-efficiency work and future pricing.


### Significant events and developments

- Backlog and sales-funnel visibility increased, with new fab and greenfield projects broadening the customer investment base and supporting 2027 confidence.
- Advanced packaging is shifting toward more front-end-like processes and higher-sensitivity tools, including laser scanning and some Gen4 deployments for packaging applications.
- DRAM process-control intensity is rising with HBM requirements, greenfield fabs and increased yield value; some DRAM products now show intensity comparable to leading-edge logic.
- Leading-edge investment is expected to broaden to 2nm and 1.4nm in 2027, alongside 3nm spending; high-performance-computing designs increase inspection and sampling needs.


### Capital allocation and financing

- A significant India land purchase for a new growth-supporting campus drove a large portion of quarterly CapEx; future annual CapEx is expected at $600 million-$800 million, with investment across sites and capacity.
- CapEx is expected to run at 2%-3% of revenue; hiring, factory expansion and supplier investments are planned to support multi-year demand, including manufacturing, service, applications and engineering.
- Capital allocation remains focused on disciplined execution, technology leadership and durable shareholder value; the debt profile is supported by investment-grade ratings.


### Market and industry conditions

- AI data-center investment and broader semiconductor applications are strengthening industry demand; growing device complexity and advanced packaging expand process-control needs.
- Customer plans span leading-edge foundry/logic, DRAM and HBM, advanced packaging and new fabs; supply constraints and facility readiness may extend the industry growth cycle.
- The prior 2030 wafer-equipment view of $220 billion ±$20 billion was characterized as conservative amid stronger industry investment scenarios; supplier planning is addressing potential 2029-2030 needs.
