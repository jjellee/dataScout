# The Manitowoc Company, Inc. — Q2 2026 (2026-08-07)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Achieved 10% year-over-year sales growth and over 85% increase in adjusted EBITDA, marking one of the strongest quarters in recent years.
- Orders exceeded $700 million, with backlog rising to $1.05 billion, up $321 million year-over-year.
- Non-new machine sales set a record, growing 7% year-over-year and surpassing $700 million on a trailing 12-month basis.
- Integration of AI into operations and aftermarket business accelerated, with over 450 users and new AI-driven initiatives.
- U.S. Department of Commerce imposed tariffs on Japanese crawler cranes, benefiting competitive positioning.


### Financial highlights

- Q2 net sales reached $595 million, up $55 million or 10% from a year ago.
- Adjusted EBITDA nearly doubled to $49 million, with EBITDA margin expanding 330 basis points to over 8%.
- Net working capital ended at $567 million, improving 280 basis points year-over-year as a percent of trailing 12-month sales.
- Free cash flow was a use of $6 million, a $68 million improvement from the prior year.
- Ended the quarter with $96 million in cash and total liquidity of $304 million.


### Outlook and guidance

- Full-year net sales expected at $2.3–$2.4 billion, adjusted EBITDA at $150–$170 million, adjusted EPS at $0.80–$1.20, and free cash flow at $50–$70 million.
- Guidance incorporates $16 million net benefit from tariff refunds and considers risks from the Iran conflict.
- Strong demand from data center and semiconductor investments expected to continue into 2027.


### Segment performance

- Americas showed strong order growth, with high crane utilization and lean dealer inventories.
- Europe experienced mixed conditions; German stimulus and inflationary pressures from the Iran conflict noted.
- Mobile crane orders grew, while tower crane orders declined due to transition to new EN standards.
- Middle East demand remained solid despite regional instability; Asia saw robust demand in South Korea, Vietnam, and Australia.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Net leverage ratio improved to 2.6x, below the 3x target.
- Book-to-bill ratio was 1.2, supporting backlog growth.
- SG&A expenses were $88 million (adjusted), or 15% of net sales, down 130 basis points year-over-year.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Ongoing geopolitical risks, particularly the Iran conflict, could impact economic activity and customer investment.
- Seasonality expected to affect Q3 results due to European holidays.


### Significant events and developments

- Received $26 million in IEEPA tariff refunds, with $12 million recognized in operating income and $4 million expected in Q3.
- Opened rapid response shop and center of excellence for aftermarket services at Shady Grove campus.
- Awarded a three-year, $2.5 million service contract at a major copper zinc mine in Peru.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Focused on maintaining net leverage below 3x, enabling opportunistic share repurchases and acquisitions.
- Capital expenditures were $14 million in Q2, including $9 million for rental fleet.


### Market and industry conditions

- Global crane market remains strong, with high utilization and positive customer sentiment.
- No significant contribution yet from oil and gas or mining sectors despite higher commodity prices.
- Data center and semiconductor sectors driving demand outlook.
