# SiTime Corporation — Q3 2026 (2026-11-04)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 was the first full quarter including TPD; combined revenue reached $298.7M, up 257% YoY, with 70.7% gross margin and 43% operating margin.
- Combined results exceeded a $1B annualized revenue run rate; management cited stronger scale, customer engagement, and growth opportunities across business units.


### Financial highlights

- Revenue was $298.7M, up 257% YoY and 90% QoQ; non-GAAP net income rose over 5X to $130.3M and non-GAAP EPS was $4.01.
- Gross margin was 70.7%, up 11.9 percentage points YoY and 3.6 points QoQ; favorable product mix and manufacturing absorption drove improvement, not price increases.
- Operating income was $129.5M, or 43% of revenue, versus 34% in Q2 and 18% a year ago; operating expenses were $81.6M, including $48.6M R&D and $33M SG&A.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q4 revenue guidance is $331M-$341M, with core operations and TPD both expected to grow sequentially; gross margin expected at ~70%, plus or minus one point.
- Q4 OpEx is expected roughly flat with Q3; interest income of $2M-$2.5M, diluted share count of ~32.8M, and midpoint non-GAAP EPS of ~$4.76.
- Visibility and backlog into 2027 are strong; management expects sequential CED growth, while noting quarterly timing shifts can affect reported growth.
- OpEx is expected to rise sequentially through Q1 and Q2 2027 as infrastructure, sales, and growth investments expand.


### Segment performance

- Core revenue excluding TPD was $212.4M, up 154% YoY; CED generated $127.3M, up 202% YoY and 26% QoQ.
- Automotive, industrial, and aerospace defense revenue was $35.5M, up 76% YoY and 43% QoQ; mobile IoT and consumer revenue was $49.5M, up 133% YoY and 58% QoQ.
- TPD revenue was $86.3M, up 67% YoY; its mix is over 80% CED, with roughly 15%-20% industrial and automotive.
- Mobile IoT and consumer growth included $42.7M from a large consumer customer; new AI smart-glasses and pendant designs are expected to ramp in 2027.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Non-GAAP gross margin was 70.7%; operating margin was 43%, up 25 percentage points YoY; EPS was $4.01, up 360% YoY.
- DSO was 61 days, up 10 days QoQ; inventory was $112M, and Q3 operating cash use was $6.6M.
- Cash and short-term investments totaled $324M at quarter-end.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Wafer supply constraints are expected to continue through 2027; TPD constraints were more acute amid demand surges, with more severe issues expected to ease exiting Q4 and into Q1-Q2 2027.
- TPD-related collection timing raised DSO and contributed to Q3 operating cash use; collections arrived in early Q4, and working capital and cash flow are expected to improve.
- CED quarterly revenue may shift with large-customer shipment timing, making seasonality difficult to predict; 2027 is expected to resemble 2026 overall.


### Significant events and developments

- TPD acquisition closed July 1; Q3 was the first full quarter of consolidated results, and integration across teams, customers, and roadmap was described as progressing well.
- A 3-for-1 stock split was announced; holders of record Dec. 3, 2026 receive two additional shares per share, with split-adjusted trading beginning Dec. 7.
- The combined portfolio is generating cross-selling in both directions, and 2027 forecasts at largest accounts increased two to five times, primarily reflecting design-win opportunities.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Q3 capital expenditures were $11.2M; continued R&D, product development, engineering capacity, and go-to-market investment support growth.
- Quarter-end cash and short-term investments of $324M provide liquidity and flexibility for continued investment.


### Market and industry conditions

- Optical transitions create opportunities for higher-value timing: XPO doubles oscillator content, NPO adds up to 2X oscillator content, and CPO adds up to 3X timing content.
- The total design funnel reached $10B, quadrupling since 2025; semiconductor-related funnel grew eight times to more than $4B, with most expected to ramp in 2028-2029.
- Aerospace and defense funnel reached $900M; automotive approached $1B and industrial reached $1.4B, supported by autonomy, robotics, smart-grid, radar, and resilient-positioning needs.
- Higher-speed optical modules, including 1.6 Tb, offer market-share and ASP uplift; management cited stronger timing content as bandwidth and precision requirements rise.
