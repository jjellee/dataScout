# Wayfair Inc. — J.P. Morgan 54th Annual Global Technology, Media and Communications Conference (2026-05-18)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Share gain drivers and strategic initiatives

- Share gains accelerated in late 2024 and are expected to continue through 2025, driven by a return to core strengths and new initiatives.
- Loyalty program launched in October 2024 is increasing purchase frequency and expanding spend in higher-frequency categories.
- Wayfair Verified, launched summer 2024, helps customers navigate the large catalog by highlighting top products.
- Storefront experience improved after replatforming (2021–2024), enabling faster site changes and enhanced delivery options like Delivery Plus.
- Ongoing improvements in delivery and site experience, combined with loyalty and product curation, are key to continued share gains.


### Loyalty program performance and customer behavior

- Loyalty members are making more frequent purchases, with most buying three or more times a year.
- Renewal rates for the loyalty program are strong, indicating sustained engagement beyond initial project-based signups.
- Loyalty incentives include free shipping on smaller orders and exclusive promotions in targeted categories.


### Financial strategy and margin outlook

- Targeting over 10% Adjusted EBITDA margin, up from break-even in 2023 and over 6% currently.
- Investments in loyalty and marketing are designed to optimize gross profit and contribution margin, supporting EBITDA growth.
- Marketing spend is held at 15% to balance contribution margin and EBITDA objectives.


### Market environment and category trends

- The category has been flat or declining, with no recovery assumed in 2026 guidance.
- Category is well below historical growth trends, with missed sales compared to pre-pandemic projections.
- Weather, energy prices, and shifting consumer priorities have impacted demand, but share gains are expected to offset category weakness.


### Tariffs, pricing, and marketplace dynamics

- Suppliers have maintained prices despite tariff pressures; any tariff refunds would benefit wholesalers, not directly impacting retail pricing.
- Revenue growth is balanced between average order value and order volume, with normalized AOV growth at about 3%.
- Marketplace model provides agility in responding to supply chain and pricing complexities.


### Universal Commerce Protocol and AI-driven commerce

- Early partner with Google on Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP) for agentic shopping, aiming to be present across major AI platforms.
- UCP transactions occur on the retailer's site, ensuring retention of first-party customer data.
- AI and LLM partnerships are expected to enhance product discovery and personalization, both on and off the platform.


### Advertising and media mix strategy

- Increased ad spend in late 2024 and early 2025 to test new channels and optimize media mix.
- Large holdout tests in 2025 led to temporary reductions in ad spend, with ongoing improvements in media efficiency.


### Omni-channel and store expansion

- Specialty retail brand stores (Joss & Main, AllModern, Birch Lane, Perigold) serve as learning platforms for larger Wayfair stores.
- Large-format Wayfair stores (up to 150,000 sq ft) are opening in major markets, with ongoing tests to determine optimal size and location.
- Stores generate both direct and halo sales, with geo-location used to track post-visit purchase behavior.
- Store rollout is coordinated with fulfillment network (CastleGate) to ensure efficient delivery.


### Delivery, logistics, and fuel price management

- Delivery uses both FedEx and proprietary home delivery, with fuel surcharges passed through to customers to protect margins.
- Inventory is strategically positioned to minimize shipping costs and exposure to fuel price volatility.
