# National Energy Services Reunited Corp. — Lytham Partners Spring 2026 Investor Conference (2026-05-28)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Company overview and market positioning

- Operates in 16 countries across the Middle East and North Africa with over 20 product and service lines.
- Only publicly listed pure-play Middle East oilfield services company, listed on Nasdaq.
- Focuses on local hiring and investment, positioning as a national champion in each country.
- Diversified business with production services (2/3 of revenue) and drilling/evaluation (1/3).
- Outperformed peers during downturns, including COVID and recent regional conflicts.


### Growth, financial performance, and shareholder returns

- Revenue grew from $450 million in 2018 to $1.34 billion in 2025, with visibility to $2 billion run rate soon.
- Targets $3 billion revenue within 2–3 years, depending on project timing.
- Healthy free cash flow and margins, with compounding growth and strong balance sheet.
- Announced inaugural shareholder return program: $50 million buyback and $0.40/share annual dividend (1.6% yield), starting Q4.
- Anticipates 40% growth in 2026 and 25% in 2027, maintaining outperformance.


### Market dynamics and strategic advantages

- Middle East market offers lowest-cost oil and gas, strong value chain, and multi-year contracts (average five years).
- Contracts range from three to nine years, providing cost optimization and durable cash flow.
- Growth supported by large-scale projects like Saudi Arabia’s Jafurah unconventional gas field.
- Won the largest oilfield services tender globally, securing the entire committed scope for Jafurah.
- Positioned to benefit from AI investment trends and regional geopolitical realignment.


### Innovation, technology, and future opportunities

- Uses a venture capital arm and open technology platform to source and adapt new technologies.
- Flexible approach appeals to national oil companies seeking tailored solutions.
- Largest frack player in the region due to Jafurah contract; expects similar opportunities in UAE and North Africa.
- Pursuing a $3 billion tender pipeline, with recent $300 million cementing award as a catalyst.
- Anticipates Western IOCs entering North Africa, unlocking further growth potential.


### Financial resilience and outlook

- Margins remain stable at 20%-25%, even during regional conflicts.
- Q1 performance exceeded peers despite geopolitical disruptions.
- Sub-5x EBITDA projected for 2027, maintaining attractive valuation.
- Growth runway remains significant due to small relative size and expanding market share.
- Free cash flow supports both reinvestment and sustainable, growing dividends.
