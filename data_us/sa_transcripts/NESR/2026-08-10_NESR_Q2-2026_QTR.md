# National Energy Services Reunited Corp. — Q2 2026 (2026-08-10)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Achieved record Q2 2026 revenue of $520.8 million, up 28.7% sequentially and 59.1% year-over-year, driven by strong performance in Saudi Arabia, Oman, and Egypt, with Jafurah contract as a key contributor.
- Surpassed $2 billion revenue run rate target ahead of schedule, setting new, more ambitious growth objectives.
- Maintained uninterrupted operations and supply chain reliability during regional geopolitical disruptions, leveraging a diversified and proactive logistics strategy.
- Continued execution of the 3B3 strategy, targeting a $3 billion revenue run rate within three years, with progress in contract wins, geographic expansion, and technology innovation.


### Financial highlights

- Adjusted EBITDA reached $106.2 million (20.4% margin), with margin expansion despite $4 million in incremental freight/logistics costs due to regional disruptions.
- Adjusted net income was $45.5 million, up 70.1% sequentially and 125.9% year-over-year; adjusted diluted EPS hit $0.44.
- Operating cash flow rose to $174 million, with free cash flow at $99.9 million, reflecting strong working capital execution and proactive inventory management.
- Gross debt reduced to $274.6 million, net debt to $99.6 million, and net debt/adjusted EBITDA at 0.3x.
- Trailing 12-month return on capital employed increased to 13.5%.


### Outlook and guidance

- 2026 revenue of at least $2 billion now seen as a minimum, with full-year adjusted EBITDA margins expected to remain in line with 2025 levels.
- Full-year capital expenditures guided to $210–$215 million, supporting growth and new contract execution.
- Free cash flow conversion expected at 35–40% of adjusted EBITDA; net income margins projected at 9–9.5%.
- Third quarter expected to see continued strong revenue growth, sequential margin improvement, and stable cash flow.


### Segment performance

- Saudi Arabia led sequential growth, driven by the ramp-up of the Jafurah contract and strong conventional operations.
- Oman and Egypt delivered solid growth; Iraq activity remained subdued due to regional disruptions.
- North Africa and Kuwait contributed to year-over-year growth, with new contract awards and technology agreements.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted EBITDA margin: 20.4% for Q2 2026.
- Net debt/adjusted EBITDA: 0.3x at quarter-end.
- Return on capital employed: 13.5% (trailing 12 months).
- Effective tax rate: ~24% expected for 2026.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Regional geopolitical disruptions increased freight and logistics costs and impacted activity in Iraq.
- Continued uncertainty in the Middle East could affect supply chain and project execution, though proactive contingency planning is in place.


### Significant events and developments

- Announced transition of auditors from Grant Thornton Dubai to PricewaterhouseCoopers Dubai for the 2027 audit, following a competitive tender process.
- Initiation of a quarterly dividend ($0.10/share) starting Q4 2026 and continuation of a $50 million share repurchase program.
- Launch of the Ahmadi Innovation Valley contract in Kuwait, entering a long-term master technology agreement.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Capital allocation framework prioritizes high-return growth investments, balance sheet strength, and shareholder returns.
- Targeting net leverage at or below 1x adjusted EBITDA, with a zero net debt position possible within two years.
- Dividend and share repurchase program reflect confidence in cash flow durability.


### Market and industry conditions

- Middle East and North Africa remain resilient and attractive for energy services, with post-conflict restart opportunities and robust tender pipelines.
- Regional clients maintaining activity levels and preparing for rapid production ramp-up post-conflict.
- North Africa poised for accelerated growth as new contracts are signed and export capacity is built.
