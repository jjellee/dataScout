# Forgent Power Solutions, Inc. — Q1 2027 (2026-11-12)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- FY 2027 Q1 revenue reached a record $479 million, up 69% YoY; Adjusted EBITDA rose 61% to $105 million and Adjusted EPS rose 83% to $0.24.
- Results exceeded the high end of guidance on production-ramp execution; FY 2027 guidance was raised, with backlog fully covering the updated outlook.
- Orders were about 2x Q1 FY 2026 levels; Data Center and Industrial led growth, while Grid moderated.


### Financial highlights

- Revenue was $479 million, up 69% YoY, all organic and about $24 million above guidance midpoint; higher throughput and ramp execution drove the upside.
- Custom products grew 73% to $354 million; Powertrain solutions grew 62% to $106 million; Standard products grew 18% to $11 million; Services more than doubled to $8 million.
- Gross profit increased 62% on volume growth; SG&A rose in absolute dollars from investment in sales, operations and engineering.


### Outlook and guidance

- FY 2027 revenue guidance raised by $50 million to $2.45-$2.65 billion, implying 80% growth at midpoint.
- FY 2027 Adjusted EBITDA guidance raised by $12 million to $587-$637 million, implying 90% growth and 24% Adjusted EBITDA margin; Adjusted EPS guidance raised $0.04 to $1.30-$1.44.
- Q2 FY 2027 revenue guided to $560-$590 million; Adjusted EBITDA to $127-$137 million, with ~23% margin.
- Revenue and Adjusted EBITDA expected to rise sequentially through FY 2027, with margin expansion as production ramps; backlog fully covers updated FY 2027 guidance.


### Segment performance

- Data Center was the largest contributor to YoY order growth; demand signals remained strong, with backlog orders generally following key project milestones.
- Industrial orders were strong and demand broadened to smaller projects, facility expansions and follow-on investment across more customers and verticals.
- Grid utility demand remained steady; renewables and storage softened due to safe-harbor order pull-forwards and policy uncertainty, viewed primarily as timing-related.
- Neoclouds and their intermediaries represented ~15% of backlog; no single customer exceeded 10% of Q1 revenue.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted EBITDA margin was 21.9% in Q1, ahead of expectations; Q1 was expected to be the year's lowest-margin quarter.
- Adjusted EPS was $0.24, up 83% YoY, outpacing Adjusted EBITDA growth due to operating leverage and lower net interest expense.
- Q2 guidance implies Adjusted EBITDA margin of ~23%, up ~260 bps YoY and ~110 bps sequentially.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Policy and permitting changes may affect data-center project locations and timing; management said customer discussions showed no change in expected capacity demand.
- Electrical equipment supply remains constrained, but Grid renewables and storage faced near-term pauses tied to policy clarity and timing.
- Direct hyperscaler and AI-lab sales require lengthy technical, operational, procurement and legal qualification; progression can take quarters and may not succeed with every prospect.


### Significant events and developments

- Direct customer traction advanced: an AI lab's initial order led to a volume order, a second AI lab placed its first order, and two hyperscalers signed product agreements.
- A third hyperscaler entered qualification; direct AI-lab orders were expected in H1 FY 2027 and hyperscaler orders in H2.
- Focus is shifting from platform creation to performance optimization through process standardization, sourcing, capacity utilization and cycle-time improvements.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Free cash flow expectations remained above $300 million; management cautioned that deposit timing makes Q1 cash flow an unsuitable annual run-rate.
- Capital allocation priorities remained organic growth, inorganic growth, then share buybacks; cash generation provides additional flexibility.
- Supplier payment-term changes generated more than $30 million of working-capital improvement; terms were renegotiated with vendors representing over 80% of materials spend.


### Notes to the financial statements

- Q1 deferred revenue growth reflected cash received, including deposits tied to strong Q4 orders, plus milestone payments; no material change to down-payment policies.
- Q1 Adjusted EBITDA included one-time production-ramp costs, primarily under-absorbed labor, which were not added back.


### Market and industry conditions

- Industry forecasts cited more than 100 GW of incremental U.S. data-center capacity from 2027-2031; electrical-equipment demand materially exceeds available industry capacity.
- Upstream power-generation suppliers have multi-year backlogs and committed capital, supporting expectations for downstream transformers, switchgear and distribution equipment demand.
- Management cited potential longer-term Grid tailwinds from alternative energy and domestic-manufacturing policies, with impacts expected over quarters rather than weeks or months.
