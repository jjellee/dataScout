# NXP Semiconductors N.V. — Q3 2026 (2026-10-27)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 revenue was $3.79B, up 19% YoY; non-GAAP operating margin reached 37.1% and non-GAAP EPS was $4.20, both above prior-year levels.
- Growth was broad-based across regions, led by automotive, industrial IoT, and data-center infrastructure; Q3 exceeded outlook across all end markets.
- Structural growth drivers include software-defined vehicles (SDVs), industrial physical AI, and data-center control-plane products; management expects double-digit revenue growth in 2027.


### Financial highlights

- Revenue was $3.79B, up 19% YoY and 8% sequentially; excluding the MEMS sensor business, revenue grew 22% YoY.
- Non-GAAP gross margin was 58.6%, up 160 bps YoY; non-GAAP operating expenses were $810M, or 21.4% of revenue.
- Non-GAAP operating margin was 37.1%, up 330 bps YoY; non-GAAP EPS was $4.20, up 35% YoY.
- Operating cash flow was $1.26B; net CapEx was $50M and free cash flow was $1.21B, approximately 32% of revenue.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q4 revenue guidance is $3.85B ±$100M, up 15% YoY or 18% excluding MEMS sensors, and up 2% sequentially; fourth consecutive quarter of double-digit annual growth expected.
- Q4 non-GAAP gross margin is expected at 59% ±50 bps; operating expenses $830M ±$10M and midpoint operating margin 37.5%.
- Q4 non-GAAP EPS midpoint is $4.36, up 30% YoY; non-GAAP tax rate expected at 17.5%.
- 2027 revenue is expected to grow at a double-digit rate, with gross margin reaching 60%; operating expenses expected near 23% of revenue in H1 and below 23% in H2.
- 2027 CapEx is expected at ~5% of revenue; full-year joint-venture investee losses are expected near $80M, with an annual effective tax rate of 17.5%.


### Segment performance

- Automotive revenue exceeded $2B for the first time, up 17% excluding sensors; growth reflected SDV content gains amid flattish vehicle production.
- Industrial IoT revenue was $802M, up 39% YoY; its industrial portion grew nearly 50%, led by data center, factory automation, and healthcare.
- Communication infrastructure and other revenue was $503M, up 54% YoY, driven by data center and secure cards; mobile revenue was $411M, down 4% YoY.
- Q4 automotive is expected to grow low double digits YoY and low single digits sequentially; industrial and IoT high 20s YoY, mobile down mid-teens YoY, and communications infrastructure and other up over 50% YoY.
- Data-center revenue is expected to exceed the earlier $500M 2026 outlook and reach over $1B in 2028; industrial AI-enabled processors are expected to be ~15% of industrial IoT processor revenue in 2026.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Cash was $3.94B and debt $10.98B at Q3-end; net leverage was 1.3x.
- Inventory declined to 148 days from 156 days in Q2, including ~13 days of pre-built inventory for planned front-end factory consolidations; ~15 days expected at 2026 year-end.
- Trailing 12-month free cash flow was ~$3.5B, or 25% of revenue; $1.7B was returned through dividends and repurchases over the same period.
- Q3 non-GAAP interest expense was $85M, tax rate 18%, non-controlling interest $15M, and joint-venture equity-accounted investee loss $4M.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Automotive supply is tight: 80% of lead times exceeded 16 weeks versus 25% a year earlier; lead times extended, backlog grew into 2027, and customer escalations rose materially QoQ.
- No broad-based automotive restocking was observed; some OEMs discussed safety stock amid supply tightness, while visibility and direct customer guidance improved.
- Back-end capacity is very tight, and front-end capacity may tighten next year; management said possible supply-demand-driven pricing effects remain uncertain.
- 2027 pricing faces higher input costs; price increases are intended to offset inflation, while competitive pressures remain and historical price erosion is expected to moderate over time.


### Significant events and developments

- Volkswagen-Rivian joint venture RV Tech standardized its zonal platform on S32K5; first China shipments are expected as early as Q1 2027.
- First S32K5 design win in humanoid robotics was secured with a leading U.S. global technology company; Ara discrete NPU is gaining traction and is expected in volume production in 2028.
- S32K5 zonal and S32N central-compute processors begin shipping in 2027; full-volume ramps for both platforms are expected in 2028.
- End-market revenue categories will be realigned beginning in 2027; Investor Day is scheduled for March 3 in Boston.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Board authorized an additional $5B of share repurchases, lifting total authorization to $6.3B, around 10% of current market capitalization; buybacks expected to accelerate in Q4 and 2027.
- Long-term policy remains to return 100% of excess free cash flow to shareholders; recent returns were below target while prioritizing deleveraging, strategic acquisitions, and manufacturing JV investments.
- Q3 investments in VSMC and ESMC totaled $218M; JV investments are expected to be over 75% complete by year-end.
- Q4 CapEx expected at ~3% of revenue, with equity payments of $86M to VSMC and $60M to ESMC.


### Market and industry conditions

- Vehicle production was flattish while SDV-led content gains advanced; SDV content per vehicle could be 2x–3x current architecture levels.
- xEV penetration is forecast at ~47.5% of production this year versus 41% last year, rising to ~55% next year; xEVs carry 2x–3x the semiconductor content of ICE vehicles.
- Management sees structural demand from SDVs, industrial physical AI, and data-center investment; these transformations may support greater semiconductor value capture and pricing power.
- Q3 pricing was about +1%, with another +1% expected in Q4, leaving 2026 pricing neutral; 2027 product mix, including higher-value SDV solutions, is expected to support revenue.
