# Arrowhead Pharmaceuticals, Inc. — H.C. Wainwright 28th Annual Global Investment Conference (2026-09-16)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Platform and pipeline overview

- Developed a broad RNAi platform targeting multiple cell types, with five represented in clinical candidates.
- Transitioned to a commercial stage with 21–22 drug candidates in clinical studies.
- Plans to access a new cell type every 18–24 months, expanding beyond liver to CNS and other tissues.
- Focused on building a broad-based company addressing diverse indications.


### REDEMPLO (plozasiran) launch and expansion

- REDEMPLO approved for FCS, targeting both genetic and clinically defined FCS populations.
- Contracting with payers established; sales force expanded to address larger clinical FCS population.
- Preparing for SHTG launch in Q2 2027, with further sales force growth planned in early 2027.


### SHASTA-3 and SHASTA-4 trial results

- Achieved ~80% reduction in triglycerides in severe hypertriglyceridemia patients.
- Statistically significant reduction in acute pancreatitis risk, with 78–100% risk reduction in high-risk subgroups.
- Clean safety profile: no significant liver, hypersensitivity, or thrombocytopenia issues; no increase in liver fat.


### Market opportunity and differentiation

- SHTG market estimated at 3.5 million in the U.S.; peak sales projected at $3–4 billion annually.
- Priority review voucher acquired to accelerate SHTG approval by four months.
- Differentiation from ASO competitors includes deeper knockdown, better safety, quarterly dosing, and no need for liver monitoring.


### Regulatory and clinical development

- SNDA for SHTG planned for submission by year-end 2026, supported by SHASTA-3, SHASTA-4, and MUIR safety data.
- MUIR study completed, providing a safety database of ~1,000 patients.


### Familial hypercholesterolemia and zodasiran

- Yosemite program fully enrolled; phase III data expected mid-2027.
- Zodasiran anticipated as next launch after SHTG, leveraging existing sales force.
- Targeting similar efficacy to Regeneron with more patient-friendly dosing.


### Obesity and metabolic programs

- Obesity pipeline includes activin E and ALK7; new data and phase II MASH study design update expected by year-end.
- Third obesity candidate to file CTA by end of 2026; phase II advancement dependent on upcoming data.
- Open to partnerships but not actively seeking them for obesity programs.


### ARO-DIMER-PA and dimer technology

- First clinical data for dimer technology: dual siRNA targeting PCSK9 and APOC3.
- Achieved >80% APOC3, >70% PCSK9 reduction, 54% LDL, >70% triglyceride, and ~50% ApoB reduction.
- Platform enables simultaneous knockdown of two genes; potential breakthrough for mixed hyperlipidemia.
- Next steps include phase IIb study to optimize dosing, followed by phase III focused on LDL reduction.


### CNS pipeline and tau program

- ARO-MAPT targets tau in Alzheimer's and tauopathies using subcutaneous siRNA with transferrin receptor targeting.
- Preclinical data showed ~60% CSF tau knockdown; healthy volunteer data to be presented soon.
- Enrollment ongoing for Alzheimer's patients; partnered programs in Huntington's, Ataxin, and alpha-synuclein.
- Plans to advance several wholly owned CNS targets to clinic within 18 months.
- Aims for tau knockdown comparable to BIIB080, with potentially better biodistribution via subcutaneous route.
