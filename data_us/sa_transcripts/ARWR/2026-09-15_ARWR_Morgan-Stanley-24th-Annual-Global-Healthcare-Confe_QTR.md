# Arrowhead Pharmaceuticals, Inc. — Morgan Stanley 24th Annual Global Healthcare Conference (2026-09-15)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Key clinical updates and pipeline progress

- ARO-DIMER-PA demonstrated first-in-class dual gene knockdown via RNAi, with strong reductions in LDL and triglycerides, and plans for additional dimers to enter the clinic in 2027.
- Early data showed ~50% ApoB reduction, outperforming some PCSK9 inhibitors, with multi-dose data expected at a future medical conference.
- Plans include a dual regulatory pathway: a pivotal program based on LDL reduction for faster market entry, followed by a cardiovascular outcomes trial.
- Quarterly dosing is likely, with potential for six-month intervals pending further data.
- Additional dimer programs are in development for obesity, MASH, and cardiovascular indications, with more details anticipated in 2027.


### Commercialization and market strategy

- The plozasiran launch in FCS has been smooth, with faster-than-expected uptake and expanded sales force to compete with new entrants.
- Payer coverage is strong, and a priority review voucher was acquired to accelerate SHTG approval.
- SHASTA-3 and SHASTA-4 data showed 80% triglyceride reduction, significant AP event reduction, and a favorable safety profile.
- The therapy is differentiated by quarterly dosing, minimal monitoring, and superior triglyceride lowering compared to competitors.
- Net price is set at $45,000/year, justified by the drug’s impact on pancreatitis risk and healthcare cost savings.


### Physician feedback and competitive landscape

- Physicians value the favorable safety profile and simplicity, reducing concerns about monitoring and dose adjustments.
- Strong risk reduction in pancreatitis was observed even in patients with moderate triglyceride elevations.
- Market entry is expected to be gradual, focusing on high-risk patients, with education needed to drive broader adoption.
- Competition is seen as beneficial for patient education and market growth.


### Pipeline innovation and technology

- ARO-MAPT targets tau protein for Alzheimer’s and other tauopathies, using subcutaneous delivery for broad brain distribution.
- Proof-of-concept data for ARO-MAPT is expected by October, with rapid expansion planned if successful.
- Obesity pipeline includes ARO-INHBE (MASH-focused) and ARO-ALK7 (potential pure weight loss), with phase II studies underway.
- AI is used for target discovery and ligand design, leveraging a large proprietary dataset to maintain a competitive edge.
- The company maintains a multi-year head start in RNAi innovation, with ongoing monitoring of global competitors, especially from China.
