# Shopify Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-11-03)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 GMV rose 29.5% to $119B, revenue increased 32% to $3.7B, and free cash flow margin was 22%.
- Growth was broad-based across merchant sizes, geographies, and channels; agentic commerce remained an emerging but rapidly growing sales and discovery channel.


### Financial highlights

- Revenue grew 32% YoY, reported and in constant currency, accelerating from 31% constant-currency growth in the prior-year quarter.
- Free cash flow was $816M, a 22% margin; roughly 150 bps of YoY margin improvement came from structural operating discipline and expense leverage.
- Gross profit increased 28% YoY, in line with guidance; gross margin was 47.6%, consistent with Q2.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q4 revenue growth expected in the low 30s YoY, with less than a 1-point FX headwind; comparison is 29% constant-currency growth in Q4 last year.
- Q4 gross profit dollars expected to grow in the high 20s; operating expenses projected at 29%-30% of revenue, reflecting higher marketing spend and pre-BFCM initiatives.
- Q4 free cash flow margin expected in line with Q4 last year, with higher taxes from payment timing; no meaningful impact expected from the merchant cash advance accounting change.


### Segment performance

- Merchant solutions revenue grew 35%, driven by GMV and higher payments penetration; subscription solutions revenue grew 20%, led by standard plans.
- Payments penetration exceeded 68%, up 3 points YoY; Shop Pay GMV grew 50% YoY, while Plus MRR was 34% of total MRR and grew 18%.
- International GMV grew 32%; North America GMV rose 28% and Europe 33% in constant currency. Offline GMV grew 30%, and B2B GMV grew 74%.
- Large, complex, multi-location merchants’ locations on the platform increased 52% YoY; 55% of merchants adding point of sale were new to the platform, and half of existing sellers sold only in-store.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Operating expenses were 32% of revenue versus 37% a year earlier; gross profit growth outpaced operating expense growth by ~12 points each quarter this year.
- Merchant solutions gross profit grew 37%, with gross margin expanding YoY for a third consecutive quarter; subscription solutions gross profit grew 16%, at 79% gross margin.
- Subscription solutions gross margin compressed YoY, primarily from incremental Sidekick AI spending; sequential margin pressure reflected higher hosting costs and infrastructure capacity added ahead of BFCM.
- Sidekick conversations increased 14% from Q2 to Q3, while cost per conversation declined 28% and total Sidekick cost fell 18%.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Agentic shopping volume remained small despite AI traffic growing 2.6x and orders 3.3x YoY; future merchant benefits depend on adoption and continued channel development.
- AI and infrastructure investment pressured subscription solutions margins; Q4 also includes planned marketing and infrastructure spending and higher tax payments.
- Forward-looking statements depend on assumptions and are subject to risks and uncertainties that could cause actual results to differ materially.


### Significant events and developments

- Canvas, the largest online store-builder update in 20 years, enabled merchants to launch more personalized stores 20% faster; Sidekick daily active merchants rose 3.7x and daily sessions 4.2x YoY.
- AI traffic was up 2.6x and orders 3.3x YoY; Catalog-powered searches converted at 2x the rate of general AI search, and AI-referred sales converted 64% more often than organic search.
- Shop GMV more than doubled YoY; offline lifetime GMV passed $200B, and the Shop app launched in Japan, reaching No. 1 in the App Store the week after full rollout.
- The shop.com domain was acquired for $100M to expand Shop’s role as a consumer shopping destination; the transition required no merchant action.
- Three-quarters of AI-referred sales came from product categories outside the top 100; first-time sellers on the platform increased 7x since 2018.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Marketing spending paused for several weeks in selected U.S. channels and geographies to test incrementality; some spending shifted to Q4, with no reported Q3 top-line impact.
- Q4 investment priorities include performance marketing, Sidekick, search capabilities, storefront resilience, and infrastructure capacity for holiday traffic.
- The $100M shop.com purchase supports a more ambitious consumer-facing role for Shop and greater online discoverability.


### Notes to the financial statements

- Roughly 150 bps of YoY free cash flow margin improvement came from structural discipline and expense leverage; the remainder reflected temporary factors: marketing-test timing, tax receivables, and the merchant cash advance accounting change.
- The merchant cash advance accounting change contributed to Q3 free cash flow improvement, with no meaningful impact expected in Q4.


### Market and industry conditions

- Over three years, U.S. merchant online sales grew at a 23% CAGR versus 8% for U.S. e-commerce; European merchant online GMV grew at 37% versus 8% market growth.
- Since the start of 2025, merchants captured nearly half of incremental U.S. e-commerce sales and more than 40% in Europe; Q3 GMV growth was evenly split between new and same-store sales.
- Commerce is fragmenting across online, offline, social, and agent-led channels; AI-referred sales are concentrated in niche categories, with three-quarters outside the top 100 product categories.
