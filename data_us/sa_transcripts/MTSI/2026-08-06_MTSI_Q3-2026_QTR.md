# MACOM Technology Solutions Holdings, Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-08-06)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Achieved record Q3 revenue of $342.2 million, up 18.4% sequentially and 35.8% year-over-year, with strong growth across all end markets, especially data center.
- Adjusted EPS reached $1.40 per diluted share; book-to-bill ratio hit a record 1.6, reflecting robust demand and backlog.
- Strategic investments in R&D and manufacturing capacity are driving innovation and supporting rapid revenue growth.


### Financial highlights

- Adjusted gross profit was $204.2 million (59.7% margin), up 120 bps sequentially.
- Adjusted operating income reached $107.7 million, up 33.9% sequentially and 69.6% year-over-year; operating margin improved to 31.5%.
- Adjusted net income was $109.8 million, up 30.2% sequentially; adjusted EPS rose to $1.40 from $1.09 in Q2.
- Cash flow from operations was $80 million; cash and short-term investments totaled $663 million.
- Capital expenditures for Q3 were $20.8 million; FY26 CapEx expected at $60–$65 million.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q4 revenue expected between $415–$425 million, with adjusted gross margin of 60–61% and adjusted EPS of $1.97–$2.03.
- Sequential Q4 growth forecast: data center +35%, industrial & defense +20%, telecom low single digits.
- Fiscal 2027 base case projects company growth in the mid-20% range, with data center potentially growing 50% year-over-year.
- Adjusted operating margin expected to reach ~37% in Q4 and targeted to exceed 40% in FY27.


### Segment performance

- Data center revenue was $137.6 million (+40% sequentially), driven by 200G PAM4, ZR light, and strong demand for photodetectors and drivers.
- Industrial & Defense revenue reached $133.4 million (+11% sequentially), with defense expected to grow 25% this year.
- Telecom revenue was $71.3 million (+2% sequentially), with LEO satellite and cable infrastructure as key growth drivers.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Book-to-bill ratio: 1.6.
- Inventory turns: 2.0, up from 1.9 sequentially.
- Days sales outstanding: 48 days, improved from 50 days.
- Adjusted income tax rate: 3%, expected to rise to mid-single digits in FY27.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Exposure to regulatory changes, such as potential bans on Chinese module makers, could shift customer mix but is not expected to materially impact demand.
- Input cost inflation (labor, utilities, materials) is being managed, with some costs passed to customers; gross margins continue to expand.
- Increasing profitability may lead to higher tax rates depending on jurisdictional income mix.


### Significant events and developments

- $61 million strategic investment in IQE to strengthen supply chain; resulted in a $41 million non-cash gain (excluded from non-GAAP results).
- Installation of new G10 epitaxial reactor in France to support European growth.
- Ongoing transition of European fab from 3-inch to 6-inch wafers to double capacity and improve quality.
- Updated and resubmitted CHIPS Act investment plan to U.S. government.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Net cash position of $322.5 million after accounting for $340.5 million in convertible notes maturing December 2029.
- CapEx focused on expanding fab capacity and R&D; new capacity typically online within a year.
- Conservative approach to cash management, with priorities on internal investment, debt repayment, and selective acquisitions.


### Market and industry conditions

- Served addressable market (SAM) for 2027 estimated at $15 billion, up 20–30% from 2026, with $6 billion each in data center and I&D, and $3 billion in telecom.
- Data center market driven by hyperscaler demand for high-speed optical connectivity; defense market benefiting from increased U.S. and European spending.
- Telecom growth supported by LEO satellite, 5G, and cable infrastructure opportunities.
