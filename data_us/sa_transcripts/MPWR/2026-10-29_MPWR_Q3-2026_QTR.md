# Monolithic Power Systems, Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-10-29)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 revenue reached $1.2B for the first time, up 22% QoQ and 63% YoY; annual revenue-support capacity exceeded $6B.
- Management described growth as accelerating, citing expansion across end markets and new product opportunities.


### Financial highlights

- Q3 revenue was $1.2B, 22% above Q2 2026 and 63% above Q3 2025.
- More than $450M of stock was repurchased during Q3.


### Outlook and guidance

- Enterprise data growth floor raised from 130% to 150% YoY, based on Q3 and Q4 trends; no 2027 growth rate was set.
- Existing enterprise data growth drivers remain intact for 2027, including customer diversification, module growth, platform content gains and potential CPU demand from agentic AI.
- Capacity exceeded $6B of annual revenue support; $8B is targeted by H2 2027, with $10B as the longer-term goal.
- 800V architecture revenue is expected no earlier than late 2027, with more likely in 2028.
- At high growth rates, OpEx is expected to fall below the prior model range as spending cannot scale as quickly as revenue.


### Segment performance

- Enterprise data: strong demand across existing customers, broader ASIC customer growth and module adoption; management expects demand to continue into 2027.
- Communications: optical modules and the switch business drove Q3 acceleration; 1.6T transitions and added switch sockets support growth into 2027.
- Storage and compute: DDR5 PMICs were the largest growth contributor; HDD and SSD also grew. Notebook demand was softer amid memory constraints.
- Storage and compute segment outlook was flattish to up a couple of points YoY, reflecting strong storage offset by notebook market constraints.
- Auto received first 48V power orders; 48V and zonal architectures are expected to drive future growth. Robotics solutions began ramping across regions.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Gross margin increased 10 bps in Q3; Q4 guidance midpoint implied another 10 bps of expansion. The stated target range remains 55%–60%.
- Operating margin has recently expanded by nearly one percentage point per quarter.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Rising wafer and other costs are partly absorbed and shared with customers; pricing was not a significant contributor to revenue growth.
- GaN is a new technology and reliability remains a key validation focus; customers can choose GaN or established silicon carbide solutions.
- Channel inventory remained at the lean end of its range, while internal inventory days declined as revenue ramped; inventory was not limiting sales.
- Notebook demand remained weaker in the near term due to downstream memory constraints.


### Significant events and developments

- First large data converter design win secured, with a ramp expected in 2027; management said it was not expected to materially affect 2026.
- GaN-based solutions entered sampling for 800V data center architectures, complementing silicon carbide designs.
- AI design tools helped expand customer reach in traditional markets and secure new desktop AI opportunities.


### Capital allocation and financing

- More than $450M of stock was repurchased in Q3.
- Fabless capacity expansion is not expected to require a large increase in capital intensity, OpEx or changes to the capital structure.


### Market and industry conditions

- AI and high-power GPU workloads are driving server power demand and adoption of vertical power delivery; management expects AI-related hardware trends to extend to desktops and notebooks over time.
- Management cited potential CPU demand from agentic AI workloads as an additional longer-term enterprise data tailwind.
- Auto growth and design pipelines were geographically and customer diverse, including activity in the U.S., Europe and Asia, particularly China.
