# Synopsys, Inc. — Investor Day 2026 (2026-09-30)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Strategic vision and business transformation

- Marking its 40th anniversary, the company is now fully integrated with Ansys, expanding its leadership in EDA, silicon IP, and simulation & analysis (SNA).
- The shift from generic to application-optimized silicon is driving demand for custom IP and co-design across the silicon-to-system stack.
- Factory One (standard IP) and Factory Two (application-optimized IP, AOIP) business models are both being scaled, with AOIP expected to reach $1B by 2030.
- Multi-year, multi-generation agreements, such as the $1B license deal for Amazon's Graviton, Trainium, and Nitro, highlight the new AOIP model's traction.
- The company is embedding itself deeper in customer workflows, enabling system-level co-design and validation, and expanding its ecosystem partnerships with major foundries and hyperscalers.


### AI-driven innovation and platform expansion

- Launched the Synopsys Agentic AI Platform (Autopilot), enabling autonomous engineering through long-horizon agents across EDA and SNA domains.
- Announced a multi-year partnership with OpenAI to develop a specialized GPT-Synopsys model, combining proprietary EDA tools and workflows with frontier AI reasoning.
- AI is expected to drive significant tool usage growth, with agentic workflows increasing license consumption by 5-10x in some cases.
- Customers can choose between full-stack Synopsys AI, hybrid models, or frontier AI integrations, with monetization via subscriptions, consumption, and outcome-based revenue sharing.
- AI is positioned as a TAM expansion for EDA, with physics-based validation remaining essential for sign-off and manufacturing.


### Financial outlook and growth guidance

- Long-term revenue growth guidance raised to mid-teens CAGR through 2030, up from prior double digits, driven by EDA (mid-teens), SNA (double digits), and IP (high teens).
- FY2027 outlook: 15% revenue growth to $11.15B, 250bps operating margin expansion to 44%, and 27% EPS growth to $19.08.
- Operating margin target increased to ~50% by 2030, supported by business model changes, AOIP royalty ramp, and synergy realization from the Ansys acquisition.
- $400M revenue synergy from Ansys integration expected by 2029, with cost synergies achieved a year early in 2027.
- Capital allocation priorities include investing in R&D, maintaining a strong balance sheet, and returning up to 50% of free cash flow to shareholders, including a $1B share repurchase.
