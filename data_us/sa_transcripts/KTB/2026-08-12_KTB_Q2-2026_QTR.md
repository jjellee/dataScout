# Kontoor Brands, Inc. — Q2 2026 (2026-08-12)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Achieved an inflection point in value creation, driven by strategic initiatives: building Wrangler momentum, integrating Helly Hansen, and completing Project Genius.
- Lee divestiture progressing well, with proceeds to fund a $400 million accelerated share repurchase and debt reduction.
- Helly Hansen integration exceeded expectations, with revenue and margin ahead of acquisition plan.
- Wrangler delivered global revenue growth, especially in female and Western categories, and continued market share gains.
- Project Genius on track to exceed $100 million in gross savings, supporting operational agility and investment capacity.


### Financial highlights

- First half 2026 revenue reached $1.2 billion, up 31% year-over-year, at the high end of outlook.
- Adjusted gross margin for the first half was 52.2%, up 590 basis points year-over-year.
- Adjusted EPS for the first half was $2.12, up 36% year-over-year.
- Second quarter adjusted gross margin rose 710 basis points to 53.8%.
- Inventory at quarter-end was $526 million, down 3% year-over-year.


### Outlook and guidance

- Full-year revenue expected between $2.66 billion and $2.71 billion.
- Full-year adjusted gross margin outlook raised to 49.8%-50%, up 330-350 basis points year-over-year.
- Adjusted operating income expected at $413-$420 million, up 15%-17% year-over-year.
- Adjusted EPS expected at $5.25-$5.35, up 27%-29% year-over-year.
- Cash from operations projected at $450 million; net leverage targeted below 1.5x by year-end.
- Over $900 million in capital returns planned for the year via share repurchases, dividends, and debt payments.


### Segment performance

- Wrangler global revenue up 1% in Q2, with 12% DTC growth and double-digit gains in female and Western.
- Wrangler international revenue up 8%, led by 27% DTC growth.
- Helly Hansen Q2 global revenue $114 million, up 6% pro forma; first half up 12% reported.
- Helly Hansen sport segment led growth in the U.S., Nordics, and Alps; workwear also grew strongly.
- Helly Hansen China JV revenue up nearly 70% in Q2.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Operating margin for Helly Hansen expanded 600 basis points to 7% in the first half.
- SG&A expenses were $221 million in Q2, or 37.8% of revenue.
- Net debt at quarter-end was $1.1 billion; $58 million cash on hand; $500 million revolver undrawn.
- Share repurchases totaled $75 million year-to-date at $75/share; $700 million remains authorized.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Ongoing macro volatility and conservative inventory management by retail partners.
- Dynamic global trade environment, with changes in U.S. tariffs and ongoing litigation affecting cost structure.
- Currency fluctuations impacting reported growth rates, especially for Helly Hansen.


### Significant events and developments

- Lee divestiture on track for Q4 close; proceeds to fund share repurchase and debt reduction.
- Received $23 million in IEEPA tariff refunds in Q3; expect full recovery by year-end.
- Section 122 tariffs ruled invalid and expired in July 2026.
- New Section 301 tariffs implemented in July 2026, mostly at 10%-12.5%.
- Launched Wrangler Toughlite jeans and expanded DTC retail footprint.
- Helly Hansen to debut at Dick’s Sporting Goods House of Sport in 18 doors in October.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Returned over $130 million to shareholders year-to-date, including $75 million in share repurchases.
- Plan to use Lee divestiture proceeds for $400 million ASR and voluntary debt repayment.
- Board declared a regular quarterly dividend of $0.53 per share.


### Market and industry conditions

- Retailers remain cautious with inventory commitments; inventory at retail described as suboptimal.
- Wrangler’s POS trends remain consistent, with continued share gains despite macro volatility.
- Helly Hansen benefiting from structural tailwinds in workwear and outdoor categories.
