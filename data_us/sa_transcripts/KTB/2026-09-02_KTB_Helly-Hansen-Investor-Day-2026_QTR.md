# Kontoor Brands, Inc. — Helly Hansen Investor Day 2026 (2026-09-02)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Strategic vision and portfolio evolution

- Focused on three core categories: denim, outdoor, and workwear, with Helly Hansen as the growth engine, targeting aggressive expansion in the U.S. and Alps, and significant growth in technical outdoor and workwear segments.
- Kontoor's acquisition of Helly Hansen and divestiture of Lee sharpened the portfolio, enabling a more focused, synergistic, and growth-oriented business model.
- Multi-brand platform and operational synergies are driving margin expansion, with Helly Hansen benefiting from Kontoor’s supply chain, sourcing, and support capabilities.
- Dedicated investments in talent, product innovation, and marketing are prioritized to accelerate growth and brand awareness, especially in North America.
- The plan includes a deliberate sequencing: first building investment capacity, then scaling growth, with profitability improvements leading in the early years and revenue acceleration in the latter half.


### Financial guidance and growth targets

- Revenue is projected to exceed $1.1 billion by 2030, reflecting a 10% CAGR, with stronger growth expected in 2028 and beyond as investments scale.
- Operating profit is targeted at approximately $165 million, with mid-teens operating margin driven by gross margin expansion and disciplined investment.
- Helly Hansen is expected to generate over $500 million in cumulative cash from operations, more than doubling the cash generation from the Lee divestiture.
- Workwear and technical outdoor are identified as the largest growth drivers, with U.S. and Alps regions prioritized for accelerated expansion.
- Marketing spend will double through 2030, shifting from performance-led to brand-led campaigns, and from centralized to category-specific marketing teams.


### Business developments and operational changes

- Workwear and sport are now managed as distinct organizations, enabling focused growth strategies and resource allocation.
- U.S. business is set to be supercharged, with a dedicated GM, expanded wholesale and D2C channels, and targeted store openings in key locations.
- Technical outdoor and workwear product innovation is driven by professional partnerships, consumer insights, and a proven playbook for category entry.
- China JV is scaling rapidly, with premium positioning and significant store expansion, offering additional upside potential.
- Workwear strategy includes a good-better-best product ladder, focus on trades, high-visibility, and footwear, and a new in-house tender department to capture large contracts.


### Market positioning and consumer insights

- Helly Hansen is recognized for technical performance, trusted brand status, and warmth, with 30% aided awareness in the U.S., well below peers, indicating significant headroom.
- Core consumers are highly engaged, active, and performance-driven, with 75% participating in at least three technical outdoor activities.
- U.S. workwear market is a major focus, leveraging premium positioning, superior product features, and increasing investments in organization and marketing.
- Retail strategy emphasizes selective store openings in ski, sailing, and key urban locations to build brand awareness and showcase the brand.
- Brand-led marketing and storytelling will be amplified, especially around the 150th anniversary, to drive awareness and consumer engagement.
