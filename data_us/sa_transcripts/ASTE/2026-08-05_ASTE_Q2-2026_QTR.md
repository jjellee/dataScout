# Astec Industries, Inc. — Q2 2026 (2026-08-05)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Achieved record quarterly revenues and adjusted EBITDA, with strong progress on strategic initiatives focused on consistency, profitability, and growth.
- Net sales rose 23.6% year-over-year, driven by both Infrastructure and Materials Solutions segments.
- Backlog increased 57.9% to $601.1 million, with Materials Solutions contributing most of the growth.
- New product launches and international expansion, including eight new models at Hillhead 2026, support future growth.


### Financial highlights

- Net sales reached $408.1 million, up $77.8 million or 23.6% year-over-year.
- Adjusted EBITDA grew 26% to $42.6 million, with a margin of 10.4%, up 20 basis points.
- Adjusted EPS was $0.94, compared to $0.90 in the prior year.
- Parts and service revenue increased 34.8% to $135.5 million, representing 33.2% of net sales.


### Outlook and guidance

- Full-year 2026 adjusted EBITDA guidance revised to $160 million–$175 million, down from $170 million–$190 million, due to delivery shifts in asphalt plants.
- Second half adjusted EBITDA expected to be split one-third in Q3 and two-thirds in Q4.
- Effective tax rate projected at 26%–30%; capital expenditures at $35 million–$45 million.


### Segment performance

- Infrastructure Solutions net sales grew 11.6% to $228.3 million; aftermarket parts and service up 4.6%.
- Infrastructure Solutions margin compressed by 130 basis points due to product mix.
- Materials Solutions net sales surged 43% to $179.8 million; adjusted EBITDA up 54.5% to $22.1 million.
- Materials Solutions backlog grew 150.6%, reflecting both organic and inorganic growth.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted EBITDA margin reached 10.4%, up 20 basis points year-over-year.
- Net leverage at 2.2x, expected to decrease to 1.7x by end of 2026.
- Total liquidity at quarter-end was $265.8 million, including $75.7 million in cash.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Delays in Federal Highway Bill renewal and higher oil prices have shifted some deliveries to future quarters.
- Market uncertainty and energy price spikes impact customer purchasing timing, especially for smaller customers.
- Interest rates remain a consideration, but rental fleet utilization is strong, mitigating risk.


### Significant events and developments

- Launched eight new models at Hillhead 2026, including the Frontier series, and introduced two new U.K. dealers.
- Strong bookings in June and July, particularly for asphalt plants and parts.
- Federal infrastructure funding outlook remains positive, with proposed increases in highway and bridge allocations.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Strong balance sheet supports strategic inorganic growth and international expansion.
- Capital expenditures planned at $35 million–$45 million for 2026.


### Market and industry conditions

- Multi-year demand expected from federal, state, and local projects, and global mining sector investment.
- Electrification trends and data center construction driving demand for materials.
- Dealer inventory levels healthy; rental conversions active, supporting ongoing demand.
