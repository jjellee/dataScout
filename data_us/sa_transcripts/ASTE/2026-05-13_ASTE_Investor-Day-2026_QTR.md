# Astec Industries, Inc. — Investor Day 2026 (2026-05-13)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Strategic vision and growth drivers

- Focus on growing recurring revenue mix and launching industry-changing solutions to achieve long-term goals, leveraging mega trends like recycling, re-industrialization, digitalization, and mining.
- Emphasis on three strategic pillars: engaged employees, customer focus, and innovation, with a strong commitment to operational excellence and continuous improvement.
- Expansion of parts and service business targeted to reach 40%-50% of revenue by 2030, up from 34%, through both organic growth and acquisitions.
- Launch of the Signal Platform to provide customers with intelligence and connectivity, transforming equipment management and enabling new digital revenue streams.
- International growth pursued via local manufacturing, organic expansion, and targeted acquisitions, with a focus on increasing market share outside the U.S.


### Financial targets and capital allocation

- Revenue growth CAGR targeted at greater than 6% through 2030, compared to a previous three-year average of 3%.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin goal of 14%-17% by 2030, building on a 440 basis point improvement over the past three years.
- Return on invested capital (ROIC) targeted at 13%-15%, with a focus on growing the capital base while exceeding the cost of capital.
- Operating cash flow growth targeted at 25%+ over the period, supported by improved EBITDA and working capital management.
- Capital deployment strategy includes $400-$600 million available for acquisitions, maintaining leverage within 1.5-2.5x, and a disciplined acquisition playbook focused on recurring revenue and cultural fit.


### Operational excellence and innovation

- Employee engagement driven by the E3 strategy (engaged, empowered, enabled), with over 300 internal promotions in 2025 and 3,000+ improvement projects submitted.
- Customer-centric approach includes tailored solutions, extensive training, and a global service team, with over 2,000 customers trained annually.
- Innovation pipeline includes over 25 new or upgraded products launched at CONEXPO-CON/AGG 2026, AI-powered tools, and digital platforms like MyAstec and Silobot.
- Digital transformation led by the Signal Platform, enabling real-time asset management, predictive maintenance, and new service models.
- Focus on sustainability and regulatory compliance through product and digital innovation, supporting customer efficiency and permitting.


### Market outlook and segment opportunities

- Infrastructure market supported by significant public funding, with $1.2 trillion from the Infrastructure Investment and Jobs Act and ongoing needs for roads and bridges.
- Materials Solutions segment positioned for growth with new products, international expansion, and increased demand from sectors like data centers, mining, and energy.
- Concrete segment provides diversification, with margin profiles comparable to asphalt and new product development underway.
- System sales and installed base leveraged for long-term parts revenue, with a strong pipeline of large-scale projects.
- Aftermarket and digital platforms expected to drive higher customer retention, margin expansion, and recurring revenue.
