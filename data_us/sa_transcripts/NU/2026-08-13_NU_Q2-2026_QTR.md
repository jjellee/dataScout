# Nu Holdings Ltd. — Q2 2026 (2026-08-13)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Achieved over $1 billion in net income for the first time, driven by strong customer growth and engagement across Brazil, Mexico, and Colombia.
- Customer base reached 139 million, with deepening engagement and activity rates, especially in Brazil where activity surpassed 86%.
- Launched new segmentation strategies, including Croma for the super core segment and continued expansion in SME and high-income segments.
- Received a full banking license in Mexico, now the largest digital bank in the country with 16 million customers.
- AI and technology investments, including the nuFormer platform, are driving operational efficiency, underwriting, and customer experience.


### Financial highlights

- Gross revenues reached $5.9 billion, up 39% year-over-year; gross profit was $2.4 billion, up 43% year-over-year and 25% sequentially.
- Net income hit $1.1 billion, up 17% sequentially and 49% year-over-year.
- Net interest income was $3.7 billion, up 9%, with net interest margin expanding 180 bps to 22.9%.
- Credit portfolio grew to $39.4 billion, up 37% year-over-year and 5% sequentially; deposits reached $45.3 billion, up 18% year-over-year and 6% sequentially.
- Efficiency ratio at 19.5%, with full-year guidance around 20%.


### Outlook and guidance

- Expect continued growth in ARPAC, especially as older cohorts mature and new segmentation strategies take effect.
- Mexico and Colombia seen as major growth drivers, with Mexico potentially reaching 60-70% of Brazil's scale.
- Risk-adjusted net interest margin expected to remain at current strong levels for the foreseeable future.
- Continued investment in technology, AI, and international expansion while maintaining operating leverage.


### Segment performance

- Brazil remains the largest market, with significant room to deepen relationships and increase ARPAC.
- Ultravioleta (high-income) and Croma (super core) segments showing strong growth in purchase volumes and assets under custody.
- SME customer base at 6.8 million, with significant untapped potential.
- Mexico: 16 million customers, rapid digital adoption, and higher ARPAC than Brazil at the same stage.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Risk-adjusted net interest margin reached a record 12.4%, up from 9.5% sequentially.
- Loan-to-deposit ratio at 35%, maintaining ample liquidity.
- Allowance coverage at 244% of NPL 90+, providing a strong balance sheet cushion.
- Return on equity at a record 33%.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Underwriting assumes future deterioration, maintaining significant cushions for credit risk.
- No evidence of broad-based weakening in consumer credit, but vigilance remains high.
- Mix shifts in lending products, not deterioration in unsecured lending, are driving changes in NPLs.
- Entry into the U.S. market will be gradual, with initial focus on data collection and model adaptation.


### Significant events and developments

- CFO transition: Rob Livingston succeeded Guilherme Lago, who remains as Special Advisor.
- AI advancements: nuFormer platform now powers underwriting, customer support, and growth, with significant efficiency gains.
- Regulatory approval for full banking license in Mexico, enabling new product launches and deeper customer engagement.
- Desenrola government debt renegotiation program impacted cost of credit and provisioning, but effects are largely absorbed.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Deposits optimization strategy in Mexico to improve funding costs while maintaining liquidity.
- Continued disciplined provisioning and coverage, with allowances built in line with portfolio growth.


### Market and industry conditions

- Digital financial adoption accelerating in Mexico, with regulatory changes fostering competition and innovation.
- Brazil's mass market remains a $30 billion profit pool, with incumbents still holding significant share.
- AI and technology are reshaping operational models and productivity across the industry.
