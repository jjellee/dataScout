# Nu Holdings Ltd. — Q1 2026 (2026-05-14)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Customer base surpassed 135 million, with over 115 million in Brazil, 15 million in Mexico, and nearly 5 million in Colombia.
- Record quarterly revenue of $5 billion, driven by ARPAC expansion and customer growth.
- Net income reached $871 million, up 41% year-over-year on an FX neutral basis.
- Efficiency ratio hit a record low of 17.6%, reflecting both structural gains and timing benefits.
- AI transformation is a core priority, with significant productivity gains and new AI-native products in development.


### Financial highlights

- Consolidated credit portfolio reached $37.2 billion, up 40% year-over-year FX neutral.
- Net interest income was $3.25 billion, up 12% quarter-over-quarter FX neutral.
- Gross profit was $1.88 billion, up 27% year-over-year FX neutral.
- Deposits totaled $42.4 billion, up 22% year-over-year FX neutral.
- Risk-adjusted NIM was 9.5%, down 100 bps sequentially due to higher provisions.


### Outlook and guidance

- Full-year 2026 efficiency ratio expected to be around 20%, in line with 2025.
- IFRS effective tax rate projected to converge to 15%-20% for 2026; managerial ETR to 30%-35%.
- Risk-adjusted NIM expected to normalize as seasonal effects subside.
- Continued investment in AI, international expansion, and return to office initiatives.


### Segment performance

- Brazil: Maintains leadership with 7% share of a $100B profit pool, strong customer engagement.
- Mexico: Customer base grew 7x in four years, now profitable under IFRS, efficiency ratio down 78 points.
- Colombia: Solid net additions, approaching 5 million customers.
- SME segment in Brazil exceeds 5 million customers, with new credit lines and cross-sell opportunities.
- High-income segment in Brazil growing rapidly, with 2 out of 5 high-income Brazilians as customers.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Efficiency ratio: 17.6% reported, 16.6% core (excluding strategic investments).
- Coverage ratio: 16.2% of portfolio, 2.5x 90+ delinquency balance.
- Gross CLA against new 90+ NPL formation at 153.8%.
- NPL 15-90 at 5%, up 89 bps from year-end, consistent with seasonality.
- 90+ NPLs at 6.5%, down 10 bps sequentially.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Higher provisions driven by seasonality, portfolio growth, and mix, not by asset quality deterioration.
- Conservative provisioning approach, with stress-tested cohorts and buffers for credit deterioration.
- Macroeconomic tailwinds include income tax exemption and Desenrola 2.0, not yet factored into provisions.
- Regulatory risks in secured lending, especially private payroll loans.


### Significant events and developments

- Achieved first quarter of IFRS profitability in Mexico, ahead of internal plan.
- Launched new SME credit products in Brazil, leveraging cross-sell and zero CAC.
- Accelerated AI adoption, with proprietary models in production for credit decisioning.
- Announced deliberate, staged U.S. expansion, with capped OPEX impact and focus on product-market fit.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Strategic investments in AI, U.S. expansion, and return to office are included in efficiency ratio guidance.
- U.S. expansion OPEX headwind capped at less than 100 bps on efficiency ratio for 2026 and 2027.


### Market and industry conditions

- Brazil and Mexico banking markets remain underpenetrated, with large profit pools and growth potential.
- Competitive landscape in secured lending, with cautious approach to private payroll due to operational and regulatory risks.
- AI seen as a transformative force in banking, not just a productivity tool.
