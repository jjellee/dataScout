# Hewlett Packard Enterprise Company — Q4 2026 (2026-11-23)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Fiscal 2026 delivered record revenue, gross margin, operating profit, EPS, free cash flow and orders across both business segments; demand and backlog supported confidence entering FY27.
- Juniper integration and portfolio expansion reshaped networking and AI capabilities; execution and profitable growth remain central priorities.


### Financial highlights

- FY26 revenue was $46.7B, up 23% on a normalized basis; orders grew 51%.
- FY26 gross margin was 38.1%; operating profit was $7.0B, up 110% reported, with 15.1% operating margin, up 530 bps; non-GAAP EPS was $3.97 and GAAP diluted EPS was $3.07.
- FY26 free cash flow was $4.5B; Q4 revenue was $14.5B, up 50%, and orders rose 88%.
- Q4 operating profit was $2.5B, up 108% YoY; non-GAAP EPS was $1.43, up 131%, versus GAAP EPS of $1.26; free cash flow was $1.9B.


### Outlook and guidance

- FY27 revenue growth is guided at 27%-35% ($59B-$63B), with ~46% in H1 and 54% in H2; growth is expected to be backend-loaded.
- FY27 networking revenue growth is guided in the high teens to low 20s; Cloud and AI revenue growth is 30%-40%.
- FY27 operating profit growth is guided at 23%-30%, operating margin at 14%-15%, non-GAAP EPS at $5.05-$5.35, GAAP EPS at $3.68-$3.98, and free cash flow at ≥$5.5B.
- FY27 networking operating margin is expected in the mid-to-high 20% range; Cloud and AI margin is guided at ~14%. Gross margin is expected to moderate in H2 as AI server volumes and Helios ramp.
- Q1 revenue is guided at $14B-$14.6B, operating profit at $2.2B-$2.4B, non-GAAP EPS at $1.30-$1.40 and GAAP EPS at $0.88-$0.98.


### Segment performance

- FY26 Networking revenue exceeded $11.3B, up ~10% normalized; operating profit was $2.5B and margin 22.3%. Orders rose 30% normalized.
- FY26 Cloud and AI revenue was $34.3B, up 29%; operating profit was $5.2B, up 150%, with 15.2% margin, up 740 bps; orders increased 63%.
- Q4 Networking revenue was $3.1B, up 12%, with $675M operating profit and 22.1% margin; orders grew 56%. Campus and branch rose 17%, data center networking 12%, and routing 10%.
- Q4 Cloud and AI revenue was $11.3B, up 69%; operating profit was $2.1B, up 235%, with 18.4% margin. Server revenue rose 95%; storage revenue rose 34%.
- Q4 Financial Services revenue was roughly flat; financing volume hit a record $2.1B, up 43%, with ROE above 25%. Total ARR exceeded $3.5B, up 12%.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- FY26 gross margin was 38.1%; Q4 gross margin was 38%, up 180 bps YoY and down 220 bps sequentially; Q4 operating margin was 16.9%, up 470 bps YoY.
- FY26 free cash flow was $4.5B; Q4 operating cash flow was $2.3B and free cash flow was $1.9B. Cash conversion cycle improved 12 days from Q3.
- Year-end cash and equivalents were $5.8B; net leverage was 1.4x, below the 2x target.
- FY27 guidance assumes 1.43B diluted shares, a 13% non-GAAP tax rate and ~$380M OI&E.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Memory, NAND and other critical components are expected to remain constrained through at least 2H calendar 2028, limiting order conversion despite strong demand.
- AI server mix and input costs may pressure gross margin, particularly in H2 FY27; Helios shipments and large AI deals add timing variability.
- Networking supply lead times range from 28 to 40 weeks or longer; Helios and Oracle contributions are expected to be backend-loaded.
- FY27 server growth is expected to shift toward units, but supply will continue to gate unit volumes; commodity price increases are expected to moderate from FY26 levels.


### Significant events and developments

- Juniper integration was completed faster than planned; FY28 annualized synergy target rose to $800M from $450M, while combined Catalyst and Juniper initiatives target ~$1.2B annual gross run-rate savings by FY28.
- Catalyst and Juniper integration costs totaled ~$375M in FY26; initiatives were ahead of plan and contributed to profitability and margin expansion.
- Q4 networks-for-AI orders reached a record $1.2B; FY26 orders grew nearly 200%, with year-end backlog of $1.7B. FY27 cumulative orders target is $7.5B-$8B.
- FY26 AI systems orders grew over 45%; revenue was $5.8B, up 28%, and backlog rose nearly 90%. Enterprise and sovereign customers represented over 60% of cumulative AI orders over four years.


### Capital allocation and financing

- FY26 shareholder returns totaled $2B, including $0.8B in dividends and more than $1.2B in repurchases; Q4 repurchases were $790M.
- Quarterly dividend was raised 19% to $0.17 per share beginning Q1 FY27; FY27 share buybacks are targeted at ~$3B.
- Long-term policy targets returning at least 75% of free cash flow to shareholders through dividends and repurchases.
- Cash on hand funded retirement of $1.25B of notes; HPE Financial Services avoided financing neocloud and other high-risk customers.


### Market and industry conditions

- AI investment is extending beyond training into inference and agentic AI; enterprise deployments are driving demand for accelerated compute, storage, networking and hybrid management.
- Networking demand is supported by AI data-center buildouts, Wi-Fi 7 upgrades and rising data traffic; Juniper and Aruba capabilities span enterprise and service-provider markets.
- Enterprise budgets are focused on cybersecurity, AI deployments, infrastructure modernization and alternatives to virtualization; customer demand and backlog remained strong exiting FY26.
