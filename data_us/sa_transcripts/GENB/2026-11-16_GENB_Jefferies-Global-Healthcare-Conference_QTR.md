# Generate Biomedicines, Inc. — Jefferies Global Healthcare Conference (2026-11-16)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Programmable biology platform

- Eight years of work combining transformer-based protein design with an integrated dry and wet lab; recent efforts added agentic, LLM-enabled infrastructure for complex therapeutic design.
- Design capabilities target difficult properties including internalization, selectivity and pH-dependent binding; the platform aims to expand druggability and make early biological exploration broader and cheaper.
- Experimental capabilities include screening 10 billion designed variants for pH-dependent binding and using four cryo-EM microscopes to resolve low-confidence structures and capture protein dynamics.
- In work with Anthropic, six scientists used agentic methods over six months and a $250,000 budget to redesign in vivo CAR T signaling domains; resulting designs were 70% shorter with logarithmic functional improvement.
- Reported early CAR T results included increased tumor-cell killing, cytokine release, surface expression and durability, plus a substantial shift in B-cell depletion; potential applications span oncology, immune reset and fibrosis.


### Partnerships and pipeline

- Collaborations include Amgen, launched in 2021; Novartis, added in 2024; and Zealand Pharma in obesity; one or two additional strategic collaborations are expected over the next 12 months.
- Lead asset GB-0895, an anti-TSLP antibody, has a 98-day half-life and 106 fM binding affinity; Phase III asthma trials began in December 2025, and COPD is also under investigation.
- GB-4362, an MMAE-toxin neutralizer, is in Phase I dose finding; after dose selection, an expansion cohort is planned to assess prevention of grade 1-to-grade 2 neuropathy progression.
- GB-5267, an armored CAR T program developed with Roswell Park, is enrolling in a Phase I trial in Buffalo, New York.


### GB-0895 asthma and COPD

- Phase I results showed biomarker modulation at levels comparable to or better than tezepelumab across reported time points; PK/PD modeling indicated 99.9% target engagement over six months.
- Two Phase III asthma studies use asthma exacerbation rate at 52 weeks as the primary endpoint and reduction in exacerbation rate in the <300 EOS cohort as a key secondary endpoint; trials are enrolling at 400 sites.
- Management expects full enrollment of both asthma studies by the end of 2027; the studies are approved across all regions.
- Severe asthma biologic penetration was cited at ~20%; the addressable opportunity includes 1.9 million biologic-eligible asthma patients and a larger, under-penetrated COPD market.
- The Phase Ib COPD trial is fully enrolled; 300 mg and 600 mg both robustly modulated key biomarkers with no dose difference, supporting the selected asthma Phase III dose for COPD.


### GB-4362 oncology program

- The program is designed to bind circulating MMAE toxin without disrupting intact ADCs, with initial evaluation alongside PADCEV plus KEYTRUDA and potential relevance across MMAE-based ADCs.
- The first dose-escalation cohort enrolled faster than expected and the second was underway; dose selection was expected by year-end 2026, followed by expansion.
- Phase I dose-finding results were expected in early 2027, with expansion-cohort data targeted by the end of 2027 to assess proof of concept in reducing peripheral neuropathy.
