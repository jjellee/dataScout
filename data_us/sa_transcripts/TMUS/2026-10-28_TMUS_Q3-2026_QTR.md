# T-Mobile US, Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-10-28)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 postpaid account net additions reached 274,000; postpaid phone and 5G broadband net additions led the industry, alongside postpaid ARPA growth of over 4% YoY.
- Results reflected a strategy balancing customer and account growth with value, supported by network, pricing and customer-experience differentiation.


### Financial highlights

- Postpaid service revenue grew 9% YoY, total service revenue 6%, and core adjusted EBITDA 11%; adjusted free cash flow margin was 26%.
- Full-year 2026 service revenue outlook is ~$76.8 billion, or 8% growth, with postpaid performance partly offset by wholesale and prepaid declines.


### Outlook and guidance

- FY26 postpaid net account additions are expected to be ~1 million; full-year postpaid ARPA growth is now expected at ~3.5%, raised from 2.5%-3%.
- FY26 core adjusted EBITDA guidance is $37.35-$37.45 billion, up $100 million at the midpoint; adjusted free cash flow is $18.7-$18.8 billion, up $150 million at the midpoint.
- FY26 cash CapEx is expected to remain ~$10 billion; Q4 organic postpaid service revenue growth is expected to be ~7% YoY.


### Segment performance

- 5G broadband net additions led the industry; FWA ARPU increased YoY and QoQ, and customer growth is tracking ahead of the pace toward 15 million customers by 2030.
- Fiber customer growth accelerated YoY and sequentially, with ~20% 12-month penetration and a target of 3 million-4 million customers by 2030.
- T-Mobile for Business recorded its 17th consecutive quarter of positive ports and double-digit YoY revenue growth; the DOJ wireless purchasing contract win expands public-sector reach.
- Wholesale revenue faces faster-than-expected TracFone and Boost MVNO roll-offs; prepaid trends reflect higher-value customers engaging more directly with postpaid brands.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Postpaid ARPA grew over 4% YoY in Q3; FY26 growth guidance increased to ~3.5%.
- Adjusted free cash flow margin was 26% in Q3; full-year adjusted free cash flow guidance is $18.7-$18.8 billion.
- 60% of new account lines are on premium plans; port-in ARPAs are 20% higher than port-out ARPAs.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Q4 typically brings higher churn and promotional intensity; guidance also factors in a staggered iPhone launch, device price increases, model availability and Q3 inventory shifts.
- A muted residual Q4 churn impact from rate-plan optimization is expected; broadband-only and fiber-only accounts structurally churn at higher rates than postpaid phone accounts.
- Faster wholesale roll-offs and ongoing prepaid trends weigh on service revenue; Q4 prepaid sequential change may be slightly higher.
- Physical AI is at an early stage, and the timing and scale of commercial infrastructure demand remain uncertain.


### Significant events and developments

- T-Mix launched, allowing customers to choose plan benefits and change them as needs evolve; 84% of surveyed customers want more control over plan structure.
- T-Life reached 32 million monthly active users; over 80% of upgrades and half of phone-line activations were completed digitally in Q3.
- Completed the first AI-RAN call in a live network setting and partnered with AWS to build Kinetic Edge infrastructure for physical AI.
- Jessica Uhl joined as CFO designate, with a planned transition alongside Peter Osvaldik over the coming months.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Share repurchases totaled $10.3 billion through Oct. 23, versus $9.9 billion for all of 2025; the dividend per share increased 15%.
- Capital allocation prioritizes leverage, core investment, spectrum and accretive M&A, followed by balanced buybacks and dividends; more capital may be reserved for 2027-28 spectrum auctions.
- Spectrum purchases will be evaluated against build-or-densify alternatives and disciplined return thresholds; the EchoStar spectrum deal was rejected as uneconomic.


### Market and industry conditions

- Median network speeds were cited as 60%-70% faster than those of the two largest competitors; OpenSignal named the network a global 5G Coverage winner.
- AI-enabled radio resource planning produced field-tested spectral-efficiency gains above expectations; Nokia has discussed potential AI-RAN gains of 20%-100%.
- Network traffic increased 5x over five years as speeds more than doubled; future capacity planning anticipates rising AI-related uplink demand and 6G deployment.
- Management expects greater Q4 competitive variability amid device price increases, a staggered iPhone cycle and limited availability of some models.
