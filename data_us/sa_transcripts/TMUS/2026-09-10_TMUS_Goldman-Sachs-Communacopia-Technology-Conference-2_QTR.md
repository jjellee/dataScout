# T-Mobile US, Inc. — Goldman Sachs Communacopia + Technology Conference 2026 (2026-09-10)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Strategic moat and growth drivers

- Strategic moat is built on growth portfolio, culture, and leadership, with deep focus on sustainable differentiation in network, value, and experience.
- Consumer wireless leads with best network, value, and experience, supported by high ARPU port-ins and premium plan adoption.
- Fixed wireless access (FWA) and fiber businesses are complementary, with FWA guidance raised to 15 million customers by 2030 and strong early fiber penetration.
- Enterprise and business segments present significant upside, with only 10-12% share in large businesses and major opportunities in edge AI.
- Financial services, including a successful credit card launch, add to the diversified growth portfolio.


### Culture and leadership initiatives

- Culture is a core differentiator, with frontline-first focus and customer obsession driving high NPS of 46.
- Major investments include the Magenta AI Institute ($100M+), Frontline Connect (manager commitments tied to frontline impact), and a $12,000 multi-year employee grant.
- Leadership transitions are carefully planned, with new CFO Jessica bringing diverse, relevant experience and a balanced management team.


### Consumer wireless and value strategy

- Value proposition focuses on addressing customer pain points and maximizing customer lifetime value (CLV), not just pricing.
- Shift to 36-month equipment installment plans reflects longer device cycles and is CLV accretive.
- Device subsidies and promotions are managed based on customer needs and CLV, with rising device costs increasingly borne by customers.


### Financial performance and guidance

- On track for 950,000 to 1,050,000 postpaid net additions for the year, with strong volume and value mix.
- Churn improved by 85 basis points year-on-year, with higher ARPU and double-digit CLV growth.


### Broadband and wireline strategy

- FWA has highest NPS among products, offers comparable speeds to fiber, and is focused on urban/semi-urban markets using a fallow capacity model.
- Fiber achieves 20% penetration in year one for greenfield builds, with minimal overlap with FWA; both are seen as complementary.


### AI and technology innovation

- Edge AI and physical AI are seen as major future growth drivers, with low-latency 5G/6G networks enabling new use cases.
- Internally, AI is used to reduce care calls, accelerate coding, optimize network investments, and personalize customer engagement.
- $2.7 billion in value attributed to AI-driven care and service improvements.


### Capital allocation and spectrum

- Capital allocation remains consistent: maintain leverage, prioritize strategic investments, then shareholder returns.
- Spectrum portfolio is sufficient for growth plans, with disciplined approach to auctions and acquisitions.


### Wholesale and competitive landscape

- 2025-2026 expected as trough years for wholesale revenue due to Dish and TracFone roll-off, with new MVNOs and advertising as future tailwinds.
- Satellite direct-to-cell is viewed as a complementary, not competitive, technology due to physical constraints.


### Forward-looking priorities

- Key priorities include driving growth in consumer wireless, rural/small markets, enterprise, FWA, and new verticals like financial services and AI.
- Central focus remains on strengthening culture and widening the strategic moat to sustain long-term growth.
