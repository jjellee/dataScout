# Paymentus Holdings, Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-11-02)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 revenue reached a record $372.2M, up 19.8% YoY; contribution profit was a record $118.5M, up 20.6%, and adjusted EBITDA a record $49.7M, up 38.6%.
- Results exceeded the high end of guidance for all three key metrics; strong bookings, pipeline and backlog supported raised FY26 guidance.
- Management sees potential for revenue and backlog combined to reach $2B in 18–24 months; this is not annual guidance and carries execution risk.


### Financial highlights

- Revenue grew 19.8% YoY to $372.2M, driven by new biller launches and same-store growth among existing billers.
- Non-GAAP net income was $33.1M, or $0.25 per share, versus $22.6M, or $0.17, a year earlier.
- Adjusted gross profit rose 23.1% YoY to $99.8M; non-GAAP operating expenses increased 10.2% YoY to $53M, reflecting hiring, agency fees and R&D investment.
- Adjusted EBITDA reached $49.7M, up 38.6% YoY, with incremental adjusted EBITDA margin of 68.4%.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q4 revenue guidance is $373M–$378M, contribution profit $120M–$122M and adjusted EBITDA $45M–$50M; midpoint YoY growth is 13.6%, 13.2% and 19%, respectively.
- FY26 revenue guidance is $1.464B–$1.469B, contribution profit $466M–$468M and adjusted EBITDA $186M–$191M.
- FY26 midpoint growth is 22.6% for revenue, 20.9% for contribution profit and 37.2% for adjusted EBITDA; adjusted EBITDA margin on contribution profit is projected at 40.4%.
- No FY27 guidance was given; management cited strong visibility into the balance of 2026 and 2027 while emphasizing prudent guidance and execution.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Transactions processed rose 20.1% YoY to 219M; average price per transaction was $1.70, consistent with the prior year.
- Contribution profit per transaction was $0.54, relatively flat YoY; contribution margin was 31.8%, versus 31.6% a year earlier.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin was a record 41.9%, up 540 bps YoY; adjusted EBITDA was 41.9% of contribution profit, versus 36.5% last year.
- Rule of 40 score was 62, versus 68 last quarter and 59 in the prior-year period; FY26 guidance implies a score of 61–62.
- DSO was 27 days, flat sequentially and down ~13% YoY; working capital was $423.2M, up 7.6% QoQ.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- The potential $2B combined revenue-and-backlog milestone depends on execution; management cautioned that it is not a formal year-specific forecast.
- Contribution profit can vary quarter to quarter with biller mix, average payment amounts and payment mix; these factors are partly outside management’s control.
- Investment may increase to convert pipeline into bookings; guidance also allows for caution as large billers ramp and new launches build a full year of operating history.
- Agentic commerce could create client disintermediation risk; management views secure, biller-connected products as a response, while acknowledging intense competition in retail commerce.


### Significant events and developments

- Q3 bookings included a sizable interchange monetization agreement using Instant Payment Network capabilities; terms and financial impact were not yet detailed.
- BillWallet user base doubled to over 200,000, with users in nearly one-third of U.S. ZIP codes and adoption outpacing legacy wallets in the exposed customer cohort without marketing.
- New clients were signed across utilities, insurance, government, property management, telecom, banking and education; channel partners were added in utilities and mortgage.
- Datos recognized the platform as best-in-class in electronic bill presentment and payment, citing client base, retention and enterprise growth.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Cash totaled $409.6M at quarter end, up $29.9M QoQ; operations generated $43.7M, investing used $9.7M, primarily for capitalized software, and employee RSU net settlement used $4M.
- Management said ample cash and operating leverage provide flexibility to invest when opportunities warrant, while calibrating operating expenses to contribution profit and prioritizing profitable growth.
