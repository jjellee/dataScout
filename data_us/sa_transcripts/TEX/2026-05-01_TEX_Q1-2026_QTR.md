# Terex Corporation — Q1 2026 (2026-05-01)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Sales grew 41% year-over-year to $1.7 billion, driven by the REV Group merger and growth in all legacy segments.
- Specialty Vehicles segment contributed significantly, with 20% growth compared to the same period last year.
- EPS increased 18% year-over-year to $0.98, including a $0.10 one-time tax benefit.
- Backlog reached $7.1 billion, providing strong forward visibility.
- Integration of REV is ahead of schedule, with synergy targets on track.


### Financial highlights

- Q1 EBITDA margin was 9.9%, down 50 bps year-over-year, mainly due to tariff impacts.
- Free cash outflow was $57 million, consistent with Q1 last year.
- Net working capital as a percentage of sales improved to 16.7% from 26% year-over-year.
- Net leverage ratio reduced to 2.4x.
- Q1 pro forma bookings were $2.1 billion, with a 109% book-to-bill ratio.


### Outlook and guidance

- Full-year 2026 sales expected to grow ~5% pro forma to $7.5–$8.1 billion.
- Pro forma EBITDA expected to grow 12% year-over-year to $930 million–$1 billion (12.4% margin at midpoint).
- EPS guidance for 2026 is $4.50–$5.00.
- Free cash conversion expected at 80–90% of net income.
- Net leverage expected to improve through the year.


### Segment performance

- Environmental Solutions: Sales up 3.3%, EBITDA margin 18% (lower due to mix), strong demand in Utilities.
- Materials Processing: Sales up 18.3% pro forma, EBITDA margin improved to 15%, strong bookings and backlog.
- Specialty Vehicles: $436 million revenue in two months, 20% growth, EBITDA margin up 160 bps to 14.2%.
- Aerials: Sales up 4.2% year-over-year, $1 billion backlog, break-even EBITDA due to seasonality and tariffs.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Q1 EPS: $0.98 (includes $0.10 one-time tax benefit).
- Effective tax rate: 11% for Q1, expected 21% for full year.
- Shares outstanding: 96.1 million in Q1, expected 115 million for Q2–Q4.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Macroeconomic and geopolitical uncertainties, including tariffs and inflation, could impact results.
- Guidance reflects current portfolio and excludes non-recurring items and synergy costs.


### Significant events and developments

- REV Group merger closed February 2, 2026; integration progressing ahead of schedule.
- $28 million in synergies expected in 2026, with $75 million run rate targeted within 24 months.
- Strategic review of Aerials business ongoing, with multiple interested parties.
- Investment in Apptronik yielding technology collaboration, including a zero-gravity arm prototype.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Focus on integration and execution post-merger; capital deployment decisions to prioritize shareholder value.
- Optionality for future M&A or buybacks remains, but current priority is operational execution.


### Market and industry conditions

- End markets more stable, with 80% of revenue from North America.
- Strong demand in fire/emergency vehicles, utilities, and construction; investments to increase capacity.
- Anticipated pre-buys in waste/recycling ahead of 2027 EPA changes.
- Aerials and MP segments showing early cycle momentum and strong bookings.
