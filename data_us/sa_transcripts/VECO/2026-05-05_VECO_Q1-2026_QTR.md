# Veeco Instruments Inc. — Q1 2026 (2026-05-05)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q1 2026 revenue reached $158 million, non-GAAP operating income was $9 million, and non-GAAP diluted EPS was $0.14, all within guidance ranges.
- Strong order momentum continued from H2 2025, with a robust pipeline and increased visibility into 2027.
- Major $250M+ orders for indium phosphide laser manufacturing tools, with shipments starting Q3 2026 and ramping in Q1 2027.
- Expansion of manufacturing capacity underway to meet rising demand, especially for Spector IBD and wet processing systems.
- Pending merger with Axcelis progressing, awaiting only China antitrust approval, expected to close H2 2026.


### Financial highlights

- Semiconductor revenue was $109M (69% of total), down 1% sequentially; compound semiconductor revenue was $19M (12%), down 6%; data storage revenue was $10M (6%), flat; scientific/other revenue was $20M (13%), down 16%.
- Q1 gross margin was 36%; operating expenses were $49M; net income was $9M; diluted EPS was $0.14.
- Cash and short-term investments ended at $383M, down $7M; cash flow from operations was $8M; CapEx was $5M.
- Gross margin decline attributed to an $8M impact from a delayed LSA system shipment to China due to BIS licensing.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q2 2026 revenue expected between $170M–$190M; gross margin 38%–40%; OpEx $52M–$55M; net income $12M–$21M; diluted EPS $0.20–$0.32.
- Full-year 2026 revenue guidance reiterated at $740M–$800M, with EPS $1.50–$1.85; growth expected to accelerate in H2.
- Semiconductor business projected to grow mid-teens in 2026, with further acceleration in 2027.
- Compound semiconductor business expected to grow ~50% in 2026, driven by silicon photonics and AI data center demand.


### Segment performance

- Semiconductor: Growth driven by AI, high-performance computing, and advanced packaging; headwinds from mature node China business.
- Compound semiconductor: Strong momentum in indium phosphide lasers, MicroLEDs, AR/VR, and GaN power; significant orders secured.
- Data storage: Order book full for 2026, with business expected to double over 2025 and remain strong into H1 2027.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Q1 gross margin: 36%.
- Q1 operating expenses: $49M.
- Q1 net income: $9M.
- Q1 diluted EPS: $0.14.
- Effective tax rate: 11%.
- Cash and short-term investments: $383M.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Pending Axcelis merger subject to remaining China antitrust approval.
- Export restrictions impacted Q1 revenue and gross margin due to delayed China shipments.
- Declining mature node business in China expected to be a headwind in 2026.


### Significant events and developments

- $250M+ in orders for indium phosphide laser manufacturing tools, with major ramp in 2027.
- Spector IBD capacity expansion planned to increase 10x by early 2027, with further potential doubling.
- Major pilot line order for 300mm GaN system from a leading IDM, targeting AI data center applications.


### Capital allocation and financing

- CapEx for Q1 was $5M.
- Customer deposits increased by $19M to $69M.


### Market and industry conditions

- AI and high-performance computing driving capital spending and technology adoption across logic, memory, and advanced packaging.
- Industry inflection in silicon photonics and transition to co-packaged optics for data centers.
- WFE environment expected to remain positive into 2027, with strong growth in advanced packaging and compound semiconductors.
