# Bath & Body Works, Inc. — Q2 2027 (2026-08-26)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Second quarter results exceeded expectations, with net sales down 2.3% year-over-year, outperforming guidance, and adjusted EPS of $0.62, boosted by $80 million in tariff refunds; excluding this, adjusted EPS was $0.31, still above guidance.
- The Consumer First Formula strategy is showing tangible progress, with sequential improvement in body care, a return to digital growth, and accelerated expansion in new distribution channels.
- The company is narrowing full-year net sales guidance to down 4% to down 2.5% and raising adjusted EPS guidance to $2.60–$2.80.
- 2026 remains an investment year, with a focus on product innovation, demand creation, and consistent execution to drive sustainable growth and target revenue growth in 2027.


### Financial highlights

- Net sales for Q2 were $1.5 billion, down 2.3% year-over-year, above the guidance range.
- Adjusted operating income was $225 million (14.8% of net sales); adjusted EPS was $0.62, including tariff refunds.
- Gross profit rate was 45.7%; excluding tariff refunds, it was 40.4%, down 90 bps year-over-year.
- Inventory ended the quarter down 10% year-over-year, with clean forward inventories.
- Free cash flow guidance raised to $650 million for the year, reflecting higher earnings and lower capex.


### Outlook and guidance

- Full-year net sales expected to decline 4% to 2.5%; adjusted EPS guidance raised to $2.60–$2.80.
- Full-year adjusted gross profit rate expected at 43.3%, including tariff refund benefit.
- Third quarter net sales expected to decline 5% to 2.5%, with adjusted EPS forecasted at $0.07–$0.12.
- Capital expenditures for 2026 expected at $240 million, down from prior guidance.


### Segment performance

- Body care declined mid-single digits but improved sequentially, driven by Everyday Luxuries and Fruit Fusion launches.
- Home fragrance declined low single digits due to strategic assortment reduction; soaps and sanitizers were flat.
- Direct channel net sales grew 3% year-over-year, benefiting from free shipping threshold changes and digital investments.
- International and other net sales rose 24.9% year-over-year, with international retail sales up high single digits.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted SGA rate was 30.8% in Q2, better than expected.
- Adjusted net non-operating expense for the year expected at $217 million.
- Adjusted tax rate expected at 26.8%; weighted average diluted shares at 203 million.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Ongoing tariff and input cost pressures, including potential Canadian retaliatory tariffs, are being actively managed.
- Store traffic remains pressured, and body care performance, while improving, is still below potential.
- Macro environment and consumer behavior are assumed to remain unchanged in guidance.


### Significant events and developments

- Received $80 million in tariff refunds in Q2, which boosted EPS and gross margin.
- Launched Fruit Fusion body care franchise, exceeding sales expectations and driving customer engagement.
- Expanded distribution through Amazon (sales tripled sequentially) and Ulta Beauty (launched in 600 stores), with no observed cannibalization of own channels.
- Completed a merchandising reset across the full store fleet, improving store experience and conversion.
- Exited the home care category to focus on core franchises.
- Redeemed $250 million of 2029 notes, reducing interest expense.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Year-to-date capital expenditures at $98 million; full-year capex expected at $240 million.
- Returned $40 million to shareholders via dividends in Q2.
- Free cash flow guidance raised to $650 million.
- Early redemption of $250 million in bonds, with interest savings reflected in outlook.


### Market and industry conditions

- Digital business returned to growth after years of decline, with improved customer acquisition and engagement.
- Amazon and Ulta channels are attracting new, younger, and more affluent consumers.
- International retail sales grew 9% year-over-year despite geopolitical challenges.
- Store traffic remains pressured, particularly in malls, but off-mall locations and international expansion are performing well.
