# Bath & Body Works, Inc. — Q1 2027 (2026-05-27)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Net sales declined 3.2% year-over-year to $1.4 billion, with adjusted EPS of $0.32, both ahead of expectations but below internal standards.
- Underlying business trends remain pressured, especially in body care, but early evidence supports the Consumer First Formula strategy.
- Transformation roadmap is on track, with full-year 2026 net sales and EPS guidance reaffirmed.


### Financial highlights

- Adjusted gross profit rate was 42.7%, down 270 basis points year-over-year, mainly due to tariffs, inflation, and category mix.
- Adjusted operating income was $151 million, representing 11% of net sales.
- Inventory ended the quarter down 10% versus prior year.
- Free cash flow for 2026 expected to be approximately $600 million, including a $66 million after-tax benefit from a litigation settlement.


### Outlook and guidance

- Full-year 2026 net sales guidance: down 4.5% to down 2.5%; adjusted EPS guidance: $2.40–$2.65.
- Q2 net sales expected to decline 5% to 3%, with EPS of $0.20–$0.25.
- Guidance assumes elevated energy prices and no benefit from potential tariff refunds.
- International net sales in Q2 expected to be down low to mid single digits due to Middle East conflict, but international retail sales to grow low double digits.


### Segment performance

- Body care declined mid-teens, mainly due to assortment changes and a shift toward accessories in collaborations.
- Home fragrance declined low single digits; candles performed slightly above expectations, offset by softness in Wallflowers.
- Soaps and sanitizers grew low single digits, driven by new moisturizing and revitalizing formulas.
- U.S. and Canadian store sales were $1.1 billion, down 4.3%; direct channel sales were $246 million, down 1.5%.
- International and other net sales were $70 million, up 9% year-over-year.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted gross profit rate: 42.7% (down 270 bps year-over-year).
- Adjusted SG&A rate: 31.7% (up 100 bps year-over-year).
- Mix-adjusted AUR was flat versus prior year.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Ongoing inflationary pressures, especially in crude oil, and tariffs remain headwinds.
- International sales outlook impacted by Middle East conflict.
- No tariff refunds included in guidance; outcome remains uncertain.


### Significant events and developments

- CFO Eva Boratto is departing; Tom Javitch appointed as interim CFO.
- $284 million in January 2027 notes redeemed in Q1.
- Opened 13 new North American stores (all off-mall), closed 17 (primarily in malls); ended quarter with 579 international locations.
- Appointment of Veronique as Chief Brand and Product Officer to unify creative functions and accelerate innovation.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Q1 capital expenditures totaled $49 million; $40 million returned to shareholders via dividends.
- 2026 capex expected at $270 million, focused on real estate, product assortment, and logistics upgrades.


### Market and industry conditions

- Off-mall stores outperformed mall locations, with high-tier malls performing best.
- Promotional environment remains value-focused, but strategy is shifting toward fewer, more meaningful events.
- Amazon launch in Q1 is driving double-digit week-over-week growth and attracting younger, more affluent consumers.
- Consumer remains value-seeking, but innovation and brand relevance are key to future pricing power.
