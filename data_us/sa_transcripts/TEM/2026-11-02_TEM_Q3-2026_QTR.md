# Tempus AI, Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-11-02)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 revenue was $411.5M, up 23% YoY, with growth across diagnostics and data/apps; Adjusted EBITDA was $25.6M.
- 2026 revenue guidance increased to $1.60B–$1.605B; Adjusted EBITDA guidance remained approximately $65M.


### Financial highlights

- Revenue reached $411.5M, up 23% YoY.
- Adjusted EBITDA was $25.6M; cash and marketable securities totaled $854.4M at quarter-end.


### Outlook and guidance

- 2026 revenue guidance increased to $1.60B–$1.605B; 2026 Adjusted EBITDA expectation remained approximately $65M.
- Management views sustained long-term growth of 25% as achievable, while noting individual years could grow faster due to oncology ASP increases or strong data demand.
- Oncology xT pricing changes begin Jan. 1, 2027, with an expected ~$80M uplift in oncology ASPs in 2027; additional xF uplift depends on FDA approval.
- MRD sales efforts are expected to increase in 2027; broader scaling depends on reimbursement and positive gross profit per test, potentially around mid-2027.


### Segment performance

- Diagnostics revenue was $303.5M, up 20% YoY; oncology revenue grew 28% on 32% volume growth.
- Oncology revenue was $172.6M and hereditary cancer testing revenue was $111.3M.
- Data and apps revenue was $108.1M, up 33% YoY; Insights, the data licensing segment, grew 29%.
- Hereditary unit growth returned to low double digits in September and October; management expects no material PAMA impact on hereditary cancer rates.
- xF order requisitions rose 25% YoY; concurrent and serial testing trends are supporting liquid testing demand.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Oncology unit volume grew 32% YoY, while data and apps revenue grew 33% YoY.
- Adjusted EBITDA reached $25.6M; management said incremental gross profit dollars are currently allocated roughly two-thirds to reinvestment and one-third to the bottom line.
- The data platform held more than 600 PB, adding more than 50 PB in Q3, and connected to approximately 8,000 providers.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Forward-looking results are subject to risks and uncertainties that could cause actual results to differ materially.
- MRD testing currently generates losses per test; expanded commercialization depends on reimbursement progress and reaching positive per-test gross profit.
- Personalis is loss-making, and its expenses would weigh on results following closing; the transaction remained subject to shareholder approval.
- Expansion into additional diagnostic categories depends on the reimbursement landscape; xF pricing uplift also depends on FDA approval.


### Significant events and developments

- A Bristol Myers Squibb collaboration will license oncology data and models for up to $137.5M over five years; a separate BMS, Pathos and Tempus collaboration pays $55M over five years for Alzheimer’s datasets and models.
- The two data collaborations are expected to generate $192.5M of additional data and applications revenue over approximately five years.
- A multi-year collaboration with Moderna and Merck will support potential commercialization of an individualized neoantigen therapy evaluated with KEYTRUDA.
- Regulatory review of the Personalis acquisition was cleared; a shareholder vote was scheduled for Nov. 30.
- The TIME clinical trial matching business shifted to an enterprise model, with a wait list for the network; management expects continued growth but not a billion-dollar business.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Quarter-end cash and marketable securities were $854.4M.
- Roughly two-thirds of incremental gross profit dollars are being reinvested, with one-third flowing to the bottom line; management expects that allocation to shift over time toward greater EBITDA contribution.
- Some reinvestment is expected to absorb Personalis losses; management expects Adjusted EBITDA improvement even after the acquisition closes.
- Management cited oncology ASP increases and long-term biopharma agreements as sources of growth funding, with investment levels adjustable as needed.


### Market and industry conditions

- Hereditary testing remains underpenetrated: current guidelines and reimbursement cover about 70M U.S. lives, while roughly 2M tests are performed annually.
- Liquid testing demand is supported by concurrent profiling, with 10%–20% of therapeutically relevant mutations potentially missed by using only solid or liquid testing.
- The MRD market was estimated at more than $20B; broader adoption is tied to reimbursement and positive per-test economics.
- Data demand is expanding beyond oncology, highlighted by the Alzheimer’s collaboration; management cited a 50M-patient dataset, with more than 80% of patients outside oncology.
