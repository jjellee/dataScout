# FormFactor, Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-10-28)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 revenue, gross margin, and EPS each exceeded the high end of outlook; revenue grew nearly 40% YoY with essentially the same manufacturing footprint.
- Fourth consecutive quarter of record revenue; Q4 outlook midpoint implies FY2026 revenue above $1 billion.
- Broad-based demand across high-performance compute, advanced packaging, DRAM, and photonics; 2027 growth depends on ramping Farmers Branch capacity.


### Financial highlights

- Q3 revenue was $280.5M, up $22.3M or 9% QoQ and nearly 40% YoY; $10.5M above the midpoint of the outlook range.
- GAAP gross margin was 54.7%, versus 50.7% in Q2; non-GAAP gross margin was 56.5%, up 320 bps QoQ.
- GAAP net income was $73.8M, or $0.93 diluted EPS; non-GAAP net income was $82.3M, or $1.04 diluted EPS, versus $65M and $0.82 in Q2.
- Non-GAAP gross margin included ~$10.5M, or ~380 bps, from primarily tariff refunds and favorable quantum sub-component costs; excluding these non-recurring items, margin was 52.5%-53%.
- Q3 free cash flow was $64.8M, versus $52.6M in Q2; operating cash flow was $104.3M.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q4 revenue expected at $285M ±$10M; non-GAAP gross margin 54% ±150 bps, operating expenses ~$69M ±$2M, and diluted EPS $0.95 ±$0.09.
- Q4 non-GAAP gross margin outlook includes tariff refunds, better mix, and higher volumes; expected tax rate is 15%-19%.
- Non-GAAP gross margin baseline is ~53% at Q3 volumes; 2027 margins are expected to remain near that level as volume and efficiency gains offset temporary factory-ramp inefficiencies.
- Farmers Branch is expected to reach ~$400M annual probe-card production capability exiting 2027, with capacity ramping roughly linearly during the year; capability is not a revenue forecast.
- 2026 TRITON revenue is expected to exceed $30M, versus an initial $10M-$20M outlook; demand growth is expected to continue in 2027.


### Segment performance

- Foundry & Logic grew 10% QoQ on broad strength across applications; Q4 growth is expected, supported by data-center CPUs, GPUs, custom ASICs, and other designs.
- Probe cards shipped in multimillion-dollar volumes for one GPU design at the leading foundry; meaningful share growth across GPU and custom ASIC designs is targeted for 2027, subject to capacity.
- DRAM probe-card revenue was essentially flat QoQ at Q2 record levels; mix shifted from HBM toward DDR5, while record DRAM revenue coincided with record gross margin.
- Systems revenue reached a record $59.5M, up $11M or 23% QoQ, driven by TRITON photonic systems; segment revenue grew 20% sequentially.
- TRITON demand spans co-packaged optics, near-package optics, and pluggables; photonics revenue growth is supported by optical probing and broader application coverage.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Non-GAAP operating expenses were $64.4M, or 23% of revenue, versus $65.7M, or 25.4%, in Q2; Q3 included $6.4M of pre-production ramp costs.
- Probe-card gross margin rose ~90 bps to 55.3%; Systems gross margin increased by more than 10 percentage points to 61%.
- GAAP and non-GAAP effective tax rates were 15.5% and 17.6%, respectively; tariff refunds contributed about $0.09 to Q3 EPS.
- Cash and investments totaled $399M at quarter-end, up $50M; Q3 free cash flow was $64.8M.
- Long-term target model: $1.6B revenue, 55% gross margin, and $5 non-GAAP EPS.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Existing manufacturing capacity is nearing its limits after substantial output gains from process improvements; capacity constraints are moderating near-term growth potential.
- New-factory ramp may bring temporary inefficiencies from added staffing and spending; execution must preserve product quality while bringing capacity online.
- DRAM probe-card mix between HBM and DDR is expected to fluctuate with customer wafer-start decisions and bit-constrained market conditions.
- Forward-looking results remain exposed to macroeconomic and geopolitical conditions, supply disruptions, tariffs, export controls, demand volatility, and product-development and production execution.


### Significant events and developments

- Farmers Branch, a nearly 300,000-square-foot facility, qualified assembly and test and shipped its first production unit; initial production shipments began ahead of the original expected timing.
- Teradyne partnership expansion includes a $300M investment; together with the multiyear Advantest collaboration, it supports development and production for next-generation semiconductor applications and an open ecosystem.
- Keystone Photonics technology and team are being more tightly integrated to advance optical probing solutions across photonics applications.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Farmers Branch 2026 cash CapEx is now expected at $100M-$110M, below the prior $140M-$170M estimate due to spending timing and favorable payment terms; project scope and total cost remain aligned with plan.
- Farmers Branch pre-production expense is expected at $25M-$30M in 2026; in Q4, ~$5M of startup costs is expected to move from operating expenses to cost of revenue, with another $5M remaining in operating expenses.
- The $300M Teradyne investment strengthens the balance sheet without changing capital-allocation priorities; cash is directed to reinvestment, targeted acquisitions, and share repurchases to offset stock-based compensation dilution.
- No shares were repurchased in Q3; $70.9M remained available under the $75M program authorized in April 2025.


### Market and industry conditions

- High-performance compute and advanced packaging demand is expanding across HBM, GPUs, CPUs, custom ASICs, and networking; advanced packaging and higher test complexity increase probe-card performance needs.
- HBM remains a growth driver into 2026 and 2027; no significant impact from HBM de-specification was observed, and the HBM4E/HBM5 design funnel and customer forecasts support continued growth.
- Photonics adoption is emerging as copper interconnect faces speed, heat, and energy limits; customers are expanding beyond CPO into NPO and pluggable applications.
- Strong current demand makes probe-card capacity the limiting factor; Farmers Branch is designed as a flexible, modular platform to add capacity as market demand develops.
