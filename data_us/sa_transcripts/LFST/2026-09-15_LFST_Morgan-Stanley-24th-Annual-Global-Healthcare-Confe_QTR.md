# LifeStance Health Group, Inc. — Morgan Stanley 24th Annual Global Healthcare Conference (2026-09-15)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Market overview and positioning

- Outpatient mental health remains highly fragmented, with many small or solo practices and limited insurance acceptance, leading to access challenges and inefficiencies.
- Demand for mental health services is rising as social stigma decreases, increasing pressure on the system.
- The organization is the largest national outpatient mental health provider, with 8,500 clinicians and 600 centers across 34 states, recently expanding into Iowa.


### Addressing supply-demand imbalance and productivity

- Despite clinician shortages, thousands of appointment slots go unused weekly due to inefficiencies.
- Clinician base grows near double digits annually, improving access for payers.
- In-person care is prioritized alongside virtual, meeting payer needs and differentiating from virtual-only entrants.
- Productivity initiatives include increasing clinician hours, optimizing scheduling, and leveraging AI for patient intake, resulting in a 7% year-over-year productivity improvement.
- Utilization of clinician capacity is about 70%, with ongoing efforts to increase this through data-driven best practices and nationwide rollout of successful retention strategies.


### Revenue, reimbursement, and growth outlook

- Revenue per visit reached the high end of low to mid-single-digit targets, driven by Medicare-linked contracts and payer negotiations.
- Medicare reimbursement for mental health saw a high single-digit increase for 2026, with expectations for continued low to mid-single-digit growth in coming years.
- Revenue growth is guided at 20% for the year, with mid-teen visit growth and a four-year CAGR of 19%.
- Growth is supported by improved reimbursement, double-digit clinician additions, and significant market expansion opportunities.


### Technology and operational investments

- Transitioning to a new, industrial-strength electronic health record (EHR) system next year, expected to enhance patient outcomes, clinician experience, and operational efficiency.
- AI and digital tools are used for intake, documentation, revenue cycle management, and patient engagement, with a focus on both growth and efficiency.
- EHR will enable measurement-based care, care pathways, and data-driven clinical decision support to improve outcomes and support value-based care.


### Service mix and specialty services

- 70% of visits remain virtual, with local dynamics influencing the in-person/virtual mix; in-person expected to rise as specialty services expand.
- Specialty services, including neuropsych testing, TMS, and SPRAVATO, are growing rapidly, with revenue expected to increase from $50M to $70M this year, mainly from TMS and SPRAVATO.
- Rollout of specialty services is deliberate, leveraging internal referrals and operational leaders, with payer reimbursement variability influencing geographic expansion.
- The organization is preparing for future psychedelic therapies, building scalable models as new treatments gain approval.


### Financial performance and capital allocation

- Adjusted EBITDA margins are guided at 13% this year, with a target of 15% by 2028 and a long-term goal of 20%, driven by center margin expansion and G&A leverage.
- Capital allocation priorities are organic growth (including 20-30 new centers this year), small tuck-in M&A for geographic expansion, and share repurchases, supported by strong free cash flow and a robust balance sheet.
