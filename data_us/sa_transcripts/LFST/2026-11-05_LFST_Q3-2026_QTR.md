# LifeStance Health Group, Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-11-05)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 revenue grew 25% to $456M; Adjusted EBITDA margin reached 15% for the second consecutive quarter, and net income was $22M. Full-year guidance was raised across revenue, Center Margin, and Adjusted EBITDA.
- Clinician base reached 8,863, up 11%, with 321 net adds in Q3; leaders cited strong recruiting and stable retention as drivers.


### Financial highlights

- Revenue of $456M increased 25%; 2.7M visits rose 17%, and total revenue per visit of $170 increased 7%, primarily on payer rate increases.
- Center Margin was $161M, up 38%, or 35.4% of revenue; Adjusted EBITDA rose 70% to $68M, or 15% of revenue.
- Net income was $22M, a $21M improvement YoY; free cash flow was $22M versus $17M in Q3 2025.
- Visits per average clinician increased 5% YoY, supported by early patient engagement and ongoing productivity gains.


### Outlook and guidance

- FY2026 revenue guidance raised to $1.741B-$1.761B, implying 23% growth at midpoint; Center Margin to $599M-$613M and Adjusted EBITDA to $242M-$250M.
- FY2026 Adjusted EBITDA midpoint implies a 14% margin and ~3 percentage points of YoY expansion; stock-based compensation expected at $60M-$70M.
- Q4 guidance: revenue $446M-$466M, Center Margin $149M-$163M, and Adjusted EBITDA $56M-$64M; planned investments include patient acquisition, AI, clinical excellence, clinician compensation, and center refreshes.
- 2027 revenue growth expected toward the low end of the long-term mid-teens range, with the EHR transition reducing growth by 1-2 percentage points; no carryover impact expected in 2028.
- Margins expected to expand in 2027 and beyond; leaders reiterated a long-term Adjusted EBITDA margin range of 15%-20%, with 20% not a ceiling.


### Segment performance

- Specialty services revenue expected to grow 40% in 2026 to ~$70M from $50M in 2025; growth is driven by TMS and SPRAVATO, with 2027 growth expected to remain meaningfully above the overall business.
- SPRAVATO outcomes: 74% of treatment-resistant depression patients had clinically meaningful severity reduction after 12 weeks; TMS: 61% after at least 30 sessions.
- TMS expanded to five additional sites and SPRAVATO to four in Q3, reaching 37 and 25 centers, respectively; rollout remains deliberate, at roughly a handful of each per quarter.
- De novo centers opened: eight in Q3 and 20 YTD; 20-30 openings planned for 2026, with a similar approximate pace considered for 2027.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Center Margin was 35.4% of revenue; Adjusted EBITDA margin was 15% in Q3, the second consecutive quarter at that level.
- Net leverage was 0.2x and gross leverage 1.2x at quarter-end.
- Total revenue per visit increased 7% in Q3; 2027 rate expectations were characterized as low-to-mid-single-digit growth.
- 2026 Adjusted EBITDA guidance implies ~3 percentage points of margin expansion YoY; longer-term margin expectations are 15%-20%.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- New EHR implementation is expected to temporarily reduce 2027 revenue growth by 1-2 percentage points; phased deployment across the clinician base is planned through year-end 2027.
- EHR implementation costs are estimated at $20M-$30M, either expensed as an Adjusted EBITDA item or capitalized.
- Payer reimbursement for SPRAVATO varies, and some payers do not yet recognize it as an approved therapy, potentially constraining service expansion.
- New therapies face unresolved regulatory, payer, reimbursement, and adoption hurdles; timing and commercial contribution remain uncertain.
- Clinician retention is stable overall, with lower retention among new clinicians; management is targeting a 5%+ long-term improvement.


### Significant events and developments

- Completed a tuck-in acquisition in Iowa, bringing operations to 34 states; management expects one to two tuck-ins per quarter, including in 2027.
- EHR transition preparation includes workflow standardization, data conversion, integration of point solutions, testing, and a phased rollout beginning with one state.
- Published outcomes data for TMS and SPRAVATO in treatment-resistant depression; management expects the evidence to support payer discussions on coverage and reimbursement.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Quarter-end cash was $219M and net long-term debt was $256M; cash balance was after $25M of Q3 share repurchases.
- Free cash flow of $22M was affected by payroll timing and the annual 401(k) contribution; management described conversion as lumpy and did not provide a specific target.
- Some H2 Adjusted EBITDA outperformance is being reinvested in strategic priorities, including patient acquisition, technology, clinical excellence, clinician compensation, and center support.


### Market and industry conditions

- Outpatient mental health remains highly fragmented; current share of the clinician population is in the low single digits, leaving room for clinician recruitment and geographic expansion.
- Payers remain under financial pressure while facing client and member demand for outpatient mental health access; some are increasing focus on quality and outcomes.
- Measurement- and value-based contracting is limited to a few payers today, but management expects adoption to broaden in coming years.
- Demand for affordable mental health care, clinician value proposition, and specialty services were cited as 2027 growth tailwinds.
