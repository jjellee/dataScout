# Figure Technology Solutions, Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-11-12)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 marketplace volume was $5.8B, up 135% YoY reported and 107% standalone; Adjusted EBITDA margin reached 58%, with Figure Connect at 74% of volume.
- Kiavi added RTL and DSCR products, roughly $8B of marketplace volume, and an expanded addressable market; acquisition expected to be materially accretive with under-four-year unlevered payback.


### Financial highlights

- Net income was $89M, flat YoY; prior-year net income included a one-time $31M tax benefit.
- Adjusted EBITDA was $164M, up 91% YoY; margin expanded to 58% from 55%.
- Ecosystem and technology fees reached roughly 40% of revenue, more than tripling YoY.
- Net take rate was 3.5% including one month of Kiavi and 3.7% standalone; the standalone result exceeded prior guidance.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q4 marketplace volume guidance is $6.2B-$6.7B, with combined gross take rate of 3.1%-3.4%.
- Seasonal Q3-to-Q4 volume declines of 10%-15% are expected; lower seasonal volume and Kiavi integration are expected to reduce Q4 Adjusted EBITDA margin.
- Q4 includes $18M-$23M of incremental adjusted expenses from Kiavi versus Q3; margins are expected to improve meaningfully in 2027 as synergies and operating leverage build.
- $30M of Kiavi's $35M run-rate cost-synergy target was actioned at close; remaining actions are planned over the next 12 months.
- Management expects continued rapid Q4 growth at attractive economics and identifies 2027 as a year of partner upsell.


### Segment performance

- Organic consumer loan marketplace volume reached a record $5.1B; Kiavi contributed approximately $670M for September.
- Figure Connect reached 72% of standalone volume and 74% including Kiavi; fee-based, capital-light volume is gaining share.
- SMB volume exceeded a $500M run rate in Q3, with more than 150 partners; depository partnerships exceeded 20.
- Kiavi RTL volume was roughly $600M in September and DSCR volume roughly $60M; the products broaden the platform beyond HELOC into a $200B+ addressable market.
- Sierra's agentic loan officer assistant lifted funded dollars per approval by 29%; roughly $260M of loans were funded with Sierra involvement after launch.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted EBITDA margin rose to 58% from 55% YoY, progressing toward the medium-term 60% target.
- Connect gross take rate outlook for the next 6-12 months: 2.9%-3.4% for HELOC and 1.5%-2% for shorter-duration RTL.
- Approximately 50% of Adjusted EBITDA converts to cash inflow after income tax payments and unsecured-debt interest.
- Median HELOC funding time is 10 days versus an industry comparison of 45 days; the platform can reprice as markets move.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Rapid rate movements can temporarily pressure consumer funnels; higher rates also support HELOC demand as borrowers retain low-rate mortgages.
- Winter seasonality can reduce Q4 volume by 10%-15% sequentially; Kiavi's pre-acquisition Adjusted EBITDA margin was at or below 40%, weighing on near-term combined margins.
- DeFi financing economics are attractive, but available stablecoin borrowing capacity was estimated at $6.5B versus more than $2.5B monthly assets placed on-chain.
- Forward-looking results are subject to risks and uncertainties, including factors described in SEC filings.


### Significant events and developments

- Kiavi closed September 1 ahead of schedule; loans were tokenized, $30M of initial cost synergies actioned, and 12 existing partners onboarded with more than 30 in the pipeline.
- An expanded $400M share-repurchase plan was announced, starting the following day; repurchases may continue beyond the initial authorization.
- YLDS settlement had 28 marketplace participants; Q3 included $2.7B of loan sales and eight securitizations.
- Navra launched as an independent venture; the executive chairman cited a 10% ownership stake and its potential to broaden on-chain capital for loan financing.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Cash and cash equivalents totaled $1.59B after the Kiavi acquisition; $600M of rated unsecured debt was raised in Q3, and a $465M revolving credit facility was expected to close in November.
- A 10b5-1 plan will execute up to $400M of authorized share repurchases; $35M of employee-tax-related share retirements this year were separate from the program.
- Prime ended Q3 with $825M lender supply and $588M matched-offer balance, including approximately $200M of third-party borrowing.
- On-chain HELOC financing was roughly $600M at SOFR plus 120 bps; a AAA ABS issuance on-chain was planned for the month.


### Notes to the financial statements

- Prior-year net income included a one-time $31M tax benefit, primarily related to the merger with Figure Markets.
- Kiavi-related one-time M&A and restructuring costs totaled $22M in Q2 and Q3.
- Loan receivables of $644M included approximately $395M of loans placed as Prime collateral and $16M of Kiavi-acquired DSCR loans.


### Market and industry conditions

- Existing partners that were active in Q3 2025 generated over 40% more volume in Q3 2026; the partner base had more than $500B of 2025 originations per HMDA data.
- Marketplace investor demand remained resilient amid volatile capital markets; Q3 added AAA buyers New York Life and Nationwide Insurance, with whole-loan sales and securitizations used for best execution.
- Higher rates have increased partner interest in HELOCs, while purchase-market activity and Kiavi's RTL and DSCR products offer additional growth avenues.
