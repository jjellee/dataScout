# Figure Technology Solutions, Inc. — Goldman Sachs Communacopia + Technology Conference 2026 (2026-09-10)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Business evolution and strategic direction

- Transitioned from direct HELOC originator to a capital markets platform focused on blockchain and real-world asset tokenization, processing $1.7 billion in monthly home equity volume with 500 partners.
- Achieved significant cost and time savings in loan origination, reducing costs from $12,000 to $1,000 and processing times from 45 to 5 days.
- Pursuing growth in core mortgage markets and expanding into auto and small business asset classes by standardizing third-party assets and offering capital markets as a service.
- Emphasizing a capital-light, exchange-like business model, leveraging first-mover advantage in home equity and expanding into tokenized capital markets.
- Building a competitive moat through standardization, liquidity, and partnerships, including with depositories and Fannie Mae ecosystem.


### Growth drivers and partner ecosystem

- Growth fueled by B2B sales rigor, robust pipeline, and expansion across mortgage banks, servicers, depositories, small business lenders, fintech, and home improvement sectors.
- Announced partnership with Flagstar Bank, unlocking more depository relationships and expanding into small business lending with a $500 million run rate.
- Origination partner growth driven by both new and existing partners, with Figure Connect now accounting for 65% of volume and expected to rise.
- Large partners increasingly onboard directly to Figure Connect, attracted by its capital-light, high-margin model and improved partner economics.
- Ecosystem includes warehouse lenders like Texas Capital Bank and Western Alliance, making onboarding easier and supporting further Connect adoption.


### Product diversification and acquisitions

- Diversification into asset classes like auto, SMB, DSCR, and RTL loans, primarily by standardizing and tokenizing third-party originated assets.
- Acquired Kiavi, a market leader in residential transition loans, to expand into investor-owned housing, with plans to integrate Kiavi’s products into Figure Connect.
- Kiavi acquisition expected to contribute $100 million EBITDA with under four-year payback, leveraging Figure’s marketplace approach for broader partner access.
- Over 50% of Kiavi’s customers are recurring, and the joint venture with Sixth Street enables capital-light origination and ecosystem fee generation.
- Announced partnership with Agora in auto, planning the first securitization of tokenized auto loans in early Q4 to lower cost of capital and set industry benchmarks.


### Financial performance and business model

- Revenue growth over 100% in recent quarters, with ecosystem fees becoming the largest P&L line item.
- First lien HELOCs now comprise 20% of volume, with cost advantages driving market share gains from traditional Fannie Mae channels.
- Take rate evolution driven by shift to capital-light Figure Connect model, with lower take rates offset by higher margins and volume growth.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin reached 55% in Q2, with a medium-term target of 60%+ as the business scales and transitions further to an exchange model.
- Innovation and expansion into third-party assets and new products are expected to sustain high incremental margins and marketplace economics.


### Market positioning and future outlook

- Positioned as a market leader in home equity and tokenized capital markets, with a $35 trillion home equity opportunity and growing relevance in other asset classes.
- Partners adopting Figure grow their home equity business 2.6x faster than before, driven by technology and liquidity advantages.
- Announced partnership with Sierra to offer an AI loan officer assistant, leveraging platform simplicity and integrated capital markets technology.
- Focus remains on volume growth, partner flexibility, and expanding the standardized, liquid marketplace for a broad range of financial assets.
- Success metrics include diversification, partner and volume growth, and continued leadership in capital markets innovation.
