# Kopin Corporation — Q2 2026 (2026-08-10)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Achieved major milestones in the U.S. Army Color MicroLED program, exceeding technical and manufacturing targets and positioning for mid-2027 domestic production.
- Secured multiple new prototype orders for Sentinel FPV goggles tied to the U.S. government's Drone Dominance Program, with volume orders expected in late Q4 2026.
- Opened a new optics and photonics design center in Dallas, Texas, to accelerate Neural I/O and AI infrastructure solutions.
- Maintained strong balance sheet discipline and operational execution, with four consecutive quarters of on-time, in-full delivery and quality performance.


### Financial highlights

- Q2 2026 revenue was $12.7 million, up 51% year-over-year from $8.5 million, driven by government MicroLED grants, Fabric.AI collaboration, and higher R&D revenue.
- Product revenue was $7.6 million, nearly flat year-over-year, with higher defense offset by lower industrial sales.
- Non-product revenue rose to $5.1 million from $1 million, mainly from grants and collaborations.
- Loss from operations narrowed to $3.5 million from $5.5 million year-over-year; net income was $0.9 million versus a $5.2 million loss in Q2 2025, aided by investment gains and tax benefits.
- Cash and equivalents totaled $24.3 million, with $50.3 million including restricted cash and marketable securities.


### Outlook and guidance

- Expects to exceed prior full-year revenue guidance of $52–$60 million, with a strong second half and positive GAAP profitability and free cash flow in Q4 2026.
- Anticipates substantial revenue growth in 2027, with tens of millions in new orders from Sentinel FPV and initial Color MicroLED production, and $100 million revenue in sight for 2028.
- Neural I/O platform expected to generate $20–$30 million in development revenue in 2027, with production revenue likely in 2028.


### Segment performance

- Defense: Over $45 million in orders year-to-date, including thermal imaging, helmet-mounted displays, and DarkWAVE programs; many contracts are sole source or IDIQ, supporting recurring revenue.
- FPV Drones: Sentinel FPV goggles gaining traction with U.S. and international customers; production capacity being expanded with in-house OLED deposition.
- AI Infrastructure: Advancing Neural I/O platform with Fabric.AI, targeting 1.6 Tbps transceiver demo at CES 2027; strong inbound interest from major OEMs and semiconductor partners.
- Europe: Progress with Theon on DarkWAVE and display opportunities, opening new NATO-related doors.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Gross margin improved due to better product mix and stable fixed costs; cost of product revenues was 86% of net product revenues, down from 94% year-over-year.
- R&D expenses rose to $4.5 million from $1.9 million, reflecting higher funded research.
- SG&A expenses were $5.1 million, up slightly due to professional fees and performance-based compensation.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Forward-looking statements subject to risks including product demand, subsidiary performance, market conditions, and ongoing litigation.
- $24.2 million of restricted cash collateralizes a bond for the BlueRadios litigation appeal, with no updates expected until mid-2027.


### Significant events and developments

- Achieved three major milestones in the IBAS MicroLED program, exceeding Army expectations.
- Opened Dallas Design Center for optics and photonics, enhancing R&D and manufacturing for Neural I/O.
- Brought OLED deposition in-house, expected to improve gross margin by at least 15 points in 2027.
- Released first AI interconnect white paper, generating strong industry interest and new NDA signings.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Major capital investment in OLED deposition tool (under $10 million), with payments spread over several quarters.
- Comfortable cash position, deploying capital against quantifiable customer demand in MicroLED, FPV, OLED, and AI infrastructure.


### Market and industry conditions

- U.S. government increasingly mandates domestic sources for critical components, benefiting onshoring strategy.
- AI infrastructure market for MicroLED transmitters estimated at $63 billion in the U.S. by 2035.
- U.S. government restricting use of Chinese-made optical interconnects, supporting domestic optics demand.
