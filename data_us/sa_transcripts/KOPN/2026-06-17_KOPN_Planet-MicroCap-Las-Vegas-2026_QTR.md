# Kopin Corporation — Planet MicroCap Las Vegas 2026 (2026-06-17)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Company history and core business

- Originated as a founder-led, technology-driven company with a focus on microdisplays for military applications.
- Built a strong foundation in the defense sector, supplying tier 1 customers such as Collins Aerospace, Lockheed Martin, and the U.S. Army.
- Operates three facilities, with the main manufacturing site in Westborough, MA, and strategic expansion planned in Scotland.


### Technology portfolio and product differentiation

- Offers four types of proprietary microdisplays, including advanced monochrome and color MicroLEDs with industry-leading brightness.
- Integrated systems combine microdisplays, optics, and packaging, tailored for military and medical applications.
- Focuses on high-brightness displays for daylight military use, differentiating from competitors who only supply components.


### Strategic partnerships and growth initiatives

- Recent investments from partners like Theon and Fabric AI support infrastructure and product development.
- Collaboration with Theon enables entry into NATO markets and development of the DarkWAVE system, adding intelligence to night vision devices.
- Pursuing scalable manufacturing by designing modular products adaptable for multiple customers.


### Market opportunities and contracts

- Military contracts, including a $15.4M award for color MicroLEDs, position the company as a key supplier for the U.S. Army's next-generation helmet systems.
- Medical device business is growing, with new wireless headsets and global adoption in surgical applications.
- Entering the drone market with first-person viewer modules, targeting high-volume contracts through competitions like Drone Dominance.


### Innovation and forward-looking strategies

- Developing AI GPU interconnects using MicroLEDs to enable high-speed, low-heat data transmission, attracting significant industry interest.
- NeuralDisplay technology enables eye-tracking and data collection, advancing AI infrastructure capabilities.
- Expects substantial revenue growth from military and AI markets, with projections of $300–$500M over seven years from Army display contracts.
