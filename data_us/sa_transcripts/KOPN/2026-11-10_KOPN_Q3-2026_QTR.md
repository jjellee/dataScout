# Kopin Corporation — Q3 2026 (2026-11-10)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Revenue reached $18.2M, up 52% YoY and 43% QoQ; operating loss narrowed $3M sequentially to $0.5M, and operations generated cash.
- Funded development programs and domestic manufacturing investments advanced across color MicroLED, Neural I/O, OLED displays, and defense products.


### Financial highlights

- Q3 revenue was $18.2M vs. $12.0M YoY and $12.7M in Q2; product revenue was $8.8M vs. $10.7M YoY and $7.6M QoQ.
- Non-product revenue was $9.4M, including $5.4M of Fabric.AI R&D revenue and $3.1M of color MicroLED grant revenue.
- Net loss was $3.4M, or $0.02 per share, vs. net income of $4.1M, or $0.02 per share, in Q3 2025; Q3 included $2.7M of investment losses, largely non-cash.
- Q3 operating cash flow was $2.4M; 9M net cash used in operating activities was $2.7M.


### Outlook and guidance

- Strong Q4 revenue and progress toward sustainable GAAP profitability expected; Q4 GAAP profitability remains achievable under current revenue projections and spending plans.
- Management expects profitability to be sustainable through 2027 under its current model; 2027 revenue visibility is good, with ~50% already in backlog.
- Drone goggle revenue is still expected to be ~$10M in 2027; additional Gauntlet 2.5 testing has shifted volume timing.
- Neural I/O commercial productization revenue is targeted for 2028; production silicon is targeted for 2028, with CES and Photonics West demonstrations planned for early 2027.
- Remaining 2026 capex is forecast at $1.0M–$1.5M; current liquidity is expected to fund operations beyond Q4 2027.


### Segment performance

- Color MicroLED received an additional $18.6M sole-source IBAS award, bringing total funding to $34M; U.S. production remains targeted for mid-2027.
- MicroLED was demonstrated for Soldier Borne Mission Command ahead of schedule; prototype orders were received, with potential program revenue described as hundreds of millions over its life.
- Neural I/O link testing began; customer bids and the opportunity pipeline expanded, with government and defense agency pipeline exceeding several hundred million dollars.
- OLED defense orders included a $2.4M European thermal weapon sight program; deliveries began in Q4 over six months, with OLED deposition expected online in early 2027.
- European defense orders were cited at ~$8.5M year to date; Drone Dominance phase additions delayed FPV volume timing, while more than a dozen customers placed prototype headset orders.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Product cost was $6.4M, or 73% of product revenue, vs. 79% YoY and 86% in Q2; favorable one-time items and lower scrap contributed, and the benefit is not expected to recur fully.
- Management expects normalized production gross margin in the low 20% range; the Q3 product cost ratio is not considered representative of future periods.
- Operating expenses were $12.3M, 68% of revenue, down 800 bps QoQ; excluding funded R&D costs, operating expenses were 43% of revenue.
- SG&A was 37% of revenue vs. 40% in Q2; operating loss was $0.5M, a $3M sequential improvement.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Drone Dominance awards and FPV volume timing remain uncertain following slower program progress and the addition of Gauntlet 2.5 testing.
- Q3 product cost improvement included favorable one-time items that are not expected to recur fully, creating uncertainty around future cost ratios.
- The BlueRadios federal appeal remains pending, with no further developments expected until approximately mid-2027; the related accrual was unchanged.
- Forward-looking results are subject to demand, subsidiary performance, market conditions, and other risks; actual outcomes may differ from expectations.


### Significant events and developments

- A Massachusetts grant of $0.8M will support advanced Westborough equipment for MicroLED and Neural I/O manufacturing; the facility expansion adds 10,000–15,000 sq. ft. of manufacturing capacity by mid-2027.
- A new adjacent 30,000 sq. ft. facility will become the headquarters, freeing existing space for manufacturing; a $1.4M tax credit was cited.
- Neural I/O demonstrations are planned for CES and SPIE Photonics West in early 2027; the CES demonstration is expected to show a bandwidth pathway of at least 1.6 terabits.
- A second Fabric.AI white paper modeled 1.3x–1.6x greater useful training throughput per GPU, equivalent to 23%–38% less training time for the same workload in a 10,000-GPU system.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Cash and cash equivalents were $25.8M at quarter-end; total cash, restricted cash, and marketable securities were $51.3M.
- Q3 capex was $1.4M for domestic MicroLED and OLED manufacturing; remaining 2026 capex is expected at $1.0M–$1.5M.
- Restricted cash included $25.5M, of which $24.2M collateralized the supersedeas bond for the litigation appeal.
- The adjacent office lease involves only a minimal increase in operating lease costs and supports future manufacturing expansion.


### Notes to the financial statements

- Q3 2025 included a $5.1M litigation accrual reduction and ~$3.3M reduction in accrued legal fees, making YoY SG&A, operating income, and net income comparisons less meaningful.
- The Q3 2026 net loss included ~$2.7M of investment losses, largely non-cash fair-value changes in strategic equity investments.


### Market and industry conditions

- Defense backlog is supported by programs with congressional budget support through 2030; sole-source and IDIQ contracts provide ordering flexibility and multi-year revenue visibility.
- AI infrastructure discussions are emphasizing data-center utilization, total system cost, power and water use, and interconnect efficiency; Neural I/O is being bid for CPO and NPO applications.
- European defense opportunities are increasing across aviation helmet displays, thermal weapon sights, and DarkWAVE; the Theon partnership continues.
- Domestic manufacturing capability is positioned to support U.S. defense and government Neural I/O applications subject to FCC production requirements.
