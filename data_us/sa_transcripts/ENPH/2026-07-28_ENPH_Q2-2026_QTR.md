# Enphase Energy, Inc. — Q2 2026 (2026-07-28)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q2 2026 revenue reached $291.9 million, with 1.59 million microinverters and 113.8 MWh of batteries shipped, and free cash flow of $25.9 million.
- U.S. revenue declined 3% sequentially, while Europe saw a 35% sequential revenue increase, driven by battery adoption.
- Channel inventory for batteries normalized; microinverter inventory slightly elevated.
- Safe harbor revenue was $84.3 million in Q2, up from $34.5 million in Q1.


### Financial highlights

- Non-GAAP gross margin was 46.8% (up from 43.9% in Q1); GAAP gross margin was 60% (up from 35.5% in Q1), aided by tariff refunds.
- Non-GAAP operating income was $56.7 million; GAAP operating income was $51.5 million.
- Non-GAAP net income was $61.5 million; GAAP net income was $36.1 million.
- Non-GAAP EPS was $0.46; GAAP EPS was $0.27.
- Cash, equivalents, and marketable securities totaled $937.7 million at quarter end.
- $41 million in tariff refunds received in Q2, with an additional $11 million after quarter end.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q3 2026 revenue expected between $290 million and $320 million, including $75 million in safe harbor revenue.
- Battery shipments forecasted at 130–150 MWh for Q3.
- GAAP gross margin guidance: 42%–45%; non-GAAP: 44%–47%, both including ~2 percentage points tariff impact.
- Non-GAAP operating expenses expected at $76–$80 million for Q3.
- Global sell-through in Q3 expected to increase 10% over Q2.


### Segment performance

- U.S. revenue mix was 78%; international 22%.
- U.S. sell-through in Q2 declined 7% sequentially and 34% year-over-year, reflecting higher interest rates and tax credit expiration.
- Europe saw strong sequential growth: Netherlands battery activations up 102%, France up 34%, Germany microinverter and battery activations up 35% and 27%, respectively.
- Propel TPO offering expanded to six states, targeting 12 by Q3 end, with originations running at ~200 per week and aiming for 500 by year-end.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Q2 non-GAAP gross margin: 46.8%; GAAP gross margin: 60%.
- Q2 non-GAAP operating margin: 19.4%; GAAP operating margin: 17.7%.
- Q2 non-GAAP EPS: $0.46; GAAP EPS: $0.27.
- Free cash flow: $25.9 million; operating cash flow: $40.3 million.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Macroeconomic uncertainty, including interest rates, continues to impact distributor caution and U.S. residential demand.
- Limited visibility on timing of IRS refunds for PTCs; future PTCs to be sold rather than direct pay.
- Regulatory and tariff changes, including reciprocal tariffs and potential bans on Chinese inverters, could affect supply chain and market share.


### Significant events and developments

- Fifth-generation IQ Battery G5 showcased, with shipments expected in Q4 2026; 50% higher energy density and 40% lower cost per kWh than previous gen.
- IQ Vault commercial battery and IQ9N microinverter launched; smart thermostat and bidirectional EV charger development progressing.
- Data center-focused IQ Solid-State Transformer (SST) development advancing, with pilots in 2027 and commercial shipments in 2028.
- $150 million PTC tax credit transfer agreement announced.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Capital expenditures were $14.4 million in Q2.
- $193.5 million of PTCs on balance sheet as of June 30, 2026; future PTCs to be monetized via sales.


### Market and industry conditions

- U.S. residential solar market stabilized but remains ~30% below prior year levels; storage and commercial solar showing stronger signals.
- European market increasingly battery-led, with regulatory changes (e.g., net metering phase-out) driving demand.
- Anticipated growth in small commercial and data center segments, with new products targeting these markets.
