# Super Micro Computer, Inc. — Q1 2027 (2026-11-03)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q1 FY27 revenue reached $19.9B, up 298% YoY and 79% QoQ, above preliminary and original outlook; ~$2.3B reflected deployments delayed from earlier periods.
- Non-GAAP gross margin was 12.8%, 330 bps higher YoY and above guidance; FY27 revenue outlook rose to $72B–$82B.


### Financial highlights

- GAAP gross margin was 12.7%; non-GAAP gross margin of 12.8% exceeded the 10.4%–10.8% outlook, but was down 480 bps QoQ from 17.6%.
- GAAP diluted EPS was $1.75 and non-GAAP diluted EPS was $1.81, both above guidance; GAAP basic EPS was $1.92.
- GAAP operating expenses were $477M, up 67% YoY; non-GAAP operating expenses were $384M, up 89% YoY.
- Operating cash use was $434M; capex was $47M and free cash flow was negative $481M.


### Outlook and guidance

- FY27 revenue guidance increased to $72B–$82B from $65B–$72B; the range excludes a repeat of Q1's ~$2.3B prior-period deployment catch-up.
- FY27 gross margin guidance is 11.8%–12.2%; second-half margin is expected below first half as AI mix moves toward the upper end of 70%–80% of revenue.
- Q2 FY27 sales guidance is $19.5B–$21.5B, gross margin 12%–12.5%, GAAP diluted EPS $1.59–$1.83 and non-GAAP diluted EPS $1.66–$1.90.
- Q2 guidance assumes capex of $70M–$80M, tax rates near 21%, preferred dividends of ~$75M and net other expense of ~$58M.
- Management expects continued sequential underlying demand growth; Q2 cash conversion cycle is expected within ±10% of Q1.


### Segment performance

- AI solutions contributed ~71% of Q1 revenue versus 59% in Q4; FY27 mix is expected at 70%–80%.
- Enterprise and channel revenue was $4.9B, 25% of revenue, up 229% YoY and down 12% QoQ; OEM appliance and large data center revenue was $14.7B, 74%, up 321% YoY and 167% QoQ.
- U.S. revenue was 80% of the total, Europe 11%, Asia 6% and rest of world 3%; U.S. revenue grew 771% YoY and 103% QoQ.
- 5G, telco and edge grew significantly YoY and QoQ; management cited stronger CPU demand across Intel, AMD, NVIDIA Vera and Arm solutions.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Non-GAAP operating margin was 10.9% versus 14.3% in Q4; Q4 and Q1 combined non-GAAP operating margin was ~12.1% versus ~5.4% a year earlier.
- Across Q4 and Q1, revenue was ~$31.1B and non-GAAP gross margin ~14.5%, versus ~$10.8B and ~9.5% in the same two quarters a year earlier.
- Cash conversion cycle improved 52 days QoQ to 97 days; inventory days fell 45 days to 74 and DSO improved 23 days to 36.
- Inventory and production turnaround improved from ~100 days to 74 days over three quarters; target is 50 days over coming quarters.
- Effective tax rate was 21.4% on both GAAP and non-GAAP bases.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Component shortages, including GPUs, and volatile pricing constrain delivery timing; land, power and data center shells also limit AI build-outs.
- Q1's large-scale AI mix and lower-margin deferred deployments weighed on sequential gross margin; management expects mix to pressure second-half margin.
- One large customer represented 44% of Q1 revenue; two customers accounted for 49% and 17% of quarter-end receivables.
- Revenue outlook depends on component availability, customer facility readiness and macroeconomic conditions; management described the guidance range as prudent.


### Significant events and developments

- Record backlog provided stronger visibility for Q2 and the remainder of FY27; management cited expanding visibility across customer sizes and geographies.
- Production capacity is ramping toward over 8,000 powerful racks monthly, including over 4,000 direct liquid cooling racks by December.
- NVIDIA Vera Rubin and VR72 rack systems were shipping; AMD MI350 and MI355X demand remained strong, alongside Intel Xeon 6+ and Arm AGI CPU developments.
- Strategic partnerships with Cisco, NetApp and Nutanix combine with DCBBS hardware, software and services; Shesha Krishnapura joined the Board and Mark Long became CFO.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Quarter-end cash was $6.9B; bank borrowing and convertible notes totaled $8.6B, leaving net debt of $1.7B versus $1.2B at Q4 end.
- A $1.8B receivables purchase facility supports cash timing and credit exposure; $5.6B raised in June was designated to fund working capital for current deliveries.
- Management reported no current plan for an equity raise; any debt-market financing would be assessed for cost and fit with working capital and supported assets.
- Q1 capex was $47M; Q2 capex guidance is $70M–$80M.


### Notes to the financial statements

- EPS uses the two-class method for mandatory convertible preferred stock issued in Q4; it carries a ~$75M quarterly dividend and converts to common stock in June 2029.
- Preferred dividends and earnings allocation to the participating security reduced earnings available to common shareholders by ~$312M in aggregate in Q1.
- Deferred revenue rose ~$3.1B, predominantly from customer prepayments; management said most deferred revenue going forward reflects prepayments or service and software revenue recognized over multiple periods.


### Market and industry conditions

- AI infrastructure demand remains strong, but industry deployment timing is gated by component supply and data center readiness, including land, power and shells.
- Large AI deployments carry lower gross margins than diversified enterprise and channel business; customer and delivery mix drives quarter-to-quarter margin variability.
- Management cited growing AI data center investment across Europe, Asia and the Middle East, alongside continued U.S. leadership in large-scale deployments.
