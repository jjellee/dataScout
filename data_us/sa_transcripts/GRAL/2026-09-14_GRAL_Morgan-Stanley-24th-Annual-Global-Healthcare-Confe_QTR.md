# GRAIL, Inc. — Morgan Stanley 24th Annual Global Healthcare Conference (2026-09-14)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Clinical evidence and adoption

- Clinical evidence is the primary driver for broader adoption, FDA approval, and payer coverage.
- Recent trials, including NHS-Galleri and PATHFINDER 2, showed Galleri detects more early-stage cancers and reduces Stage IV diagnoses, though not all endpoints were met.
- High positive predictive value (over 60%) and sensitivity for cancers causing most mortality were demonstrated.
- Real-world and external data from health systems like Mayo and Dana-Farber align with clinical trial results.
- Education and workflow improvements, such as EHR integration and partnerships, are key to increasing routine use.


### Regulatory and payer landscape

- FDA approval is expected to clarify the market, increase physician and payer confidence, and open doors for broader coverage.
- Labeling will likely focus on intended use in adults 50+, with transparency about cancer types and continued guideline screening.
- CMS engagement is ongoing, with the REACH study in Medicare populations supporting future NCD review.
- Commercial insurers are expected to expand coverage post-FDA approval, with pilots and tailored data generation planned.


### Competitive differentiation and future development

- Galleri is distinguished by the scale and depth of its clinical and real-world data, as well as rigorous FDA-reviewed validation.
- Ongoing R&D aims to improve performance, personalize testing, and reduce costs, leveraging AI and large-scale infrastructure.
- International expansion is supported by partnerships like Samsung, with FDA approval aiding regulatory discussions abroad.


### Market impact and underappreciated aspects

- Early detection enables access to novel therapies, potentially reducing cancer mortality.
- Adoption curve is just beginning, with significant potential for broader impact as evidence and payer support grow.
- AI and machine learning are central to ongoing test evolution and expanding access globally.
