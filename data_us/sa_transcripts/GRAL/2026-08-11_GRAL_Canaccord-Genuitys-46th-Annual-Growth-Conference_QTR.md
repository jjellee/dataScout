# GRAIL, Inc. — Canaccord Genuity's 46th Annual Growth Conference (2026-08-11)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Financial and operational performance

- Achieved 30% revenue growth and 42% test volume growth in the first half, with over 117,000 tests conducted.
- Expanded sales force, now fully trained and active, expected to drive further growth.
- Digital health and employer channels showed strong traction, with digital health volumes doubling year-over-year.
- Average selling price (ASP) declined slightly to $700 per test, with expectations to reach $500 as Medicare reimbursement approaches.
- Operating margins are targeted at 50%-60% at scale, supported by automation and fixed cost leverage.


### Strategic partnerships and technology integration

- Quest Diagnostics and athenahealth integrations have streamlined test ordering for physicians.
- Epic integration is expected by year-end, further reducing friction for test adoption.
- Samsung partnership in Korea began with a $110 million investment and will start with distribution, potentially expanding to Japan and local lab development.


### Regulatory and clinical milestones

- FDA Advisory Committee (AdCom) meeting for Galleri test scheduled for September 23, focusing on test performance and efficacy.
- PATHFINDER 2 and NHS-Galleri studies provided robust data, with PATHFINDER 2 showing consistent or improved performance and NHS-Galleri demonstrating a 20%+ reduction in Stage IV cancers.
- Additional 12-month follow-up for NHS-Galleri expected to read out in the first half of 2027; nested mortality data anticipated in 2028.
- REACH study, important for Medicare coverage, is ongoing with final readout expected in 2030 or 2031, but interim data may be available earlier.


### Market positioning and competitive landscape

- Only test with clinically validated data in the intended use population; competitors lack comparable data.
- Test detects a broad range of cancers with a low false positive rate (0.5%), outperforming traditional single-cancer screens.
- Peer-reviewed publications for NHS-Galleri and PATHFINDER 2 are forthcoming, expected to further validate the product.


### Financial outlook and capital management

- Cash burn expected to be under $300 million for the year, with $900 million in cash on hand at quarter-end.
- Recent $110 million financing from Samsung extends runway; future capital needs depend on revenue growth and reimbursement progress.
