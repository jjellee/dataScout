# Deere & Company — Q4 2026 (2026-11-25)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- FY2026 closed with Equipment Operations (Equip Ops) operating margin of 12.2% and net income of $4.86B; Q4 Equip Ops margin was 9.3%.
- Results reflected challenging row-crop agriculture alongside resilient Construction, Compact Construction, and Small Ag & Turf markets; execution and cost discipline supported profitability.
- FY2027 guidance anticipates an early Ag recovery, with wider segment sales ranges reflecting uncertainty across regions and end markets.


### Financial highlights

- FY2026 net sales and revenues rose 4% to $47.7B; Equip Ops net sales increased 6% to $41.3B.
- FY2026 net income attributable to Deere & Company was $4.86B, or $17.95 per diluted share.
- Q4 net sales and revenues fell 2% to $12.1B; Equip Ops net sales declined 1% to $10.5B.
- Q4 net income attributable to Deere & Company decreased 1% to $1.05B, or $3.89 per diluted share.


### Outlook and guidance

- FY2027 net income guidance is $4.5B–$5.25B; operating cash flow is projected at $4.5B–$5.5B and effective tax rate at 23%–25%.
- PPA net sales are expected up 5%–15%, with operating margin of 12.5%–14.5%; guidance includes ~3 points of price realization and 1.5 points of unfavorable FX.
- Small Ag & Turf net sales are expected roughly flat, with operating margin of 12.5%–13.5%; C&F sales are forecast flat to up 10%, with margin of 9.5%–11.5%.
- Financial Services FY2027 net income is forecast at $900M, versus $885M in FY2026, driven by improved financing spreads and lower credit-loss provisions.
- Guidance includes roughly $500M of higher pension-related impacts YoY, including a tax-adjusted, one-time non-cash pension settlement charge of $250M–$400M, expected in Q2.


### Segment performance

- Q4 PPA sales fell 15% to $4.0B; operating profit was $406M and margin 10.1%, with lower shipments and mix partly offset by lower production costs and FX.
- Q4 Small Ag & Turf sales rose 10% to $2.7B; operating profit increased to $233M on higher volumes, favorable mix and price, and lower warranty and production costs.
- Q4 C&F sales rose 12% to $3.8B; operating profit was $339M and margin 8.9%, as higher production, SA&G, and R&D costs offset favorable price realization.
- FY2026 PPA sales fell ~9% with an 11.3% margin; Small Ag & Turf sales grew ~15% with a 15.1% margin; C&F sales rose nearly 22% with a 10.6% margin.
- Q4 Financial Services net income was $231M, down YoY on unfavorable financing spreads, a lower average portfolio, and higher credit-loss provisions.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- FY2026 Equip Ops operating margin was 12.2%, versus more than 12% for Equip Ops overall as PPA remained at sub-trough industry levels.
- Equip Ops margin was more than 400 bps above 2016 at a comparable cycle point, and nearly 700 bps higher excluding tariffs.
- FY2026 delivered double-digit margins in every segment; PPA achieved double-digit margins in every region despite difficult markets.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Tight farm margins, elevated input costs, commodity-price volatility, and crop-weather risk may moderate the pace of Ag recovery, especially in Brazil and Europe.
- Data-center investment supports North American earthmoving demand, but a slowdown could temper momentum; soft housing continues to constrain residential construction and forestry recovery.
- Raw-material and freight costs are rising; 2027 tariff run rate is expected near $1B, with a net headwind of roughly $300M versus FY2026 due to non-repeat of refunds.
- UAW labor agreements expire at FY2027 year-end; contingency planning for a possible work stoppage is expected to increase working capital notably in H2, with modest 2027 income-statement impact.
- Forward-looking results remain subject to uncertainties and risks; non-GAAP measures were discussed with reconciliations referenced in earnings materials.


### Significant events and developments

- Digital engagement expanded: 530M+ Engaged Acres, 200M+ Highly Engaged Acres, 500,000+ monthly active users, and 1.2M+ connected machines.
- See & Spray covered ~12M acres globally in 2026, delivering average herbicide savings above 50%; roughly one-third of recent North American sprayer orders included the technology.
- JD AI assistant reached 6,000+ users in 76 countries and 10,000+ conversations in its first week of full availability.
- Construction technology sales grew ~30% in 2026; more than 90% of orders for new 20-metric-ton excavators included grade control.
- Orchard AI acquisition and Tenna platform work support expansion into high-value crops, orchards, turf, and digital construction solutions.


### Capital allocation and financing

- FY2027 cash priorities remain maintaining an A rating, funding business growth, and returning excess cash through dividends and share repurchases.
- Structural investments include modernizing and automating production facilities, expanding technology and digital platforms, and growth initiatives in Small Ag & Turf and C&F.
- Financial Services expects FY2027 net income of $900M as financing spreads improve and customer credit conditions stabilize.
- Labor contingency plans include building inventory to support FY2028 sales and maintaining operations during a potential stoppage; working capital is expected to rise in H2 FY2027.


### Notes to the financial statements

- FY2027 net income guidance includes an estimated one-time, tax-adjusted, non-cash pension settlement charge of $250M–$400M, expected in Q2 from a voluntary lump-sum option for certain U.S. salaried retirees.


### Market and industry conditions

- U.S. and Canada Large Ag industry sales are forecast up 5%–15%; Early Order Program orders were up double digits YoY, supported by aging fleets and tight new-equipment inventories.
- Small Ag & Turf industry sales in U.S. and Canada are expected roughly flat; Europe is forecast flat to down 5%, South America flat to up 5%, and Asia roughly flat.
- U.S. and Canada construction and compact construction sales are expected flat to up 5%, supported by infrastructure, non-residential projects, rental fleet refresh, and data-center investment.
- Road-building sales are expected flat to up 5%; global forestry markets roughly flat as infrastructure supports road work while soft housing limits forestry improvement.
