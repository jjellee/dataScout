# Insmed Incorporated — Q3 2026 (2026-10-29)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- YTD total revenue exceeded $1B; BRINSUPRI generated more than $1B in its first four full quarters, reaching blockbuster status.
- Three lead respiratory assets are forecast to grow at a CAGR above 20% over the next 10+ years, with estimated peak sales above $14B combined.


### Financial highlights

- Q3 2026 revenue: BRINSUPRI $400.1M, up 29% sequentially; ARIKAYCE $124.4M, up 9% YoY.
- Cost of product revenues was $78.2M, or 14.9% of revenue, vs. 20.6% in Q3 2025, reflecting BRINSUPRI's contribution to gross margin.
- Combined R&D and SG&A expenses rose 40% YoY, reflecting BRINSUPRI launch support and pipeline investment.


### Outlook and guidance

- FY 2026 BRINSUPRI revenue guidance raised to $1.35B-$1.4B; midpoint increased $50M from prior guidance.
- FY 2026 ARIKAYCE revenue guidance raised to $460M-$470M, with the low end up $10M; gross-to-net guidance remains mid-to-high 20s for BRINSUPRI and low-to-mid 20s for ARIKAYCE.
- Cash flow positivity expected in 2027, supported by BRINSUPRI's revenue trajectory; 2027 BRINSUPRI guidance was expected to be discussed around the usual investor conference.
- Phase II studies for INS1033 in rheumatoid arthritis and INS1148 in IPF are planned for 2027, subject to positive INS1033 Phase I data; one to two IND submissions annually remain the goal through decade-end.
- Initial DMD and ALS gene therapy data are expected in 2027; PALM-ILD enrollment is expected to complete by H1 2028 at the latest.


### Segment performance

- BRINSUPRI Q3 revenue was $400.1M, up 29% sequentially; payer approvals, reauthorizations, persistency and dispenses were described as strong, with new patient starts expected to grow through 2027.
- BRINSUPRI launched in Japan in October; the Phase III study showed a 68% annualized exacerbation-rate reduction in Japanese patients vs. ~20% globally, alongside greater lung-function improvement.
- ARIKAYCE Q3 revenue was $124.4M, up 9% YoY; U.S. and Japan expanded-label launches are expected in H1 2027 if both applications succeed.
- TPIP's four Phase III PALM studies cover PH-ILD, PAH, PPF and IPF; PALM-ILD was more than 35% enrolled, PALM-PAH enrolled its first patient, and PALM-PPF began site activation.
- INS1201's DMD Phase I study is fully enrolled with 12 pediatric patients; INS1202's ALS Phase I study is enrolling in cohort 2, with up to 23 patients planned.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Product cost was 14.9% of revenue in Q3 2026 vs. 20.6% in Q3 2025.
- 2026 gross-to-net guidance: BRINSUPRI mid-to-high 20s; ARIKAYCE low-to-mid 20s, with both within range to date.
- Medicare catastrophic-coverage cost share for ARIKAYCE rises to 5% in 2027 under the small-manufacturer phase-in, reaching a 20% maximum in 2031.
- Cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities totaled approximately $1.1B at Q3-end.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- BRINSUPRI seasonality remains uncertain; management said it was too early to assess Q4 effects and would wait until spring 2027 for a clearer view.
- ARIKAYCE's expanded U.S. and Japan labels depend on regulatory success; FDA action is targeted for January 28, 2027, and Japan's submission was planned for Q4 2026.
- TPIP's peak-sales potential and 2027 launch-preparation assumptions depend on successful Phase III trials and approvals; management also cited potential challenges from other companies.
- The estimated undiagnosed bronchiectasis population in COPD and asthma remains wide-ranging; additional imaging analysis is underway to refine prevalence estimates.


### Significant events and developments

- FDA granted ARIKAYCE priority review for its supplemental application; no advisory committee meeting was planned, and the PDUFA date is January 28, 2027.
- BEST-BE, a two-year U.S. real-world study, plans to enroll 1,000 adults across 13 sites; EMBARC's European study plans to enroll up to 3,000 patients over three years.
- A U.S. disease-educator program launched with 16 nurses, while retrospective CT analysis and AI diagnostic tools are being developed to identify undiagnosed bronchiectasis.
- Nine research programs across four research centers could enter clinical development by 2029; several may qualify for accelerated approval.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Q3-end liquidity was approximately $1.1B; management cited a clear path to cash flow positivity in 2027 while continuing growth investment.
- Prior-year TPIP spending included manufacturing investment to prepare for four Phase III trials; spending is expected to shift toward clinical-trial investment.
- Commercial resources are being focused on deeper engagement with high-potential prescribers; branded TV and digital campaigns are additive, with demand impact expected in H1 2027.


### Market and industry conditions

- The diagnosed U.S. non-CF bronchiectasis population was estimated at roughly 500,000-550,000 using claims data three years ago; treatment availability and increased detection may expand the addressable population.
- Published estimates place COPD/bronchiectasis comorbidity at 4%-64%; the low end implies 800,000 COPD patients, while some experts estimate prevalence near 20%.
- COPD and asthma comorbidity is excluded from BRINSUPRI's more than $7B peak-sales estimate; management sees potential for several million additional patients if a single-digit percentage of 32M U.S. patients have undiagnosed bronchiectasis.
- Expanded ARIKAYCE labels could increase its U.S. and Japan addressable markets from approximately 15,000 patients each to more than 100,000 each.
- Peak-sales estimates exceed $7B for BRINSUPRI, $6B for TPIP across four indications, and $1B for ARIKAYCE if U.S. and Japan approvals are secured.
