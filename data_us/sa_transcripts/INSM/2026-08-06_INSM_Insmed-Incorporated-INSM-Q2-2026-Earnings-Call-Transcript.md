To contextualize how unique and impressive those results were, let's begin with a recap of the randomized Phase IIb trial that preceded it.

On a placebo-adjusted basis, TPIP demonstrated a 35% reduction in PVR, a 35.5-meter improvement in 6-minute walk distance and a 60% reduction in NT-proBNP at the end of 16 weeks of treatment.
