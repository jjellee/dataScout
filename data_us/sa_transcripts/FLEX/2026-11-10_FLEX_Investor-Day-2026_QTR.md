# Flex Ltd. — Investor Day 2026 (2026-11-10)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Strategic direction

- The spin creates two purpose-driven businesses: Axiom takes the power product portfolio and system IP; the remaining EMS business retains contract manufacturing and refocuses capital and management attention on new growth opportunities.
- The value-creation playbook combines financial rigor, productivity improvement, and portfolio optimization, building on seven years of margin, earnings, cash-flow, and share-count progress.
- Capital and investment will focus on six high-value markets: medical devices, drug delivery, energy infrastructure, robotics, advanced networking, and satellite communications.
- A 77-factory global network, design, vertical integration, fulfillment, and aftermarket services support complex product launches, regionalized supply chains, and higher-value customer programs.


### High-value markets and business developments

- The six markets represented 24% of FY 2026 revenue; management expects nearly 30% in FY 2027 and 35%-40% in FY 2029, with double-digit growth and margins above the company average.
- RMS targets healthcare, energy infrastructure, and robotics; medical design programs earn an average 250 bps margin premium over base EMS programs, while energy infrastructure and robotics are each on track to double revenue in FY 2027.
- Healthcare programs benefit from regulatory barriers and long customer relationships; drug delivery is ramping with pharmaceutical customers, and GLP-1 therapies grew more than 25% annually in recent years.
- Robotics deployments of the proprietary MAP platform improved productivity by more than 30% on deployed processes; hundreds of platforms have been installed across operations over the past 18 months.
- ITS will prioritize advanced networking and satellite communications; both are expected to grow double digits in FY 2027, while lower-return programs may be repriced, reduced, or exited.


### Financial outlook

- FY 2027 expectations remain unchanged: RMS revenue growth mid- to high-single digits, ITS low-double-digit to low-teens growth, total growth high-single digits to low-double digits, and adjusted operating margin 5.9%-6.1%; pro forma post-spin margin 5.7%-5.9%.
- FY 2028 directionally calls for continued revenue growth and margin expansion, double-digit EPS growth, free cash flow conversion of 80% or more, and capex of approximately 2% of revenue.
- FY 2029 targets are mid-single-digit revenue growth and pro forma adjusted operating margin of 7% or more; the margin expansion from FY 2027 levels is expected to add more than $200 million of adjusted operating income.
- Management expects adjusted operating margin to improve 20-40 bps in FY 2027; approximately two-thirds of FY 2027-FY 2029 margin expansion is expected from high-value-market volume and mix.
- Mid-single-digit organic revenue growth, margin expansion, debt reduction, and buybacks are expected to support earnings growth faster than revenue; debt reduction could lower interest expense by approximately $100 million versus FY 2027 pro forma levels.


### Capital allocation and execution

- Priorities are to maintain investment grade and reduce leverage, fund organic growth and productivity, pursue selective M&A in high-value markets, then return excess capital primarily through share repurchases.
- Retained Axiom shares may be exchanged tax-efficiently starting as early as the first full post-spin quarter, with completion within 24 months; proceeds are intended for debt reduction, subject to market, tax, and lockup considerations.
- Capex is planned at approximately 2% of revenue; investment in high-value markets is expected to target returns around twice weighted average cost of capital, while free cash flow conversion targets 80% or more.
- Share repurchases remain the preferred shareholder-return mechanism; diluted shares outstanding fell approximately 14% over the prior two years.
- Productivity initiatives will extend MAP deployment across more processes and sites, harmonize factory data, and enable broader physical AI use in manufacturing, planning, and scheduling.
