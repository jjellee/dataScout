# Flex Ltd. — Q2 2027 (2026-10-28)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q2 revenue grew 25% YoY; adjusted EPS reached a record $1.18, up 49%, with margin expansion across all three segments.
- RMS and ITS together grew 16%, marking their fourth consecutive quarter of YoY growth; the planned Axiom spin-off is targeted for early 2027.


### Financial highlights

- Revenue was $8.5B, up 25% YoY; adjusted gross profit was $842M and adjusted gross margin was 9.9%, up 60 bps.
- Adjusted operating profit was $607M, up 48% YoY; adjusted operating margin was 7.1%, up 110 bps.
- Adjusted EPS was $1.18, up 49% YoY; Q2 free cash flow was $261M, including a $61M negative impact from one-time spin-related cash costs.


### Outlook and guidance

- FY27 revenue guidance is $34.9B–$35.9B, up 27% at midpoint; adjusted operating margin 7.6%–7.8%, adjusted EPS $4.84–$5.16, and FCF conversion ~40%.
- FY27 CPI revenue is expected to grow 75%–85%, including EPC Power; combined RMS and ITS revenue is expected to rise 8%–11%.
- FY27 adjusted operating margin outlook: CPI 11.5%–11.7%; combined RMS and ITS 5.9%–6.1%; adjusted tax rate ~21%.
- Q3 revenue guidance is $8.87B–$9.17B, up 28% at midpoint; adjusted operating income $685M–$715M and adjusted EPS $1.19–$1.25.
- Q3 segment revenue outlook: RMS up low-to-mid single digits, ITS up mid-to-high single digits, and CPI up 105%–115%.


### Segment performance

- RMS revenue was $2.7B, up 9% YoY; adjusted operating income was $172M and margin was 6.4%, up 50 bps, primarily on industrial strength.
- ITS revenue was $3.3B, up 22% YoY; adjusted operating income was $181M and margin was 5.5%, up 20 bps, with communications growth offset partly by consumer weakness.
- CPI revenue was $2.5B, up 54% YoY; adjusted operating income was $268M and margin was 10.7%, up 240 bps, on power and cloud/cooling growth and favorable power mix.
- Power operating margin reached ~16%, up ~550 bps YoY, reflecting strong volume and scale benefits from prior investment.
- RMS and ITS strength came from industrial, energy infrastructure, robotics, communications and advanced networking; lifestyle weakness was offset by communications.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted gross margin was 9.9%, up 60 bps YoY; adjusted operating margin was 7.1%, up 110 bps YoY.
- Inventory rose 13% sequentially and 39% YoY; inventory net of working-capital days was 60 days, up five days YoY, largely due to mix and higher-value program needs.
- Q2 net CapEx was $240M, ~3% of revenue; FY27 CapEx guidance is $1.5B–$1.6B.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Component shortages and constrained supply-demand conditions are expected to persist for several years; guidance includes estimated shortage impacts and planned buffers.
- Elevated near-term interest expense from EPC acquisition debt is expected to decline after the convertible preferred stock transaction closes.
- Axiom revenue is expected to be concentrated among hyperscalers; strategy is to diversify across customers through multiple products and programs per customer.
- Spin-off timing, approvals and related plans remain subject to completion and other uncertainties; forward-looking results may differ materially.


### Significant events and developments

- EPC Power acquisition closed October 14, strengthening power-conversion capabilities and complementing development in 800-volt architecture and solid-state transformers.
- Axiom filed its Form 10 registration statement and announced expected leadership teams; the planned separation is targeted for early 2027.
- Prefabricated AI Modules were introduced at OCP; they are designed to accelerate AI factory deployment by up to 50%.
- New cooling product launches are planned, while aftermarket rack services are expanding, including a ramp in Memphis.


### Capital allocation and financing

- An agreement to sell $2B of Axiom convertible preferred stock is expected to provide net proceeds to repay approximately $2B of the $4.4B acquisition debt incurred for EPC.
- FY27 CapEx of $1.5B–$1.6B is an outsized investment year; roughly two-thirds is allocated to CPI and about one-third to RMS and ITS, with most CPI spending supporting FY28 capacity.
- Post-spin, RMS and ITS CapEx is expected to return to historical levels of 2% of revenue or below.


### Market and industry conditions

- AI infrastructure demand is driving growth in power, cloud and cooling; capacity, factory power and utility connections have been planned to support ramps.
- Custom cloud configurations, advanced networking, rack integration and aftermarket services are strategic areas; standard configurations are generally not the focus.
- Current digital rectifiers can support 97% power efficiency while the industry awaits longer-cycle transitions such as solid-state transformers; 800-volt technology remains a priority.
- Healthcare, automotive and communications offer complexity, regulation, long product cycles, technology differentiation or global scale that support durable competitive positioning.
