# QUALCOMM Incorporated — Q4 2026 (2026-11-04)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q4 FY26 non-GAAP revenue was $10.4B and EPS was $2.17; QCT revenue was $9B, up 6% QoQ and at the high end of guidance. QTL revenue was $1.3B.
- FY26 non-GAAP revenue was $43.2B and QCT revenue was $37.2B; combined Automotive and IoT revenue grew 24% YoY.
- FY27 begins with QTL renewals completed, the Apple modem transition incorporated into planning, and growth initiatives expanding beyond handsets.


### Financial highlights

- Q4 QCT EBT margin was 24%, in line with expectations; QTL EBT margin was 70%, also in line with expectations.
- QCT returned $2B to stockholders in Q4, split evenly between share repurchases and dividends.
- QCT handset revenue was $5.1B, reflecting industry-wide memory dynamics in the global smartphone market.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q1 FY27 revenue guidance is $10.4B-$11.2B and non-GAAP EPS is $2.15-$2.35; QTL revenue is $1.3B-$1.5B and QCT revenue is $9B-$9.6B.
- FY27 data center revenue target remains $5B, with revenue above $650M in Q1 and a ~$7B exit run rate; supply, rather than demand, may constrain upside beyond $5B.
- FY27 QCT non-handset revenue is expected to grow more than 60% YoY and exceed 50% of QCT revenue for the year.
- FY29 targets include $40B in non-handset revenue, with more than $15B from data center.
- Global handset units are planned flat to down in calendar 2027; QCT handset revenue is expected to decline mid-single digits sequentially in Q1 FY27.


### Segment performance

- QCT Automotive recorded its first $2B revenue quarter, up 89% YoY; FY26 ended at an ~$8B annualized run rate, above the prior $7B expectation.
- QCT IoT revenue was $1.9B, up 4% YoY; industrial, networking and robotics products grew 20%, partly offset by memory constraints on consumer products.
- QCT combined non-handset revenue grew 36% YoY in Q4; FY26 Automotive and IoT growth was 24% YoY.
- QTL completed long-term license agreements with Apple, Huawei and Transsion; its revenue run rate and long-term forecast remain unchanged, with the next major renewal cycle beginning FY30.
- Data center shipments began in October for custom accelerators; Q1 FY27 revenue above $650M is expected, with most FY27 revenue from two hyperscaler accelerator engagements and connectivity also contributing.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Q1 FY27 QTL EBT margin guidance is 72%-76%; QCT EBT margin guidance is 20%-22%, including a 3-point impact from adding Data Center to QCT.
- QCT's long-term EBT margin target remains 30%; management expects data center custom silicon to support operating margins while diluting gross margins.
- Pricing actions are expected to return baseline gross margin to the historical 48%-50% range, at its low end initially; the full pricing benefit is expected in H2 FY27.
- Q1 FY27 non-GAAP operating expense guidance is ~$2.8B.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Memory pricing is pressuring handset market size; management projects a low-to-mid-teens market decline for 2025-26 and flat-to-down conditions in 2026-27.
- Data center growth is expected to dilute gross margins; potential revenue upside above the FY27 $5B target is currently more supply-constrained than demand-constrained.
- QCT handset revenue faces continued Apple product revenue runoff, while FY27 handset market assumptions remain subject to broader market uncertainty.
- Semiconductor input cost increases are being addressed through product price increases, with the full margin benefit expected only in H2 FY27.


### Significant events and developments

- SpaceX selected Dragonwing infrastructure platforms for Starlink Mobile's U.S. terrestrial 5G network under a multi-year agreement; deployment begins in 2027, with revenue contribution expected mainly in FY28.
- Design-win pipeline grew 70% YoY; strategic agreements were announced with Meta, Amazon, BMW and Stellantis.
- The jury found Arm breached its architecture license agreement; management said the outcome does not affect the Oryon CPU roadmap or ability to develop and ship products.
- High Bandwidth Compute lab results exceeded expectations for power efficiency and bandwidth, with yields on track and increased customer interest.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Q4 stockholder returns totaled $2B: $1B in share repurchases and $1B in dividends.
- The Amazon warrant arrangement was described as an exception; warrants were at-the-money, while other data center engagements generally follow traditional semiconductor-industry structures.
- Acquisitions were made to expand hardware and software capabilities; the Alphawave acquisition brought optical connectivity assets into the portfolio.


### Market and industry conditions

- Management expects memory-related pressure to keep global handset units flat to down in calendar 2027; China Android revenue grew sequentially after reaching a low point in the June quarter.
- The move to agentic AI is driving new smartphone and wearable designs and greater on-device compute needs; leading AI firms are targeting local 100B-parameter smartphone models by 2028.
- Automotive compute content is increasing with fifth-generation platforms; content versus third-generation platforms was described as 8x, supporting continued SoC revenue growth.
- AI infrastructure demand is broadening across custom silicon, accelerators, CPUs and optical connectivity; data center customers and partners include AWS and Meta.
