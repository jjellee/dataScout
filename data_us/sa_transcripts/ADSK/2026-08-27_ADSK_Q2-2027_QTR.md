# Autodesk, Inc. — Q2 2027 (2026-08-27)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Revenue and EPS exceeded the high end of guidance, prompting an upward revision of full-year billings and revenue outlooks, now including MaintainX following its acquisition.
- Strategy centers on converging design, make, and operate workflows, leveraging project intelligence and AI to drive efficiency and resilience across asset life cycles.
- Strong performance in construction and emerging markets, with normalization in Americas, APAC, Eastern Europe, and Middle East ahead of Western Europe.


### Financial highlights

- Q2 total revenue grew 16% as reported and 14% in constant currency year-over-year; billings increased 10% as reported and 12% in constant currency.
- GAAP operating margin was 29% (up 4 pts YoY); non-GAAP operating margin was 41% (up 2 pts YoY).
- Free cash flow for Q2 was $561 million.
- Share repurchases totaled 2.1 million shares for $453 million in Q2.


### Outlook and guidance

- Fiscal 2027 billings guidance raised to $8.575B–$8.65B; revenue guidance raised to $8.295B–$8.345B, reflecting MaintainX contribution and improved expectations.
- MaintainX expected to add ~$60M to H2 revenue and ~$70M to H2 billings, weighted to Q4.
- Fiscal 2027 GAAP operating margin guidance revised to 25%–27%; non-GAAP margin unchanged, with dilution from MaintainX offset by operating leverage.
- Fiscal 2027 free cash flow guidance narrowed to $2.725B–$2.75B.
- Fiscal 2028 non-GAAP margin expected to improve modestly from 39% in 2027; target of 41% by 2029.


### Segment performance

- Construction business continues to grow north of 20%, driven by technology adoption and integration of design and make.
- Fusion business shows robust growth in user acquisition, ACV, revenue, and multi-seat purchases, with AI features providing a tailwind.
- MaintainX acquisition expands operational capabilities, with plans to leverage synergies in enterprise accounts and across AEC and manufacturing sectors.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Renewal rates remained strong across segments.
- Stock-based compensation as a percent of revenue expected at 9% for FY27, down from 11% in FY26.
- New transaction model provided a 2-pt tailwind to Q2 revenue growth and is expected to add 1.5 pts for the full year.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Guidance incorporates prudence for variability in sales productivity normalization, especially in Western Europe.
- Largest EBA renewal cohort concentrated in Q4, creating tough YoY comps.
- MaintainX integration presents short-term margin drag and operational costs.


### Significant events and developments

- Acquisition of MaintainX completed in August, now included in guidance and reporting.
- Changes to revenue presentation: discontinuation of design/make revenue disclosure, MaintainX revenue to be disclosed for four quarters.
- EVP of AEC Solutions, Amy Bunszel, announced retirement after 23 years.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Continued commitment to share buybacks, targeting ~50% of free cash flow.
- MaintainX acquisition resulted in ~$45M of transaction expenses in FY27.


### Market and industry conditions

- Technology adoption in construction and manufacturing is a key growth driver, with low penetration providing runway.
- Macro environment assumed to remain broadly stable through the year.
- Flex pricing changes have accelerated down-market adoption and are net accretive.
