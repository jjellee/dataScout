# Alnylam Pharmaceuticals, Inc. — Morgan Stanley 24th Annual Global Healthcare Conference (2026-09-14)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### TTR franchise performance and market dynamics

- AMVUTTRA achieved over $1 billion in Q2 revenue, driven by strong HELIOS-B data and rapid adoption for TTR cardiomyopathy.
- Access initiatives ensure most patients have $0 out-of-pocket costs and 90% can access therapy within 10 miles.
- Market share exceeds 50% among prescribing physicians, with a focus on expanding first-line use.
- Guidance for the TTR franchise was revised to $4.2–$4.5 billion due to normalization of first- and second-line demand.
- 80% of the market remains undiagnosed and untreated, representing significant growth potential.


### Pricing, access, and regulatory environment

- Net price is expected to decline modestly and gradually as volume increases.
- Proposed 340B rule changes are under review, with a decision expected around November; robust site-of-care networks are in place to maintain access.
- Focus remains on ensuring broad patient access regardless of regulatory changes.


### Competitive landscape and clinical data insights

- HELIOS-B demonstrated superior TTR knockdown (81%) compared to eplontersen in CARDIO-TTRansform (less than 70%), supporting AMVUTTRA’s efficacy.
- Early treatment and patient selection are critical for optimal outcomes in TTR cardiomyopathy.
- Physician feedback and surveys indicate no significant change in silencer use following CARDIO-TTRansform results.
- siRNA therapies are perceived as more effective than ASOs in this space.


### International expansion and future pipeline

- AMVUTTRA launches in Germany and Japan are progressing well; a commercialization agreement is in place for China pending regulatory approval.
- Nucresiran, with 95% TTR knockdown, is in the TRITON-CM study, incorporating learnings from prior trials; study size was recently increased.
- Generic tafamidis is now expected in 2031, reducing near-term pricing pressure and aligning with upcoming data from TRITON studies.


### Growth drivers and strategic initiatives

- Key growth drivers include expanding first-line use, improving diagnosis rates, and broadening the prescriber base.
- Partnerships with organizations like Viz.ai and the American Heart Association aim to enhance diagnosis and patient identification.
- Continued geographic expansion and investment in the TTR franchise are priorities.


### Pipeline highlights and innovation

- Huntington’s disease program targets both full-length and exon 1 fragments, with phase I data expected in October.
- ALN-6400 for bleeding disorders is in phase II, with potential for rapid advancement if results are positive.
- Obesity program aims to deliver siRNA to adipose tissue, with proof-of-concept data expected later this year.
- Zilebesiran for hypertension focuses on durable blood pressure control and improved adherence, with a large outcome study ongoing.
- AI is being leveraged across commercial and R&D functions, including a partnership with Inceptive Nucleics to accelerate siRNA innovation.
