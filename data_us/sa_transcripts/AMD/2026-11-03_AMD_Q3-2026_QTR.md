# Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-11-03)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Record Q3 revenue of $13.4B, up 45% YoY, and non-GAAP diluted EPS of $2.09, up 74%; growth was led by Data Center and supported by Embedded.
- Data Center reached 64% of revenue versus 47% a year earlier; management expects revenue growth to accelerate in Q4 and the segment to exceed 80% of total revenue in 2027.
- Management expects total 2027 revenue growth above 80%, with server revenue doubling and Data Center AI growing significantly more than 2x.


### Financial highlights

- Revenue was $13.4B, up 45% YoY and 16% sequentially, exceeding the high end of guidance.
- Non-GAAP diluted EPS was $2.09; GAAP diluted EPS was $1.46, including a $1.1B non-cash charge primarily related to customer warrants.
- Operating income rose 75% YoY to a record $3.9B; operating expenses increased 29% to $3.6B as investment in R&D continued.
- Free cash flow was $554M; operating cash flow from continuing operations was $1.2B, with working-capital investment and capital expenditures weighing on free cash flow.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q4 revenue is expected at $17B, plus or minus 3%, implying ~66% YoY and ~27% sequential growth; non-GAAP gross margin ~55% and operating expenses ~$3.75B.
- Q4 segment outlook: Data Center revenue up ~50% sequentially, Client and Gaming down >20%, and Embedded growing by double digits.
- For 2027, total revenue is expected to grow >80%; Data Center is expected to exceed 80% of revenue, server revenue to double, and Data Center AI to grow significantly more than 2x.
- 2027 OpEx growth is expected below half the revenue growth rate; management expects significant operating-margin expansion, while quarterly gross margin will vary with Data Center AI/server mix.


### Segment performance

- Data Center revenue rose 97% YoY to a record $8.5B and 27% sequentially; operating income increased 168% to $2.9B, with margin expanding to 34% from 25%.
- Client and Gaming revenue fell 8% YoY to $3.7B; Client grew 7% to $2.9B, while Gaming declined 40% to $773M. Segment operating income was $573M, or 15% of revenue, versus $867M, or 21%, a year earlier.
- Embedded revenue grew 29% YoY and 14% sequentially to $1.1B; operating income was $453M, or 41% of revenue, versus $283M, or 33%, a year earlier.
- Record EPYC sales and rising adoption supported server growth; Instinct deployments and MI450 shipments drove record Data Center AI revenue.
- Ryzen mobile demand supported Client growth; lower semi-custom sales weighed on Gaming, while aerospace and defense, automotive, communications, and industrial demand lifted Embedded.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Gross margin was 56%, up 190 bps YoY; operating margin was 29%, expanding more than 5 percentage points YoY.
- Data Center operating margin reached 34%, up from 25% YoY; Embedded operating margin was 41%, up from 33%.
- Client and Gaming operating margin was 15%, down from 21% YoY.
- Inventory increased ~$3B sequentially to $11.5B to support Data Center demand.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Demand currently exceeds supply; wafer, substrate, advanced-packaging, memory, and other component availability may constrain growth despite efforts to expand capacity.
- PC market unit shipments are planned to decline by a double-digit percentage in 2027 as higher memory and component costs weigh on demand.
- Memory and other component-cost inflation is pressuring inputs; cost sharing with customers is being pursued, with value proposition tied to competitive total cost of ownership.
- Gross margin may vary by quarter as Data Center AI and server mix changes; supply constraints add uncertainty to the 2027 mix outlook.
- Customer-warrant fair-value adjustments will vary with share price and valuation assumptions, creating quarterly non-cash GAAP expense volatility.


### Significant events and developments

- MI455X began volume shipments in Q3, and initial Helios racks entered customer use; Helios deployments are expected to rise substantially in Q4 as OEM systems ship.
- EPYC Venice revenue shipments began in Q3; OEM/ODM partners committed to more than 240 platforms, 20% more than for Turin, with initial OEM systems expected in Q4.
- ROCm 10 launched with enhancements for large-scale production workloads and agentic performance tuning.
- Taalas and World Labs acquisitions were announced, with both expected to close by year-end; World Labs co-founder Fei-Fei Li is expected to join as chief scientist.
- More than $18B of design wins are expected to close in 2026, marking a third consecutive record year; embedded wins are expected to support strong double-digit annual revenue growth over the next few years.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments totaled $15.8B; total debt was $7.1B, including $4.75B of senior notes issued in August.
- Investment priorities include R&D, supply-chain and capacity expansion, and selective investment alongside partners to support multi-year infrastructure plans.
- Inventory and capacity investment is expected to continue in 2027; Q3 free cash flow reflected working-capital needs and capital spending to secure equipment and backend capacity.


### Notes to the financial statements

- Q3 GAAP diluted EPS included a $1.1B non-cash other income and expense charge, primarily from share-price movements and changes in estimated warrant-condition probabilities.
- Customer warrants reduce reported net revenue at grant-date fair value; later fair-value remeasurement is non-cash and does not affect non-GAAP results or free cash flow. Share dilution depends on shipment, vesting, and exercise conditions.


### Market and industry conditions

- Agentic workloads are expanding CPU and accelerator demand; more than 10M people activated Meta Muse agents in its first six weeks.
- Server compute demand is accelerating across cloud, enterprise, agentic, and superintelligence workloads; the server TAM is projected to reach ~$220B by 2030.
- PC sell-through slowed late in Q3 amid higher memory and component costs; plans assume a double-digit percentage PC market decline in 2027.
- Data-center infrastructure readiness, including land, power, and facilities, is being planned jointly with customers; demand for compute remains higher than supply.
