# Viavi Solutions Inc. — Q1 2027 (2026-11-04)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q1 FY27 revenue and operating margin exceeded guidance; growth was led by data center and aerospace and defense demand.
- Management expects sequential growth through the rest of FY27, with strong NSE demand offsetting seasonal OSP softness in Q2.


### Financial highlights

- Revenue was $469.3M, up 5.9% QoQ and 56.9% YoY; operating margin was 29%, up 5.0 percentage points QoQ and 12.7 points YoY.
- EPS was $0.47, up $0.13 QoQ and $0.32 YoY; tariff refunds and an additional week of variable expenses provided a net ~$0.02 benefit.
- Operating margin included a net ~110 bps benefit from an ~$11M tariff refund, partly offset by variable costs from the additional week.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q2 FY27 revenue guide: $505M-$515M; NSE $426M-$434M and OSP $79M-$81M.
- Q2 operating margin guide: 29.3% ±30 bps overall, 28.1% ±30 bps NSE and 35.7% ±20 bps OSP; EPS $0.50-$0.52.
- Q2 revenue is expected to rise sequentially; NSE growth should more than offset seasonally lower OSP demand, while sequential growth is expected to continue through FY27.
- Management expects to reach a $2B revenue run rate during Q2 and sees strong momentum continuing into the second half of FY27.


### Segment performance

- NSE revenue was $376.1M, up 74.1% YoY; growth reflected data center, aerospace and defense demand and Spirent product lines, which contributed $52.7M.
- NSE gross margin was 66.2%, up 310 bps YoY; operating margin was 25.5% versus 8.2% a year earlier, above guidance on higher fall-through.
- OSP revenue was $93.2M, up 12.2% YoY on 3D sensing, anti-counterfeiting and other products; gross margin was 57.1%, up 460 bps YoY.
- OSP operating margin was 43.2%, up 600 bps YoY and within guidance; Q2 revenue is expected to decline sequentially on seasonal demand.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Operating cash flow was $104.7M versus $31M YoY, driven by higher net income and working-capital timing; CapEx was $12M versus $8.5M.
- Effective tax rate was 8%; management characterized incremental fall-through as high-50s to low-60s this quarter and low-to-mid-50s as a prudent baseline.
- Fully diluted share count was 266.2M versus 227.9M YoY and 268M in Q1 guidance.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Forward-looking expectations depend on sustained customer capex, market-share gains, timely product delivery, component availability and stable macro conditions; actual results may differ materially.
- Wireless remained a weak service-provider area amid anemic spending; OSP faces seasonal Q2 demand declines in 3D sensing and anti-counterfeiting.
- Q2 tax expense is expected at ~$12M ±$0.5M based on jurisdictional mix; other income and expenses are expected to be a net $3.5M expense.


### Significant events and developments

- Initial semiconductor packaging and test shipments began in September, with an early ~$1M shipment supporting predominantly CPO; shipments are expected to accelerate in coming quarters.
- Semiconductor test could become a >$100M segment in a few years; expanded opportunities include fiber and connector manufacturing and optical circuit switching.
- The U.S. Army selected the T/Rx system for electromagnetic test environments supporting drones, counter-drones and interceptor development.
- Spirent integration is complete; co-designed products are reaching market, with fully integrated portfolio launches expected next calendar year.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Cash and short-term investments ended Q1 at $535.3M, versus $656.7M at the end of FY26 Q4.
- Repurchased ~3.949M shares for ~$150M at an average $37.98; ~$18M remained on the existing authorization and the board approved a new $500M plan.
- Capital allocation priorities remain organic growth, potential M&A and opportunistic buybacks.


### Market and industry conditions

- Data center demand remains strong; management cited 3-4 year technology refresh cycles, increasing fiber density and expansion into semiconductor test as growth drivers.
- 800G remains the highest-volume optical module shipment; 1.6T is in early ramp, with 800G and 1.6T expected to run concurrently and 1.6T potentially surpassing 800G by end of next calendar year.
- Service providers showed stronger seasonal demand and early signs of a multi-year equipment refresh; fiber build-outs and DOCSIS 4.0/DAA supported demand.
- Aerospace and defense momentum is supported by PNT demand, while wireless recovery is a longer-term prospect tied to AI-RAN and 6G development.
