# GoDaddy Inc. — Goldman Sachs Communacopia + Technology Conference 2026 (2026-09-09)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Strategic vision and customer focus

- Evolving from a domain provider to an operating system for entrepreneurs and micro-businesses, focusing on tools that enable value creation and business management in a single platform.
- Unique customer base targets micro and small businesses, emphasizing high retention and loyalty through tailored care and support.
- The Entrepreneur's Wheel concept integrates technology for all business needs, reinforcing platform stickiness and customer engagement.


### AI innovation and product evolution

- Airo, the AI-powered platform, consolidates multiple functionalities, driving faster product adoption and higher customer lifetime value.
- Customers on Airo attach a second product 30% faster, with 70% having at least two products, boosting retention and cohort quality.
- AI lowers barriers for small business creation, increases domain registrations, and enhances operational efficiency across engineering, care, and G&A.


### Infrastructure and industry collaboration

- Agentic Name Service (ANS) extends DNS protocols to authenticate and register agents, ensuring trust and security in agentic internet interactions.
- ANS is open, contributed to the Linux Foundation, and co-developed with major tech firms, positioning for future monetization as agent registration grows.


### Traffic, demand, and market positioning

- Over 60% of traffic is direct and organic, with increasing focus on optimizing presence in LLMs to attract high-intent customers.
- Strategy prioritizes quality over quantity, aiming for customers likely to adopt multiple products and drive higher LTV.


### Financial discipline and investment approach

- Maintains strong margin and free cash flow profile through ruthless prioritization and disciplined investment in customer-driven innovation.
- AI is viewed as margin-neutral, driving both operational efficiency and innovation investment, with no near-term cost headwinds.
- M&A criteria remain strict; AI capabilities raise the bar for acquisitions as in-house innovation accelerates.


### Outlook and investor priorities

- Free cash flow per share remains the primary performance metric, driven by revenue growth, margin expansion, and share buybacks.
- Confident in achieving at least 25% CAGR in free cash flow per share over three years, with continued focus on customer-centric innovation.
