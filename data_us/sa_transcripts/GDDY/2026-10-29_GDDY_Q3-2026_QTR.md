# GoDaddy Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-10-29)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 bookings grew 6% to $1.4B; revenue grew 6% to $1.3B, while normalized EBITDA margin expanded to 33%.
- Airo adoption accelerated, with its annualized bookings run rate more than doubling to over $100M and contributing incrementally to bookings growth.


### Financial highlights

- Revenue grew 6% to $1.3B, above the high end of guidance; ARR grew 5% to $4.5B.
- Normalized EBITDA rose 10% to $449M; normalized EBITDA margin expanded ~120 bps to 33%.
- Free cash flow grew 9% to $481M; trailing 12-month free cash flow was $1.8B, with conversion from normalized EBITDA above 1:1.
- International revenue grew 3% to $436M; most Q3 aftermarket transactions were domestic, explaining variation versus prior quarters.


### Outlook and guidance

- FY2026 revenue expected toward the high end of the $5.215B-$5.255B range; aftermarket outperformance in the first three quarters supported the outlook.
- FY Applications and Commerce revenue growth expected at ~10%; Core Platform revenue growth expected at ~4%.
- Q4 bookings growth expected to accelerate and be slightly ahead of revenue growth, led by Airo adoption in Applications and Commerce; FX excluded from the initial outlook framing.
- Reaffirmed FY normalized EBITDA margin above 33%, representing >100 bps expansion, and free cash flow of ~ $1.8B with conversion above 1:1.


### Segment performance

- Applications and Commerce revenue grew 9% to $524M; productivity and commerce growth partly offset ongoing migration from Websites + Marketing to Airo.
- Applications and Commerce segment EBITDA margin expanded ~120 bps to 47%.
- Core Platform revenue grew 4% to $818M, on high-value transactions and strength in new primary domain registrations and renewals; segment EBITDA margin was flat at 33%.
- Applications and Commerce bookings grew 8%; productivity improved from targeted pricing actions, partly offset by fewer email seats at renewal.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Retention remained above 85% and continued to improve; more than half of customers had at least two paid products.
- ARPU reached $253; customers spending over $500 annually with at least two paid products have nearly perfect retention.
- Net debt was $2.7B, with trailing 12-month net leverage of 1.4x; cash was $1.1B and total liquidity $2.3B.
- Fully diluted shares outstanding were 123M at quarter-end.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Airo carries lower gross margins than internally developed Websites + Marketing; gross margin is expected to deteriorate as Airo scales, although efficiencies are expected to support normalized EBITDA margin expansion.
- Airo inference and token costs precede subscription revenue recognition over time; margin improvement is expected to begin showing in 2027, but remain below Websites + Marketing levels.
- Domain discounting and freemium hosting offers intensified across the industry; new pricing and bundles are planned for testing near year-end into early 2027.
- AI-driven customer behavior, credit-pack usage, and LLM-originated traffic remain early and dynamic; LLM traffic increased from a small base in Q3.
- Email renewals had fewer seats, reflecting microbusinesses needing fewer accounts; no indication tied this trend to agents.


### Significant events and developments

- Airo became the default website option in the domains purchase path late in Q3; marketing and internal resources are being increased, with Q4 spend offset by efficiencies elsewhere.
- New Airo customers attached a second paid product 70% of the time, above the average of slightly more than half; domain and email are common attached products.
- Expanded agent-friendly APIs now cover domains, hosting, professional email, and commerce; adoption is early, with work underway to improve integration and discovery.
- Chain of Trust demonstration with Infoblox, Google, Cloudflare, and Singapore government collaboration enabled agents to transact across platforms with verified identities and blocked imposters.
- Agent Name Service remains a long-term opportunity; potential monetization is being explored but is not included in current financials.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Repurchased 5M shares for $477M in Q3; year-to-date through Oct. 26 repurchases totaled $1.4B, reducing fully diluted shares outstanding by 12%.
- FY free cash flow and disciplined, returns-based capital allocation support a three-year free cash flow CAGR target above 25%, exceeding the prior 20% target.
- Balance sheet ended Q3 with $1.1B cash, $2.3B total liquidity, $2.7B net debt, and 1.4x net leverage, within the stated target range.


### Market and industry conditions

- Google shifted much of relevant search toward AI Mode in Q3; observed traffic impact was small, supported by direct traffic and brand resilience.
- Management believes AI coding tools are driving industry growth and bringing new customer cohorts, including developers, through coding assistants, command lines, and agent interfaces.
- LLM-originated traffic jumped in Q3 from a small base after several improvements; direct traffic remains the larger traffic source and a key focus.
- Competition is increasing through domain discounts and freemium hosting; pricing decisions weigh bookings, retention, conversion, product attach, and customer lifetime value.
