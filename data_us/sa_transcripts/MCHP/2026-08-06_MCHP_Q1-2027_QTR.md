# Microchip Technology Incorporated — Q1 2027 (2026-08-06)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Net sales for the June quarter reached $1.485 billion, up 13.2% sequentially and 38% year-over-year, exceeding guidance on all key non-GAAP metrics.
- Data center exposure is broad, with total net sales from data centers expected to grow 69% from $591 million in 2025 to about $1 billion in 2026.
- Distribution inventory correction is complete, with replenishment expected in coming quarters and strong sequential growth in distribution sell-through.


### Financial highlights

- Non-GAAP gross margin was 63.8% in the June quarter, up 222 basis points sequentially; GAAP gross margin was 63.2%.
- Non-GAAP operating income was 35.1% of sales; non-GAAP net income was $438.6 million, with EPS of $0.76, $0.07 above guidance midpoint.
- Adjusted EBITDA was $587.8 million (39.6% of net sales); trailing 12-month adjusted EBITDA was $1.798 billion.
- Cash flow from operations was $511.5 million; adjusted free cash flow was $478.6 million.
- Net debt decreased by $170 million to $5.2 billion; net debt to adjusted EBITDA at 2.85, expected to drop below 2.5 next quarter.


### Outlook and guidance

- September quarter net sales expected to be up 8% sequentially (±1%), or 40.6% year-over-year.
- Non-GAAP gross margin guidance is 66%-67%, above long-term target, driven by favorable product mix, licensing, price increases, and lower underutilization charges.
- Non-GAAP EPS guidance is $0.91–$0.95, up 165.7% year-over-year and 22.4% sequentially at midpoint.
- December quarter expected to be better than typical seasonality, which is usually down 3%-5%.


### Segment performance

- June quarter end market breakdown: Industrial 32.2%, Data Centers 17.1%, Aerospace & Defense 16.7%, Automotive 15%, Communication 8.2%, Consumer Appliances 7.4%, Compute 3.4%.
- Year-over-year growth: Data center +97.8%, Aerospace & Defense +45.6%, Industrial +24.3%, Automotive +29.3%, Communication +53.3%, Consumer Appliances +19.1%, Compute +9.6%.
- Data center design wins increased to 14 (12 Gen 6 switch, 2 Gen 6 retimer), with broad exposure across product lines.
- Aerospace & Defense and Data Center segments are leading growth; Industrial and Automotive are recovering and expected to catch up.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Inventory at quarter-end was $1.047 billion, with 175 days of inventory (down 10 days sequentially); distributor inventory at 25 days.
- Non-GAAP cash tax rate was 7.5%, expected to remain for fiscal 2027.
- Capital expenditures were $13.9 million for the quarter; full-year capex expected at $100 million.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Supply chain constraints persist, especially in foundry and OSAT capacity, impacting lead times and requiring customers to provide longer-term orders.
- Gross margin guidance includes one-time benefits from licensing and inventory price adjustments, not expected to be repeatable.
- Pricing increases are not expected to recur unless significant new cost pressures arise.


### Significant events and developments

- Acquisition of Hailo, an AI-on-the-edge company, expected to close in September, advancing product roadmap by five years.
- Closure of Fab 2 and settlement of two longstanding legal matters resulted in special charges during the quarter.
- Commitment to quarterly end market breakout disclosures going forward.


### Capital allocation and financing

- All excess cash flow beyond current dividend will be used to pay down debt; no plans for share buybacks or dividend increases until leverage is further reduced.
- No plans for large-scale M&A; focus remains on small, strategic technology acquisitions.


### Market and industry conditions

- Strong demand in data center, aerospace & defense, and communication segments; industrial and automotive markets are in recovery.
- AI-driven demand is crowding out OSAT and foundry capacity, contributing to supply constraints.
- Distribution channel inventory is at the low end of historical range, with replenishment expected as customers resume ordering.
