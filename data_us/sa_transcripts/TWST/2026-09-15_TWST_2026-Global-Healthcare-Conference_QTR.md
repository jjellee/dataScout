# Twist Bioscience Corporation — 2026 Global Healthcare Conference (2026-09-15)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Technology and product strategy

- Miniaturized DNA synthesis on silicon chips enables production of up to 1 million unique DNA sequences per chip, significantly increasing capacity and reducing costs.
- Product portfolio expansion has grown the serviceable addressable market from $2B in 2021 to $7B in 2026, with a target of $13B in the future.
- Focus areas include diagnostics (notably cancer and rare disease) and therapeutics, with applications in NGS, DNA synthesis, and protein solutions.
- Strategy centers on maximizing chip utilization by adding diverse, high-value applications.


### Financial performance and growth outlook

- Achieved 23% revenue growth in Q3 and guided for 25% in Q4, with more than 20% annual growth expected through 2026.
- DSPS segment revenue grew 39% in Q3, with gene shipments up 56%; therapeutics revenue grew 49% year-over-year.
- Profitability milestone targeted for Q4, aiming for adjusted EBITDA break-even.
- Long-term plan to double revenue by 2031, implying mid-teens compound annual growth.
- Gross margin improved from 30% to over 50%, with a goal of reaching 60%.


### AI-driven drug discovery and data services

- AI-enabled drug discovery is a major growth driver, with orders expected to grow from $25M in 2025 to $100M in 2027.
- Confidence in future growth is based on customer cohort analysis, showing pilots convert to multi-million dollar orders over time.
- Data services now command higher value, with offerings ranging from DNA to protein to binding and developability data.
- Partnerships with Anthropic and Amazon Bio Discovery highlight leadership in AI-driven protein design and wet lab validation.


### Market dynamics and customer segments

- Diagnostics (NGS) revenue grew 12% in Q3, with 20% growth guided for Q4; growth driven by customers moving from R&D to commercial production.
- Academic and government segment rebounded with 32% growth in Q3, attributed to high value and competitive pricing as funding returned.
- Average selling price per gene appears to decline due to higher volume of shorter antibody genes, but overall business mix is shifting toward high-value drug discovery.


### Key opportunities and future focus

- Top growth opportunities identified as AI-led discovery, MRD (minimal residual disease) diagnostics, and mRNA personalized cancer therapeutics.
- Business model designed for diversification, with multiple applications leveraging the same core chip technology.
- Ongoing focus on expanding product offerings, increasing chip utilization, and maintaining high growth and profitability.
