# The Trade Desk, Inc. — Q2 2026 (2026-08-06)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Revenue growth for Q2 2026 was below expectations, attributed to macroeconomic pressures and internal execution challenges.
- The business remains fundamentally strong, with most divisions and clients growing, especially outside the largest CPG and auto advertisers.
- Strategic focus is on product innovation, leadership enhancement, and deepening client partnerships to drive long-term growth.


### Financial highlights

- Q2 2026 revenue was $715 million, up 3% year-over-year.
- Adjusted EBITDA was $241 million (34% margin); net income was $64 million (9% of revenue); adjusted net income was $158 million.
- Free cash flow was $136 million; cash and equivalents totaled $1.5 billion at quarter end.
- CTV and audio channels saw double-digit growth; audio was the fastest-growing channel at 7% of business.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q3 2026 revenue expected to be at least $650 million; adjusted EBITDA guidance is $160 million.
- Investment will focus on high-priority growth initiatives, with disciplined resource allocation.
- No meaningful improvement in the macro environment is assumed in the near-term outlook.


### Segment performance

- Video (including CTV) represented a low fifties percent share; mobile high twenties; display low double digits.
- EMEA and APAC regions grew nearly 30% year-to-date; China grew over 100%.
- CTV growth exceeded 50% year-over-year in both EMEA and APAC.
- Outside the top 500 advertisers, revenue grew over 50% year-over-year.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted EBITDA margin: 34%.
- Net income margin: 9%.
- Joint Business Plans (JBPs) with 217 clients, up 38% year-over-year; JBP revenue grew 6x faster than overall revenue.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Macroeconomic headwinds, especially in CPG and auto sectors, due to tariffs, oil prices, and consumer bifurcation.
- Some advertisers shifting to lower-cost, less sophisticated media buying approaches.
- Limited visibility into near-term improvement in the operating environment.


### Significant events and developments

- Launch of new measurement framework (in alpha) to improve attribution across the customer journey.
- Audience Unlimited product moving to open beta, showing >25% efficiency gains in early campaigns.
- Major platform usability upgrade (Zuma) launching, enhancing AI-driven workflows.
- Strengthened leadership team with key hires in commercial, business development, and client strategy roles.
- Renewed partnership with Walmart; expanded partnerships with global media and data companies.


### Capital allocation and financing

- $78 million used for share repurchases in Q2; $269 million remains authorized.
- Continued investment in owned data centers to support AI and reduce reliance on public cloud.


### Market and industry conditions

- Global ad market estimated at $1 trillion; company participates in ~1% of TAM.
- AI and connected TV are expanding addressable market and creating new opportunities.
- Retail media and data-driven advertising are key secular growth drivers.
- Competitive landscape includes walled gardens prioritizing owned inventory; objectivity and data protection seen as differentiators.
