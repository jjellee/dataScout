# The Trade Desk, Inc. — Q1 2026 (2026-05-07)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Delivered a solid Q1 2026 with continued profitability and resilience amid a dynamic macro environment, including geopolitical tensions and global economic pressures.
- Strong conviction in long-term growth opportunities, especially as advertising shifts to data-driven, programmatic models and the open internet.
- Emphasized leadership in innovation, AI-driven decisioning, and retail media, with a focus on objectivity and independence.


### Financial highlights

- Q1 2026 revenue reached $689 million, up 12% year-over-year.
- Adjusted EBITDA was $206 million (30% margin); net income was $40 million (6% of revenue), or $0.08 per diluted share.
- Adjusted net income was $134 million, or $0.28 per diluted share.
- Net cash from operating activities was $392 million; free cash flow was $276 million.
- Ended Q1 with $1.4 billion in cash equivalents and short-term investments.
- Repurchased $164 million of Class A common stock in Q1.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q2 2026 revenue expected to be at least $750 million; adjusted EBITDA estimated at $260 million.
- Full-year 2026 adjusted EBITDA margin expected to be at least 40%, in line with 2025.
- Headcount growth to remain below revenue growth, prioritizing productivity and operating leverage.


### Segment performance

- CTV and audio channels drove strong growth; video (including CTV) was a low 50s% share of business, mobile high 20s%, display low double digits, and audio 6% (fastest-growing channel year-over-year).
- U.S. accounted for 82% of revenue; international 18%, with strong momentum in EMEA and APAC.
- Medical health, automotive, and events verticals showed strong growth; home and garden and food and drink faced pressure.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Q1 operating expenses were $622 million, up 11% year-over-year; excluding stock-based compensation, $513 million, up 18%.
- Income tax expense was $39 million.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Macro environment remains complex due to geopolitical tensions, tariffs, and global economic pressures, impacting some verticals like CPG and autos.
- Measurement challenges and evolving industry standards present headwinds for brand builders.


### Significant events and developments

- Signed 45 JBPs in March; total JBP count grew 55% year-over-year, with new JBP deal spend up 40%.
- Won back a major pharmaceutical client from Amazon, securing a 114% year-over-year increase in spend.
- Chief Strategy Officer Samantha Jacobson is leaving for OpenAI but remains on the board.
- Launched Audience Unlimited, delivering 30% lower CPMs, 38% lower data costs, 75% more efficient CPA, and 2.7x higher conversion rates.
- New partnerships with Lyft Ads, Kevel, and Dollar General to expand retail media and sponsored listings.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Continued share repurchases to offset dilution and opportunistically return capital.
- Strong cash flow and liquidity support ongoing disciplined investment in innovation and platform enhancements.


### Market and industry conditions

- Advertising market is in a historic buyer's market due to excess supply; open internet is thriving as premium publishers and advertisers align.
- AI and agentic technologies are reshaping advertising, with The Trade Desk positioned as a hub for innovation.
- Retail media, CTV, and AI-driven measurement are key growth areas.
