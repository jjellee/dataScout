# Amphenol Corporation — Q3 2026 (2026-10-28)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Record Q3 sales of $10.2B, up 65% in U.S. dollars and local currencies and 37% organically YoY; adjusted diluted EPS reached a record $0.80.
- Record orders of $12.17B, up 99% YoY and 13% sequentially; book-to-bill was 1.19:1, positive in all but one end market.
- Results exceeded the high end of guidance for sales and adjusted diluted EPS; five of seven end markets achieved organic growth above 20%.


### Financial highlights

- GAAP and adjusted operating income were approximately $3B; GAAP and adjusted operating margins were 29.5% and 29.7%, respectively.
- Adjusted operating margin rose 220 bps YoY and declined 10 bps sequentially; excluding the larger Q2 tariff-recovery benefit, margin improved ~70 bps sequentially.
- GAAP diluted EPS was $0.79, up 61% YoY; adjusted diluted EPS was $0.80, up 74% from $0.46.
- Operating cash flow was $2.1B and free cash flow was $1.6B, both records; each equaled 102% and 80% of net income, respectively.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q4 sales guidance is $10.3B-$10.5B and adjusted diluted EPS $0.81-$0.83, assuming current market conditions and constant exchange rates; excludes pending acquisitions.
- FY2026 guidance is sales of $36.9B-$37.1B and adjusted diluted EPS of $2.82-$2.84, increases of ~60% and ~70%, respectively.
- Q4 market outlook: IT datacom sales up mid-single digits; mobile devices up ~35%; defense up mid-single digits; commercial air steady; industrial moderating; automotive slightly lower; communications networks declining further.
- FY2026 CommScope outlook increased to $5.1B sales and $0.18 adjusted EPS contribution.


### Segment performance

- Communications solutions sales were $6.6B, up 99% reported and 51% organically YoY; operating margin was 33.1%.
- Harsh environment solutions sales were $2.0B, up 35% reported and 29% organically YoY; operating margin was 30.1%.
- Interconnect and sensor systems sales were $1.6B, up 17% reported and 11% organically YoY; operating margin was 20.0%.
- IT datacom, 48% of sales, grew 117% reported and 77% organically YoY, and 30% sequentially; AI represented ~three-quarters of IT datacom sales.
- Industrial sales grew 60% reported and 25% organically YoY; automotive grew 13% reported and 9% organically, while mobile devices rose 31% reported and 29% organically.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted effective tax rate was 27%, unchanged YoY; GAAP effective tax rate was 27.4%, versus 23.5% in Q3 2025.
- Q3 adjusted EBITDA was $3.4B; net leverage was 1.1x at quarter-end.
- September 30 total debt was $17.9B and net debt was $12.6B; total liquidity was $8.3B, including $5.3B cash and short-term investments.
- Q3 operating cash flow was 102% of net income and free cash flow was 80%; inventory days, DSO and payable days remained within normal ranges.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Communications networks organic sales declined 28% YoY and 13% sequentially amid softer broadband and operator demand; further Q4 decline is expected.
- Automotive demand has uncertain pockets; Q4 sales are expected to moderate slightly from Q3.
- Acquisitions can dilute margins relative to existing operations, while management expects organic growth to support further margin expansion.
- CommScope cited constraints in staffing and materials, including glass; it is diversifying suppliers and securing supply to support customer demand.
- Management noted potential exposure if global military spending falls sharply, partially offset by replenishment needs, next-generation technology and broad product coverage.


### Significant events and developments

- Empire Electronics acquisition closed in early September; expected 2026 revenue is ~$320M, and it joined interconnect and sensor systems.
- Definitive agreements signed for AdvancedPCB (~$270M expected 2026 sales), Dexgreen (~$90M) and Humanetics Sensors (~$70M); closings expected by end of Q1 2027.
- CommScope IT datacom sales are expected to more than double in FY2026 and represent roughly half of its sales, versus about one-third last year.
- A two-for-one stock split became effective September 3, 2026; all share and per-share figures on the call reflect the split.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Q3 repurchases were 3M shares at an average ~$81; total shareholder returns, including the quarterly dividend, were ~$553M.
- Quarterly dividend increased 20% to $0.15 per share, effective for payments beginning January 2027.
- A new three-year $4B repurchase plan begins January 1, 2027; remaining purchases under the current plan are expected to finish by December 31, 2026.
- Management expects long-term capital intensity to remain around 3%-4%, with periods slightly above 4% during rapid growth.


### Market and industry conditions

- AI-related demand drove most sequential IT datacom growth; management expects ongoing high customer procurement and Q4 growth from Q3 levels, with no project-based lumpiness observed in Q3.
- Defense demand was broad-based, with nearly every tracked application growing double digits; geopolitical investment, technology upgrades and multi-year replenishment support the outlook.
- Industrial growth spanned building automation, instrumentation, oil and gas, alternative energy, electrification, factory automation and heavy equipment; most tracked submarkets grew organically in double digits.
- Customers are seeking more copper, optical and power interconnect for next-generation AI and accelerated-compute architectures; 800-volt and higher power formats are an expanding opportunity.
