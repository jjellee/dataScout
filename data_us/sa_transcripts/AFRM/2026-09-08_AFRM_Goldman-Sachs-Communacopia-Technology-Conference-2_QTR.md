# Affirm Holdings, Inc. — Goldman Sachs Communacopia + Technology Conference 2026 (2026-09-08)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Growth strategy and business outlook

- Targeting $100 billion in annual GMV with a 25% growth algorithm, leveraging point-of-sale, direct-to-consumer, new products, and international expansion.
- U.S. market still offers significant growth potential, especially in top e-commerce retailers and offline channels.
- International expansion, particularly in the U.K. and Australia, is progressing well, with a dedicated SVP now leading global strategy.
- ISV and wallet partnerships are driving both GMV growth and customer acquisition, opening new verticals and use cases.
- Focus remains on expanding product offerings and network reach through innovation and strategic partnerships.


### Leadership and organizational changes

- Leadership responsibilities consolidated under two long-tenured executives, freeing up the CEO to focus on innovation.
- No staff reductions; changes aimed at increasing executive bandwidth for strategic projects.
- CEO now dedicates significant time to the ZT 1 (Zero To One) innovation team, fostering new product development.


### Innovation and product development

- ZT 1 operates as an internal lab, responsible for launching high-impact products like the Affirm Card.
- Goal is to launch a major new product annually to drive future GMV growth.
- Recent ZT 1 products have shown high consumer engagement, with some users spending up to $8,000 annually.
- BSP (Brand Sponsored Promotions), a major ZT 1 project, is now live and scaling rapidly.


### AI and operational efficiency

- AI tools are used to increase productivity and accelerate feature development without reducing headcount.
- Cost per feature shipped is steadily declining, enabling more output with the same team size.
- AI-driven underwriting and product optimization are central to scaling 0% financing and other offerings.


### Distribution channels and marketing

- Card product has grown to nearly 20% of GMV, primarily through internal channels and merchant partnerships, with minimal external marketing.
- Focus remains on saturating the existing customer base before expanding marketing efforts.
- Universal Financing Programs ensure consistent offers across all channels, supported by advanced AI and legal frameworks.
- Wallet and ISV partnerships are key growth drivers, providing access to new customers and industries.


### 0% financing and merchant solutions

- 0% loans are a core offering, enabled by adaptive AI-driven underwriting and multiple funding models, including merchant and brand sponsorship.
- Programs like BSP allow brands to subsidize consumer financing without revealing sensitive margin data.
- 0% offers significantly increase merchant basket size and conversion rates.


### International expansion

- U.K. market has responded positively to transparent, long-term, no-fee products.
- Organizational changes include appointing a senior executive to lead global strategy.
- International volume is growing, with further expansion planned and strong executive involvement.
