# Mohawk Industries, Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-10-30)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 results were in line with expectations as demand softened late in the quarter; price and mix actions plus productivity offset underlying inflation.
- Net sales were $2.8B, up 1% reported and essentially flat adjusting for FX and an extra shipping day; volumes fell 4%, while price and mix rose 4%.
- New four-pillar framework prioritizes market outperformance, innovation and brands, operational excellence, and cash generation with disciplined capital deployment.


### Financial highlights

- Net earnings were $163M, or 5.9% of sales; adjusted EBITDA was $353M, with a 12.7% margin.
- Operating income was $188M; adjusted operating income was $200M, down $5M YoY. EPS was $2.72 reported and adjusted.
- Price and mix contributed $85M and productivity $47M, offsetting approximately $117M of underlying input cost inflation.
- Cash from operations was $471M; free cash flow was $390M, up 26% YoY, and YTD free cash flow was $626M, up 78%.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q4 adjusted EPS guidance is $1.35–$1.45; adjusted net sales are expected to grow low single digits YoY on a days- and FX-adjusted basis.
- Q4 assumes softer demand than Q3, four fewer shipping days YoY, and underlying inflation similar to Q3; price, mix, and productivity are expected to fully offset inflation.
- 2027 planning assumes declining market volumes and mid-single-digit underlying cost-of-sales inflation into early year; pricing, mix, and productivity are expected to offset inflation dollar-for-dollar.
- 2026 capital expenditures are now expected to be ~$445M, versus prior guidance of $460M; baseline annual capital needs are estimated at ~$375M.


### Segment performance

- Global Ceramic sales were $1.1B, up 2% reported; adjusted operating income was $74M, or 6.5% of sales, down 160 bps YoY.
- Flooring North America sales were $950M, up 1% reported and down less than 1% excluding the extra shipping day; adjusted operating income was $77M, or 8.1% of sales, up 80 bps YoY.
- Flooring Rest of the World sales were $707M, down ~1% reported; adjusted operating income was $66M, or 9.3% of sales, up 100 bps YoY.
- Rest of World constant volumes fell ~10%; price and mix contributed ~7% to sales growth, while weak residential demand affected Europe, Australia, and New Zealand.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted EBITDA margin was 12.7%; net earnings were 5.9% of sales.
- Adjusted segment operating margins: Global Ceramic 6.5%, Flooring North America 8.1%, and Flooring Rest of the World 9.3%.
- Net debt leverage improved to 0.7x at quarter-end; long-term target is up to 2.0x net debt to adjusted EBITDA, subject to cyclical risk and investment-grade ratings.
- Q4 tax-rate assumption is 23.5%; Q3 rate was 19.3%, with the full-year 2026 rate expected to remain in line with prior expectations.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- U.S. demand weakened late in Q3 and remained soft in October, particularly in builder channels; higher mortgage rates, affordability constraints, and lower builder confidence weighed on construction.
- European consumer demand remained soft, while natural gas prices roughly doubled since Q3 began; higher energy, chemical, freight, labor, and raw-material costs added inflation pressure.
- Q4 lower volumes, four fewer shipping days, and seasonal weakness are expected to pressure margins and fixed-cost absorption despite dollar-neutral price-cost actions.


### Significant events and developments

- Paul De Cock began his first earnings call as CEO; Jeff Lorberbaum remains Chairman and continues to support the leadership transition.
- Management introduced four strategic pillars: outperform markets; strengthen product innovation and brands; improve profitability and operations; and generate cash with capital discipline.
- A comprehensive portfolio review will assess market attractiveness, competitive advantage, cash profitability, and return on invested capital, with repositioning or other actions possible.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Q3 repurchases were $261M, reducing outstanding shares by more than 3%; a further $34M was returned early in Q4, completing the prior authorization.
- A new $500M repurchase authorization was approved; excess capital after high-return core investment and disciplined bolt-on opportunities is intended for consistent buybacks.
- Baseline annual capex of ~$375M is intended to fund maintenance, productivity, safety, and innovation; limited near-term capacity additions are planned.
- Balance-sheet leverage may rise over time toward 2.0x; deployment pace is not specified, and liquidity options include free cash flow and other capital-structure flexibility.


### Market and industry conditions

- U.S. residential demand was weak, with new construction most affected; specialty retail performed better and gained share, while home-center point-of-sale trends remained slow.
- U.S. commercial demand was more constructive than residential, supported by renovation; return-to-office helped carpet tile, and commercial LVT recorded a sixth consecutive month of order growth.
- European residential activity and showroom traffic remained weak, while commercial projects held up better; premium collections and selected ceramic and laminate offerings continued to outperform.
- Management estimates long-term industry growth at low single digits annually and plans to pursue share gains through market cycles.
