# Marvell Technology, Inc. — Investor Day 2026 (2026-10-06)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Strategy and market opportunity

- Strategy has centered on data infrastructure since 2017; roughly $40B invested over the past decade, split roughly evenly between acquisitions and organic investment.
- AI workloads are making efficient connectivity a key infrastructure constraint; FY26 revenue was $8.2B, with 75% from data center and roughly 70% from connectivity.
- CY30 addressable market is about $400B, growing at a 45% CAGR from CY25; this is 4x the prior estimate of less than $100B. Data center CapEx is modeled at roughly $3T by CY30, growing at 35%.
- CY30 TAM by business: interconnect about $65B, switching and storage about $85B, custom about $235B, and communications and other about $15B.
- Strategy centers on the top 10 hyperscalers, with the top four U.S. customers alone supporting most long-term goals; hundreds of products and many sockets diversify exposure.


### Revenue outlook

- FY28 total revenue outlook raised to about $20B from $18B in August; this implies about 67% YoY growth, with data center revenue of about $18B and roughly 80% growth.
- Custom revenue is expected to exceed $12B in FY29, more than tripling from FY28 and exceeding the prior target of more than $10B; broader XPU attach wins drove the increase.
- FY31 revenue target is $70B-$90B, or $80B at midpoint; data center is $67.5B-$87.5B and communications and other contributes about $2.5B.
- FY26-FY31 revenue growth framework is a 55%-60% CAGR, about 10x growth from FY26; data center revenue is expected to grow at roughly 65% CAGR at the midpoint.
- FY31 segment midpoints: interconnect $37.5B, switching and storage about $10B, custom about $30B, and communications and other about $2.5B.


### Business and technology developments

- Interconnect TAM is projected to reach about $65B by CY30, growing roughly 65% annually; scale-up optics adds a new growth avenue as bandwidth pushes connections from copper toward optics.
- Scale-out switching is expected to exceed $1B next FY; the Teralynx T100 is a 100T monolithic switch with 20% or more lower latency and power than leading competitors.
- Data center interconnect revenue is expected to rise from roughly $500M in FY26 to safely over $1B in FY28, with a path to multiple billions in FY29-FY31.
- Custom silicon was about a $1.5B business in FY26, with shipments to all four U.S. hyperscalers; XPU attach expands into networking, memory, storage, infrastructure management, and inference acceleration.
- XPU attach opportunities are described as sticky, multi-generational sockets; estimates cited 1-2 sockets per XPU and ASPs around $1,000, with individual categories potentially reaching billions.


### Execution and financial model

- FY24 R&D spend was $1.5B against $2.2B of data center revenue; more than 80% of compute infrastructure is now in the cloud, supporting faster design scaling and delivery.
- Capacity planning relies on five-year supplier forecasts and long-term relationships; management said demand and supply are in place for the FY28 $20B revenue plan.
- FY31 targets: gross margin 56%-59% depending on mix; operating margin 44%-46%, six points above the prior long-term target; tax rate 15% and free cash flow margin above 36%.
- Non-GAAP EPS is expected to exceed $30 in FY31; operating expenses are expected to grow at roughly half the revenue growth rate, supporting earnings growth above revenue growth.
- Capital priorities remain growth investment and shareholder returns: more than 50% of free cash flow to be returned on average over time, while maintaining a strong, flexible balance sheet.
