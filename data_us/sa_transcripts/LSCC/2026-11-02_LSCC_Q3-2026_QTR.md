# Lattice Semiconductor Corporation — Q3 2026 (2026-11-02)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Record Q3 revenue of $291M, up 45% QoQ and 118% YoY; non-GAAP EPS of $0.72 exceeded guidance, supported by broad-based growth and operating leverage.
- Q4 revenue midpoint of $335M implies ~130% YoY growth; accelerating bookings and backlog into 2028 underpin expectations for at least 60% revenue growth in 2027.


### Financial highlights

- Q3 FPGA revenue was $252M, up ~90% YoY and 25% QoQ; AMI contributed $39M during its first two months after acquisition, above expectations.
- Q3 non-GAAP operating expense was $86.7M, up ~29% QoQ, primarily due to AMI and product roadmap investment.
- GAAP operating cash flow was $95M versus $88.3M in Q2; free cash flow was $77.6M, with higher-than-run-rate capex for test capacity and R&D capabilities.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q4 revenue guidance is $325M-$345M; non-GAAP gross margin 72% ±1%, operating expense $95M-$101M, tax rate 4%-6%, and EPS $0.82-$0.86.
- FY2026 revenue is expected to be ~ $1B, roughly 90% growth YoY; FPGA revenue is expected to exceed $900M, and non-GAAP EPS is expected at ~ $2.50, up ~140%.
- 2027 revenue growth is expected to be at least 60% over the projected ~$1B 2026 base; planning conservatively assumes slower growth in H2 than H1, while retaining meaningful YoY H2 growth.
- Long-term outlook calls for revenue CAGR above 30% over five years; management expects EPS growth to outpace revenue growth over time.


### Segment performance

- Compute & Communications revenue grew 132% YoY and 36% QoQ, on record demand tied to data-center AI applications and higher FPGA content and attach rates.
- Industrial & Embedded revenue rose 35% YoY and 7% QoQ; management expects growth to accelerate in Q4 and continue into 2027 as design wins ramp and channel inventory normalizes.
- AMI delivered $39M revenue in its partial Q3, with higher-than-expected revenue and favorable high-margin mix; management sees a ~$200M run-rate business and meaningful growth in 2027.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Q3 non-GAAP gross margin was 70.9%; non-GAAP operating margin expanded 280 bps QoQ to 41.1%, and EBITDA margin rose 200 bps to 45%.
- Q3 non-GAAP EPS was $0.72, up ~160% YoY and 36% QoQ; GAAP operating cash flow was $95M and free cash flow was $77.6M.
- Q4 non-GAAP gross margin guidance is 72% ±1%; AMI margins are expected to expand into the high 70s as its non-core business exits.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Q3 supply constraints and expedite costs affected execution; supply availability and lead times improved, delinquencies declined significantly, and multi-year supplier agreements were secured.
- Supplier cost increases and expedite fees are being passed through to customers; management characterized pricing as a smaller growth factor than unit growth, higher-value applications, and new products.
- Management cited environmental uncertainty and adopted a prudent 2027 plan, particularly for H2; demand remains strong, with backlog extending into 2028.
- Channel inventory is intended to remain near current levels, while internal inventory is being built to support expected growth; management reported no signs of double ordering in Industrial & Embedded.


### Significant events and developments

- AMI acquisition closed July 27; follow-on launches included AMI Rack Manager, four joint rack solutions, and the Secure Control and Management Plane Initiative with industry participants.
- Lattice Prompt launched to generate and validate FPGA applications using natural-language prompts; Mach-N2, a secure-control FPGA platform with post-quantum cryptography, entered customer sampling.
- Security-retrofit solution is expected to enter production with its first customer in Q4 2026, with broader 2027 production and meaningful revenue contribution expected in 2028.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Capital priorities remain investment for growth, financial flexibility, rapid acquisition-debt reduction, and opportunistic repurchases; $50M of debt was repaid in Q3.
- Net leverage is expected below 2x EBITDA by year-end 2026, with aggressive debt reduction planned for 2027 while continuing growth investment.
- Near-term focus is debt repayment and successful AMI integration; cash generation is directed toward reducing debt rather than building cash balances.


### Market and industry conditions

- Data-center demand is supported by AI infrastructure investment, server deployments for agentic inference, greater AI content per server, and rising security requirements; backlog and bookings extend into 2028.
- Server units are expected to rise from ~20M in 2026 to ~25M in 2027; AI server share is projected to increase from ~25% to ~40%.
- Growth vectors include higher FPGA attach rates, new Nexus and Avant products, physical AI, factory automation, robotics, aerospace and defense, and broader secure-control applications.
