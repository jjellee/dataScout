# HeartFlow, Inc. — Q2 2026 (2026-08-13)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q2 revenue reached $64.1 million, up 48% year-over-year, with U.S. revenue up 51%, marking the fastest growth in eight quarters.
- Growth was driven by strong plaque activations, durable FF-RCT utilization, new account additions, and CCTA market expansion.
- Plaque adoption and utilization exceeded expectations, with a record pace of new account activations and physician engagement.
- The company was selected as the exclusive plaque provider for the NIH-funded PRE-EMPT Study, reinforcing clinical leadership.
- Innovation pipeline advanced with the launch of Plaque Staging and ongoing rollout of PCI Navigator.


### Financial highlights

- Total Q2 revenue: $64.1 million, up 48% year-over-year; U.S. revenue: $59.6 million, up 51%.
- Plaque revenue: $7.8 million; OUS and other revenue: $4.5 million.
- Global revenue cases: 84,491, up 74% year-over-year.
- Gross margin: 83.3%, up from 75.6% in Q2 2025.
- Non-GAAP operating loss: $7.9 million (vs. $11.5 million prior year); non-GAAP net loss: $5.8 million, or $0.07 per share.
- Cash, equivalents, and investments: $246.8 million at quarter end.


### Outlook and guidance

- Full-year 2026 revenue guidance raised to $246–$250 million, representing 40–42% year-over-year growth.
- Plaque revenue outlook increased to $29–$31 million; plaque activated accounts expected to reach ~1,250.
- Non-GAAP gross margin guidance raised to ~82%; mid-term target remains 85%.
- Full-year non-GAAP operating expenses as a percentage of revenue expected to decline year-over-year.
- Cash flow profitability targeted by mid-2028.


### Segment performance

- Plaque: Accelerating activations and utilization, with strong physician adoption and expanding coverage (~78%).
- FF-RCT: Durable utilization across existing and new accounts, with strong volume growth and favorable customer mix.
- PCI Navigator: Early rollout focused on high-volume PCI hospitals, with strong initial feedback.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Gross margin expanded by 770 basis points year-over-year.
- Operating expenses as a percentage of revenue improved by 600 basis points year-over-year.
- Non-GAAP operating expenses were 96% of revenue (vs. 102% prior year).
- Weighted average shares outstanding: 86.4 million.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Ongoing patent litigation and DOJ investigation are expected to be multi-year processes but are not seen as distractions.
- CMS reimbursement changes for AI-enabled software are viewed as constructive, with no near-term negative impact anticipated.


### Significant events and developments

- Selected as exclusive plaque provider for the NIH PRE-EMPT Study.
- Launched Plaque Staging, validated in over 23,000 patients.
- Presented eight new data sets at SCCT, including studies validating plaque analysis precision and risk stratification.
- Initiating three RCTs targeting high-risk asymptomatic populations, aiming to expand TAM by $6 billion.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Continued disciplined investment in R&D and commercial expansion.
- Balance sheet supports operations through profitability.


### Market and industry conditions

- CCTA market continues to expand, supported by updated guidelines and reimbursement.
- Plaque and FF-RCT adoption benefiting from strong clinical evidence and differentiation.
- CMS's proposed reimbursement framework for AI-enabled software aligns with the company's value proposition.
