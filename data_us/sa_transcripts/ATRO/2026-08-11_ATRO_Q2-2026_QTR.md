# Astronics Corporation — Q2 2026 (2026-08-11)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Achieved record second quarter results with all-time highs in revenue, operating profit, bookings, and backlog, driven by strong market demand and operational execution.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin reached 19.8%, a modern-day high, reflecting significant margin improvement from prior years.
- Raised full-year revenue guidance to $1.02 billion–$1.04 billion, crossing the billion-dollar threshold for the first time.


### Financial highlights

- Q2 sales reached $260 million, up 27% year-over-year; gross profit was $86.9 million (33.4% of sales), up from $52.8 million (25.8%).
- Net income was $35.1 million ($0.75 per diluted share); adjusted net income was $32.6 million ($0.70 per share).
- Adjusted EBITDA was $51.5 million, more than double the prior year; adjusted EBITDA margin expanded 340 bps sequentially to 19.8%.
- Operating income was $40.5 million (15.6% of sales); adjusted operating income was $43.2 million (16.6% margin).
- Interest expense declined 24.7% year-over-year due to refinancing.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q3 sales expected in the $265 million–$275 million range, with Q4 revenue to improve modestly.
- Full-year revenue guidance raised to $1.02 billion–$1.04 billion.
- Margins expected to benefit from volume leverage, U.S. Army radio test program, and potential future tariff refunds.


### Segment performance

- Aerospace sales hit a record $237.3 million, up 22.6% year-over-year, with growth across all markets.
- In-flight entertainment and connectivity sales grew 19% to $126 million; lighting and safety up 5.5% to $59.2 million.
- Flight critical electrical power sales rose 49.4% to $23.7 million; seat motion sales increased to $22.2 million, including Bühler Motor Aviation contribution.
- Aerospace segment operating profit was $48.3 million (20.3% margin); adjusted margin was 21.4%.
- Test systems sales were $22.7 million, up $11.6 million; segment operating profit was $600,000, with margin improvement expected as Army radio test ramps.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Gross margin expanded 760 bps year-over-year to 33.4%.
- SG&A expense declined to 13.7% of sales from 17.8%.
- Book-to-bill ratio for aerospace was 1.02; test systems was 2.78.
- Aerospace backlog at quarter-end was $657.2 million; test systems backlog was $123.3 million.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Ongoing exposure to inflation and timing of repricing remaining long-term contracts.
- Uncertainty regarding the final outcome of the long-running patent dispute with Lufthansa Technik in Europe.
- Mix variability and timing of tariff refunds may impact margins.


### Significant events and developments

- Secured $27 million FLRAA MV-75 development booking and $45 million U.S. Army radio test production order.
- Executed a 20% B share distribution to shareholders to rebalance share count.
- Positive developments in the patent dispute with Lufthansa Technik, with favorable rulings in the U.S. and U.K.
- Bühler Motor Aviation acquisition performing well, with integration underway.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Generated $30.1 million in operating cash flow; CapEx was $5.7 million for the quarter, $16.9 million YTD.
- Long-term debt reduced by $24.1 million to $310.3 million; liquidity at $253.2 million.
- Capital priorities are internal investment and debt reduction, with selective M&A considered.


### Market and industry conditions

- Strong demand across commercial, military, and general aviation markets.
- Labor market has stabilized post-pandemic, with improved retention and hiring conditions.
- Transition from GEO to LEO satellite connectivity causing short-term disruption but long-term opportunity.
- Emerging aircraft trends (eVTOL, drones) expected to contribute more meaningfully in 2027.
