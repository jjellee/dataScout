# Bausch Health Companies Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-10-28)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- 14th consecutive quarter of YoY revenue and Adjusted EBITDA growth, excluding Bausch + Lomb; quarterly Adjusted EBITDA exceeded $1B for the first time.
- Strong first nine months supported a second consecutive quarterly increase to FY 2026 guidance for revenue, Adjusted EBITDA and adjusted cash flow from operations.


### Financial highlights

- Consolidated Q3 revenue was $2.941B, up 10% reported and 8% organic YoY; Adjusted EBITDA was $1.305B, up $319M or 32%.
- Consolidated adjusted gross margin was 75%, up 230 bps YoY; adjusted cash flow from operations was $782M, up $274M or 54%.
- Excluding Bausch + Lomb, Q3 revenue was $1.566B, up 12% YoY; Adjusted EBITDA was $1.022B and adjusted cash flow from operations was $539M, up $192M YoY.
- First-nine-month revenue and Adjusted EBITDA, excluding Bausch + Lomb, increased 14% and 26%, respectively.


### Outlook and guidance

- FY 2026 guidance, excluding Bausch + Lomb: revenue $5.525B–$5.625B, midpoint growth 8% YoY; Adjusted EBITDA $3.225B–$3.275B, midpoint growth 13%.
- Adjusted cash flow from operations guidance is $1.600B–$1.675B, midpoint growth 29% YoY; guidance midpoints rose $150M for revenue, ~$190M for Adjusted EBITDA and $200M for cash flow.
- Q4 gross-to-net accrual for the 2027 XIFAXAN Medicare price change is expected to reduce revenue growth by 800 bps and Adjusted EBITDA growth by 1,400 bps; normalization of channel inventory may reduce growth by another 300 bps and EBITDA growth by 500 bps.
- Solta expects mid-single-digit overall growth in Q4, with strong reported growth and organic growth also expected; results include timing effects related to China distributor integration.


### Segment performance

- Salix revenue was $831M, up $115M; XIFAXAN revenue grew 16% YoY on favorable net realized pricing and demand, with Q3 volume also boosted by higher channel inventory.
- International revenue was $299M, up 4% organically; EMEA grew 3%, LATAM top 10 products rose 18%, and Canada organic revenue increased 7%.
- Solta revenue was $165M, up 18% reported but down 4% organically in Q3; YTD revenue rose 34% reported and 8% organic.
- Diversified segment revenue was $271M, up 5% YoY, driven primarily by double-digit dermatology growth; Bausch + Lomb revenue was $1.375B, up 7% organically.
- Thermage FLX grew 21% globally; Fraxel FTX and Clear + Brilliant Touch grew 52% and 49%, respectively, off more modest bases.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Last-12-month revenue increased ~24% cumulatively since year-end 2023; last-12-month Adjusted EBITDA margin expanded ~740 bps versus 2023.
- Salix total retail prescriptions for XIFAXAN, excluding Medicaid, grew 1.8%; new-to-brand patients increased by 1,800 sequentially in Q3.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- XIFAXAN Q4 results face gross-to-net accrual adjustments ahead of the Jan. 1, 2027 Medicare price change and assumed channel-inventory normalization.
- China aesthetics demand faced softer consumer confidence and local and Korean competitors; U.S. Solta was flat overall amid competition and field-execution work.
- XIFAXAN summary judgment has been heard with no ruling yet; the FDA framework for the Norwich 2 remand had not yet returned to the FDA.
- Larsucosterol’s Phase III program remains subject to successful clinical development and regulatory review; no interim analysis is planned, and approval submission remains targeted for 2029 if Phase II results are replicated.


### Significant events and developments

- CEO Thomas Appio was appointed Interim CEO of Bausch + Lomb and reiterated his commitment to both businesses; operating teams and priorities remain in place.
- Phase III development of larsucosterol for alcohol-associated hepatitis is progressing toward a 2029 approval goal, subject to trial and regulatory outcomes.
- Business development remains a priority, with opportunities under evaluation in hepatology, gastroenterology, dermatology and neuroscience.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Net debt declined $521M in Q3 and more than $1B since year-end; cash on hand was $2B.
- Capital allocation may include early redemption of 2027 notes and up to $100M of open-market repurchases of public debt.
- A $50M share repurchase program was announced, primarily to offset expected dilution from management equity awards vesting in 2027.


### Market and industry conditions

- XIFAXAN volume benefited from higher channel inventory in Q3; anticipated Medicare pricing changes and subsequent inventory normalization are expected to weigh on Q4 growth.
- China aesthetics markets faced softer consumer confidence and spending, with local Chinese and Korean competition affecting demand.
- Solta’s 2025 quarterly comparisons were affected by tariff volatility on U.S. imports to China and uneven revenue phasing; Korea equipment placements had also been elevated in prior years.
