# Aura Minerals Inc. — Mining Forum Americas 2026 (2026-09-28)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Strategy and operating scale

- Shareholder-value approach: grow production, pay significant dividends, maintain manageable leverage and improve valuation multiples.
- Operations expanded from three producing mines three years ago to six: four gold mines in Brazil, one in Honduras and one copper mine in Mexico.
- 2026 production guidance: 340,000-390,000 oz gold equivalent; production has increased more than 170% since 2018.
- Growth model combines building greenfield projects on time and budget with acquiring and improving non-core assets.


### Costs, cash flow and capital returns

- 2025 AISC was $1,458/oz, placing operations in the gold sector’s first quartile; excluding MSG, AISC is in the $1,600 range.
- MSG AISC is above $3,000/oz in 2026 during its turnaround; costs are expected to approach $2,000/oz from 2027.
- EBITDA rose from $134 million in 2023 to more than $800 million in the last 12 months; strong EBITDA-to-free-cash-flow conversion supports investment and distributions.
- Almost $700 million invested in expansion, mainly acquisitions and project construction; geology and exploration spending has accelerated since 2022, with reserves growing faster than depletion.
- More than $440 million distributed through dividends and buybacks over the last 4.5 years; dividend yields typically ranged from 5%-6%, while net debt/EBITDA never exceeded 0.7x and is now 0.2x.


### Growth projects and production targets

- Portfolio projects are expected to support at least 600,000 oz of production in the medium term; acquisitions are being pursued toward a 1 million oz producer over that timeframe.
- Borborema reached commercial production and is ramping up, with full production in 2026; Era Dorada is under construction, and Matupá is ready to build after Era Dorada advances.
- Era Dorada is expected to add more than 110,000 oz/year; construction is expected to finish by end-2027, with production starting in early 2028.
- Almas plant capacity is expanding from 1.3 million to 3 million tonnes this year; the project was built for $76 million and has an NPV close to $1.2 billion.
- Borborema had CapEx below $200 million and NPV close to $1.7 billion; proven and probable reserves increased from below 800,000 oz at construction announcement to almost 2 million oz.
- Era Dorada has $380 million CapEx and $1.3 billion NPV at a $3,200 gold-price assumption.


### Guatemala and ESG

- Era Dorada is designed to use 100% clean energy, supplied by a geothermal project; approved water plans aim to deliver potable water to the local population.
- In 2025, 1,000 hours were spent engaging communities and assessing environmental concerns; the project shifted from open-pit to underground mining amid opposition to open-pit development.
- Water measures increase CapEx and OpEx but are intended to address community concerns and create local value; Era Dorada is expected to become an ESG benchmark.


### Near-term catalysts and funding

- MSG turnaround work is focused on mine development, identified as the main bottleneck; by year-end 2026, operations are expected to be positioned to deliver a strong 2027.
- Almas plant expansion is progressing from 2 million to 3 million tonnes by year-end 2026; part of the expansion is complete.
- Borborema has an estimated 34-year mine life based on reserves; expansion scenarios are under review, with an announcement expected by year-end 2026.
- Q2 cash was raised to $200 million to fund expansion; the expansion plan is fully funded, with market capitalization near $7 billion.
