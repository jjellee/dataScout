# Aura Minerals Inc. — Q2 2026 (2026-08-06)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q2 2026 saw weaker production compared to Q1, but groundwork was laid for stronger output in Q3 and Q4, maintaining full-year guidance.
- H1 2026 production totaled 158,000 ounces, with H2 guidance set at 182,000–232,000 ounces, targeting a significant ramp-up.
- Revenue for the quarter was $336 million, impacted by lower gold prices and production, while adjusted EBITDA reached $197 million.
- Net income hit $218 million, boosted by non-cash gains from gold derivatives; adjusted net income was $97 million.
- Announced a $60 million dividend ($0.72/share) and a $200 million share buyback program.


### Financial highlights

- Q2 net revenues: $336 million, down due to lower production and gold prices.
- Adjusted EBITDA: $197 million for Q2; $800 million over the last 12 months, marking 12 consecutive quarters of growth.
- Net income: $218 million, with $126 million from mark-to-market gains on gold derivatives.
- Recurring cash flow: $80 million; excluding hedge losses, would have been $120 million.
- Cash at quarter-end: ~$260 million; net debt: $168 million; leverage ratio stable at 0.2x.


### Outlook and guidance

- Full-year production guidance reaffirmed at 340,000–390,000 ounces, with stronger H2 expected.
- All-in sustaining cash cost (AISC) guidance maintained, with improvements expected in H2 as higher grades and production come online.
- MSG mine turnaround and Borborema debottlenecking to drive H2 and 2027 growth.
- Era Dorada construction progressing on schedule, with commercial production targeted for 2028.


### Segment performance

- MSG: Lower Q2 production due to turnaround and mine development; expected to ramp up in H2 and reach 80,000 ounces/year by 2027.
- Borborema: Q2 output 14,000 ounces; higher grades and new filters to boost H2 production.
- Almas: Capacity upgrade to 3 million tons/year underway, supporting gradual production increases.
- Minosa: Q2 production down to 14,000 ounces; expected to recover in Q4.
- Apoena: Q2 output 6,000 ounces; higher grades anticipated in H2.
- Aranzazu: Mine sequencing to deliver higher grades and stronger H2 performance.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- AISC for Q2 near $2,000/oz, inflated by MSG turnaround; excluding MSG, AISC would be ~$1,600/oz.
- Leverage ratio (net debt/EBITDA): 0.2x, stable quarter-over-quarter.
- Dividend yield remains among the highest in the sector.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Exposure to gold price volatility and macroeconomic factors, including FX and oil/chemical prices.
- Potential operational impacts from El Niño, particularly in Central America and Honduras, though not expected to materially affect overall results.
- Ongoing inflationary pressures, mitigated by internal cost initiatives and strategic sourcing.


### Significant events and developments

- $60 million dividend and $200 million share buyback program announced, with future returns to shareholders split between the two.
- Sale of São Francisco mine completed; Tolda Fria under review pending regulatory changes in Colombia.
- MSG reserves and resources significantly increased post-acquisition, with underground development performance up 80–90% year-over-year.
- Daily trading volume on Nasdaq increased from $1–2 million to ~$100 million.


### Capital allocation and financing

- $54 million allocated to expansion CapEx; $68 million returned to shareholders via dividends and buybacks in Q2.
- Strong operational cash flow funds both growth projects and shareholder returns, with leverage used selectively for greenfield projects.


### Market and industry conditions

- Gold price volatility and macroeconomic uncertainty persist, but fundamentals for gold remain strong due to U.S. deficit, geopolitical tensions, and central bank demand.
- Company remains constructive on gold price outlook, targeting $5,000–$6,000/oz in the medium term.
- Inflation and FX headwinds in Brazil and Mexico offset by internal cost controls.
