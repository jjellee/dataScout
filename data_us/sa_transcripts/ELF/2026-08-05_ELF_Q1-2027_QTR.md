# e.l.f. Beauty, Inc. — Q1 2027 (2026-08-05)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Achieved 36% net sales growth year-over-year in Q1, marking the 30th consecutive quarter of net sales growth, with all brands expected to grow for the remainder of the year.
- Raised fiscal 2027 outlook to 18%-20% net sales growth, up from 12%-14% previously, reflecting strong momentum across the portfolio.
- Rhode contributed approximately $160 million in Q1 net sales, with a record-breaking $27 million DTC sales day and strong international expansion plans.
- Continued diversification with non-e.l.f. sales now over 30%, skincare at nearly 25%, and digital penetration at 30%.


### Financial highlights

- Q1 net sales grew 36% year-over-year; U.S. net sales up 29%, international up 61%.
- Q1 gross margin reached 83%, up 1,400 basis points, including a $50 million IEEPA tariff refund; excluding this, gross margin up 350 basis points.
- Adjusted EBITDA was $168 million, up 93% year-over-year; excluding tariff refunds, up 36%.
- Adjusted net income was $105 million ($1.75/share), up from $51 million ($0.89/share) last year.
- Ended Q1 with $344 million in cash, up from $170 million a year ago.


### Outlook and guidance

- Fiscal 2027 net sales growth expected at 18%-20%, adjusted EBITDA at $401M-$407M, and adjusted EPS at $3.50-$3.55.
- Rhode expected to contribute 13 percentage points to net sales growth; organic growth outlook raised to 6%-7%.
- Gross margin for fiscal 2027 expected to be up 200 basis points year-over-year, with marketing and digital spend at the high end of 23%-25% of net sales.
- Full reinvestment of $50 million tariff refund into pricing actions and marketing.


### Segment performance

- e.l.f. Cosmetics holds #1 unit share and #2 dollar share; e.l.f. Skin now #11 in mass skincare.
- Naturium is the fastest-growing skincare brand among the top 50, with international expansion via Sephora.
- Rhode achieved #1 beauty brand ranking at Sephora and Mecca, with major European expansion planned.
- e.l.f. Hair launched exclusively with Target, with nearly half of purchasers new to the brand.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted EBITDA margin projected at approximately 21% for fiscal 2027.
- Net debt to adjusted EBITDA less than 1.5x.
- Marketing and digital spend to reach or exceed 25% of net sales in Q2-Q4.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Potential impact from changes in tariffs, inflation, and global supply chain dynamics.
- Reinvestment of one-time tariff refunds may not yield expected returns in future periods.


### Significant events and developments

- Received $50 million in IEEPA tariff refunds in Q1, to be fully reinvested.
- Major international expansions: e.l.f. into Boots UK, Dollar General, Sephora Brazil; Naturium into Sephora Canada and Mexico; Rhode into 19 European countries.
- Price discovery test led to permanent price reductions on 10% of SKUs to drive unit momentum.
- Repurchased $50 million of common stock in Q1.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Focused on reinvesting in business growth, technology (including AI), and working capital for global expansion.
- Cash to be used for Rhode earn-out payment later in the year.


### Market and industry conditions

- Value proposition remains critical amid consumer economic concerns; average e.l.f. price point is $7 vs. $10+ for mass and $30+ for prestige.
- Haircare category in the U.S. is $17 billion and growing faster than cosmetics and skincare.
- International beauty peers have over 70% of sales outside the U.S., indicating significant runway for global growth.
