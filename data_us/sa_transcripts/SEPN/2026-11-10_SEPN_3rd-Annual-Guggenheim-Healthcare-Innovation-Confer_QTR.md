# Septerna, Inc. — 3rd Annual Guggenheim Healthcare Innovation Conference (2026-11-10)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Pipeline and milestones

- Four highlighted programs: SEP-479 in Phase I, SEP-204 newly announced for Graves’ disease and thyroid eye disease, SEP-631 past Phase I, and an early discovery-stage osteoporosis program.
- Novo collaboration covers five targets and four concurrent programs; work is progressing.
- SEP-631 is a small-molecule MRGPRX2 negative allosteric modulator; mast cell indications are under evaluation.
- The osteoporosis program targets PTH receptor activation with a short-acting approach; development is supported by a revised endpoint pathway and unmet need for oral anabolic agents.


### SEP-479 for hypoparathyroidism

- The Phase I trial is ongoing in Australia; single- and multiple-ascending-dose portions assess safety, tolerability, PK, endogenous PTH and calcium.
- SAD data showed a 3–4-day half-life with limited variability across tested cohorts; MAD was extended to 14 days to approach steady state.
- Q1 2027 readout expected; healthy-volunteer biomarker goals are ~50% lower endogenous PTH and calcium increase up to ~0.5 mg/dL, subject to hypercalcemia risk limits.
- Phase II is expected to proceed directly in H2 2027, following Phase I completion in Q1; chronic animal safety studies are underway ahead of patient enrollment and a planned open-label extension.
- Phase II will assess two or three starting doses to guide titration and select a Phase III dose; an oral once-daily pill of reasonable size is anticipated.


### SEP-204 for Graves’ disease and thyroid eye disease

- Oral small-molecule TSHR negative allosteric modulator blocks receptor activation even with Graves’-derived autoantibodies; selectivity over related FSHR and LHR exceeds 1,000-fold.
- Rat studies showed lower doses normalized thyroid hormone levels and a higher dose suppressed them; mouse studies showed reversal of thyroid changes and improvement in proptosis.
- GLP toxicology studies in rats and dogs are complete; first-in-human entry is targeted for Q1 2027, with projected human half-life of 20–40 days.
- The oral approach could address patients with Graves’ disease who relapse or cannot tolerate antithyroid drugs, as well as thyroid eye disease across its severity spectrum.


### Development strategy and finances

- Program selection emphasizes biological validation and early clinical learning, pursuing oral small molecules for challenging GPCR activation and allosteric modulation.
- For hypoparathyroidism, an oral hormone-replacement option could reach moderate patients with symptoms, urinary calcium issues or high calcium-and-vitamin-D pill burden, beyond current injectable use in more severe patients.
- More than $565 million in cash was reported at Q3 end, sufficient to fund the full pipeline through at least mid-2029; runway excludes Novo milestones.
