# Septerna, Inc. — 12th Annual Cantor Fitzgerald Global Healthcare Conference (2026-09-10)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Pipeline overview and strategic focus

- Focused on GPCR drug discovery, enabling novel compounds for challenging targets, including small molecule agonists and allosteric modulators.
- Lead program SEP-479 is in phase I for hypoparathyroidism, with additional programs targeting mast cell diseases, thyroid disorders, and osteoporosis.
- Novo Nordisk partnership targets metabolic diseases, leveraging novel binding pockets in incretin receptors.
- Strong financial position with $516 million in cash, providing runway into 2029.


### SEP-479 (hypoparathyroidism) development

- SEP-479 designed to mimic PTH peptide, showing promising translation from animal models to healthy volunteers.
- Phase I study uses single and multiple ascending dose design, with a long half-life (~3-4 days) considered optimal.
- Plans to rapidly transition to global phase II, including open-label extension and chronic toxicology studies.
- Phase II will test starting doses and titration protocols, leveraging learnings from Yorvipath.
- Pivotal studies expected to be around 100 patients, with potential for combining phase II and III data.


### Commercial and competitive landscape for SEP-479

- Oral therapy aims to address unmet needs for patients not well-controlled on current standard of care or unwilling to use injectables.
- Switch studies planned to facilitate transition from injectable to oral therapy.
- Ex-U.S. commercialization strategy includes comprehensive dossiers to support pricing and access globally.
- Product expected to appeal to both severe and mild patients, potentially expanding the treatable population.


### Osteoporosis and thyroid programs

- Osteoporosis program in early discovery, aiming for a short-acting PTH agonist; regulatory changes now allow BMD as a surrogate endpoint.
- Oral anabolic agents seen as a major opportunity due to convenience and evolving treatment paradigms.
- Experience in PTH receptor biology expected to accelerate candidate selection.
- Thyroid-stimulating hormone receptor program progressing, with a focus on optimizing potency and pharmaceutical properties.


### MRGPRX2 and other pipeline updates

- MRGPRX2 program has a best-in-class profile, with robust phase I pharmacodynamics and safety.
- Shifted strategy to smaller, capital-efficient signal-finding studies in indications where mast cells are central.
- Biomarker development is key for new indications, with examples like interstitial cystitis and asthma under consideration.
- Novo Nordisk collaboration covers five metabolic targets, with milestone progress reflected in financials.
