# Zebra Technologies Corporation — 46th Annual William Blair Growth Stock Conference (2026-06-04)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Market positioning and growth strategy

- Maintains leadership in mission-critical workflows, supporting 80% of Fortune 500 companies and operating in 180 countries.
- Positioned at the center of durable growth trends like automation, digitization, and AI, targeting 5%-7% organic growth over the cycle.
- Operates in two segments: Connected Frontline Solutions and Asset Visibility & Automation, with significant revenue from both.
- Focuses on innovation in machine vision, RFID, and mobile computing, with recent acquisitions expanding capabilities.
- Sees strong opportunities in Asia Pacific and Latin America, and across diverse end-markets including retail, manufacturing, T&L, and healthcare.


### Technology and product innovation

- New mobile computing platform integrates embedded RFID and AI capabilities, enabling new use cases and productivity gains.
- Acquisitions of Photoneo (3D machine vision) and Elo Touch (touchscreen, self-service kiosks) enhance product suite and differentiation.
- Portfolio enables real-time visibility, data capture, and automation across the supply chain, supporting complex customer needs.
- Leverages a global partner network of 10,000+ to deliver endpoint solutions and expand reach.
- Unified software stack from Elo acquisition allows seamless integration of fixed and mobile screens for retail and QSR customers.


### Financial performance and capital allocation

- Capital-light business model supports strong margins, free cash flow, and disciplined capital allocation.
- Invests 9%-10% of sales in R&D, focusing on RFID and machine vision.
- Repurchased over $800 million in shares over the past three quarters, balancing investment and shareholder returns.
- Guidance for 2024 is 5% organic growth with incremental margin and solid free cash flow.
- Exited robotics business and mitigated tariff and FX headwinds, contributing to margin resilience.


### Supply chain and external factors

- Actively manages memory supply challenges, offsetting $120 million headwind through price increases and operational actions.
- No significant demand impact from price increases; competitors also raising prices.
- Shifted manufacturing footprint from China to Southeast Asia and Mexico, reducing tariff exposure and increasing supply chain resilience.
- Continues to monitor and adapt to tariff changes, leveraging a diverse supplier base.
- Confident in supply chain setup to support growth and profitability targets.


### Distribution and partner ecosystem

- Two-tiered distribution model with significant overlap between acquired and existing channels.
- 20,000 resellers and ISVs provide local expertise, configuration, and value-added services.
- Reseller network enables broad market reach and capital-light expansion.
- Strong community and integration with resellers, enhancing customer relationships and ecosystem value.
- Customers benefit from a wide partner network for bespoke applications and integration.


### Forward-looking statements and opportunities

- Double-digit growth in RFID and machine vision, with new use cases driving adoption.
- Large refresh opportunity in last mile delivery mobile computers expected over next 2-3 years.
- Elo acquisition expected to deliver $25 million in synergies by year three, with $10 million already identified.
- Ongoing focus on innovation, operational efficiency, and capital allocation to sustain leadership and growth.
- Confident in achieving guidance and leveraging AI leadership for frontline productivity.
