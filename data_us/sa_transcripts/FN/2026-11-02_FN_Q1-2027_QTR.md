# Fabrinet — Q1 2027 (2026-11-02)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q1 FY27 revenue reached $1.474B, up 51% YoY and 12% QoQ; record non-GAAP EPS of $4.34 exceeded guidance. Growth spanned all three revenue categories and multiple customers.
- Q1 marked the 14th consecutive quarter of revenue growth and seventh consecutive quarter of accelerating YoY growth; management expects momentum to continue into Q2.


### Financial highlights

- GAAP net income was $103M, or $2.86 per diluted share; non-GAAP net income was $157M, or $4.34 per diluted share.
- Operating expenses were 1.3% of revenue; non-GAAP operating margin was 10.8%, up 20 bps YoY and down 10 bps QoQ.
- Operating cash flow was $21M; CapEx was $159M and free cash flow was an outflow of $137M.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q2 revenue guidance is $1.60B-$1.65B, representing 43% YoY growth at the midpoint; non-GAAP EPS guidance is $4.77-$4.92.
- Q2 growth is expected across data center, communications infrastructure, and automotive, industrial and other; efficiencies and leverage are expected to improve margins.
- Management sees sustainable broad-based demand and expects operating margin to expand through FY27 and beyond; new programs are expected to contribute increasingly during the year.
- NPO revenue is expected in the latter half of FY27; CPO is further out. OCS and NPO each could represent multi-hundred-million-dollar opportunities, though shipments are just starting.


### Segment performance

- Data center revenue was $777M, up 78% YoY and 16% QoQ, or 53% of total revenue; growth came from transceivers, DCI and high-performance computing.
- Communications infrastructure revenue was $444M, up 35% YoY and 8% QoQ, with growth across telecom systems, components and satellite communications.
- Automotive, industrial and other revenue was $253M, up 20% YoY and 8% QoQ; laser demand led sequential growth in the category.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Non-GAAP gross margin was 12.1%, down 10 bps QoQ due primarily to annual merit increases and 20 bps YoY, primarily due to FX headwinds.
- Non-GAAP effective tax rate was 4%, excluding a $20M non-cash Pillar Two top-up tax provision.
- Fixed costs were about 5% of cost; management cited ROIC above 30% and expects operating margin to expand with growth.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Components remain in tight supply across the industry; management said it is securing supply to meet customer demand and avoid capacity constraints.
- FX remains the largest variable in the business model; hedges point to flattening rates and no further FX headwinds into H2 FY27.
- Thailand tax credits intended to offset Pillar Two effects are progressing but not implemented, creating possible variability in reported tax expense.
- Management noted readiness for potential downturns, citing an asset-light model and low fixed costs.


### Significant events and developments

- Santa Clara site purchase more than doubled Silicon Valley capacity; customer discussions are underway. Building 10 in Chonburi is nearing completion, with initial revenue already generated.
- Building 11 construction is planned after Building 10 completion; it is expected to add 1.5M sq. ft. and about $3B of revenue capacity, coming online at the end of FY28.
- Capacity is expected to reach about $10B in January; Building 10 adds about $3.5B, while Nava One adds about $400M and Santa Clara about $300M.
- Estimated potential capacity across current and planned sites was revised from about $14B to about $16B, reflecting higher revenue density.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Cash and short-term investments ended Q1 at $757M, down $119M QoQ; share repurchases totaled $29M for about 77,000 shares at an average of about $380.
- The board added $103M to repurchase authorization, bringing remaining authorization to $200M; buybacks will use both the Rule 10b5-1 plan and opportunistic purchases.
- Growth investment remains the top capital priority; Q1 CapEx included $77M for the Santa Clara campus, while ordinary CapEx is running at about 5% of revenue, excluding large capacity additions.
- Thailand borrowing was described as cheaper than liquidating U.S.-invested cash; further CapEx increases are expected as Building 11 and additional capacity projects proceed.


### Notes to the financial statements

- GAAP net income included a $20M non-cash Pillar Two top-up tax provision and a $21M non-cash unrealized loss on the Raytek minority investment; both were excluded from non-GAAP results.


### Market and industry conditions

- Demand was broad-based across customer accounts and served industries; management attributed Q1 growth to both established programs and new ramps, rather than a single customer.
- Management expects pluggable transceivers to persist alongside NPO and CPO; it views the technologies as complementary, with NPO opportunities nearer term.
- Electronics-photonics convergence and demand for optical circuit switching, NPO and CPO are creating opportunities; manufacturing expertise helps customers improve time to market, cost and yield.
