# Schrödinger, Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-11-04)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 momentum reflected broad ACV growth, hosted software adoption, and therapeutics milestones; raised FY26 ACV and drug discovery revenue guidance.
- Bunsen and predictive toxicology are expanding platform capabilities, while materials science and partnered therapeutics support longer-term growth.


### Financial highlights

- Q3 revenue was $64.5M, up 19%; software revenue was $38.2M and drug discovery revenue was $24.2M versus $13.0M in Q3 2025.
- Operating expenses were $64M, down 13% YoY from $74M, mainly on lower clinical CRO and headcount costs; net loss was $38M versus $33M.
- Other expense was $11.4M, primarily from mark-to-market changes in public equity positions; quarter-end cash and marketable securities were $373M.


### Outlook and guidance

- FY26 ACV guidance raised to $222M-$232M, implying 12%-17% growth over 2025; drug discovery revenue guidance raised to $75M-$85M from $65M-$75M.
- FY26 operating expenses expected below 2025; 2027 planning is early, with investment focused on platform capabilities and new products to support top-line growth.
- Three-year drug discovery revenue view is $150M, including a baseline near $50M annually; milestones and business development can add revenue, but milestone timing is difficult to predict.


### Segment performance

- ACV was $44.7M, up 17% YoY and 24% excluding contribution ACV; trailing four-quarter ACV was $214M. Growth spanned top 20 pharma, biotech, and materials science customers.
- Software revenue was $38.2M; hosted revenue was $17.2M, up 49%, and 45% of software revenue versus 32% in Q3 2025.
- Software gross margin was 75% versus 77% in Q3 2025; the hosted-license transition temporarily moderates reported software revenue through ratable recognition.
- Drug discovery revenue rose to $24.2M from $13.0M, primarily on the Structure oral amylin milestone; predictive toxicology evaluations contributed to ACV.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Fully diluted share count was 74.9M; hosted revenue was 32% of software revenue on a trailing four-quarter basis, up from 23% in Q3 2025.
- Each 1% increase in hosted revenue is associated with an estimated $2M-$3M reduction in reported revenue, depending on contract length and renewal timing.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Forward-looking plans and expectations depend on current information and assumptions; actual results may differ materially due to SEC filing risk factors.
- Milestone timing is difficult to forecast, and hosted-license transitions can temporarily moderate reported software revenue.


### Significant events and developments

- Structure Therapeutics milestone followed initial dosing in the Phase I/II-A multiple ascending dose study of oral amylin candidate ACCG-2671.
- Takeda's zasocitinib NDA was accepted for FDA Priority Review; approval was expected early 2027.
- Zilemaltib completed Phase I dose escalation; an ASH oral presentation will cover observed safety and clinical activity in relapsed/refractory B-cell malignancies.
- Bunsen remained in early access, with open beta expected in 2026; internally it handled ~80% of high-throughput calculations. Predictive toxicology covered more than 80 off-target proteins.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Tectora launched with $55M funded by NEA and RA Capital; contributed programs SDGR-4594 and SDGR-8139 in exchange for equity and eligibility for milestones and royalties.
- Quarter-end cash and marketable securities totaled $373M; management cited disciplined expenses and plans to selectively invest in platform growth.


### Market and industry conditions

- Biotech funding conditions improved versus 2025, supporting renewed research investment and contributing to results; top pharma customers also showed interest in broader deployments.
- Materials science adoption of computational methods is earlier than in pharma but increasing across chemicals, energy storage, semiconductors, and electronics; management sees substantial long-term market potential.
- Management positioned physics-based simulations as reliable ground-truth data for predictive AI; Bunsen is intended to improve workflow efficiency and broaden platform usage.
