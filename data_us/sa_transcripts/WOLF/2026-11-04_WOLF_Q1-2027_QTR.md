# Wolfspeed, Inc. — Q1 2027 (2026-11-04)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q1 FY27 revenue was $155M, above the midpoint of guidance; operational improvements lifted adjusted non-GAAP gross margin sequentially.
- Strategy centers on SiC and GaN growth, broader customer and end-market reach, operational execution, and financial discipline.


### Financial highlights

- Revenue was $155M; Materials contributed ~$38M and Power ~$117M, with Power up 10% QoQ, largely on I&E and data center growth.
- Adjusted non-GAAP gross margin was -12.8%, improving 710 bps QoQ on higher revenue, utilization gains, and a more favorable Power mix.
- Non-GAAP operating expenses were $64M versus $62M in the prior quarter; adjusted non-GAAP EBITDA was -$55M, improving $8M, or 12%, QoQ.
- Operating cash flow was -$84M, including $32M of interest paid; gross capex was ~$11M. Working capital contributed ~$60M to cash in Q1.


### Outlook and guidance

- Q2 FY27 revenue guidance is $155M-$175M; non-GAAP gross margin is expected to improve sequentially and approach break-even.
- Q2 non-GAAP operating expenses are expected at $65M-$69M, a modest sequential increase primarily for anticipated AI data center growth.
- FY27 is expected to mark a turning point toward more meaningful longer-term revenue and profitability growth.
- Gross-margin break-even is now estimated below $200M quarterly revenue; Mohawk Valley wall-to-wall utilization of 25%-30% was cited as sufficient.


### Segment performance

- Power revenue was ~$117M, up 10% QoQ, led largely by I&E, including data centers; Materials revenue was ~$38M.
- AI data center revenue more than doubled YoY on a trailing 12-month basis; order book is accelerating and the market is viewed as the fastest-growing end market.
- Automotive signed a majority-share, long-term supply agreement with BorgWarner for its next-generation 800-volt SiC inverter platform.
- Infineon supply agreement was expanded to include 200-mm wafers alongside 150-mm wafers; proprietary transparent SiC technology was commercially introduced.
- GaN opportunities target data center voltage step-down and humanoid robotics, using existing IP and fungible manufacturing tools, with some dedicated tooling needed.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Adjusted non-GAAP gross margin was -12.8%, up 710 bps QoQ; underutilization remains the primary constraint on margin.
- Adjusted non-GAAP EBITDA was -$55M, a 12% sequential improvement; operating cash flow was -$84M.
- Working capital improvements contributed ~$235M to cash over the past five quarters; optimization initiatives were largely completed.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Underutilization remains the main driver of gross-margin performance, making factory utilization a key near-term lever for margin expansion.
- The $1.5B government loan commitment is conditional on completing due diligence and the credit agreement, plus financial commitments, a limited equity raise, and other balance-sheet de-risking measures.


### Significant events and developments

- Q1 included completion of a previously planned workforce reduction of approximately 6%, further streamlining operations.
- Megmeet presented its Strategic Partner Award, as engagement with hyperscalers and technical, commercial, and supply support for customer programs expands.
- Optical-grade transparent SiC is being developed for potential AR, quantum computing, and other emerging applications.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Conditional 30-year, $1.5B U.S. Department of War loan commitment includes an initial $600M tranche to refinance remaining first-lien notes due 2030.
- Refinancing is expected to save more than $50M in annual interest; with the first five years' interest paid in kind, annual cash interest savings are expected to be ~$100M.
- Additional tranches would fund project-based investments in SiC scale-up, domestic GaN power devices, GaN-on-SiC RF materials, and radiation-hardening capabilities, leveraging existing assets rather than building new sites.
- Due diligence and credit agreement work were expected to take another couple of weeks; management aimed to complete the process before year-end.


### Market and industry conditions

- AI data center build-out, including deployment of 800-volt systems and disaggregated compute and power racks, is increasing SiC demand and power content.
- Management sees AI data centers and automotive demand helping absorb capacity built in prior years and improve SiC supply-demand balance.
- RF demand tied to satellite communications and related applications supports the materials opportunity; U.S. capabilities were cited as a competitive advantage.
- SiC serves high-voltage, high-power applications, while GaN extends the portfolio into lower-voltage designs, including data center power and humanoid robotics.
