# FIGS, Inc. — Q2 2026 (2026-08-06)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Net revenues grew 29% year-over-year to $196.6 million, marking the third consecutive quarter of 25%+ growth and surpassing outlook expectations.
- Growth was broad-based across channels, product categories, geographies, and customer cohorts, with record highs in scrub wear sales and international expansion.
- Active customers increased 13% to 3.1 million, with average order value (AOV) reaching a record $127 and net revenues per active customer hitting $229.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin surged to 18.6%, driven by margin expansion from full price selling, improved returns, and expense leverage.
- A new $100 million share buyback authorization was announced, reflecting confidence in the brand and future growth.


### Financial highlights

- Q2 net revenues: $196.6 million, up 29% year-over-year.
- U.S. net revenues rose 22% to $158.7 million; international net revenues soared 67% to $37.9 million.
- Scrub wear grew 27% (82% of net revenues); non-scrub wear surged 40% (18% of net revenues).
- Gross margin improved 820 bps to 75.2%, including a 780 bps benefit from a $15.4 million tariff refund.
- Net income was $28.4 million (EPS $0.15), up from $7.1 million (EPS $0.04) last year.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin reached 18.6%, up from 12.9% year-over-year.


### Outlook and guidance

- Full-year 2026 net revenues expected to grow ~20%, up from prior 14%-16% guidance.
- Q3 net revenue growth projected at ~20% year-over-year; Q4 at ~10% year-over-year.
- Full-year GAAP gross margin expected at 69.5%, including tariff refund benefits.
- Full-year operating margin outlook raised to ~10.8%; adjusted EBITDA margin outlook increased to 14.8%-15%.


### Segment performance

- International delivered 67% growth, with strong contributions from Europe, Latin America, Mexico, Canada, Australia, and the Middle East.
- Community hubs (retail stores) and teams (institutional sales) both achieved record net revenues, with new store openings planned.
- Non-scrub wear now represents nearly 20% of business, with strong growth in under scrubs, outerwear, and accessories.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Active customers: 3.1 million, up 13% year-over-year.
- Average order value: $127, up 9% year-over-year.
- Net revenues per active customer: $229, a record high.
- Inventory decreased 12% year-over-year to $119.6 million.
- Net cash, equivalents, and short-term investments: $296.3 million.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- U.S. Customs Withhold Release Order prevents imports from a key Jordanian supplier, but supply chain flexibility and mitigation efforts are in place.
- Use of air freight to expedite production may offset some tariff refund benefits.
- Non-scrub wear carries lower margins, potentially impacting long-term gross margin expansion.


### Significant events and developments

- $15.4 million tariff refund recognized, reducing cost of goods sold and boosting gross margin.
- Acquisition of V Coterie, expanding into healthcare-themed jewelry and accessories.
- Share repurchases totaled $24 million in Q2; cumulative buybacks now $81 million, with $119 million total capacity after new authorization.
- Four new retail leases signed for openings later in the year.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Continued share repurchases and new $100 million buyback authorization.
- Capital expenditures of $2.6 million in Q2, mainly for software and leasehold improvements.


### Market and industry conditions

- Healthcare and social assistance projected as the fastest-growing industry over the next decade, with over 50,000 new jobs monthly in 2026.
- Macro tailwinds and non-discretionary, replenishment-driven demand for healthcare uniforms support sustained growth.
