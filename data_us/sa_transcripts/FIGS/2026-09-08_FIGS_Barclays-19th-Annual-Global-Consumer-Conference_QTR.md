# FIGS, Inc. — Barclays 19th Annual Global Consumer Conference (2026-09-08)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Leadership and brand evolution

- FIGS has evolved from a disruptor to a globally recognized brand, driven by product innovation and deep community engagement.
- The CFO, with a background at Lululemon, has focused on financial discipline, profitability, and growth initiatives including international expansion and category diversification.


### Consumer and market dynamics

- Healthcare professionals, the core customer base, represent a stable, fast-growing, and non-discretionary market segment with strong employment and wage stability.
- The company has experienced accelerating growth, with +25% top-line growth over the last three quarters, driven by both new and returning customers.


### Product innovation and community moat

- Product innovation, such as proprietary fabrics and technical features, is a key differentiator, with ongoing expansion into layering systems, accessories, and collaborations.
- A strong, loyal community is fostered through advocacy, ambassador programs, and direct engagement, making the brand difficult to replicate.


### Marketing, customer acquisition, and retention

- Active customer base reached 3.1 million, growing 13% in the quarter, with a significant runway given the global healthcare workforce.
- Marketing has evolved to a full-funnel approach, driving both awareness and efficient acquisition, with notable improvements in brand metrics and customer retention.


### Competitive landscape and growth opportunities

- The competitive moat has widened as legacy and copycat competitors struggle to match scale, innovation, and community engagement.
- Repeat purchases are driven by product quality and necessity, with average revenue per customer at a record $229 and strong non-scrubwear growth.


### Product pipeline and TAM expansion

- The company is focused on category creation, with innovations like the scrub jogger and new fabrications, and is expanding into accessories and lifestyle products.
- The acquisition of V Coterie and growth in non-scrubwear categories are expanding the total addressable market.


### Financial performance and margin outlook

- Gross margin is guided at 69.5% for the year due to tariff refunds, with long-term sustainability expected around 67%-68%.
- Margin expansion is expected to come from operating leverage rather than further gross margin gains, with ongoing cost management and scale benefits.


### International and channel expansion

- International business now represents 20% of revenue, with 67% growth in core markets and ongoing localization and ambassador programs.
- Community Hubs serve as acquisition, awareness, and profitability drivers, with strong financial performance and plans for further expansion.


### Long-term vision

- The brand aims to build an iconic, enduring presence by balancing growth and profitability, maintaining authentic connections, and continually innovating for healthcare professionals.
