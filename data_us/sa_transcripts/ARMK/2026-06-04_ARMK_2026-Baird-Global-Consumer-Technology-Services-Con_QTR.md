# Aramark — 2026 Baird Global Consumer, Technology & Services Conference (2026-06-04)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Business overview and new initiatives

- Operates as a global food and facilities outsourcer, serving diverse sectors like universities, hospitals, and sports teams, with a strong focus on client-centric solutions.
- Launched Nexus, a new business line targeting AI data center construction, leveraging expertise in remote and complex environments.
- Secured two major data center contracts, each potentially generating $100 million in annual revenue, with hundreds more sites anticipated over the next several years.
- Nexus business is expected to benefit from synergies with existing hospitality-focused GPO operations.


### Supply chain and technology advancements

- Supply chain is central to operations and margin improvement, focusing on both economic optimization and product variety for clients.
- AI-driven technology, including the Mosaic harmonization engine and chatbots, enhances spend visibility, compliance, and operational efficiency.
- $20 billion+ in contracted spend is managed, with high compliance and optimization for both internal and GPO clients.
- International GPO expansion under the Avendra International brand has driven double-digit growth, supported by both organic efforts and M&A.
- Strong relationships with major food distributors globally ensure redundancy, risk management, and economic benefits.


### Margin, labor, and operational excellence

- Achieved average annual margin expansion of 40 basis points over the past three years, with supply chain and GPO growth as key contributors.
- Labor optimization is supported by AI tools like Labor IQ, enabling real-time, demand-driven staffing adjustments across business lines.
- KPIs such as sales-to-labor ratios, overtime, and agency labor are closely monitored to balance service quality and cost efficiency.


### Sales, retention, and growth outlook

- Customer retention is above 98%, exceeding the typical 96% benchmark, with a net new business target of 4%-5%.
- Recent wins include some of the largest contracts in company history, attributed to a strengthened sales force and long-cycle sales efforts.
- Pricing is aligned with inflation at around 3%, and capital investments are integrated into long-term client contracts.
- Volume growth is robust in sports, entertainment, and B&I sectors, with double-digit growth in B&I and increased non-sports events at venues.
- Participation in major events like FIFA and a strong summer concert lineup are expected to further boost volumes.


### Capital allocation and M&A strategy

- Free cash flow is about 40% of AOI, with leverage expected to fall below 3x by end of 2026, opening the door for more meaningful share buybacks.
- M&A remains a priority across U.S., international, and GPO segments, focusing on bolt-ons, capability enhancements, and scale in hospitality and senior living.
