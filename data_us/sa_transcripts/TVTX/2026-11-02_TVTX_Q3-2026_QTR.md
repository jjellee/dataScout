# Travere Therapeutics, Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-11-02)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 execution drove record quarterly FILSPARI net product sales above $200 million, supported by demand across IgAN and FSGS, patient conversion and persistence.
- Management reiterated confidence in achieving or exceeding $3 billion in annual FILSPARI sales by 2033, with pipeline programs adding longer-term growth opportunities.


### Financial highlights

- Total U.S. net product sales were $220.2 million; FILSPARI contributed $200.2 million and THIOLA/THIOLA EC contributed $20 million.
- Licensing collaboration revenue was $4.4 million, bringing total Q3 revenue to $224.6 million.
- GAAP R&D and SG&A expenses totaled $168.7 million, including approximately $23.8 million in non-cash stock-based compensation and depreciation.
- Q3 included $113.1 million of IPR&D expense, primarily tied to the $112.5 million upfront payment for the Everest licensing agreement.


### Outlook and guidance

- FILSPARI remains on track to achieve or exceed $3 billion in annual sales by 2033; management expects strong demand while acknowledging quarter-to-quarter variability.
- Q4 FILSPARI sales are expected to be reduced by approximately $8 million as specialty pharmacy partners draw down elevated inventory; full-year gross-to-net discounts are expected in the mid-20% range.
- Pegtibatinase HARMONY phase III enrollment progressed, with top-line results expected in H2 2027; civorebrutinib U.S. IND submission is anticipated by year-end.
- Management expects continued demand in both indications, while declining to provide patient-start-form projections or magnitude of Q4 growth.


### Segment performance

- FILSPARI generated $200.2 million in U.S. Q3 net sales, up approximately 120% YoY; 1,932 new patient start forms were received across IgAN and FSGS.
- IgAN demand remained high but patient start forms decreased slightly QoQ, consistent with summer seasonality and Q2 pent-up demand; FSGS start forms grew slightly QoQ.
- FSGS launch adoption and conversion progressed rapidly, with time to paid conversion approaching the IgAN level; most payers had updated coverage policies for FSGS.
- Approximately 40% of first-time FSGS prescribers in Q3 were new to FILSPARI; pediatric patients represented up to 10% of FSGS commercial use, with access similar to adults.
- THIOLA and THIOLA EC contributed $20 million in U.S. net product sales in Q3.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- FILSPARI U.S. net product sales grew approximately 120% YoY to $200.2 million in Q3.
- Cash equivalents and marketable securities totaled approximately $471.8 million as of September 30, 2026.
- FILSPARI had a one-time gross-to-net benefit of less than $2 million in Q3; full-year gross-to-net discounts are expected in the mid-20% range.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- IgAN market activity and constrained nephrologist time may create variability in patient starts; summer seasonality also affected Q3 demand patterns.
- Elevated specialty pharmacy inventory added approximately $8 million to Q3 FILSPARI sales, with a corresponding expected reduction in Q4 sales as stock normalizes.
- Pegtibatinase, civorebrutinib and sibeprenlimab remain dependent on ongoing clinical, regulatory and development progress.


### Significant events and developments

- CEO Eric Dube plans to step down in December; Bradley is expected to join on December 1.
- An IgAN methods-of-use patent was issued and a FSGS methods-of-use patent application received a notice of allowance; both terms extend into 2037 upon issuance for FSGS.
- Everest licensing and collaboration agreement became effective during Q3, adding civorebrutinib to the pipeline for immune-mediated kidney diseases.
- FILSPARI SPARX enrollment is complete, with data anticipated in 2027; SPARLIGHT phase IV initiation is targeted for later in 2026.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Cash and marketable securities of approximately $471.8 million, alongside growing revenue, were cited as sufficient to support FILSPARI growth and pipeline advancement with current resources.
- Management plans disciplined investment, prioritizing programs with patient impact and long-term value potential.


### Notes to the financial statements

- Q3 IPR&D expense of $113.1 million related primarily to the $112.5 million upfront cost associated with closing the Everest licensing agreement.
- Royalty expense was approximately $8 million, including THIOLA royalties and amortization of capitalized FILSPARI contractual milestones and royalties over the intangible assets’ accounting life.


### Market and industry conditions

- FILSPARI remains positioned as a kidney-targeted treatment in evolving IgAN care, with physicians considering sequencing and combination regimens alongside immune-directed therapies.
- The HHS Kidney Care National Plan emphasizes earlier detection and intervention for rare kidney disease, potentially supporting earlier diagnosis and treatment consideration.
- Management cited more than 100,000 addressable IgAN and FSGS patients; civorebrutinib’s potential spans immune-mediated kidney diseases representing more than 130,000 prevalent patients.
- New SPARTAN data showed 75% of newly diagnosed patients achieved complete remission at least once over two years, with stable kidney function without immunosuppression.
