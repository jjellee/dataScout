# Travere Therapeutics, Inc. — Stifel 2026 Virtual Immunology and Inflammation Forum (2026-09-24)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Leadership transition

- Eric Dube plans to depart after nearly eight years; Bradley Campbell is scheduled to become CEO on Dec. 1, with experience in rare disease, HCU, FSGS and enzyme replacement therapy.
- Dube cited health issues after prioritizing delivery of the first medicine for the FSGS community; he said the business is in a strong position and its best years lie ahead.


### FILSPARI commercial outlook

- FILSPARI generated 2,012 patient start forms in the prior quarter across IgAN and FSGS; management expects strong, sustainable demand in both indications, while noting one quarter is insufficient to establish trends.
- FSGS uptake may reach peak sooner than IgAN, reflecting a smaller, more severe population and a launch without IgAN’s rolling approval and REMS milestones; summer seasonality may affect near-term trends.
- Physician uptake was broad: 30% of FSGS prescribers had not previously prescribed for IgAN, while 70% of FILSPARI-prescribing physicians already treated IgAN patients; use spans FSGS subtypes excluding active nephrotic syndrome.
- Gross-to-net is expected in the mid-20% range for the balance of the year; 3Q and 4Q are expected slightly above 2Q, partly due to greater CMS exposure in FSGS than IgAN.
- Management sees approved therapies and combination use expanding the IgAN market in line with KDIGO guidance; earlier FILSPARI initiation is contributing to growth, with early signs of combination use and limited payer barriers.


### FILSPARI intellectual property

- IgAN method-of-use patent is listed in the Orange Book with an October 2037 date; a similar FSGS notice of allowance is expected to support Orange Book listing through October 2037.
- Management also expects a patent term extension and is pursuing additional applications, describing protection as a portfolio approach supporting continued investment and evidence generation.


### Civorebrutinib development

- Civorebrutinib is an oral, selective, reversible BTK inhibitor; primary membranous nephropathy and immune subsets within FSGS are key potential indications, with longer-term safety and tolerability differentiation under evaluation.
- In pMN, management cited rapid, profound reductions in PLA2R followed by proteinuria; it is assessing whether these biomarker changes can support faster development, subject to evidence and stakeholder alignment.
- Management plans to monitor PARASOL and meet with FDA on pivotal-trial expectations; it did not commit to a shorter-than-two-year study or to sequential versus parallel development across indications.


### Pegtibatinase and HCU

- Phase II showed a 67% homocysteine reduction, with every treated patient below guideline target levels; phase III is ongoing, with topline data expected in H2 2027.
- Management is confident FDA will accept total homocysteine as a biomarker endpoint, citing precedent, alignment and breakthrough therapy designation; a diet sub-study will assess protein reintroduction but is not required for approval.
- Estimated U.S. addressable population is conservatively 3,500 diagnosed, uncontrolled patients under specialist care; screening and identification work may expand the population after therapy approval.
- Pricing will be evaluated against the broad enzyme-replacement therapy range, with phase III data and potential clinical benefit informing value assessment.


### Spending and business development

- Expenses are expected to rise moderately through 2026 and into 2027; SG&A is not expected to change materially, while R&D investment is set to increase.
- R&D drivers include ongoing FILSPARI evidence generation and transplant studies, pegtibatinase phase III and commercial-scale manufacturing, plus potential late-stage civorebrutinib studies.
- Additional spend is anticipated in 2027; business development will remain disciplined, targeting assets in rare kidney disease and rare metabolic disorders without an urgent need to fill the pipeline.
