# IonQ, Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-11-05)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Record Q3 revenue of $173.8M, up 336% YoY; sixth consecutive quarterly revenue record, with growth across the platform and foundry businesses.
- Strategy shifts from R&D toward productization and commercial scale, supported by vertically integrated manufacturing and broader customer adoption.
- More than 40% of Q3 Platform segment revenue came from customers purchasing multiple products, supporting cross-platform expansion opportunities.


### Financial highlights

- Q3 revenue was $173.8M, up 336% YoY; results exceeded internal expectations.
- Net income attributable to IonQ was $564M, including an $896M non-cash gain from warrant mark-to-market valuation; the gain did not reflect operating performance.
- GAAP operating expenses were $606.9M; non-GAAP operating expenses were $306.6M.
- Adjusted EBITDA was -$133.6M, reflecting integration investment concentrated in the quarter.


### Outlook and guidance

- FY2026 revenue guidance raised to $505M–$515M from $450M–$460M; management expects to be the first pure-play quantum business to exceed $500M in GAAP revenue.
- FY2026 quantum computing revenue growth forecast increased to more than 110%, from the prior outlook of 100%.
- Q4 Adjusted EBITDA is expected to improve from Q3; management said the Capital IQ consensus estimate of -$127M was not unreasonable.
- Superion 256 capacity for 2027 is sold out; management said order books are beginning to fill into 2028 and plans to expand system manufacturing capacity.
- Longer-term focus is profitable growth, with structural profitability expected as scale develops; 2027 priorities include cost discipline, integration, pricing, and site strategy.


### Segment performance

- Platform segment revenue was $90.3M, up 127% YoY, led by quantum computing deployments and growth in sensing and space-based products.
- SkyWater Foundry Services revenue was $83.5M for two months after the acquisition closed; results exceeded expectations, excluding intercompany revenue.
- SkyWater quantum foundry revenue grew more than 30% YoY, excluding IonQ revenue; D-Wave agreement extended for multiple years and a multi-year agreement was signed with Qolab.
- Platform Q3 revenue mix: commercial customers ~65%, international customers ~35%, and multi-product customers 40%.
- SkyWater’s CMOS foundry business had a multi-year take-or-pay supply agreement with Infineon; advanced imaging activity accelerated to address defense demand.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Remaining performance obligations totaled $1.2B, including $689M for the Platform segment and $479M from SkyWater.
- Platform RPO rose from $485M to $689M, supported by approximately $290M of new Q3 contracts; approximately $180M came from quantum computing sales, particularly Superion 256.
- Q3 included approximately $40M of one-time G&A costs related to the SkyWater acquisition; these were excluded from Adjusted EBITDA.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Forward-looking statements, including 2026 guidance, are based on current expectations and are not guarantees; actual results may differ materially due to risks and uncertainties.
- Superion 256 demand exceeds 2027 capacity; scaling production of complete systems, deployment, and commissioning remains important to meeting demand.
- Acquisition integration requires continued investment in systems, processes, and teams; management identified potential efficiencies but said integration work remains ongoing.


### Significant events and developments

- Advanced to Stage C of DARPA’s Quantum Benchmarking Initiative, the only trapped-ion participant at that stage; program value is up to $300M.
- Superion 256 partners include NVIDIA, Florida International University, University of Cambridge, Horizon Quantum, and SDT; the NVIDIA center deployment will connect the system with AI supercomputing infrastructure.
- Closed the SkyWater acquisition and launched SkyWater Quantum Solutions; wafer shipments began less than a month after the foundry offering launched.
- Closed the Elisity acquisition to expand quantum cybersecurity capabilities; Elisity brings 100 enterprise deployments and identity-based microsegmentation technology.
- Won a $28M DARPA atomic-clock contract, with an option for 100 additional clocks; also secured contracts with NIST and the Naval Research Laboratory.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Raised $2B in October at a premium to the share price, bringing pro forma cash to $3.7B; proceeds are intended for long-term investment rather than a specific near-term system need.
- Investment priorities include R&D, talent, manufacturing capacity, and commercial infrastructure; year-to-date R&D investment exceeded $425M, including $96.8M non-GAAP in Q3.
- Management plans disciplined capital allocation and operating reviews, while aiming to preserve the growth trajectory and strengthen operating leverage.


### Notes to the financial statements

- The $896M warrant mark-to-market gain was non-cash and drove reported net income above operating performance.
- SkyWater acquisition accounting included purchase price accounting and fair-value adjustments to acquired assets; Q3 consolidation covered two months of SkyWater results.


### Market and industry conditions

- Management cited growing global demand across quantum computing, sensing, and security, with customer interest in hybrid QPU-GPU systems and sovereign access.
- Quantum security discussions are increasingly reaching CEO level; Elisity’s software is described as recurring revenue and high margin, with a potential addressable market that could exceed quantum computing over time.
- Vulnerability of GPS-based positioning, navigation, and timing systems is increasing demand for resilient sensing, particularly among defense customers.
