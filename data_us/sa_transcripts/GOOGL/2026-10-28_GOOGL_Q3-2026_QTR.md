# Alphabet Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-10-28)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 revenue grew 25% YoY to $127.6B; annual revenue run rate reached $500B, with strong Search momentum and Google Cloud growth.
- AI models, infrastructure and products drove usage and demand; first-party model APIs processed nearly 32B tokens per minute, up 40% QoQ and more than 4x YoY.


### Financial highlights

- Operating income rose 30% to $40.6B; operating margin was 31.8%.
- Net income and EPS decreased, primarily due to negative $8.2B of other income and expenses, including an unrealized loss on SpaceX investment.
- Operating cash flow was $42.3B; free cash flow was negative $9.1B, reflecting $51.4B CapEx, TPU inventory build and a $5.2B EC fine payment.
- Cost of revenue increased 24% to $51.4B and operating expenses rose 20% to $35.6B; R&D increased 36%, driven by AI talent compensation and depreciation.


### Outlook and guidance

- FY 2026 CapEx guidance remains $195B-$205B; CapEx is expected to increase significantly in 2027.
- Q4 consolidated revenue faces a slightly larger FX headwind than Q3, primarily in Search and YouTube ads; Search growth also laps late-2025 acceleration.
- TPU system sales are expected to scale in Q4, with a meaningful revenue ramp in 2027.
- Cloud margins face near-term headwinds as third-party compute capacity usage ramps in Q4; higher infrastructure depreciation and data-center energy costs will weigh on expenses.
- Management expects ROIC to remain meaningfully above cost of capital and strong operating cash flow, while infrastructure investment and TPU inventory will pressure FCF.


### Segment performance

- Google Services revenue rose 11% to $96.3B; operating income grew 17% to $39.2B and margin was 40.7%.
- Search and other ad revenue rose 12% to $63.3B; YouTube ad revenue increased 9% to $11.2B, and Network ad revenue grew 1% to $7.5B.
- Subscription platforms and devices revenue rose 11% to $14.3B, led by YouTube subscriptions and Google One AI plans.
- Google Cloud revenue more than doubled, up 103% to $30.7B; operating income more than tripled to $11.1B and margin expanded to 36.2% from 23.7%.
- Other Bets revenue was $372M with a $2B operating loss; corporate-level operating loss was $7.7B, driven by shared AI R&D.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Consolidated operating margin was 31.8%; Google Services margin was 40.7% and Google Cloud margin was 36.2%.
- Cash and marketable securities totaled $265.1B, including $90.4B of marketable equity securities; long-term debt was $125.1B.
- Cloud backlog reached $561B; just over 55% is expected to be recognized as revenue over the next 24 months.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- Supply constraints and third-party compute reliance may pressure Cloud margins; capacity limitations are affecting the ability to meet strong demand.
- FX is expected to be a larger Q4 headwind; Search growth also faces tougher comparisons after late-2025 acceleration.
- AI Mode query growth carried lower monetization, contributing to Search growth-rate deceleration; ads are being scaled as experiments demonstrate results.
- Infrastructure investment, inventory buildup and contractual commitments create cash-flow exposure; future purchase commitments were $924B and pre-commencement leases and other obligations were $152B, at maximum contractual amounts.
- Consumer agentic products require high safety, trust, security and governance standards, while broad deployment also depends on model efficiency, compute, latency and secure runtime capacity.


### Significant events and developments

- Gemini 4 Argon and Carbon launched; Argon developer-platform usage in its first week ramped 3.5x faster than Gemini 3, while Carbon is rolling out first to trusted cyber defenders.
- Gemini app reached 1B users; YouTube Music and Premium exceeded 150M subscribers, including trials.
- Ads in AI Mode are expanding internationally, with multi-turn ads launching in the U.S.; Universal Commerce Protocol supports direct checkout for hundreds of thousands of brands.
- Waymo surpassed 300M autonomous miles and operates in 15 U.S. cities; public service opened in San Antonio, San Diego and Tampa.
- YouTube Shopping added Amazon, while expanded partnerships included American Express, Unity, Oracle and Crux AI for cloud and AI initiatives.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Q3 CapEx was $51.4B, mostly for AI technical infrastructure; 65% of Q3 infrastructure investment was servers and networking, and 35% data centers.
- Cash and marketable securities were $265.1B against $125.1B long-term debt; the board declared a $0.22-per-share quarterly cash dividend payable in December.
- Multi-year infrastructure, inventory, lease and energy agreements supported capacity expansion; maximum future purchase commitments were $924B, plus $152B of pre-commencement leases and other obligations.
- TPU inventory is built after customer contracts are signed, with cash outflows preceding revenue and customer billing; additional sales are expected through 2027.


### Notes to the financial statements

- Other income and expenses included a negative $8.2B impact, primarily from an unrealized loss on the SpaceX investment following a Q3 stock-price decline.
- Operating cash flow and FCF included a $5.2B cash payment for a 2018 EC fine after the appeal process concluded.
- Q3 infrastructure spending included servers and networking equipment with generally six-year useful lives, and data centers with useful lives of seven to 40 years.


### Market and industry conditions

- Demand for AI infrastructure and solutions is strong; Cloud growth reflected both AI and core GCP, while TPU demand spans enterprises, AI labs and financial customers.
- Search query growth was the highest in five years; AI Mode queries are longer than traditional searches and increasingly multimodal, while monetization remains lower in AI Mode.
- Search growth comparisons reflected FX, retail impacts from the EU de minimis exemption change, and normalized media and entertainment spend after a strong Q2.
