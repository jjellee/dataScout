# Cadence Design Systems, Inc. — Q3 2026 (2026-10-26)

## 요약(stockanalysis 자동 생성)

### Executive summary

- Q3 revenue grew 22% YoY; all key metrics exceeded guidance, with record backlog of $8.2B and strong demand across product groups.
- 2026 revenue growth outlook raised to 21%; midpoint non-GAAP EPS outlook increased by $0.30 to $8.40.


### Financial highlights

- Q3 revenue was $1.633B; GAAP operating margin 26.2%, non-GAAP operating margin 44.1%, GAAP EPS $1.05 and non-GAAP EPS $2.11.
- Operating cash flow was $424M; cash balance $1.452B and principal debt outstanding $2.650B.
- Recurring revenue grew 18% YoY, versus 11% in the prior-year quarter; growth reflected agentic AI, underlying software tools, design starts, IP and hardware.


### Outlook and guidance

- FY 2026 revenue $6.387B-$6.417B; GAAP operating margin 28.2%-29.2%, non-GAAP 44.2%-45.2%; GAAP EPS $4.84-$4.90, non-GAAP EPS $8.37-$8.43.
- FY 2026 operating cash flow expected at ~$2B; plan to use >50% of free cash flow for share repurchases.
- Q4 revenue guided to $1.695B-$1.725B; GAAP operating margin 30%-31%, non-GAAP 44%-45%; GAAP EPS $1.23-$1.29, non-GAAP EPS $2.19-$2.25.
- Management expects FY 2026 revenue growth of 21%, operating margin of 44.7% and EPS of $8.40 at outlook midpoints; Rule of 40 measure expected above 65%.


### Segment performance

- YoY revenue growth: core EDA 20%, IP 24% and SDA 30%; recurring revenue grew 18%.
- Core EDA logged 16 new full-flow wins; Palladium and Protium delivered another record quarter, while Spectre FX gained 2 nm projects.
- IP had a record Tensilica revenue quarter; SDA momentum included Allegro displacement at an EV customer and Millennium/Reality Digital Twin selection by Switch.
- Hardware demand remained strong, with a strong Q4 pipeline and expectation for another record year; growth can vary with product deliveries and installation schedules.


### Key financial ratios and metrics

- Q3 non-GAAP operating margin was 44.1% versus GAAP margin of 26.2%; full-year non-GAAP margin midpoint is 44.7%.
- DSO was 66 days; cash was $1.452B against $2.650B principal debt.
- Management cited roughly $1.1B revenue growth and ~$500M non-GAAP operating profit growth for 2026; Hexagon D&E weighs on incremental margins this year.


### Risk factors and uncertainties

- FY outlook assumes current export-control regulations remain substantially similar for the rest of 2026.
- Hardware revenue may be lumpy quarter to quarter due to delivery and customer installation timing.
- Backlog and bookings convert to revenue based on arrangement structure and delivery timing.


### Significant events and developments

- Introduced Maestro, domain-specialized AI models for chip and system design; initial tests showed up to 30% higher quality and 5x token efficiency versus general-purpose models.
- AI Super Agents had >100 active customer engagements; ChipStack RTL output achieved average 24% area and 18% power reduction versus foundation-model-generated code.
- Customer examples included MediaTek verification coverage improvement of 50%-70% and 1.4x-2x lower engineering effort; Renesas reported 34x productivity improvement with InnoStack.
- Nokia reported 5x acceleration in key design and analysis workflows using AuraStack; Socionext expects up to 5x productivity gain with InnoStack while maintaining design quality.
- Strategic agreements expanded with a global systems customer and a top telco; the telco named Cadence its primary design partner.


### Capital allocation and financing

- Repurchased $400M of shares in Q3; the board authorized an additional $2.5B for the repurchase program.
- FY 2026 plan targets share repurchases exceeding 50% of free cash flow.


### Notes to the financial statements

- Q3 G&A expense reflected annual charitable contributions recognized in Q3 versus Q4 last year; management said not to extrapolate the timing impact into the underlying run rate.
- Hexagon D&E is expected to contribute about $160M revenue in 2026 with little operating profit, weighing on incremental margin; integration is progressing and actions are underway to improve performance next year.


### Market and industry conditions

- Management described design activity across AI, HPC, memory, analog, automotive and systems as exceptionally strong, with share gains across EDA, IP and hardware.
- AI agents are viewed as demand accelerators for core EDA, potentially driving more design exploration and consumption of underlying tools.
- TSMC’s roadmap presentation cited 48x chip-size growth over five years, driven by scaling and 3D-IC; larger designs and more chiplets support demand for verification hardware and die-to-die IP.
- System companies now account for 45% of business, with more nontraditional designers—including space, finance and automotive players—pursuing custom chips.
